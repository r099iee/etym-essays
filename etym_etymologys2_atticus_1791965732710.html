Note: the origin of Ἀττική is disputed. Antiquity connected it with ἄκτη "headland, coast", and the mythical king Atthis was invented as an eponym; modern scholarship often treats the name as pre-Greek. No Proto-Indo-European source can be demonstrated, so the chain begins at the attested Greek place-name. Latin Atticus is simply the Greek adjective Latinised, used as a cognomen ex loco.
