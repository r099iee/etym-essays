percept

↓
English (plural noun) (modern)
percepts
"mental objects or impressions of perception"

Note: The noun percept was deliberately coined in 19th-century philosophical psychology by analogy with concept (Latin conceptum, from concipere). It directly borrows the Latin substantivized neuter participle perceptum ("something perceived") to serve as a precise technical term distinct from the mental faculty or cognitive act of perception.
