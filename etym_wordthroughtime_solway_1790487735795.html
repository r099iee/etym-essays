- 1218 Sulewad as ford - a muddy crossing at Eskmouth; practical, local, legal meeting-place
- 13th-16th c. Solewath to Solway water - ford-name spreads to whole upper estuary; Border, reiver, administrative
- 1542-1707 frontier firth - Solway Moss battle, Border laws, smuggling; martial and illicit
- 1815-1824 Scott's Solway - Guy Mannering and Redgauntlet romanticise salmon-spearing and lugger chases; literary and heroic
- 1830s Ruskin boyhood measure - Solway fishermen as the standard of hard work kings must beat; moralised and admired
- Modern conservation Solway - SAC, Ramsar, AONB and partnership; ecological, managed, bird-haunted
- Contemporary everyday Solway - weather forecasts, salmon labels, ferry news; familiar and domestic
