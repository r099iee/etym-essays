• Individually: Similar because both mean considering or treating items or persons one by one; differs because individually is a formal, analytical term emphasizing the distinct identity and personal characteristics of each member, whereas singly emphasizes serial separation or the physical absence of companions.
  • Separately: Similar because both describe executing actions away from a group or collective whole; differs because separately emphasizes physical partition, spatial disconnection, or distinct categorization (keeping things apart), while singly highlights the numerical unit of one at a time.
  • One by one: Similar because both denote serial progression of individuals in order; differs because one by one is a phrasal idiom evocative of casual, visual pacing (like people passing through a gate), while singly is a compact, formal adverb favored in academic, legal, and perceptual prose.
  • Solitarily: Similar because both evoke being alone without company; differs because solitarily carries strong emotional and existential connotations of loneliness, seclusion, or living in isolated exile, whereas singly is predominantly neutral, procedural, and descriptive.
  • Alone: Similar because both mean unaccompanied by others; differs because alone is a versatile adjective and adverb that can imply emotional abandonment or exclusive ownership ("he alone knows the truth"), while singly focuses on the method of action or discrete enumeration.
  • Severally: Similar because both describe individual accountability apart from a group; differs because severally is a specialized legal term (as in "jointly and severally liable"), meaning each party is individually responsible for an entire legal obligation, a nuance absent from singly.
  • Singularly: Similar because both derive from the same Latin root singulus; differs decisively because singularly means exceptionally, remarkably, or strangely (e.g., "singularly gifted" or "singularly inappropriate"), whereas modern singly strictly means one at a time or unaccompanied.
  • Serially: Similar because both describe events occurring in sequential order; differs because serially emphasizes temporal continuity and structured progression within an ongoing series or chain, whereas singly focuses on the discrete independence of each unit.
  • Piecemeal: Similar because both describe addressing things in separate fragments rather than all at once; differs because piecemeal implies a fragmented, haphazard, gradual, or unsystematic process over time, whereas singly simply denotes one at a time with full deliberation.
  • Unaided: Similar because both describe accomplishing a task without assistance; differs because unaided specifically highlights the absence of mechanical, financial, or personal help, whereas singly emphasizes the solitary state of the agent.
