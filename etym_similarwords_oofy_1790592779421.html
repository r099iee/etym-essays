• wealthy: Similar because it denotes possession of abundant money and assets; differs because it is formal and neutral, while oofy is slangy, dated, and emphasizes ready cash and comic envy.
  • loaded: Similar because it is slang for very rich and flush with money; differs because it is contemporary and blunter, while oofy is vintage 1920s and more affectionate.
  • affluent: Similar because it means flowing with wealth and prosperity; differs because it is polite and sociological, while oofy is clubman banter focused on touchability.
  • flush: Similar because it means having plenty of ready money at the moment; differs because flush is temporary and situational, while oofy suggests habitual moneyed status.
  • moneyed: Similar because it describes the class of people with money; differs because moneyed can imply established wealth, while oofy often implies new, spendable, slightly vulgar cash.
  • well-heeled: Similar because it is idiomatic for rich and well-provided; differs because it images shoes and appearance, while oofy images the grunt of cash itself.
  • plush: Similar because in slang it can suggest luxury and richness; differs because plush primarily describes lavish surroundings, while oofy describes the person who pays for them.
