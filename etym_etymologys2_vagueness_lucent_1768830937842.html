VAGUENESS

↓
PIE (uncertain)
*h₁wegʷʰ-
"to move, wander"

↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
vagus
"wandering, vague"

↓
French (12th c.)
vague
"vague, indefinite"

↓
English (16th c.)
vague
"vague, unclear"

↓
English (modern)
vagueness
"unclearness or lack
of sharp detail"

Debate: the PIE root *h₁wegʷʰ- is uncertain; it may connect with Sanskrit vrajati "he wanders", but the reconstruction is disputed.
