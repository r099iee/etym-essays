• Alighted: Similar because both describe a bird landing on a surface; differs because alighted describes the act of landing itself (arrival), while perched describes the settled, balanced state of rest after landing.
  • Roosted: Similar because both describe birds resting; differs because roosted specifically means sleeping or resting at night, usually on a regular perch in a hen-house or tree.
  • Sat: Similar because both describe being in a seated position; differs because sat is general and stable, while perched adds elevation, narrowness, and precarious balance.
  • Stood: Similar because both describe an upright stationary position; differs because stood is weight-bearing and grounded, while perched implies a narrow support and height.
  • Balanced: Similar because both describe equilibrium on a narrow support; differs because balanced emphasises the physical equilibrium, while perched emphasises the resting or alighting and the elevated setting.
  • Squatted: Similar because both describe a low, compact seated position; differs because squatted is close to the ground and implies crouching, the opposite of the elevation of perched.
