embalm

Note: the -l- of Greek bálsamon points to a later Semitic variant (Aramaic balsāmā); the precise Semitic dialect is debated, though a Semitic origin is certain. The meaning narrowed from “to treat with balm” to “to preserve a corpse” in the late medieval period, and extended to chemical preservation only in the 19th century.
