• Ensure: Similar because both mean to make certain, guarantee, or secure an outcome; differs because modern standard English (especially British and formal American editing) strictly restricts 'ensure' to guaranteeing an outcome ('ensure victory'), while reserving 'insure' for financial policies of indemnification.
  • Assure: Similar because both involve establishing certainty and removing anxiety; differs because 'assure' is directed toward a human listener to comfort them or instill confidence ('she assured him that everything was fine'), rather than taking out a financial policy or guaranteeing an objective result.
  • Indemnify: Similar because both describe legal mechanisms for financial protection; differs because 'indemnify' is a formal legal term meaning to compensate someone for harm or loss already incurred, or to grant legal exemption from liability.
  • Underwrite: Similar because both are commercial insurance activities; differs because 'underwrite' is the specific action performed by the financial institution or insurer who evaluates the risk and signs their name to assume liability in exchange for premium payments.
  • Hedge: Similar because both are financial risk-management strategies; differs because 'hedging' involves taking an offsetting investment position (such as options or futures) in financial markets to reduce the risk of adverse price movements, rather than purchasing a commercial insurance policy.
  • Guarantee: Similar because both involve making an outcome certain or promising quality; differs because a 'guarantee' is a formal pledge or warranty regarding the durability, quality, or performance of a product or service.
