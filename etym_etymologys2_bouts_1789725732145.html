Note: OED derives bout as a variant of bought bend, loop, from bugan, via the sense of a circuit or turn of work. Etymonline agrees (1540s a circuit). The link is phonological shortening of -ought to -out in some dialects. An alternative derivation from to bout sift is rejected. Development from bend to round to contest to illness-spell is analogical and well attested.
