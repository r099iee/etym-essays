Note: Aristotle recorded in his Meteorology that Graikoi was the archaic name of the people later known as Hellenes, native to the mountainous region around Dodona in Epirus. Historical linguists traditionally connect Graikos to the Proto-Indo-European root *ǵerh₂- ('to grow old', hence 'elders' or 'venerable ones', cognate with Greek geron 'old man' and graia 'old woman'), though some modern scholars consider it an indigenous Epirote tribal ethnonym of Illyrian or Pre-Greek origin. The Romans encountered this tribal name through the Greek colonies of Magna Graecia (such as Cumae) and generalized Graecus to refer to all Hellenes, a designation that subsequently passed into every major Western European language.
