Note: the noun ply (a layer, strand), first recorded in the late 14th c., is a separate borrowing of Old French pli “a fold”, also from Latin plicāre; it is a homograph of the verb, not a sense development of it. The PIE root shape *pleḱ- is standard but reconstructed.
