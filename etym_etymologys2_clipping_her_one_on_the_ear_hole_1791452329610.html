clip — “to strike smartly”

Old Norse (9th–13th c.)
klippa
"to cut, shear, clip"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200)
clippen
"to cut, shear"
↓
English (16th c.)
clip
"to strike sharply"
↓
English (modern)
clipping
"striking smartly"
Note: the “strike” sense is probably a development of the cutting sense, a sharp cut being felt as a sharp blow. The ultimate origin of the Germanic verb is echoic and uncertain; no Proto-Germanic antecedent is securely attested, so the chain begins with the Old Norse form.
