Note: Fencing French masculine le fort ('the strong part') entered English in the 17th century with an unetymological terminal -e, likely influenced by the French feminine form forte or Italian musical forte. Meanwhile, Latin fortis derives from Proto-Italic *forktis, related to PIE *bʰerǵʰ- ('high, fortified') or *dʰerǵʰ- ('firm, steadfast').
