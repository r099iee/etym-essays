- [14th century the Great Flood] - ['The Deluge' names Noah's biblical Flood; grand, solemn, providential catastrophe]
- [17th century any overwhelming flood] - [Extended from scripture to any devastating inundation; retains biblical scale and the sense of total, world-remaking destruction]
- [18th-19th century figurative torrent] - [Applied to floods of words, mail, and requests — a great quantity arriving at once, keeping the feeling of being overwhelmed]
- [20th century to present figurative overload] - [Common metaphorically (a deluge of emails, data, information); the catastrophic grandeur softens into modern overload, though the biblical echo of judgment remains]
