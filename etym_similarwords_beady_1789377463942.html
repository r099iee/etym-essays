• Piercing: Similar because both characterize eyes that seem to look deeply and sharply into an observer; differs because piercing emphasizes acute emotional intensity, intellectual penetration, or discomforting depth, whereas beady emphasizes smallness, roundness, and dark, shiny opacity.
  • Shrewd: Similar because both can describe a look indicating calculating intelligence or sharp discernment; differs because shrewd is an abstract intellectual or psychological trait, whereas beady is an explicit physical description of round, gleaming ocular morphology.
  • Beadlike: Similar because both denote an explicit physical resemblance to small spherical beads; differs because beadlike is a neutral, literal geometric comparison applied to inanimate items (such as beadlike seeds or nodules), whereas beady carries heavy idiomatic and stylistic resonance when applied to eyes, demeanor, and sweat.
  • Glinting: Similar because both describe eyes or surfaces that reflect bright points of light; differs because glinting focuses exclusively on the flashing, momentary reflection of light, whereas beady specifies the small, spherical, fixed physical shape of the eyes themselves.
  • Globular: Similar because both denote rounded, spherical forms; differs because globular is a formal, scientific morphological term used in chemistry, anatomy, and astronomy without any of the vivid, suspicious, or rodent-like connotations inherent in beady.
  • Punctate: Similar because both describe surfaces marked by tiny points or dots; differs because punctate is a technical medical and biological term describing patterns of tiny spots or lesions, whereas beady evokes raised, shiny, three-dimensional globules.
