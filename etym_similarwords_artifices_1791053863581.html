• Devices: Similar because they are contrived tools or schemes; differs because device is neutral-technical, artifice adds cleverness or cunning.
  • Contrivances: Similar because they are ingeniously made things; differs because contrivance stresses mechanical ingenuity, artifice stresses mental cunning.
  • Contraptions: Similar because they are odd ingenious devices; differs because contraption is homely and often clumsy, artifice is skilful and elegant.
  • Gadgets: Similar because they are handy inventions; differs because gadget is modern-small-practical, artifice is literary and can be a ruse.
  • Stratagems: Similar because they are cunning plans; differs because stratagem is military-scheming, artifice broader from tools to manners.
  • Ruses: Similar because they deceive; differs because ruse is purely deceptive, artifice can be innocent ingenuity.
  • Wiles: Similar because they ensnare by cunning; differs because wiles suggest bewitching allure, artifices suggest constructed tricks.
  • Ploys: Similar because they are manoeuvres to gain advantage; differs because ploy is short-term tactical, artifice can be elaborate system.
  • Subterfuges: Similar because they evade by trick; differs because subterfuge stresses evasion/excuse, artifice stresses clever construction.
  • Dodges: Similar because they are tricks to avoid; differs because dodge is colloquial-quick, artifice formal-elaborate.
  • Deceptions: Similar because they mislead; differs because deception is result-focused, artifice is means-focused.
  • Expedients: Similar because they are practical makeshifts; differs because expedient stresses convenience, artifice stresses ingenuity.
  • Inventions: Similar because they are newly devised things; differs because invention stresses novelty, artifice stresses cleverness/cunning.
  • Techniques: Similar because they are skilful methods; differs because technique is neutral-learnable, artifice hints at hidden cunning.
  • Manoeuvres: Similar because they are skilful moves; differs because manoeuvre is procedural, artifice is trick-laden.
