Note: Latin pius has no secure PIE etymology. De Vaan lists it as of unknown origin, often tentatively linked to *peh2- protect (cf. pastor, pabulum) or to *pew- purify, but neither is proven and the morphology is unclear. The chain above gives the usual tentative root with explicit caution; all stages from Latin pius onward are fully attested.
