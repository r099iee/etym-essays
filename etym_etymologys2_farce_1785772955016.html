Debate: the origin of Latin farcīre is uncertain; it may derive from PIE *bhrekw- "to cram together" (cf. frequens "crowded"), but this is speculative. The dramatic sense came from comic passages "stuffed" into medieval liturgical texts (pseudo-Latin farsia, 13th c.) and religious plays.
