Debate note: The ultimate origin is uncertain. French friser has been linked to a reconstructed Germanic *frisil- meaning "curl," but no attested ancestor survives; some scholars instead derive friser from an earlier Romance sense "to brush, to graze." The culinary "fry crisp" sense of 1838 is an internal English extension, possibly reinforced by a blend with fry + sizzle.
