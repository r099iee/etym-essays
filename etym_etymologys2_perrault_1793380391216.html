Note: The French surname Perrault is a patronymic hypocoristic formed from Pierre (the French development of Latin Petrus, from Greek Pétros "stone") combined with the diminutive suffix -ault (derived from Old French -ot / -aud, ultimately of Germanic origin *-wald / *-ald). The spelling with -ault reflects standard Early Modern French chancery orthography featuring a non-pronounced parasitic l before t. Through Charles Perrault's central leadership in the *Querelle des Anciens et des Modernes* and his world-famous *Contes*, his patronymic surname became permanently embedded in international literary criticism and folklore.
