• crowfoot: Similar because the formal folk synonyms for Ranunculus species; differs because broader, includes white water-crowfoots and less familiar to non-botanists.
  • celandine: Similar because another small glossy yellow spring ranunculid of woods and banks; differs because earlier flowering, heart-shaped leaves and fewer petals.
  • dandelion: Similar because a common yellow meadow composite of the same habitat; differs because aster family with milky sap, clock seed-heads and toothed leaves, not cup-shaped.
  • marsh marigold / kingcup: Similar because a large golden cup-shaped wetland ranunculid often confused with buttercups; differs because much larger, with round leaves and marsh habitat.
  • meadow-rue: Similar because a meadow ranunculid sharing family and habitat; differs because feathery, petal-less and wind-pollinated, lacking showy yellow cups.
  • cinquefoil: Similar because a low five-petalled yellow potentilla of pastures; differs because rose family, palmate leaves and duller, non-glossy petals.
  • hawkbit / cat's-ear: Similar because yellow dandelion-like meadow flowers; differs because composites with strap florets, not true cups.
