NOTE — DEBATED / NUANCED STAGES: (1) The origin of Old French frete is uncertain: it is usually traced to a Frankish (Germanic) source such as *fretil or the verb fretier "to bind with a ferrule", but the details are debated and no secure pre-Old-French ancestor is attested — the dictionaries mark the origin "uncertain, probably Frankish". (2) The guitar-fret sense (16th c.) is a specialised offshoot of the same band/ferrule image: the metal ridge on a fingerboard was originally a "fret" or band. Both chains use only attested forms; the Frankish stage is flagged as probable-but-uncertain rather than asserted.
