• Inseparable: Similar because both mean not to be parted or severed; differs because inseparable is descriptive of things habitually or logically found together and can be broken by circumstance, while indissoluble asserts that the bond cannot be dissolved at all.
  • Inextricable: Similar in meaning impossible to disentangle; differs because inextricable stresses entanglement and complexity (a knot that cannot be worked loose), while indissoluble stresses the integrity of a bond or compound that cannot be dissolved.
  • Insoluble: Similar because both derive from Latin solvere and both mean "not capable of being dissolved"; differs because insoluble applies to solids in a liquid and to unsolvable problems, while indissoluble applies to bonds, unions, and compounds that cannot be undone — the two are not interchangeable.
  • Indivisible: Similar in meaning not to be split into parts; differs because indivisible is used of wholes that cannot be divided (atoms, political unity), while indissoluble is used of bonds and compounds that cannot be dissolved into their elements.
  • Unbreakable: Similar in meaning incapable of being broken; differs because unbreakable is plain, physical, and general (a material, a promise), while indissoluble is Latinate and applies specifically to what cannot be dissolved or undone.
  • Permanent: Similar in denoting lasting endurance; differs because permanent is a temporal word about duration, while indissoluble is a modal word asserting that dissolution is impossible, not merely that it has not happened.
  • Everlasting: Similar in denoting endless duration; differs because everlasting is about time, while indissoluble is about the impossibility of being dissolved or undone.
  • Immutable: Similar in meaning not subject to change; differs because immutable is about change and alteration, while indissoluble is about dissolution and the separation of the parts of a bond or compound.
  • Irrevocable: Similar in meaning impossible to call back or undo; differs because irrevocable applies to decisions, acts, and gifts, while indissoluble applies to the bond or union itself.
  • Indestructible: Similar in meaning incapable of being destroyed; differs because indestructible is broad and physical, while indissoluble is specific to dissolving, loosening, and separation.
  • Indissolvable: Similar as a near-doublet with the same meaning; differs because indissolvable is the rarer, less standard form built on dissolvable, while indissoluble is the standard form.
