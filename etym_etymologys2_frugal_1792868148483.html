frugal

Note: Latin frux is traditionally grouped with fruor, to enjoy, and through it with a PIE root for enjoyment and use, but de Vaan marks the deeper connection as uncertain; the chain above therefore starts from securely attested Latin and notes the earlier stage as debated rather than inventing it.
