Chain 2: take / taker
Proto-Germanic (c.500 BCE - 200 CE)
*takan
"to grasp, touch"
↓
Old Norse (8th - 11th c.)
taka
"to take, grasp"
↓
Middle English (c.1200 - 1500)
taken / taker

↓
Modern English (17th - 19th c.)
undertaker
"one who takes on burial duty"

Note: take has no secure PIE ancestor; Old Norse taka from PG *takan is accepted but deeper PIE *deh2g- link is debated, so the chain starts at Proto-Germanic.
