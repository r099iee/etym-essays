- The place and title - From the medieval town of Mirandola in northern Italy the name becomes a dynastic title (the Counts of Mirandola), giving Giovanni Pico his full style, Pico della Mirandola.
- Renaissance humanist fame - In the late 15th century the name is attached to Pico's Oration on the Dignity of Man, the manifesto of Renaissance humanism, making Mirandola a byword for syncretic intellectual ambition.
- Modern scholarly icon - Today Pico della Mirandola is studied as a type of the brilliant, many-sided Renaissance humanist, and 'Mirandola' functions as a cultured allusion in Renaissance studies and literary criticism.
