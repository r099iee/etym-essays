Note: The apparent semantic paradox—calling a graduation ceremony that concludes years of study a commencement ("beginning")—arose from the guild structure of medieval European universities. A student completing their studies incepted into the guild of scholars, officially "commencing" their active career as a licensed master (magister) authorized to teach others. The ceremony marked the beginning of their professional mastership rather than the conclusion of their schooling.
