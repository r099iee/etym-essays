- Humoral-medical sense - a technical term in early physiology for the pathological thickening or coagulation of the bodily humours, tied to decay and stagnation.

- Alchemical & chemical sense - adopted into alchemy and early chemistry to describe any gradual densifying or inspissation of a substance, losing some of its moral weight.

- Archaic & clinical sense - retreats into specialized, historical registers, describing a gradual increase in density or viscosity in biological or chemical processes.

- Contemporary sense - a rare, deliberately archaic noun for creeping, morbid thickening, evoking insidious loss of vitality and the slow congealing of what was once fluid and healthy.
