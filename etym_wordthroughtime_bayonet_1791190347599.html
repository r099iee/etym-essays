- Bayonne plug dagger (1640s) - ingenious peasant-cutler expedient, admiration for doubling musket into spear
- Vauban socket revolution (1680s–1700s) - universal infantry arm, abolishes pikeman, perception as modern rational efficiency
- 18th–19th c. cold steel cult - bayonet charge as moral test and battle-winner, heroic and terrifying prestige from Fontenoy to Borodino
- American Civil War to 1914 - still decisive in memory, but rifled fire makes closing costly, doubt creeps in
- World War I disillusion - wire, machine guns, and trenches marginalize the charge, image shifts to futile drill and sawback atrocity stories
- Post-1945 survival - rarely bloodied but retained for guard, ceremony, and aggression conditioning, museum and parade-ground familiarity
