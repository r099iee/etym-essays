• Chattogram: Similar because it is the same city under its 2018 official Bengali name; differs because Chattogram is the current administrative form, while Chittagong is the historical Anglicized form found in older texts.
  • Chittagong Hill Tracts: Similar because it denotes the upland region administered from Chittagong; differs because it names the territory and its peoples, not the port city itself.
  • Dhaka: Similar because it is another major Bangladeshi city and administrative center; differs because Dhaka is the inland capital, while Chittagong is the coastal commercial port.
  • Kolkata (Calcutta): Similar because it is a historic Bay of Bengal colonial port; differs because it lies in West Bengal, India, with a distinct Bengali metropolitan history.
  • Rangoon / Yangon: Similar because it is another Bay of Bengal colonial port linking South and Southeast Asia; differs because it is the Burmese capital-port with a different linguistic and ethnic hinterland.
  • Porto Grande: Similar because it was the 16th-century Portuguese name for Chittagong; differs because it reflects the early European trading-post perspective, not the indigenous Bengali name.
  • Cox's Bazar: Similar because it is a nearby southeastern Bangladeshi coastal district; differs because it is known for beaches and fishing rather than heavy industry and container shipping.
  • Arakan / Rakhine: Similar because it is the adjacent coastal region across the Naf River with intertwined history; differs because it lies in Myanmar with a distinct Arakanese Buddhist-Muslim history.
  • Chakma: Similar because the Chakma are the largest people of the Chittagong Hill Tracts; differs because Chakma names an ethnic group, while Chittagong names a place.
  • Marma: Similar because the Marma are another major Hill Tracts people; differs for the same reason — an ethnonym versus a toponym.
  • Entrepôt: Similar because it describes Chittagong's function as a transshipment hub; differs because it is a generic economic term, not a place name.
  • Hinterland: Similar because the Hill Tracts form Chittagong port's forested hinterland; differs because hinterland is a geographic concept, not a proper noun.
