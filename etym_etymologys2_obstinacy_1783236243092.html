Debated: English obstinacy is formed from Latin obstinatus "stubborn" plus the suffix -cy; some sources instead posit a direct loan of Latin obstinacia "obstinacy". The verb obstinare combines ob- "against" with *stinare, a weakened variant of stare "to stand".
