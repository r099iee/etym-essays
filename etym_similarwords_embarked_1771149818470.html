• boarded: Similar because both mean to get onto a vehicle; differs because boarded is mechanical and routine (a bus, train, plane) with no sense of adventure or commitment, while embarked implies a significant, committed departure.
  • set sail: Similar because both mean to begin a sea journey; differs because set sail is specifically nautical (raising the sails) and purely literal, while embarked also has broad figurative uses (embark on a career).
  • launched: Similar because both mean to begin something new; differs because launched usually refers to an external thing (launch a product, a rocket) — transitive — while embarked refers to the person's own entry into an undertaking and is intransitive with "on."
  • commenced: Similar because both mean to begin; differs because commenced is abstract and formal, used of proceedings (the trial commenced), with no physical departure, while embarked implies stepping into a journey or enterprise.
  • began: Similar because both mean to start; differs because began is fully general and colorless, while embarked adds the image of departure, commitment, and risk.
  • undertook: Similar because both mean to take on a task; differs because undertook emphasizes accepting responsibility for a task, while embarked emphasizes beginning a journey or venture.
  • entered upon: Similar because both mean to begin a course of action; differs because entered upon is formal and legalistic, while embarked retains the nautical image of boarding a vessel.
  • set forth: Similar because both mean to begin a journey; differs because set forth is archaic and literary and implies motion outward, while embarked can be literal boarding or figurative commitment.
  • departed: Similar because both involve leaving; differs because departed stresses leaving a place (what you leave behind), while embarked stresses entering a vessel or undertaking (what you join).
  • sallied forth: Similar because both mean to set out on an expedition; differs because sallied forth is archaic, military, and heroic in tone, while embarked is neutral and current.
