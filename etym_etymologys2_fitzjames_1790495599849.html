Note: Latin filius from suckle-root is the standard account (de Vaan: suckling), but the exact PIE formation (*dheh₁-ileh₂) is debated. Ya'aqov to James passes via Greek Iakobos, Late Latin Jacomus/James (with J for Dj), Old French James — the same name as Jacob and Diego. FitzJames is thus a double compound joining a Latin-derived prefix to a Hebrew-derived name through French.
