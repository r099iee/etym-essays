Note: OED derives caftan as a borrowing from Turkish qaftan from Persian khaftan. Deeper Persian origin is obscure with no secure PIE root; no guesswork beyond Persian is offered. Russian kaftan is a parallel borrowing from the same Turkish source, not the direct parent of English.
