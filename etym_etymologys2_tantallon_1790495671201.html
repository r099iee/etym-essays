Note: the ultimate origin is debated and unresolved. Gaelic teine-talamh fire-ground and Brittonic din-tal fort-end have both been suggested but neither is supported by early forms or parallels, and authorities mark the name as of uncertain origin. No PIE root can be responsibly assigned; the chain above starts from the first attestation rather than inventing one.
