- Literal hunting term (15th-16th c.) - To hunt rabbits with a caged ferret; rural, technical, neutral.
- Verbal harassment sense (early 17th c.) - To worry or drive like prey; aggressive, persecutory nuance (ferret Papists from hiding).
- Generalized search metaphor (late 17th-18th c.) - To search tenaciously for secrets, goods, meanings; admiring or nosy depending on context.
- Modern investigative praise (19th-20th c.) - Journalists, scholars, detectives ferret out truth; positive connotation of diligence against cover-up.
- Present phrasal verb - Ferret out (find) vs ferret about (rummage) split; informal-vivid, never fully formal; animal origin largely forgotten.
