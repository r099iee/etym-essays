=== ELEMENT 1: CHIMNEY ===
Ancient Greek (5th–4th c. BCE; Herodotus, Aristophanes)
káminos (κάμινος)
"furnace, smelting forge, oven, kiln"
(ancient Mediterranean technical term for high-temperature enclosed hearths)
↓
Classical Latin (2nd c. BCE – 1st c. CE; Cato, Cicero, Pliny)
camīnus
"furnace, forge, smelting kiln; fireplace"
↓
Late Latin / Vulgar Latin (4th–6th c. CE)
camināta / caminus
"room heated by a fireplace; hearth, stove"
↓
Old French (12th c.)
cheminee
"fireplace, hearth, smoke-flue"
↓
Anglo-Norman / Middle English (13th–14th c.)
chimeneye / chemne / chimenei
"fireplace, hearth, smoke-conduit"
↓
Modern English (present)
chimney
"vertical masonry structure venting smoke from a fireplace"

=== ELEMENT 2: POT ===
Late Latin / Proto-Germanic / Low German (reconstructed / attested c. 6th–8th c. CE)
pottus (Late Latin) / *puttaz / *pottaz (Germanic)
"earthen pot, drinking vessel, jar"
(widespread early medieval European term for ceramic earthenware containers)
↓
Late Old English (c. 1000 CE; in glosses and wills)
pott
"pot, vessel, earthenware bowl"
↓
Middle English (12th–14th c.)
pot (plural pottes)
"pot, earthenware vessel, metal cooking pot"
↓
Early Modern to Present English (compound attested late 18th c.)
CHIMNEY-POTS
"ceramic pipes atop chimney stacks to improve draft and discharge smoke"
