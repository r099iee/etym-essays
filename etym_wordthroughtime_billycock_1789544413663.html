- 18th Century (c. 1720–1780) - "Bully-cocked" hats were fashionable cocked tricorn hats worn by swaggering young gallants and bullies in London.
- Mid-19th Century (1849–1860s) - Name shifted/conflated with William Coke (1st Earl of Leicester) who commissioned Lock & Co. to create a stiff bowler hat for gamekeepers.
- Victorian & Edwardian Eras (1870s–1910s) - Became standard British colloquial slang for the everyday hard round felt bowler hat worn across social classes.
- 20th & 21st Centuries (1920s–present) - Transitioned into historical and period literature (notably Sherlock Holmes studies) as an iconic Victorian artifact.
