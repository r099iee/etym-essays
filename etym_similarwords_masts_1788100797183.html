• spar: Similar because both are shipboard poles of timber or metal; differs because spar is the general term for all such poles (yards, booms, gaffs, masts), while mast names the specifically vertical, standing spar that carries the sails.
  • pole: Similar in being a long vertical stick; differs in being wholly unspecialised, without rigging, sail, or maritime force.
  • pylon / tower: Similar because both are tall support structures; differs because these are latticed or concrete land-based structures, not spars on vessels.
  • derrick / crane boom: Similar as tall lifting arms of engineering; differs in mechanical function on land and in being movable rather than fixed in a hull.
  • flagstaff / flagpole: Similar as a vertical pole flying a flag; differs because a flagstaff is a fixed land structure with no sails or rigging attached.
  • yard / yardarm: Similar as sail-carrying timber; differs because yards are the horizontal cross-spars lashed across the mast, not the vertical mast itself.
  • boom: Similar as a spar of the sail-plan; differs because the boom is the horizontal or swinging spar along the foot of a fore-and-aft sail.
  • antenna: Similar as a modern tall signal-carrier; differs by being a wire or metal conductor, not a timber structure, and carries no sails.
