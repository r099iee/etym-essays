Note: The ultimate origin of Old French fagot is unknown; the Greek phakelos "bundle" and Vulgar Latin *facus links are both speculative, and a link to Latin fagus "beech" is unfounded. The offensive slur for a gay man (20th c.) is a separate development from this "bundle of sticks" sense.
