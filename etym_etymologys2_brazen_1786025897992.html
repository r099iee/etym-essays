Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*bʰres- / *bʰerəs-
"to break, crack, burn, brass / copper ore (?)"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*brasną / *brasaz
"brass, metallic alloy, glowing metal"
↓
Old English (c. 9th–10th c.)
bræs (noun) / bræsen (adj)
"made of brass, metallic, hard"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–14th c.)
brasen / brasun
"made of brass; hard, loud, metallic"
↓
Early Modern English (c. late 16th c.)
brazen
"shameless, insolent, bold as brass"
↓
Modern English (present)
brazen
"shamelessly bold, audacious, insolent"
