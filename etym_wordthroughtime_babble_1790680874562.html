- Imitative baby and water sound - ba-ba as natural lip and liquid noise, pre-linguistic and affectionate
- Middle English babelen - to speak confusedly, Tower of Babel echo, foolish or foreign speech
- Early Modern prattling - babbling idiots, babbling politicians, moral condemnation of empty talk
- Romantic nature poetry - babbling brooks become soothing and musical, positive aesthetic transfer
- Developmental psychology - canonical babbling as milestone, reduplicated to variegated, scientific and celebratory
- Contemporary extended use - data babble, media babble, streams and crowds babble, information overload as noise
