- Old English twigge - forked branch, small shoot; everyday woodland word, neutral
- Middle English twigge / twig - shoot, switch, divining rod; rustic and practical
- Early modern dialectal twing - nasalised variant in northern and Scots speech; local and oral
- 19th c. Ruskin's broom twings - fossilised in Praeterita family anecdote; anecdotal, disciplinary, old-world
- Modern twigs, twings extinct - standard twigs for brooms and branches; twings survives only as a textual curiosity and Anki trap for twine/twins/twinges
