Note on the debate: the OED and Etymonline flag the origin of “ripple” as uncertain. The chain above is the most commonly cited route (frequentative of the Germanic “rip” family), but an alternative proposes Middle Dutch rimpelen “to wrinkle” (cf. English rimple) as the donor of the water sense, and the 1660s flax-comb “ripple” is a separate loan from Middle Dutch repel “to beat flax” that merged into the same spelling. No single chain is universally accepted; the PIE stage in particular rests on reconstruction rather than attestation.
