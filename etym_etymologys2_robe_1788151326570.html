Debate note: The step from Germanic *rauba to Old French robe is agreed in outline — invaders’ plunder included clothing, so “spoils” narrowed to “garment” — but the exact transmission is disputed: some scholars posit a Gallo-Roman or late Latin *rauba “goods, clothing” formed on the Germanic root, while others derive Old French robe directly from Frankish *rauba. Both routes agree on the ultimate Germanic origin. The native Old English word for a loose garment (hakele) was displaced.
