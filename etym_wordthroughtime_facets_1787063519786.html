- 1620s - 'small polished face of a cut gem' - introduced from French gem-cutting - technical, lapidary register
- 17th-18th c. - stays in jewellery and crystal trades - a cutter's word, precise and concrete
- 19th c. - 'an aspect or side of a complex thing' - abstract extension into problems and situations - polite, analytical journalism
- 20th c. - 'facets of the personality', 'multi-faceted' - psychological and biographical commonplace; enters crystallography, then computer graphics
- Today - both concrete (gems, crystals, 3D models) and figurative (every field) - flexible, slightly formal, with a sparkling connotation
