Note: the blend of blot plus botch to form blotch is the standard OED and Etymonline account but remains conjectural. Blot itself is of obscure origin, perhaps Scandinavian, compare Old Norse blettr stain, and botch is likewise obscure, perhaps from Middle English bocchen to mend clumsily. No PIE root can be asserted for either without guesswork, so the chain properly begins with attested Middle English forms. The -ed participial suffix is regular.
