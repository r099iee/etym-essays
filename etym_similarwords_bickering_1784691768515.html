• Squabbling: Similar because both mean noisy, petty, undignified quarrelling; differs because squabbling stresses the undignified noise and scuffle, while bickering stresses the rapid, repetitive, snapping quality of the exchange.
  • Quarrelling: Similar because both mean contention between people; differs because quarrelling is the general term and may cover serious disputes, while bickering is restricted to trifling, repetitive disputes.
  • Wrangling: Similar because both mean prolonged, contentious arguing; differs because wrangling suggests stubborn, argumentative dispute over matters of substance or procedure, while bickering is petty and over trifles.
  • Arguing: Similar because both mean exchanging opposing views; differs because arguing is neutral and can be rational and weighty, while bickering is noisy, petty, and irrational.
  • Disputing: Similar because both mean contending in words; differs because disputing is formal and can be scholarly or legal, while bickering is informal and domestic.
  • Scrapping: Similar because both can mean a minor, undignified fight (physical or verbal); differs because scrapping stresses the physical tussle, while bickering is primarily verbal.
  • Sparring: Similar because both mean light, repeated exchanges; differs because sparring is a controlled boxing exercise or friendly argument, while bickering is ill-tempered and petty.
  • Nagging: Similar because both mean persistent, wearying fault-finding; differs because nagging is one-sided complaint (one person harrying another), while bickering is an exchange between two or more.
  • Flickering / Rippling / Pattering (imitative sense): Similar because all name the quick, light, repeated motion or sound that bicker also describes; differs because these are the neutral sound-words for flame, water, and rain, while bickering in that sense is literary and now rare.
