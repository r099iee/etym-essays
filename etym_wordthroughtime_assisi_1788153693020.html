- [Roman Asisium - a modest Umbrian hill-town promoted to a Roman municipium] - [Perception: an ordinary provincial centre, unknown beyond central Italy]
- [1202–1226 - Francis and Clare live and preach there; after Francis’s death the town is already a shrine] - [Perception: a holy city in the making, swarming with pilgrims]
- [1228–1253 - the great basilica rises; Cimabue and Giotto paint the legend of Francis] - [Perception: Assisi becomes one of the sacred capitals of Christendom]
- [14th–15th c. - Franciscan learning and the Sacred Convent; the town is a papal and order stronghold] - [Perception: weighty, monastic, austere]
- [16th–18th c. - the basilica and tombs are enriched; the town slowly declines into a provincial papacy town] - [Perception: holy but sleepy]
- [19th c. - Risorgimento-era rediscovery; the frailty of the basilica after the 1832 earthquake; romantic medievalism] - [Perception: the ruin-framed ‘sweet Umbrian town’ of the Grand Tour]
- [1960s–70s - restoration of the Giotto frescoes; mass pilgrimage tourism arrives] - [Perception: art treasure and pilgrimage economy combined]
- [1986 - John Paul II summons the world’s religious leaders to pray for peace] - [Perception: ‘the spirit of Assisi’ is born — the town becomes the world’s symbol of interfaith encounter]
- [Today - UNESCO (2000); millions of pilgrims and tourists; the name headlines every interreligious gathering] - [Perception: at once cradle of Francis, museum city, and global peace brand]
