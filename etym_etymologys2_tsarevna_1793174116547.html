Note: the ultimate origin of Latin Caesar itself is debated; ancient guesses linked it to caesaries, hair, or to Moorish caesar, elephant, and modern scholars suspect an Etruscan source, so the chain above begins at the first fully attested stage rather than inventing a deeper root.
