• contiguous: Similar in denoting physical nearness or touching; differs because contiguous is strictly technical, of things touching at edges, with no suggestion of a bond, while propinquital fuses the spatial with the relational - proximity itself generating the tie.
  • proximal: Similar in being a formal adjective of nearness; differs because proximal belongs to anatomy and geometry, a neutral position relative to a centre, while propinquital reaches beyond position into kinship and attachment.
  • proximate: Similar in expressing nearness; differs because proximate names closeness in degree, time, or cause rather than the causal generation of a bond that propinquital implies.
  • neighbourly: Similar in suggesting closeness between persons; differs because neighbourly is sociable and chosen, the goodwill of those who live near, while propinquital claims mere adjacency, the brute fact of shared space, as the cause of the bond.
  • adjacent: Similar in lying close by; differs because adjacent is a bare statement of position with no relational consequence, whereas propinquital makes nearness do causal work.
  • nearby: Similar in denoting physical closeness; differs because nearby is colloquial and purely spatial, with none of propinquital's learned, kinship-bearing, quasi-scientific charge.
  • intimate: Similar in denoting close connection between people; differs because intimate implies inwardness and chosen closeness, while propinquital is clinical and accidental - attachment filed under a scientific heading.
  • kindred: Similar in expressing closeness of relation; differs because kindred names kinship by blood or similarity of nature, not the nearness of shared space that propinquital asserts.
  • cognate: Similar in expressing a natural connection; differs because cognate is used of languages and families sharing an origin, while propinquital concerns the physical and social nearness of things and persons.
  • vicinal: Similar in denoting nearness in space; differs because vicinal is a rare, technical term for local proximity, while propinquital carries the psychological claim that proximity produces attachment.
