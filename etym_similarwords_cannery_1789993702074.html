• canning factory: Similar because it is the literal equivalent, a plant that cans food; differs because it is the formal descriptive phrase, while cannery is the compact traditional noun.
  • packing plant: Similar because it processes food at scale; differs because packing need not involve sealed cans — meat and produce are often merely boxed or chilled.
  • packing house: Similar because it prepares harvests for shipment; differs because it is commonest for fruit and meat houses without retorts and seaming lines.
  • processing plant: Similar because it is the modern umbrella term for food factories; differs because it is deliberately neutral, covering freezing, drying, and canning alike.
  • factory: Similar because every cannery is a factory; differs because factory is generic, losing the tin-can and seasonal specificity.
  • works: Similar because it denotes an industrial establishment; differs because it is British-coloured and archaic-sounding beside the American cannery.
  • conservas factory: Similar because it cans fish and vegetables artisanally; differs because it is the prestige Iberian term, implying gourmet tins rather than bulk pack.
