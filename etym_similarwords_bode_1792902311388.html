• Portend: Similar because both mean to foreshadow what is coming; differs because portend is weightier and usually ominous, a formal prophecy of marvels or disasters, while bode lives quietly inside well and ill.
  • Foreshadow: Similar because both cast a shadow of the future onto the present; differs because foreshadow is the literary critic's tool, hinting at plot, while bode is the omen-reader's verdict on fortune.
  • Presage: Similar because both announce coming events as signs; differs because presage can name the feeling of foreboding itself, while bode stays with the sign and its promise.
  • Augur: Similar because both read present signs for future fate; differs because augur recalls Roman priests dissecting birds, technical divination, while bode is homely weather-wisdom.
  • Betoken: Similar because both make one thing a token of another; differs because betoken can simply indicate the present, a smile betokens kindness, while bode always faces forward.
  • Promise: Similar because to bode well is to promise good fortune; differs because promise commits a person to act, while bode commits nobody, the sign merely speaks.
  • Threaten: Similar because to bode ill is to threaten trouble; differs because threaten needs an agent with intent, clouds threatening rain excepted, while bode needs only a sign.
  • Foretell: Similar because both concern knowledge of the future; differs because foretell claims explicit prediction in words, while bode merely implies it through omens.
