• Boors: Similar because both refer to crude, ill-mannered, and unrefined individuals; differs because a boor is characterized by social clumsiness, insensitivity, and lack of etiquette, whereas a Yahoo implies active barbarity, physical or verbal rowdiness, and bestial vulgarity.
  • Philistines: Similar because both describe persons deficient in cultural sophistication and hostile to higher learning; differs because a philistine is smugly materialistic, bourgeois, and indifferent to art or philosophy, whereas a Yahoo is aggressively savage, visceral, and chaotic.
  • Louts: Similar because both describe clumsy, uncultivated, and offensive persons; differs because a lout emphasizes awkward stupidity, physical oafishness, and sluggish rudeness, whereas Yahoo carries connotations of violent malice, noise, and feral degradation.
  • Ruffians: Similar because both designate rough, dangerous, and uncivilized actors; differs because a ruffian focuses on criminality, street toughness, and lawless physical violence, whereas a Yahoo encompasses intellectual backwardness, boorish vulgarity, and animalistic lack of self-control.
  • Barbarians: Similar because both refer to those perceived as uncivilized, wild, and lacking civic polish; differs because barbarian originally designates an outsider or foreigner lacking classical culture, whereas Yahoos are the internal, domestic rot of civilized society itself.
  • Hooligans: Similar because both designate rowdy, disruptive, and antisocial troublemakers; differs because hooligans are typically urban youths engaged in vandalism or gang violence, whereas Yahoo is a broader moral and intellectual archetype applicable to corrupt rulers, loud crowds, or vulgar elites.
  • Ignoramuses: Similar because both designate unlearned and unrefined minds; differs because an ignoramus simply lacks knowledge or education, whereas a Yahoo exhibits active contempt for truth, beauty, and civilized decorum.
  • Vulgarians: Similar because both describe individuals lacking taste and cultural refinement; differs because a vulgarian is often wealthy and ostentatious yet tacky and pretentiously tasteless, whereas a Yahoo is fundamentally feral, crass, and primitive.
  • Brutes: Similar because both depict beings dominated by raw animal nature rather than intellect; differs because a brute emphasizes sheer physical insensibility and animal unreason, whereas Yahoo retains Swift's satirical sting of human nature deliberately degrading itself through moral corruption.
