• Splashed: Similar because both describe movement in water with scattered droplets; differs because splashed is more vigorous and energetic, while dabbled is gentle, light, and repeated — and never carries the figurative "superficial engagement" sense.
  • Paddled: Similar because both describe moving the hands or feet in water gently; differs because paddled emphasises a smooth, propulsive motion (and a canoe paddle), while dabbled stresses light, playful, repeated dipping.
  • Dipped: Similar because both mean briefly submerging something partially in liquid; differs because dipped is a single, deliberate plunge, while dabbled is repeated and playful.
  • Dunked: Similar because both describe putting something in liquid; differs because dunked means fully and forcibly submerging, whereas dabbled is only partial and gentle.
  • Immersed: Similar because both describe putting something into a liquid; differs because immersed means complete submersion (and total involvement), while dabbled is shallow and partial.
  • Waded: Similar because both involve standing in water; differs because waded means walking through water that reaches the legs, while dabbled is a gentle hand-or-foot play at the edge.
  • Moistened: Similar because both describe wetting something; differs because moistened is a dry, functional dampening, while dabbled is playful and watery.
  • Sprinkled: Similar because both describe scattering small amounts of liquid; differs because sprinkled distributes droplets from above, while dabbled involves the hands, feet, or beak in the liquid itself.
  • Spattered: Similar because both describe marks of liquid; differs because spattered implies accidental, irregular splashes, while dabbled is intentional, gentle play.
  • Dappled: Similar because both can describe light or gentle marking (dabbled light); differs because dappled means round spots of contrasting colour or shade (a dappled horse, dappled sunlight), and has no water or superficial-engagement sense.
  • Tinkered (with): Similar because both describe casual, non-serious engagement with an activity; differs because tinkered involves physically fiddling with mechanisms, while dabbled can apply to any subject and never implies hands-on repair.
  • Trifled (with): Similar because both describe treating something lightly; differs because trifled implies foolish or disrespectful play that undervalues the thing, while dabbled is a lighter, more innocent amateurism.
  • Toyed (with): Similar because both describe casual, unserious engagement; differs because toyed suggests flirtation or manipulation, while dabbled suggests merely dipping into an interest.
  • Played at: Similar because both describe doing something unseriously; differs because "played at" can imply pretending or shamming, while dabbled implies genuine (if shallow) contact.
  • Flirted (with): Similar because both describe a tentative, uncommitted approach to an idea; differs because flirted with is even more tentative and is used mainly of dangerous or attractive ideas, while dabbled is the settled word for shallow hobbies.
  • Sampled: Similar because both describe a light, partial experience of something; differs because sampled is a deliberate test or taste of one thing, while dabbled describes ongoing but shallow engagement.
