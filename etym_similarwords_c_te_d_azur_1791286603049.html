• French Riviera: Similar because it names the same resort coast; differs because Riviera is the English touristic term, while Côte d'Azur is the French original with chic Francophone prestige.
  • Riviera: Similar because it denotes a sunny resort littoral; differs because Riviera alone can mean Italian Riviera or any riviera, while Côte d'Azur is specifically French.
  • Costa del Sol: Similar because it is another sun-selling Mediterranean coast brand; differs because it is Spanish (Andalusia) and sells sunshine, not azure blueness.
  • Costa Blanca: Similar because it markets Spanish shoreline by colour (white); differs because its whiteness refers to houses and light, not to Wodehousian high-society tailoring.
  • Amalfi Coast: Similar because it is a glamorous Mediterranean littoral; differs because it is Italian, cliffier and more vertical, without the Anglo-French wintering history.
  • South of France: Similar because it locates the same holiday south; differs because it is vague and English, covering inland Provence, while Côte d'Azur is specifically coastal.
  • Provence: Similar because it overlaps administratively (PACA); differs because Provence is historic inland country of lavender and wine, while the Côte is its seaside playground.
  • Monaco: Similar because it sits within the Côte d'Azur strip and shares glamour; differs because Monaco is a sovereign casino principality, not the whole coast.
  • Nice: Similar because Nice is the capital of the Côte; differs because Nice is one city with the Promenade des Anglais, while the Côte is the entire littoral.
  • Cannes: Similar because Cannes epitomises Côte glamour and film festival; differs because Cannes is a single resort town, not the region.
  • Saint-Tropez: Similar because it epitomises Côte chic and yachting; differs because it is one port village mythologised by artists and stars, not the whole coast.
  • Belle Époque resort: Similar because it captures grand-hotel seaside luxury; differs because it is a period style found anywhere (Biarritz, Brighton), while Côte d'Azur is the specific place.
