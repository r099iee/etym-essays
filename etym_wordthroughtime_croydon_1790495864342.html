- Old English Crogedene (c. 861) - valley farm, saffron or crooked; small, Saxon, agricultural
- Domesday Croindene - archbishop’s manor, church, mill; Norman, recorded, valuable
- Medieval Croydon market-palace - fairs, shambles, archbishops summer; thriving, coaching
- Coaching Croydon (1700s) - Brighton road inns including Old King’s Head; bustling, horsy
- Railway-suburban Croydon (1803-1900) - iron railway, London & Croydon, villas; growing, respectable
- Ruskin grandmother’s Croydon - Market Street inn-keeping, Sienese sign wished; homely, remembered
- Airport-office Croydon (1920-1990s) - first London airport, then office towers; pioneering, concrete
- Modern borough Croydon - tramlink, Whitgift, diverse metropolis; large, contested, cosmopolitan
