Note: English borrowed germane directly from Latin germānus; it is a doublet of german, which came through Old French germain. The relevance sense is traditionally credited to Shakespeare's Hamlet V.ii. Latin germānus is reconstructed as a derivative of germen “sprout, seed,” from PIE *ǵenh₁- “to beget.”
