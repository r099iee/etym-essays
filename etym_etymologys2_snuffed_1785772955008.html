Debate: the candle-extinguishing sense of snuff is of uncertain origin in OED: it may share the imitative root *snuf- or derive from the noun snuff "burnt end of a wick", possibly related to snub "to cut short". The tobacco snuff is a separate Dutch borrowing (snuiftabak).
