• strenuous: Similar because both describe activities demanding great energy and exertion; differs because strenuous focuses on the intensity of active physical or mental effort exerted by the person (from Latin strenuus "brisk, nimble, active"), whereas arduous focuses on the grueling, uphill nature of the task itself.
  • laborious: Similar because both involve heavy, sustained work; differs because laborious emphasizes tedious, painstaking, repetitive toil and drudgery (often lacking inspiration), whereas arduous conveys a noble, heroic, or steep mountain-like challenge requiring supreme endurance.
  • onerous: Similar because both describe demanding undertakings; differs because onerous carries a distinct connotation of oppressive burden, legal obligation, or unpleasant encumbrance (from Latin onus "burden, load"), making it unwelcome, whereas an arduous task is often undertaken eagerly for great honor or artistic triumph.
  • taxing: Similar because both describe something that drains stamina; differs because taxing emphasizes the depleting, exhausting effect on one's reserves of patience, mental energy, or physical resources, whereas arduous emphasizes the sheer magnitude and steepness of the climb.
  • herculean: Similar because both describe immense tasks requiring great strength; differs because herculean invokes the superhuman strength of the demigod Hercules, specifically applied to monumental, extraordinary feats that seem to surpass normal human capacity, whereas arduous can describe any sustained uphill struggle.
  • burdensome: Similar because both characterize difficult commitments; differs because burdensome emphasizes the oppressive downward weight and psychological strain of a duty or debt, whereas arduous emphasizes the steep upward climb and grueling expenditure of energy.
  • demanding: Similar because both imply high standards and heavy effort; differs because demanding is a general, unmarked term that can refer to difficult people, tight schedules, or high precision, lacking the visceral physical imagery of steep cliffs and breathless uphill toil inherent in arduous.
  • rigorous: Similar because both demand discipline and endurance; differs because rigorous emphasizes strict adherence to rules, uncompromising exactitude, scientific accuracy, or harsh climatic conditions, whereas arduous focuses on the exhausting physical and mental exertion required to complete a journey or work.
