Note: The PIE root is usually written *ǵʰel- "to shine, glow, yellow" (with laryngeal variants in some systems); it also underlies glow, gold, yellow, Gleam-family words and Greek khlorós "green-yellow". The pair glōaną (verb "glow") and glōdiz (noun "ember") are ablaut sisters, not ancestor and descendant — the noun was formed from the same root with a different vowel grade. Beware the unrelated homonym: glede "the kite (bird of prey)" comes from Old English glida, from glīdan "to glide".
