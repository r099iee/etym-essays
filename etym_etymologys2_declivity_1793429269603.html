Note: The PIE root *ḱley- is the prolific ancestor of English lean, ladder, climate, climax, and client (originally ‘one who leans on a patron for protection’). In Latin, prefixing ad- gave acclīvitās (‘ascent’), pro- gave prōclīvitās (‘mental leaning’), and dē- gave dēclīvitās (‘downward slope’).
