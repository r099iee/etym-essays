• Decided: Similar because both mean a choice was made; differs because 'decided' is neutral and everyday, while 'resolved' implies deliberation and firmness of purpose.
  • Determined: Similar because both convey firmness of purpose; differs because 'determined' is an adjective of character or attitude, while 'resolved' can be either a verb (decided to) or an adjective (mind made up).
  • Settled: Similar because both mean brought to a conclusion; differs because 'settled' applies to disputes and arrangements and lacks the personal willpower colouring of 'resolved'.
  • Concluded: Similar because both mean brought to an end; differs because 'concluded' stresses the ending rather than the deciding.
  • Elected: Similar because both mean chose to do something; differs because 'elected' is formal and rare for actions (more for offices), and lacks the 'untie the knot' depth of 'resolved'.
  • Opted: Similar because both mean chose among options; differs because 'opted' is lighter, about preference rather than firm resolve.
  • Chose: Similar because both mean selected; differs because 'chose' is the plainest word, while 'resolved' adds determination and deliberation.
  • Committed: Similar because both mean pledged oneself to a course; differs because 'committed' emphasises the ongoing obligation rather than the moment of decision.
  • Vowed: Similar because both mean pledged solemnly; differs because 'vowed' is oath-like, religious or romantic, while 'resolved' describes rational deliberation.
  • Resolute (adj.): Similar because both mean determined; differs because 'resolute' describes a steady character, while 'resolved' describes a decision made or a mind made up.
  • Solved: Similar because resolving a problem = solving it; differs because 'solved' is the everyday word for problems, while 'resolved' is more formal and also covers doubts, chords, vectors, and screens.
