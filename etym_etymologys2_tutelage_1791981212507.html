Note: the PIE stage is a reconstruction, not an attested form. The pre-history of Latin tuērī is disputed; some dictionaries posit a root meaning “to observe, heed”, while others treat the verb as having no secure Indo-European cognates. The French mediation is also uncertain: English may have formed tutelage directly from Latin tūtēla plus the suffix -age.
