Classical Latin (1st c. BCE)
halcyōn / alcyōn

Old / Middle French (13th–14th c.)
alcion / halcyon
"fabled kingfisher; bird of tranquil weather"

Middle English (late 14th c.)
alcioun / halcion
"solstice sea bird; 14-day winter calm"

Early Modern to Contemporary English (late 16th c.–present)
