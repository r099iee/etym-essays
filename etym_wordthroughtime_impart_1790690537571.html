- Latin bestowal (Antiquity) - impartire means to give a share of honours or knowledge; civic-generous, participatory giving.
- Medieval grant (1300s–1500s) - imparten means to bestow grace or tidings; religious-solemn, God and priests impart.
- Enlightenment communication (1600s–1800s) - Locke and science normalize impart knowledge, motion, flavour; polite-formal for valuable transfer.
- Institutional jargon (1900s) - schools impart values, statutes impart powers; bureaucratic-formal, slightly stiff.
- Present formal alternative (21st c.) - impart survives as dignified synonym for give/tell with abstract objects; everyday speech prefers give, share, pass on.
