- c. 80–150 CE Roman Taum / Tava - tidal estuary reached by Agricola; Pictish water; frontier and marvel
- c. 600–1100 Gaelic Toi / Toe / Tatha - tribes of the Tay, saints and kings at Scone and Dunkeld; sacred and royal
- 12th–16th c. medieval Tay at Perth - bridging point, burgh, coronation road; commercial and ceremonial
- 18th–19th c. salmon and sublime Tay - anglers, poets, and painters praise its beauty and fish; romantic and sporting
- 1879 Tay Bridge Disaster - collapse fixes the name worldwide as engineering warning; tragic and famous
- 20th c. Tayside region and road bridge - administration, oil, universities; everyday and civic
- Modern heritage Tay - salmon conservation, flood management, childhood memory in Ruskin; nostalgic and ecological
