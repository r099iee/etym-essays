Tithing continues Old English teoðung, formed from the verb teoðian "to take or pay a tenth" with the suffix -ung; the base is teoða "tenth", from PIE *déḱm̥ "ten". A separate, unrelated homonym "tithing" (a group of ten households) is distinct in formation.
