- Early craft pliancy - Describes softened leather, moist clay, young shoots that submit to hands; wholly practical, praised by artisans.
- Medieval bodily tenderness - Extends to tender flesh, youthful limbs, soft conscience; connotation of delicacy, vulnerability, and Christian humility in yielding.
- Renaissance grace - Supple dancer, supple verse become aesthetic ideals; bending now signals elegance and courtly control, not weakness.
- Enlightenment adaptability - Supple mind, supple reason praised as open and progressive versus rigid scholasticism; political suppleness can be double-edged as diplomacy or flattery.
- Modern wellness and management - Supple body, supple skin, supple economy; contemporary usage celebrates healthy flexibility and agile responsiveness as premium qualities in fitness, cosmetics, and business.
