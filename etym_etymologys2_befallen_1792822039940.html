Proto-Indo-European (reconstructed, c. 4000 BCE)
*pol-
‘to fall’
(conventional reconstruction)
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*fallaną
‘to fall, drop’
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*bi- + *fallaną
‘upon’ + ‘to fall’
‘to fall upon’
↓
Old English (9th c.)
befallan
‘to fall upon, happen to’
↓
Middle English (14th c.)
bifallen
‘to happen to, befall’
↓
Modern English (current)
befallen
‘having happened fatefully’

Note: the Germanic verb *fallaną is securely attested across the family (Gothic fallan, Old Norse falla, Old High German fallan); the deeper PIE attribution to *pol- is the conventional handbook reconstruction and is less certain than the Germanic stages, which rest on direct attestation.
