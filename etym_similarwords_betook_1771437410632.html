• Went: Similar because both describe movement to a place; differs because went is the colourless everyday verb with no hint of purpose or resolve, while betook implies gathering up one's whole self and deliberately directing it.
  • Repaired to: Similar because both are formal ways of saying 'went to'; differs because repaired to suggests going to one's proper or habitual place and has a legal-flavoured formality, while betook emphasizes the reflexive self-commitment of the journey.
  • Resorted to: Similar because both mean turning to a place or means; differs because resorted to implies turning to something as a resource or expedient in need, while betook simply describes going to a destination with dignity.
  • Adjourned to: Similar because both mean moving to a place; differs because adjourned to is lighter and jocular, implying a break in a larger activity (adjourn to the pub), while betook is solemn and self-committal.
  • Proceeded to: Similar because both describe going forward to a place or action; differs because proceeded to stresses orderly continuation or sequence, while betook stresses the deliberate giving of oneself to the destination.
  • Hied: Similar because both are archaic, reflexive verbs of taking oneself somewhere; differs because hied implies speed and urgency, while betook implies composed, dignified, purposive motion.
  • Took himself off: Similar because both use reflexive self-directed motion; differs because took himself off is informal and often marks abrupt departure, while betook himself is formal, deliberate, and ceremonious.
