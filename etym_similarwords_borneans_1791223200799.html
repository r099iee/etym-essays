• Dayaks: Similar because Dayaks are the largest indigenous grouping on Borneo; differs because Dayak names a specific ethnolinguistic family, while Borneans covers everyone on the island including Malays and Chinese.
  • Ibans: Similar because Ibans are the most numerous Dayak people of Sarawak; differs because Iban is one group, while Borneans is the island-wide collective.
  • Dusuns / Kadazan-Dusuns: Similar because they are major native peoples of Sabah; differs because the name is Sabah-specific, while Borneans spans three nations.
  • Bruneians: Similar because Bruneians live on Borneo's northwest coast; differs because Bruneian denotes citizenship of Brunei, while Bornean is geographic and broader.
  • Sabahans: Similar because Sabahans are Malaysians from North Borneo; differs because Sabahan is a state identity, while Bornean includes Sarawak, Brunei, and Kalimantan.
  • Sarawakians: Similar because Sarawakians inhabit Malaysian Borneo's largest state; differs for the same state-versus-island reason.
  • Kalimantan peoples: Similar because they inhabit Indonesian Borneo (the island's largest portion); differs because Kalimantan is the Indonesian administrative term.
  • Malays: Similar because coastal Malays are prominent Bornean communities; differs because Malay is a peninsula-wide ethnicity and language, not island-specific.
  • South Sea Islanders: Similar because the source pairs them with Borneans as communal Pacific societies; differs because South Sea Islanders denotes Oceania, not Borneo.
  • Sumatrans: Similar because they are peoples of the neighboring Indonesian island of Sumatra; differs because Sumatra is a different island with Acehnese, Batak, and Minangkabau cultures.
  • Javanese: Similar because they are the dominant people of nearby Java with historic links to Borneo; differs because Javanese culture is court-centered and densely agrarian, unlike Bornean longhouse-swidden traditions.
  • Indigenous peoples: Similar because Borneans in the source means tribal communities; differs because indigenous peoples is a global generic category, while Borneans is island-specific.
