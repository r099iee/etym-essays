• Substantial: Similar because both describe a considerable, solid amount; differs because substantial stresses solidity and weight of content, while sizable stresses sheer dimension or quantity.
  • Considerable: Similar because both mean large enough to merit attention; differs because considerable leans toward ‘worthy of consideration’ in thought, while sizable leans toward measurable extent.
  • Large: Similar because both denote above-average dimensions or amounts; differs because large is the plain generic word, while sizable adds a formal note of assessed, respectable magnitude.
  • Big: Similar because both mean of great size or amount; differs because big is informal and all-purpose, while sizable is measured and report-like, never childish.
  • Significant: Similar because both mark something as important in scale; differs because significant stresses meaning and consequence, while sizable stresses extent first, importance only by extension.
  • Hefty: Similar because both describe impressively large amounts or weights; differs because hefty is vigorous and physical, suggesting weight you can feel, while sizable is cooler and more administrative.
  • Ample: Similar because both describe a generously large supply; differs because ample stresses ‘more than enough’, sufficiency, while sizable stresses magnitude alone, with no promise it suffices.
  • Extensive: Similar because both describe wide-ranging scale, as of literature or grounds; differs because extensive stresses spread and coverage, while sizable stresses bulk or volume.
  • Respectable: Similar because both grant quiet approval to an amount; differs because respectable judges decency and creditability, while sizable judges dimension, with approval only implied.
  • Handsome: Similar because a handsome profit or sum is a sizable one; differs because handsome is an old idiom of generous reward, while sizable is the neutral modern measure.
