• Loyalties: Similar because both name steadfast devotion to persons or causes; differs because loyalties is the broader everyday word for faithful feeling, while allegiances stresses a binding duty or sworn obligation.
  • Fidelities: Similar because both denote faithfulness kept over time; differs because fidelities leans marital or contractual and sounds literary, while allegiances leans civic and feudal, owed upward to a power.
  • Devotions: Similar because both describe earnest commitment of the heart; differs because devotions is warmer and often religious or affectionate, while allegiances is cooler and juridical, a duty acknowledged.
  • Fealties: Similar because both descend from feudal sworn service; differs because fealty is the archaic oath-bound bond itself, while allegiances is the living modern word covering nations, parties, and ideas.
  • Attachments: Similar because both tie a person to something valued; differs because attachments is psychological and often personal, while allegiances is public and principled, declared before others.
  • Affinities: Similar because both draw people toward groups or ideas; differs because affinities are natural likings or temperamental sympathies, while allegiances are committed bonds that persist past liking.
  • Partisanships: Similar because both describe siding with a camp, exactly the source sense toward theories; differs because partisanship stresses heated one-sidedness, while allegiances stresses the loyalty beneath it.
  • Oaths: Similar because allegiance is classically sealed by oath; differs because an oath is the spoken vow at one moment, while allegiances are the enduring obligations the vow creates.
