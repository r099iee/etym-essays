Note: the remoter origin is uncertain. The OED describes the adjective as of uncertain origin; the most widely followed account treats English glib as a shortening of glibbery, probably from Low German glibberig, ultimately from a Germanic gl- group connected with PIE *ǵʰelh₃- “to shine.” The Proto-Germanic *glib- stage is a reconstruction, not an attested word.
