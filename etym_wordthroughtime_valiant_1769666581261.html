- Medieval chivalric sense - Brave and physically strong in battle; the badge of a worthy knight, charged with noble honor and martial strength.
- Literary heroic sense - Became a poetic, admiring word for courage put into noble action, living on in adventure tales and epics rather than everyday speech.
- Contemporary usage - Still admiring but noticeably formal and literary; now applied to any resolute bravery of will, even of small or unarmed beings, rather than to physical might.
