Debate note: The decisive shift from “regard, consideration” to “delay, postponement” happened in late Latin legal usage: the respectus a court granted (its consideration of a party) became the extra time granted. The Old French form respit (11th c.) then narrowed further toward “rest” in everyday speech, and English borrowed both the legal and the everyday senses together in the thirteenth century.
