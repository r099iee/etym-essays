Note: first element debated. Alternative proposals derive it from Old Norse sul "pillar" referring to the Lochmaben Stane, or sula "solan goose, gannet", but early spellings show a short vowel, favouring Old English sol "mud" or *sulr cognate with sulla "to swill". Second element Old Norse vath / vath "ford" cognate with English wade is secure. Scandinavian origin of the compound is agreed; exact first-element vowel remains nuanced.
