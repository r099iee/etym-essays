Debate: Origin uncertain. PGmc *huppōn is reconstructed but not directly attested; the word may derive from Latin humulus, itself of obscure origin (possibly from humus "ground" or a substrate word). Cognates (OE hymele, ON humli, Slavic chmelь) point to an ancient, non-Indo-European plant-word of unknown source.
