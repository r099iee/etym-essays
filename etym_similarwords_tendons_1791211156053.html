• sinews: Similar because it is the native word for tendons; differs because it is poetic/archaic and also means strength generally.
  • ligaments: Similar because they are fibrous bands stabilizing joints; differs because they connect bone to bone, not muscle to bone.
  • muscles: Similar because they work with tendons to move; differs because muscle contracts, tendon only transmits.
  • cartilage: Similar because it is resilient connective tissue; differs because it cushions rather than pulls.
  • hamstrings: Similar because they include prominent tendons behind knee; differs because it names the muscle-tendon group of posterior thigh.
  • Achilles tendons: Similar because they are the largest example; differs because specific to heel, proverbial vulnerable spot.
  • cords: Similar because tendon-threads served as cords; differs because generic for any twisted string.
  • threads: Similar because split tendons become threads; differs because usually plant or synthetic sewing filament.
  • fibres / fibers: Similar because tendons are collagen fibres; differs because scientific general term for any threadlike structure.
  • gristle: Similar because chewy tissue in meat; differs because informal, undesirable mouthfeel.
  • connective tissue: Similar because it is the superordinate category; differs because formal histological umbrella including bone and fat.
  • aponeuroses: Similar because they are flat tendinous sheets; differs because broad and sheetlike, not cordlike.
