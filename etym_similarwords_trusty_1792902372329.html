• Trustworthy: Similar because both mean deserving of confidence; differs because trustworthy is the modern moral term, assessed and serious, while trusty is warmer, older, and more affectionate.
  • Reliable: Similar because both promise consistent performance; differs because reliable is cool and technical, machines and data are reliable, while trusty implies loyalty proven in fellowship.
  • Faithful: Similar because both name steadfast loyalty over time; differs because faithful stresses devotion and accuracy to a vow, while trusty stresses the dependability others can lean upon.
  • Loyal: Similar because both stand by their people through danger; differs because loyal names allegiance to a person or cause, while trusty names the tested record that earns such allegiance.
  • Dependable: Similar because both can be counted on without fail; differs because dependable is plain prose for everyday steadiness, while trusty carries ballad-music and affection.
  • Staunch: Similar because both hold firm under pressure; differs because staunch stresses unyielding firmness of support, while trusty stresses the whole proven character.
  • Steadfast: Similar because both are fixed and unwavering; differs because steadfast is resolute purpose that does not waver, while trusty is proven service that does not fail.
  • Tried and true: Similar because both praise what testing has vindicated; differs because tried and true is the idiom of explicit testing, while trusty compresses the whole history into one fond word.
