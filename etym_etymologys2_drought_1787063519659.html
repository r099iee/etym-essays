Note: the noun was formed in Germanic from the adjective "dry" with the abstract-noun suffix *-ōþu (Old English -oþ / -uþ). The Modern English spelling "drought" reflects Old English drūgaþ (its h written as "gh"), while the standard pronunciation /draʊt/ continues a northern and dialectal form "drouth"; the spelling with -ough- was fixed in the 17th century. Cognates of the base "dry" include Dutch droog and German trocken.
