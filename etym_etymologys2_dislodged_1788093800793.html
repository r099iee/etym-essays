Note: the prefix is Latin dis- ‘apart, away, reversal’, via Old French des-. English lodge, Italian loggia and English lobby are triplets from the same Frankish *laubja; English leaf (OE lēaf) is the direct continuant of *laubą — so DISLODGED is a distant cousin of ‘leaf’, whose own ‘shelter of foliage’ became the hut.
