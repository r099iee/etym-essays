Note on Etymology: Morphologically derived from Latin lūx ("light") compounded with the Proto-Indo-European instrumental suffix *-dʰlom (which regularly developed into Latin -brum), yielding an unrecorded intermediate noun *lūcibrum ("lamp, candle, instrument of light"). From this instrumental stem was built the denominative verb lūcubrāre ("to work by the lamp"), whence the abstract noun lūcubrātiō was formed with the suffix -tiō.
