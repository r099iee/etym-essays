Note: Proto-Germanic *raipaz yielded Old English rāp, Old Norse reip, Old Frisian rēp, Middle Dutch reep, and Gothic raip. The Old English long vowel ā regularly rounded and raised through Middle English open ǭ to modern /oʊ/ or /əʊ/. The regular weak verbal inflection -ed attaches to form the preterite and past participle.
