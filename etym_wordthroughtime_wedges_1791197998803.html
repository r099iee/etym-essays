- Stone splitter (Neolithic) - Indispensable survival tech; neutral admiration for the sliver that fells trees and opens bone.
- Saxon wecg (500-1100) - Everyday farm and forge staple; unmarked, taken-for-granted peasant competence.
- Medieval quoin and war wedge (1100-1500) - Dual prestige: mason's corner craft and captain's flying wedge battle tactic, disciplined force.
- Early modern proverb carrier (1500-1800) - Moral warning; thin end of the wedge warns against small concessions, suspicious tone.
- Industrial shim and quarry tool (1800-1950) - Precise engineering; feather-and-wedge splitting of granite admired as skilled labour.
- Modern sport and politics (1950-present) - Playful to divisive; golf bag essential (positive expertise) versus wedge issue driving voters apart (cynical strategy).
