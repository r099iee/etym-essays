Note: first element debated. OED takes literal goose + berry as probable (animal-plant names often inexplicable). Skeat, Klein, Watkins propose folk-etymology from Middle Dutch croesel / German Krausbeere / Krauselbeere "crisped, curly berry" from kraus "curly" or Old French grosele "gooseberry" from Germanic kr- "bend, curl, rounded mass", refashioned as gooseberry. Berry deeper PIE beyond PGmc *bazja- is uncertain. Bush further etymology beyond West Germanic disputed, possibly substrate or Latin boscus borrowing; see S3.
