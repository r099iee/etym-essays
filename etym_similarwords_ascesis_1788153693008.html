• asceticism: similar as the near-synonym for the ascetic life; differs as the doctrine or system, whereas ascesis is the practice/discipline itself.
  • askesis: similar — the same word romanised without the -s ending (from Greek askēsis); differs by modern anglicised spelling variant.
  • self-discipline: similar for disciplined self-command; differs by losing the connotation of deliberate renunciation and of training with a purpose.
  • austerity: similar for strict, spare living; differs as a quality of style or life without the idea of methodical training.
  • self-denial: similar for refusing indulgence; differs by emphasising refusal rather than formation — the negative side of ascesis.
  • renunciation: similar for giving up goods or pleasures; differs by its religious-legal force (taking vows) rather than exercise.
  • mortification: similar in Catholic asceticism; differs by the harsher, punitive image of "putting to death" the flesh.
  • abnegation: similar for formal self-renunciation; differs by the abstract, Latinate, almost political tone.
  • training: similar as the plain English for askēsis’s root sense; differs by dropping all ascetic-religious colour.
  • rigour: similar for severity and exactness of discipline; differs as a quality rather than a regimen.
