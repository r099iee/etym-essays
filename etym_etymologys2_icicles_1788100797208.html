Notes: (1) PIE *yeg- “ice” rests mainly on the Germanic family (and possibly Avestan yaxa-); some handbooks treat *jekilaz as of uncertain ultimate origin — stated, not guessed. (2) Modern spelling icicle re-analyses the word as ice + -icle; this folk etymology is half-true, since the ending really is the eroded gicel.
