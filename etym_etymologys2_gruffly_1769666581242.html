Note: The Middle Dutch origin is the standard account, but the deeper links are uncertain: PGmc *grobaz is a reconstruction, and the proposed PIE *gʰrebʰ- "to dig, scrape" is speculative. Some scholars instead connect gruff with Low German grof, a cognate of the Dutch form.
