• fitful: Similar because it means occurring in fits; differs because fitful stresses disturbed rest or sleep, spasmodic stresses jerky bursts of action.
  • intermittent: Similar because it means stopping and starting; differs because intermittent is neutral and technical, spasmodic adds abruptness and lack of control.
  • sporadic: Similar because it means irregular and scattered; differs because sporadic stresses infrequency, spasmodic stresses convulsive intensity when it occurs.
  • erratic: Similar because it means unpredictable; differs because erratic stresses wandering from a norm, spasmodic stresses burst-pause rhythm.
  • jerky: Similar because it means abrupt and uneven; differs because jerky is physical and colloquial, spasmodic formal and temporal.
  • convulsive: Similar because it means like a convulsion; differs because convulsive stresses violent shaking, spasmodic stresses intermittence.
  • irregular: Similar because it means not steady; differs because irregular is the generic term, spasmodic specifies burst-pause with wasted energy.
  • desultory: Similar because it means lacking plan and continuity; differs because desultory stresses jumping from topic to topic, spasmodic stresses surging and lapsing.
  • episodic: Similar because it means divided into episodes; differs because episodic can be structural and neutral, spasmodic implies failure to sustain.
  • stop-start: Similar because it means alternating motion and halt; differs because stop-start is colloquial and mechanical, spasmodic literary and organic.
  • unsustained: Similar because it means not kept up; differs because unsustained states the result, spasmodic evokes the jerky manner.
  • flickering: Similar because it means wavering on and off; differs because flickering suggests faint light going out, spasmodic suggests violent energy bursting out.
