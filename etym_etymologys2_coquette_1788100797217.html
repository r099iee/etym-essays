Debate note: the origin of French coq is disputed. The traditional derivation (Le Robert, FEW) runs from Latin coccum “kermes berry, scarlet”, the red comb being likened to the scarlet dye-berry; some scholars prefer an imitative origin, coq echoing the bird's call (compare cocorico, “cock-a-doodle-doo”). Late Latin glosses also use coccum for the bird itself. The chain to coquette itself is secure once coq exists.
