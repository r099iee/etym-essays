fickle

Note: a Germanic-internal word; the ultimate PIE origin is unknown. The older sense "deceitful, treacherous" shifted to "changeable, inconstant" by the 16th century. A link to PIE *peik- "to adorn, fashion" is speculative and rejected by most etymologists.
