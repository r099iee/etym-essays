• Longan: Similar in being a small round Southeast Asian fruit with a thin shell and translucent sweet flesh; differs because longan has a smooth tan shell and a musky, less aromatic taste, while a lychee is rosier-skinned and more fragrant.
  • Rambutan: Similar in being a tropical fruit with juicy translucent flesh around a single seed; differs because the rambutan is covered in spiky red hairs and its flesh is firmer and less floral than the lychee's.
  • Litchi: Similar in denoting the exact same fruit; differs only in spelling - litchi is an older romanisation - while 'lychee' is the now-standard form, and the choice signals style, not a different fruit.
  • Mangosteen: Similar in being a prized Southeast Asian fruit with sweet, white, juicy segments; differs because the mangosteen has a thick purple rind and segmented flesh like orange sections, while a lychee has a thin red shell and a single seed.
  • Peach: Similar as a classic simile for soft, smooth, youthful skin; differs because a peach is fuzzy-skinned, soft, and blushing, while a lychee is glossy-shelled, translucent, and firm.
  • Grape: Similar in having translucent, juicy, sweet flesh; differs because a grape grows in clusters with a smooth thin skin and pip-like seeds, while a lychee is a single-stone fruit in a rough shell.
  • Cherry: Similar as a small round fruit with a single stone, used in similes of youth and freshness; differs because a cherry is glossy, dark, and firmer-skinned, while a lychee is pale, translucent, and delicately fragrant.
