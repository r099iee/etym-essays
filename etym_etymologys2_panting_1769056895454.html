PIE (prehistoric)
*bʰeh₂-
"to shine; to appear"
↓
Greek (4th c. BCE)
φαντασία (phantasía)
"an appearance; a vision"
↓
Greek (Hellenistic)
φαντασιόω (phantasioō)
"to have visions; to be excited, agitated"
↓
Latin (Vulgar Latin)
*pantasiare
"to have visions; to gasp, pant"
↓
Old French (12th c.)
pantaisier
"to pant, gasp; to be breathless"
↓
English (16th c.)
pant
"to breathe hard and quickly"
↓
English (present)
panting
"breathing with short, quick breaths"

Debate: the shift from Greek phantasioō "to have visions" to Old French pantaisier "to pant" is notable; it is usually explained through the agitated, gasping state accompanying visions, transmitted via Vulgar Latin *pantasiare.
