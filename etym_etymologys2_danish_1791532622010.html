Note: the origin of the tribal name is uncertain. The derivation from PIE *dhen- "low, flat" (source of Old High German tenni "threshing floor" and Old English denu "valley") would describe the flat land of Jutland, but it is a hypothesis, not a certainty. The -ish suffix continues Old English -isc, from Proto-Germanic *-iskaz. The name is first recorded by Jordanes in his Getica (c. 551 CE).
