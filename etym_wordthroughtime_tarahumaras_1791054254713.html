- Pre-contact self-name - intimate kin sense: rarámuri as we-people, runners in foot-race culture
- 17th–18th c. mission contact - externalised colonial label Tarahumara for mission subjects and language
- 19th c. travel writing - exoticised primitive ingenuity, bird-snaring and cliff-dwelling curiosities
- Early 20th c. anthropology - admired endurance physiology, dream of every youth to run
- Late 20th c. running boom - romanticised light-footed philosophers of Born to Run
- Present - dual perception: respected Rarámuri endurance culture and vulnerable community facing land and violence pressures
