Note: Roman grammarians themselves debated whether satira came from satura (the full dish) or from Greek satyr (the wild woodland creature); Quintilian records the uncertainty. Modern scholarship firmly prefers the satura derivation and treats the satyr connection as folk etymology, but the ancient debate is worth noting.
