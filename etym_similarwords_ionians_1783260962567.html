• Dorians: Similar because both are major ancient Greek tribal divisions with their own dialect, cults, and identity; differs because the Dorians, exemplified by Sparta, were famed for austerity, discipline, and land-based military power, whereas the Ionians, exemplified by Athens and Miletus, were famed for philosophy, art, trade, and naval power.
  • Aeolians: Similar because the Aeolians are another of the four traditional Greek tribes with a distinct dialect (Aeolic); differs because the Aeolians were associated with Thessaly, Boeotia, and the island of Lesbos, and are not famed for founding the great philosophical schools or the leading colonial cities of the Asian seaboard.
  • Achaeans: Similar because the Achaeans are the fourth traditional Greek tribe and the Homeric name for the Greeks who fought at Troy; differs because in historical times the Achaeans were chiefly associated with the northern Peloponnese, and their fame is legendary and epic rather than tied to a settled Asian homeland and a continuing philosophical tradition.
  • Hellenes: Similar because Hellenes is the collective national name of the Greek people to which the Ionians belong; differs because Hellenes is the universal, inclusive term (Greece as Hellas, its people as Hellenes), while Ionians designates one specific tribe, region, and dialect within that larger nation.
  • Athenians: Similar because Athens was the most famous Ionian city and spoke Ionic-Attic Greek; differs because Athenians is a city-based ethnonym, while Ionians covers a whole tribal confederation spread across Attica, the Aegean islands, and Asia Minor.
  • Yavana / Yunan / Yauna: Similar because these are the Sanskrit, Arabic, and Old Persian names for the Greeks, derived directly from the Ionian ethnonym; differs because they are foreign labels that generalised one tribal name to mean all Greeks and even Greece itself, rather than the Greeks' own precise term for a single tribe.
  • Milesians: Similar because Miletus was the leading Ionian city and the cradle of the Ionian philosophical school; differs because Milesians names only the citizens of that single city, while Ionians is the wider tribal and regional identity.
  • Greek colonists / settlers: Similar because the Ionians were the great colonisers of the Aegean, Propontis, and Black Sea coasts; differs because these are generic functional labels for any founders of overseas settlements, whereas Ionians is a specific ethnic, historical, and cultural name.
