Onomatopoeic / expressive (no secure PIE root)
*kok(k)-
"sharp call of the male fowl"

cock (verb)

↓

Modern English (17th c.)

↓

Modern English
COCKED

Debate note: the word is imitative of the bird's call, so no Indo-European etymon can be securely reconstructed; cognate forms appear across Germanic and Romance (Old Norse kokkr, Old French coc). The verb-to-participle development is regular English word-formation.
