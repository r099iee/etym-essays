• Hairy: Similar because both mean covered with hair; differs because hairy is general and carries no softness, while woolly suggests soft, dense, fleece-like curls.
  • Furry: Similar because both mean covered with hair or fur; differs because furry suggests animal pelt, while woolly is wool-like and softer, drawn from sheep rather than from furred animals.
  • Fleecy: Similar because both mean soft and wool-like; differs because fleecy is more sheep-specific or cloud-like, while woolly is the broader soft-fiber word.
  • Fuzzy: Similar because both mean soft and unclear; differs because fuzzy can mean either tactile or mental blur, while woolly centres on the soft-wool image and adds warmth.
  • Downy: Similar because both mean soft and feathery-light; differs because downy is finer, lighter, and bird-plume-like, while woolly is thicker, denser, and fleece-like.
  • Fluffy: Similar because both mean soft and light-textured; differs because fluffy emphasizes airy lightness, while woolly emphasizes dense curly fiber.
  • Vague (figurative sense): Similar because both mean unclear in thought or statement; differs because vague is neutral and general, while woolly adds the soft, enveloping, clouded image of wool.
