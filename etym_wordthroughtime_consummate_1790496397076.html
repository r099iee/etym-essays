- Latin consummare - to sum up, finish, perfect; farmers, accountants, priests; practical and ritual
- Late Latin consummatio - completion, perfection, the end of the world; theological and eschatological
- 1400s Middle English consummate (verb) - to complete, fulfil, seal; wills, treaties, marriages; legal and solemn
- 1500s marriage law - consummation by bodily union; annulment for non-consummation; canonical and intimate
- 1600s adjective perfect - a consummate statesman, consummate folly; literary and political; morally neutral
- 1800s domestic paragon - the consummate housewife, needlewoman, hostess; evangelical formation; feminine and formidable
- Modern professional accolade - consummate performer, surgeon, diplomat; reviewing and referential; prestigious and formal
