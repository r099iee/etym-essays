• Threaded: Similar because both mean putting objects along a cord in sequence; differs because threaded stresses the passage of a thread through an eye or hole (a needle, a screw thread), while strung stresses loading a line with items under tension.
  • Hung: Similar because both describe items held by a support; differs because hung is suspension from above by gravity, while strung is sequential arrangement along a cord drawn between points.
  • Suspended: Similar because both describe things held off the ground; differs because suspended is general free hanging, with none of the cord-and-sequence loading of strung.
  • Set: Similar because both describe placing gems or objects; differs because set fixes stones in metal mounts or places things in position, without the line, cord, or sequence of strung.
  • Slung: Similar because both are strong pasts involving cords and suspension; differs because slung is loose, thrown, or looped roughly over a support, while strung is neat, deliberate, sequential threading.
  • Stringed: Similar because both are past forms of the same verb string; differs because stringed is the older weak form, now chiefly the musical adjective (stringed instruments, fitted with strings), while strung is the strong modern past for threading and tensioning.
  • Beaded: Similar because both describe decorated or assembled items with beads; differs because beaded names the result (covered in beads) and can be done by gluing or sewing, while strung names the specific act of threading them on a line.
  • Laced: Similar because both involve threading a cord through a series of openings; differs because laced is threading to fasten two edges (shoes, bodices, corsets), while strung is loading a cord with beads, lights, or trophies along its length.
  • Roped: Similar because both involve cords or lines; differs because roped uses a heavy rope for binding or enclosing, while strung uses a fine string for threading or tensioning.
  • Linked / chained: Similar because both describe items joined in a row; differs because linking and chaining connect units to each other, while strung runs them along one continuous cord.
  • Festooned: Similar because both describe decorating with suspended garlands, lights, or flowers; differs because festooned is lavish decorative draping, while strung is the plainer act of threading or stretching a line of objects.
