Note: there is no PIE root; this is a toponymic chain from tribal name to town to cloth, like damask from Damascus or denim from Nîmes. OED and Etymonline agree the cloth is named for the city, famed for weaving by the 1300s. The Celtic meaning of Atrebates itself is debated and not needed for the English word; zero guesswork beyond attested town-to-cloth metonymy.
