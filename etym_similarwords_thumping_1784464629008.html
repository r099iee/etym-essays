• Pounding: Similar because both mean repeated heavy blows; differs because pounding is the near-synonym for physical force, while thumping adds the dull impact sound and the emphatic “very large” sense.
  • Thudding: Similar because both name a dull heavy sound; differs because thudding stresses only the impact noise, not the rhythmic force or the intensifying meaning.
  • Bashing: Similar because both mean forceful impact; differs because bashing is more violent and less rhythmic, with none of thumping’s musical heaviness.
  • Smashing: Similar because both can describe violent impact; differs because smashing implies shattering into pieces, whereas thumping describes dull blows that may leave things whole.
  • Beating: Similar because both describe rhythmic heavy blows; differs because beating is the general verb of repeated striking, without thumping’s onomatopoeic dull-bang quality or its “very large” slang sense.
  • Heavy: Similar because both cover the “very large” sense; differs because heavy is a plain, general word with none of thumping’s onomatopoeic force.
  • Whopping: Similar because both are informal intensifiers meaning “remarkably large”; differs because whopping is purely an intensifier with no literal beating sense, while thumping still sounds like impact.
  • Hefty: Similar because both can mean large and weighty; differs because hefty denotes weight and size statically, without thumping’s percussive, rhythmic energy.
