PIE (reconstructed, c. 4000 BCE)
*legh-
‘to lie, lay, settle’
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*lagjaną
‘to lay, place down’
↓
Old English (9th c.)
ālecgan
‘to lay down, put down’
↓
Middle English (13th–14th c.)
aleyen, allayen
‘to quell, pacify, relieve’
↓
Modern English (current)
allay
‘to calm, relieve, diminish’

Note: the spelling was reshaped by association with lay; a separate verb allay meaning to alloy metals comes instead from Old French alaiier, to combine, and is not part of this chain.
