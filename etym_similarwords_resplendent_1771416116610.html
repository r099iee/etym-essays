• Splendid: Similar because both describe something impressive and excellent; differs because splendid can mean merely 'very good' (a splendid idea) without visible radiance, while resplendent is purely visual and ceremonial.
  • Brilliant: Similar because both describe shining; differs because brilliant emphasizes intense light or intellectual shine and does not presuppose ornament, while resplendent builds grandeur out of color and decoration.
  • Magnificent: Similar because both describe grandeur; differs because magnificent stresses scale and dignity (a magnificent building may be of dark stone), while resplendent requires luminosity and color.
  • Radiant: Similar because both describe glowing with light; differs because radiant suggests light emitted from within (a face, the sun), while resplendent suggests rich, reflected, ornamented brilliance.
  • Dazzling: Similar because both describe blinding brightness; differs because dazzling suggests a momentary, almost overwhelming flash, while resplendent implies a composed and sustained ceremonial radiance.
  • Glittering: Similar because both describe sparkling brightness; differs because glittering suggests small, flashing points of light (glittering stars), while resplendent is a grand, unified glow of color and ornament.
  • Gorgeous: Similar because both describe striking beauty and color; differs because gorgeous is a broader term for rich beauty (a gorgeous sunset) without resplendent's insistence on ceremonial, reflected shine.
  • Lustrous: Similar because both describe a rich quality of light; differs because lustrous refers to the soft sheen of a surface (lustrous pearls, hair), while resplendent refers to an entire display of colored brilliance.
  • Luminous: Similar because both describe giving or having light; differs because luminous means emitting or reflecting light steadily (a luminous dial), lacking the color, ornament, and ceremony of resplendent.
  • Gleaming: Similar because both describe shining brightly; differs because gleaming suggests a clean, hard shine (gleaming floors), while resplendent implies the warm glow of pigment, gold, and decoration.
