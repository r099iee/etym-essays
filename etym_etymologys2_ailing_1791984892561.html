Note: the link between Germanic *aglijaną and PIE *h₂egʰ- depends on an -l- suffix that is not fully explained, and some handbooks keep the Germanic family separate; the comparison with Greek ἄχος “grief” and Sanskrit aghám “evil, pain” is traditional rather than certain. The adjective ailing is a comparatively late lexicalisation of the participle.
