- 18th-century technical term - Rare mathematical and logical term for adding a series; dry and specialist.
- 19th-century rhetorical elevation - Broadened to grand summaries of history, character and argument; solemn, Victorian weight (summation of a life).
- 20th-century legal specialization (US) - Became standard for the closing jury address; adversarial, dramatic courtroom sense.
- Late 20th-century scientific extension - Neural, acoustic and computational summation; neutral technical systems sense.
- Present formal-math/legal doublet - Everyday use remains formal-literary (summation of wisdom), while maths and law keep precise senses; never colloquial like summary.
