↓
Medieval Latin / Romance (8th-13th c.)
nigro- / negro
"black"
↓

negro

↓
Early Modern English (1550s-1600s)
Negro / neger
"Black African person"
↓
Modern English (19th-20th c.)
Negro

↓
Modern English (1970s-present)
Negro

Note: Latin niger is of uncertain
origin; no secure PIE root.
Link to *nekʷ- (night) is
speculative, not accepted.
English form is via Spanish /
