Note: the ultimate origin is debated (OED: "of uncertain origin"). Persian šamšīr is the usual etymon, but the Romance reshaping — the -imitarra / -imeterre vocalism — is irregular, and a minority of scholars doubt the Persian derivation; no PIE root can be traced, and none is invented here.
