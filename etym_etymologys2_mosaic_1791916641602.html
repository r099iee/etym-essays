Note: the derivation of Latin musivum from Greek mouseios is the usual explanation but not certain, and the two English words are unrelated despite identical spelling. The Hebrew origin of Moses is debated: Exodus 2:10 derives it from māšāh “to draw out”, while most scholars prefer an Egyptian name from msy “is born”.
