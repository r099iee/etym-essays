Note on Etymological Nuance: Philologists observe that rumbullion likely represents an expressive dialectal blend combining an imitative base (compare Middle English romblen / modern rumble, from Germanic *hrum-) or the 16th-century cant adjective rum ("fine, strong, heady") with French bouillon ("boiling liquid, bubbling agitation"), metaphorically depicting both a seething crowd brawl and a boiling distillery vat.
