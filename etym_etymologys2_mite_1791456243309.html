Note: the PIE root is debated: some scholars connect *mītǭ with *mey- “small”, others with *meh₂y- “to cut”, in reference to the insect’s bite. The homonymous coin word mite is a separate borrowing from Middle Dutch mijte / Middle Low German mīte, though the two are probably ultimately cognate. The chain above follows the native creature word, which is the sense used in the Anki context.
