- Old Norse house-master - husbondi as bonded master of a house, not yet spouse
- Middle English farmer-steward - husband as tiller and manager; husbandry as tillage and household economy
- Early Modern moral art - husbandry manuals preach diligence, thrift, and Christian stewardship; good versus bad husbandry
- Eighteenth-century science - improved husbandry, new rotations and breeds; agriculture enlightened
- Modern narrowed and revived - husband means spouse, husbandry means livestock science plus sustainable stewardship
