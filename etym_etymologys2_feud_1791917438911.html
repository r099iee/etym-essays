Note: the link from *faihaz to PIE *peyḱ- is probable but not certain; the semantic bridge is usually explained as “marked, stained” → “outlawed” → “hostile”. The unrelated homograph feud “fief” derives from Medieval Latin feodum, whose own origin is disputed and which has no connection with this chain.
