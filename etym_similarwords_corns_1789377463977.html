• Calluses: Similar because both are hyperkeratotic thickenings of the epidermis caused by repeated friction and mechanical pressure; differs because calluses are broad, flat, relatively diffuse areas of thickened skin without a central focal core and are typically painless physiological adaptations, whereas corns are smaller, sharply demarcated, cone-shaped lesions with a hard keratin plug that penetrates deeply into the dermis and causes acute, piercing pain.
  • Bunions: Similar because both are agonizing podiatric afflictions of the feet exacerbated by ill-fitting footwear; differs because a bunion (hallux valgus) is a structural, bony deformity and subluxation of the metatarsophalangeal joint at the base of the big toe, whereas a corn is strictly a dermatological hyperkeratosis of the skin itself.
  • Plantar Warts (Verrucas): Similar because both appear as circumscribed, painful keratinized lesions on the soles or toes; differs because plantar warts are infectious viral neoplasms caused by the Human Papillomavirus (HPV) featuring pinpoint black petechiae (clotted capillaries) and painful lateral squeezing, whereas corns are non-infectious mechanical lesions that hurt primarily under direct vertical pressure.
  • Blisters: Similar because both represent cutaneous reactions to friction and shearing forces from tight shoes; differs because blisters are acute, fluid-filled epidermal bullae caused by sudden mechanical shearing that separates skin layers, whereas corns develop slowly over months of chronic friction into dense, dry, keratinized structures.
  • Chilblains: Similar because both cause discomfort and inflammation on the toes; differs because chilblains are painful, itchy, erythemic inflammatory reactions of small cutaneous blood vessels caused by repeated exposure to cold and damp conditions, whereas corns stem purely from mechanical pressure and friction.
  • Spurs (Heel Spurs): Similar because both produce sharp, localized foot pain during ambulation; differs because a heel spur is an internal osteophytic bony outgrowth projecting from the calcaneus bone, whereas corns are superficial hyperkeratotic cutaneous plugs.
