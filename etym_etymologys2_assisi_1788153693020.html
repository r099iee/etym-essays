Note on the debate (zero guesswork): The pre-Roman meaning of the name is unknown. Folk etymologies (e.g. from Greek aseis, or from a supposed phrase about the wind) are inventions; serious etymologists note only that Asisium is of Umbrian or Etruscan substrate origin, possibly a place-name of the *Asis- family, and that no securely attested ancestor word survives.
