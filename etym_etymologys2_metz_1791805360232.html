Note: the Gaulish form is attested only in Latin sources; matīr is reconstructed from the plural matres and from cognates. The reduction of Mediomatrici to Mettis is not documented stage by stage, and the sense of the compound — “middle mother”, perhaps referring to the Matres worshipped in the region — is debated.
