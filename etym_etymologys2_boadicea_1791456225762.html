Proto-Celtic (c. 1000 BCE)
*boudi-
"victory, gain, profit"

↓
Common Brittonic (1st c. CE)
*Boudīkā
"victorious woman"

↓
Latin (c. 98–110 CE)
Boudica / Boudicca
"Boudica, queen of the Iceni"

↓
Early Modern English (1624–1658)
Boadicea
"Boudica, British queen"

↓
Boadicea
"the Iceni queen; a warrior woman"

Note: the pre-Celtic PIE origin of *boudi- is disputed. Proposed sources include *bʰewd- “gain, profit” and a derivative of *gʷew- “to increase”; because no single derivation is secure, the chain begins at the securely reconstructed Proto-Celtic stage rather than inventing an earlier link. The spelling Boadicea is a later scribal and antiquarian form, not a classical one.
