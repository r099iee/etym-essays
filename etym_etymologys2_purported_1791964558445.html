Notes: the prefix pur- continues Latin prō “forth, forward” through the Old French variant por-/pur-. The pre-Latin origin of portāre is disputed: the traditional derivation from PIE *per- “to lead, pass over” is not accepted by all scholars (de Vaan treats it as uncertain), so the chain begins with the attested Latin verb rather than a reconstructed root.
