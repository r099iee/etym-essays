- [Negative formation sense] - Formed as the opposite of "content," "discontented" originally meant simply "not content" — lacking ease of mind or satisfaction with one's situation.
- [Restless dissatisfaction sense] - It grew to describe a deeper, pervasive emotional restlessness — chronic gnawing unhappiness with one's lot, distinct from a one-off complaint about a specific matter.
- [Social & political resonance] - In historical usage "the discontented" could name a class or faction resentful of social or political conditions, giving the word overtones of agitation and grumbling against authority.
- [Contemporary Sense] - Today "discontented" denotes a general state of dissatisfaction and unease with one's circumstances, often implying self-pity, restlessness, or a chronically unfulfilled spirit.
