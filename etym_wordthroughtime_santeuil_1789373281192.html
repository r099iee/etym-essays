- Classical French Era (17th–18th c.) - Ecclesiastical Wit and Neo-Latin Poetics: Associated primarily with Jean-Baptiste Santeul (Santeuil), the eccentric Parisian canon-poet celebrated in the memoirs of Saint-Simon.
- 19th c. - Rural French Toponymy: Existed quietly as the geographic name of modest agricultural communes in the Paris basin and Centre-Val de Loire.
- Fin de Siècle (1895–1899) - Secret Manuscript in the Shadows: Hand-written across hundreds of loose manuscript sheets by Marcel Proust, abandoned in trunks as a perceived creative dead end.
- Mid-20th c. (1951–1952) - Miraculous Literary Resurrection: Discovered by Bernard de Fallois and published posthumously to international acclaim, transforming the name into a major literary sensation.
- Contemporary Era - Crucial Proustian Milestone: Studied worldwide as the essential aesthetic bridge revealing the evolution of modern fiction from third-person realism to first-person stream of consciousness.
