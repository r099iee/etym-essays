Old French
embroder
"to embroider, ornament
with needlework"
origin uncertain
↓
English (14th c.)
embroider
"to decorate fabric with needlework"
↓
English (present participle)
embroidering
"sewing patterns onto fabric;
embellishing"

Debate: the origin of Old French embroder is uncertain: it may derive from Frankish *brozdon "to braid, embroider" (cf. Old High German brort "edge") or be formed in French from en- + brode "embroidery", a word attested only later.
