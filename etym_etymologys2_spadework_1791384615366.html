Note: As an authentic compound noun, spadework unites two ancient Proto-Germanic stems. Root 1 (spade) descends from the PIE root for a flat piece of wood or blade, sharing cognates with Ancient Greek spáthē ("broad flat blade/spatula") and Spanish espada ("sword", whence the suit of spades in playing cards). Root 2 (work) represents the prolific PIE root *werǵ-, which produced Greek érgon ("work") and German Werk. The compound first emerged literally in horticultural manuals before undergoing a permanent metaphorical shift to intellectual and diplomatic groundwork in late Victorian English.
