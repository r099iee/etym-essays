• Arch-fiend: Similar in denoting a preeminent, supreme embodiment of demonic malice; differs because arch-fiend (often applied to Satan himself) refers directly to a chief devil of hell, whereas Antichrist represents a specific prophetic, deceptive human or pseudo-messianic figure who mimics and opposes Christ on the earthly stage.
  • Beast of Revelation: Similar in featuring as the catastrophic apocalyptic adversary in Christian eschatology; differs because the Beast (from Greek therion) is a symbolic apocalyptic monster representing oppressive imperial power and blasphemous state tyranny, whereas Antichrist focuses on theological counterfeit, spiritual deception, and false messianism.
  • False Prophet: Similar in operating through religious deception and leading humanity astray; differs because the False Prophet serves as an auxiliary propagandist and enabler to the primary apocalyptic adversary rather than being the ultimate supreme antagonist himself.
  • Son of Perdition: Similar as a direct biblical title for the apocalyptic apostate and arch-villain (used of Judas Iscariot and the Man of Lawlessness in 2 Thessalonians); differs because Son of Perdition is a poetic, fatalistic epithet highlighting doomed damnation, whereas Antichrist explicitly marks direct opposition and replacement of Christ.
  • Man of Sin / Man of Lawlessness: Similar as Pauline terminology for the culminating figure of rebellion; differs because this term emphasizes moral anarchy and rebellion against divine law, while Antichrist emphasizes theological opposition and mimicry of Christ.
  • Usurper: Similar in seeking to illegitimately claim supreme spiritual and worldly sovereignty; differs because usurper is a secular political term for anyone seizing power unlawfully, lacking the apocalyptic, metaphysical dimension of Antichrist.
  • Nemesis: Similar in personifying a formidable, relentless opposing force; differs because nemesis (from Greek nemein, "to distribute/avenge") denotes an inescapable agent of retributive justice or a bitter rival, whereas Antichrist is an agent of evil deception.
  • Satan / Lucifer: Similar in personifying supreme metaphysical evil; differs because Satan is the primordial fallen archangel and ruler of darkness, whereas the Antichrist in traditional theology is Satan's earthly instrument or incarnate human counterpart.
