NOTE: The Germanic dell-family is an e-grade variant of the dale-family: Proto-Germanic *daljō- "dell" stands beside *dalaz "dale, valley" (Old English dæl, Old Norse dalr, Old High German tal, German Tal; Middle Dutch delle and Middle Low German delle "hollow, pit"). The Indo-European stage is debated: some scholars (following Pokorny) group these forms with a root meaning "curve, hollow" (Greek thólos "dome, vault"), but no PIE reconstruction is universally accepted, and the family may be an independent Germanic formation. No attested PIE form exists; *daljō is a reconstruction, and the chain therefore begins at the earliest reconstructable Germanic stage.
