Note: the PIE root *pel- "pale, gray" is a standard reconstruction, but its exact semantic range is debated and some scholars treat the Latin pallere family as an isolated lexical group. Latin pallidus is a parallel adjective derived from pallere, not an ancestor of the French verb, and is therefore omitted from the direct chain. The Old French prefix a- is generally explained as a Romance development of Latin ad- "to", though an intensive function has also been suggested. The noun pall "funeral cloth" is from Latin pallium "cloak" and is not related.
