Note on nuance: The connection of Germanic kind- to a PIE root *kand- "to glow, shine" (also seen in Latin candēre "to shine, be white-hot" and Sanskrit cand- "to shine") is a standard reconstruction following Pokorny and Watkins, but some handbooks treat the Germanic base as isolated. The English verb shows the frequentative suffix -le (Old Norse kynda beside kindla), and the Latin prefix re- is itself of debated origin, possibly related to retrō "backwards".
