- [Medieval Europe] - [A luxury white winter fur of the stoat; sumptuary laws restrict its use to royalty, high nobility, and judges]
- [Renaissance] - [Becomes the emblem of purity and incorruptibility: a creature that supposedly dies rather than soil its white coat; central to heraldry and emblem books]
- [Contemporary sense] - [A historic and literary term for the fur and its regal, judicial associations, or for the stoat in its white winter coat]
