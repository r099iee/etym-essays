Note: deeper origin debated. Greek Idomeneus (Ἰδομενεύς) contains the common heroic suffix -eus, which Beekes regards as Pre-Greek substrate, so no secure PIE root exists. Ancient guesses linked Ido- to Mount Ida in Crete or to idos + menos "strength"; these remain unproven folk-etymologies. Best attested chain therefore starts at Greek.
