↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE–200 CE, reconstructed)
*sahsą
"knife, short sword"

↓
West Germanic (c. 1st c. BCE–4th c. CE, reconstructed)
*sahsō
"seax-man, Saxon"

↓
Latin (2nd–4th c. CE)
Saxones
"the Saxon people"

↓
Old English (9th–11th c.)
Seaxe
"Saxons"

↓
Middle English (12th–15th c.)
Saxons
"the Saxon people"

↓
Saxons
"Germanic settlers of early England"

Note: the derivation of the ethnonym from the blade-word *sahsą is a well-supported reconstruction, not an ancient statement; the West Germanic stage *sahsō is inferential and the exact route from "knife" to "tribe" is debated.
