Note on debate: the origin of Latin pīla "pillar, pier" is uncertain. It may be of Etruscan origin, like many Latin words for structures, or connected with the verb pīlāre "to ram, to compress" (the source of the verb "to pile (things up)"). The English word "pile" meaning "a heap" descends from the same Latin pīla by a different semantic route (a "pillar" built of stacked material) or possibly from a separate Latin pīla "ball"; the two words are identical in spelling but distinct in development. The fabric "pile" (nap) is yet another word, from Latin pilus "hair".
