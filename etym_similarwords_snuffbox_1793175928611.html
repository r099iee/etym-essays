• Pillbox: Similar because both are tiny lidded pocket containers; differs because a pillbox holds tablets or capsules for dosing, while a snuffbox historically held powdered tobacco for sniffing.
  • Tinderbox: Similar because both are small 18th-century pocket boxes with hinged lids; differs because a tinderbox held flint, steel, and tinder for striking fire, while a snuffbox held scented tobacco for pleasure.
  • Patch box: Similar because both are ornamental 18th-century toilette boxes, often enamelled and jewelled; differs because a patch box held beauty spots and patches for the face, while a snuffbox held tobacco.
  • Tabatière: Similar because it is literally the French word for a snuffbox and names the same object; differs because tabatière belongs to French luxury vocabulary and connoisseurship, while snuffbox is the plain English term.
  • Snuff bottle: Similar because both are pocket vessels for powdered tobacco with a tight seal; differs because the snuff bottle is the Chinese stoppered-flask tradition in glass or jade, while the snuffbox is the European hinged-box tradition in metal or shell.
  • Tobacco tin: Similar because both are portable containers that keep tobacco fresh; differs because a tobacco tin is a plain utilitarian canister for pipe or rolling tobacco, while a snuffbox is a small dressed-up case for powdered tobacco and display.
  • Bonbonnière: Similar because both are dainty lidded boxes meant to be offered round company; differs because a bonbonnière holds sweets and sugared almonds, while a snuffbox held tobacco, and its etiquette was masculine and clubbable where the sweet-box is festive.
  • Trinket box: Similar because both are small decorative keepsake containers; differs because trinket box is the broad modern term for any jewel or keepsake casket, while snuffbox names the specific historical tobacco-box form.
  • Pyx: Similar because both are tiny lidded boxes carried on the person, often of precious material; differs because a pyx is a sacred vessel for the reserved sacrament, while a snuffbox is secular and sociable.
