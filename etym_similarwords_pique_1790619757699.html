• provoke: Similar because it means to rouse to action or feeling; differs because it is broader and more hostile, lacking the light prick and curiosity senses.
  • stimulate: Similar because it means to excite interest; differs because it is neutral and scientific, without wounded pride.
  • whet: Similar because it means to sharpen appetite or curiosity; differs because it is almost always pleasant and appetitive, never resentful.
  • nettle: Similar because it means to sting pride into irritation; differs because it stresses itching annoyance rather than dignity.
  • offend: Similar because it means to wound feelings; differs because it implies moral violation, while pique implies vanity.
  • umbrage: Similar because as a noun it means resentment at a slight; differs because it is purely nominal and more formal.
  • huff: Similar because it means a fit of pique; differs because it is colloquial and stresses sulky withdrawal.
  • rile: Similar because it means to irritate; differs because it is informal and implies agitation rather than wounded pride.
  • goad: Similar because it means to spur with a point; differs because it stresses driving to action rather than brooding resentment.
  • titillate: Similar because it means to teasingly excite; differs because it is often sensual or frivolous, without offence.
