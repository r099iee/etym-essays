- [17th century cannot be drained] - [First applied to wells, springs, mines, and funds of money that cannot be drawn dry; concrete and physical]
- [18th century boundless resources] - [Extends to supplies, patience, and energy — a fund that never runs out, admired as a wonder of nature or character]
- [19th century intellectual appetite] - [Applied to curiosity and wonder, as of a scholar; an inner spring that every new fact feeds rather than drains]
- [20th century to present hyperbolic] - [Common for energy, patience, curiosity, and 'sources of inspiration'; keeps the image of a supply resisting depletion but is often loose and admiring]
