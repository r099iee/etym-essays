Note on the debate: the Old English form *tūcian is reconstructed rather than directly attested, and the link to PIE *deuk- "to lead, draw" is plausible but not certain; some handbooks treat the Germanic verb as an isolated expressive formation imitating a tugging motion. The Middle English tucken and the modern senses are secure.
