Note: The word's deep ancestry is debated. A probable link is to the PIE root *kreus- ('to begin to move, push, shake'), but the immediate Old French source reuser 'to drive back' is the commonly accepted immediate origin, filtered through Middle English falconry. The connection to *kreus- is plausible but not certain, so treat the topmost stage as an informed reconstruction rather than a firmly attested form.
