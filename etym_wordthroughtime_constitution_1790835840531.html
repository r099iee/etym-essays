- c. 1300s-1400s (Middle English): Constitucioun referred to an enacted law, statutory ordinance, decree, or physical establishment.
- c. 1500s-1600s (Renaissance): Emergence of the medical sense (innate bodily health, humoral temperament) alongside legal definitions.
- c. 1700s (Enlightenment): Fusion of the body politic metaphor into the modern political sense: supreme written or unwritten charter of state governance (e.g. US Constitution, 1787).
- c. 1800s-Present: Robust coexistence of the biological health sense, the chemical/material structure sense, and the supreme political-legal sense.
