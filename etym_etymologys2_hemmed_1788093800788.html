Note: the ultimate Indo-European origin of *hamjaną is uncertain — a connection with a PIE root meaning "to cover" has been suggested but is debated, so the chain is presented only from the attested Germanic stage. The noun hem "border of cloth" (Middle English hemme) is closely intertwined with the verb: edging a garment encloses its cut edge, which is why the "border" and "enclose" senses share one family. The imitative "hem" of "hem and haw" is a separate, later word.
