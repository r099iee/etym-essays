[Root Word 1: aśva (horse)]
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*h₁éḱwos
"horse, the swift one"
(source of Latin equus, Greek hippos)
↓
Proto-Indo-Iranian (c. 2000 BCE)
*Hácwas
"horse, steed"
(source of Avestan aspa)
↓
Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE)
áśva- (अश्व)
"horse, stallion, courser"

[Root Word 2: medha (sacrifice/offering)]
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*méd-o- / *maydʰ-
"to measure, rule; sacrificial fat, sap, offering"
(source of Avestan mazda, Latin modus)
↓
Proto-Indo-Iranian (c. 2000 BCE)
*mádʰas / *máyzdʰas
"sacrificial offering, ritual victim"
↓
Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE)
médha- (मेध)
"sacrificial slaughter, oblatory offering, essence"
↓
Sanskrit Compound (c. 1000 BCE)
aśvamedhá- (अश्वमेध)
"the grand imperial horse sacrifice"
↓
Modern Anglicized Plural (c. 18th c. – Present)
aśvamedhas
"multiple imperial horse sacrifices proving sovereign suzerainty"
