• Bear: Similar because both describe meeting a cost or burden; differs because bear stresses enduring weight or pressure (bear the expense, bear the blame), while afford stresses having sufficient means before the cost is met.
  • Manage: Similar because both express coping with limited resources; differs because manage is general contrivance (manage a smile, manage the rent), while afford specifically weighs means against price or consequence.
  • Sustain: Similar because both imply keeping something going at a cost; differs because sustain stresses continued support over time (sustain a loss, sustain growth), while afford stresses the single judgement that the cost is bearable.
  • Spare: Similar because both concern giving up time, money, or attention; differs because spare stresses the leftover fragment (spare a minute, spare a coin), while afford stresses the whole capacity to meet the outlay.
  • Provide: Similar because in the literary sense both mean to supply or yield; differs because provide is the plain neutral word of supplying, while afford in this sense is formal and usually names pleasures or opportunities (afford delight, afford insight).
  • Yield: Similar because both describe what land, books, or inquiry gives forth; differs because yield stresses production under pressure or cultivation, while afford stresses the generous offering laid open to the observer.
  • Offer: Similar because both present something for acceptance; differs because offer stresses the voluntary gesture of presenting, while afford stresses what the thing itself makes available by its nature.
  • Grant: Similar because both bestow something on a recipient; differs because grant is official and authoritative (grant permission, grant asylum), while afford is quieter, often describing what circumstances bestow.
  • Risk: Similar because cannot afford to converges with daring or hazarding; differs because risk names the gamble itself, while afford names the prior judgement that the gamble is beyond one's means.
