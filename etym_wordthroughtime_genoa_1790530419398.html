- Antiquity (c. 3rd c. BCE - 5th c. CE) - Latin Genua; important naval base and port of the Ligures tribe allied with Rome during the Punic Wars; key road junction on the Via Aurelia.
- Middle Ages & Maritime Republic (1000s-1500s) - Serenissima Repubblica di Genova emerges; crusader fleets, naval wars with Pisa and Venice; establishing colonies in Crimea (Caffa), Chios, and Corsica; Birthplace of Columbus.
- Early Modern & Spanish Age (1500s-1700s) - 'El siglo de los Genoveses' (The Century of the Genoese); Andrea Doria allies with Charles V; Genoese bankers finance the Spanish Empire; city becomes a capital of Baroque art and banking.
- Napoleonic Era (1797-1815) - Republic overthrown by French revolutionary forces; becomes Ligurian Republic and annexed by Napoleon in 1805 (the event in Tolstoy's War and Peace); later awarded to Kingdom of Sardinia at Congress of Vienna (1815).
- Risorgimento & Modern Era (1848-present) - Crucible of Italian unification under Mazzini; emerges as the premier seaport of modern Italy and key component of the industrial triangle (Milan-Turin-Genoa).
