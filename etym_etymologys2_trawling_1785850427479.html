Debate: English trawl (1560s) was borrowed from Middle Dutch traghelen/trawelen; the noun trawl (c. 1630s) is probably a back-formation from the verb, though it may draw directly on Middle Dutch traghel. Middle Dutch traghel is generally taken from Latin tragula (derived from trahere), but the exact route of transmission is uncertain. The figurative sense "to search systematically" developed only in the 20th century.
