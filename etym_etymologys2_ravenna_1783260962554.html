CHAIN — THE PRE-ROMAN TOPONYM (single, uncertain root): Stage 1 — Pre-Roman Italic (Etruscan or Umbrian / local substrate) *Ravenna — the original settlement name, meaning UNKNOWN. It belongs to the stock of pre-Latin place-names of central-northern Italy; no secure PIE etymology exists, and none should be fabricated. Some scholars have speculated about an Etruscan connection or a link to words for "slope" or "ravine", but these remain guesses. Stage 2 — Latin Ravenna (attested from the 1st century BC — in Cicero's letters and speeches and in Caesar's campaign record of 52 BC; later in Pliny and the historians; a Roman municipium and, from 402, the capital of the Western Roman Empire). Stage 3 — Italian Ravenna (the unbroken continuation of the Latin name in the spoken Romance of northern Italy). Stage 4 — English Ravenna (proper name; taken into English historical and travel writing from the 16th century onwards and universalized as a learned place-name). DEBATE NOTE: the name's origin is genuinely unknown. It shows no demonstrable Indo-European derivation and is best classified as a substrate (Etruscan / North Italic) relic; the "raven" association is pure folk etymology, for the city is not named after the bird. From Latin Ravenna onward the chain is historically secure.
