PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*(s)peu- / *bʰit- (uncertain / substrate)
"to swell; marrow, core"
Note: Deep PIE origin is debated; historical linguists treat it as a primary West Germanic vegetative root.
