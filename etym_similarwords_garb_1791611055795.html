• attire: Similar because both refer to complete clothing worn on the body; differs because attire focuses on formal, elegant, or decorative apparel (e.g., "business attire", "festive attire"), whereas garb specifically emphasizes distinctive professional, cultural, or symbolic identity.
  • apparel: Similar because both serve as elevated terms for clothing; differs because apparel is a commercial, industrial, and generic umbrella term (e.g., "outerwear apparel", "athletic apparel"), whereas garb carries narrative, historical, or role-playing associations.
  • costume: Similar because both designate distinctive clothing associated with a specific era, role, or culture; differs because costume strongly implies theatrical performance, disguise, masquerade, or artificial impersonation, while garb can describe genuine everyday professional or spiritual clothing (e.g., monastic garb).
  • vestments: Similar because both denote ceremonial or institutional dress; differs because vestments is strictly confined to liturgical, sacred, or high ecclesiastical robes (e.g., priests, bishops, choir members), whereas garb applies across secular, literary, and mechanical domains.
  • guise: Similar because both denote outward appearance or external presentation, especially when concealing an inner reality; differs because guise refers primarily to an assumed semblance, deceptive pretense, or outward manner without necessarily involving physical textiles, while garb remains anchored in physical clothing or book formatting.
  • raiment: Similar because both are elevated synonyms for dress; differs because raiment is an archaic, poetic, and biblical term evoking splendor, beauty, or spiritual investiture, whereas garb is versatile, analytical, and readily applied to everyday workwear or publishing formats.
  • habit: Similar because both describe distinctive dress associated with a particular calling; differs because habit is restricted almost entirely to religious orders (monks and nuns) or equestrian riding clothes, whereas garb encompasses all varieties of cultural and material presentation.
