• Crete: Similar because it is a large historic Greek island with Minoan and Venetian layers; differs because it is far larger and associated with Minos and the labyrinth rather than Helios and the Knights.
  • Cyprus: Similar because it is an eastern Mediterranean island at a cultural crossroads; differs because its history stresses copper, Aphrodite and modern division rather than Doric maritime league.
  • Cos (Kos): Similar because it is a neighbouring Dodecanesian island with Hellenistic poets and medicine; differs because it is smaller and famed for Hippocrates rather than the Colossus.
  • Sicily: Similar because it is a large strategically placed island fought over by empires; differs because it is Italian, far larger, and tied to Greek tyrants and Rome rather than Hospitallers.
  • Malta: Similar because it was also a Hospitaller fortress island; differs because its language and British colonial history are distinct and it lacks ancient Greek polis heritage.
  • Lindos: Similar because it is an ancient Rhodian city on Rhodes itself; differs because it is a town part, not the whole island.
  • Dodecanese: Similar because Rhodes is its capital group; differs because it is the archipelago, not the single island.
