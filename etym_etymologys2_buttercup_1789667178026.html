Notes: Greek boútyron is traditionally bous, cow, plus tūrós, cheese, but tūrós itself has no secure PIE etymology and Beekes treats it as Pre-Greek substrate, so the ultimate pre-Greek stage is left open. Latin cūpa / cuppa is of unknown deeper origin; proposed PIE *keup-, hollow vessel, is unproven per de Vaan and is not asserted here. Modern buttercup as a compound is first attested in Middle English.
