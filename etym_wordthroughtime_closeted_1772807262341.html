- [Small private room] - "Closet" enters English for a small private room or chamber for study, prayer, or confidential business; "closeted" means shut away in such a room.
- [Secret conference] - In literary and political English "closeted with" comes to mean shut away with someone for a private interview or discussion, excluding outsiders.
- [Modern hidden-identity branch] - In the 20th century the word branches into "in the closet / closeted" for concealing one's identity — a different semantic offshoot of hiddenness that coexists with the older conference sense.
