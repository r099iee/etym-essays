Debate: Horeb is traditionally connected with the root ḥāraḇ "to be dry, desolate" — the "desolate, dry place" — but this is a popular etymology whose origin is not certain, and the identification of Horeb with Sinai is itself debated. "Oreb" is the English form used by Milton; the KJV uses "Horeb".
