Debate note: διθύραμβος (dithyrambos) is a pre-Greek / non-IE loanword of uncertain origin. Ancient folk etymologies (e.g. "entering through two doors," from θύρα "door") are fanciful. The English adjective comes via Latin dithyrambicus and French dithyrambique.
