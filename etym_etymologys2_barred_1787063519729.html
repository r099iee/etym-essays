Latin (1st c. BCE)
barra
"bar, rod, beam, barrier"

Old French (10th-11th c.)
barre
"bar, bolt, barrier"
↓
Old French (12th c.)
barrer

↓
Middle English (13th c.)
barren
"to fasten with bars; to block"
↓
Modern English (past participle)
barred
"fastened or blocked; marked with bars"

Note: Latin barra is of uncertain origin; it appears late in Latin and is possibly borrowed from a Gaulish (Celtic) word for "beam, pole, pointed rod."
