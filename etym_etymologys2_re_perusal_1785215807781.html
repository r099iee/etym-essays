Notes: (1) The PIE reconstruction for ūtī is debated (variants *h₁ewH- / *h₂ew- are cited); Latin ūsus, past participle of ūtī, underlies the frequentative ūsāre, hence Old French user "to use". (2) Peruse originally meant "to use up" (per- intensive "through, completely"); under Latin influence it came to mean "to go through, examine" and from the 1520s "to read through". (3) re- is the Latin prefix "again, back", added in English to perusal.
