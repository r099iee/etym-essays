• Limbs: Similar because arms are limbs; differs because “limbs” covers legs and branches too and has no weapons sense.
  • Branches: Similar because both name projecting parts (of a tree, mountain or organisation); differs because “branch” is organic and neutral, while “arm” implies a longer, narrower reach.
  • Spurs: Similar because a mountain spur is a projecting ridge, as an arm of a mountain is; differs because “spur” is a technical-geological term for a sharp lateral ridge, with no body or weapons senses.
  • Ridges: Similar because both describe mountain features; differs because “ridge” is a long, narrow crest, not necessarily arm-like, and lacks the personification.
  • Projections: Similar because both name things that jut out; differs because “projection” is a colourless technical term with none of the human imagery of “arm”.
  • Appendages: Similar because arms are appendages; differs because “appendage” is clinical and includes tails, antennae and attached parts generally.
  • Weapons: Similar because “arms” (weapons sense) means exactly weapons; differs because “weapons” is the plain modern word, while “arms” is formal, historical and military in register.
  • Armaments: Similar because both mean military equipment; differs because “armaments” is institutional and technical (navies, budgets), while “arms” is the older, broader word.
  • Firearms: Similar because firearms are a major subclass of arms; differs because “firearms” names only guns, not swords, spears or shields.
  • Weaponry: Similar because both are collective terms for weapons; differs because “weaponry” is modern and abstract, while “arms” carries the heraldic and soldierly heritage.
  • Emblems / bearings: Similar because “arms” in heraldry are emblems of identity; differs because “emblem” is generic, while “arms” specifically means the granted heraldic device.
  • Handles / levers: Similar because the arm of a machine is its projecting working member; differs because these name specific machine parts rather than the general concept of reach.
