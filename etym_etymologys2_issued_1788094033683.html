NOTE: The verb may derive from the English noun issue (16th c.) or directly from the Old French past participle issu; either way the sense history is identical. The Latin verb īre "to go" is from PIE *h₁ey-, one of the most widely attested Indo-European roots (Sanskrit éti "he goes," Greek eîmi "I go"), and survives in English in exit, itinerary, initial, and transit.
