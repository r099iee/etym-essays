"swiftest, fastest"
↓
New Latin / German (1859)
Tachistoskop (A.W. Volkmann)
"swiftest-viewer: device for"
"instantaneous visual display"
↓
Late 19th c. English (c. 1880s)
tachistoscopic + -ally
"by rapid timed exposure"
↓
Modern English (20th c.)
tachistoscopically
"by means of a tachistoscope;"
"at brief, microsecond exposure"

Note: Coined from Greek τάχιστος (tákhistos, 'swiftest', superlative of takhýs) + σκοπέω (skopéō, 'to view, examine', from PIE *speḱ- 'to observe'); coined in 1859 by German physiologist Alfred Wilhelm Volkmann for his gravity-drop visual exposure apparatus.
