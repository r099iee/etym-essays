• Blind: Similar because it refers to the inability to see; differs because "blind" implies a more permanent or complete loss of sight, while "dazzle" is temporary and often caused by excessive light.
  • Glare: Similar because it involves intense, often uncomfortable light; differs because "glare" can refer to the light itself or the act of staring fiercely, while "dazzle" is the effect of that light on vision.
  • Stun: Similar because it implies being overwhelmed or incapacitated; differs because "stun" can be caused by a blow, shock, or surprise, not necessarily light, and often implies a more physical or mental paralysis.
  • Bewilder: Similar because it means to confuse or perplex; differs because "bewilder" focuses on mental confusion, while "dazzle" specifically links this confusion to an overwhelming sensory (often visual) input or impressive display.
  • Amaze: Similar because it means to fill with great wonder or astonishment; differs because "amaze" is generally positive and focuses on the wonder, while "dazzle" can also imply a temporary incapacitation or even a superficiality.
  • Overwhelm: Similar because it means to overpower or engulf; differs because "overwhelm" is a broader term for being overcome by any strong emotion, force, or quantity, while "dazzle" specifies the cause as brilliance or intense light.
  • Mesmerize: Similar because it means to hypnotize or enthrall, captivating attention; differs because "mesmerize" often implies a more sustained, almost trance-like state of fascination, while "dazzle" can be more sudden and transient.
