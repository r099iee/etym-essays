Note on the first step (debated): the link between Latin mateola and Old English mattuc is judged probable by the OED but is not certain — no intermediate form is attested anywhere, and any Late Latin or Romance derivative (e.g. a reconstructed *matteuca) is unrecorded conjecture. The Old English suffix -uc/-oc is a native noun-forming suffix, so the word may even have been shaped on English soil; no PIE etymon is established for either form, so the chain begins with the first attested Latin word rather than a reconstructed root.
