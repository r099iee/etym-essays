PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*dret- / *dred- (or PIE root of Proto-Germanic *tredaną)
"to step, tread, walk, run"
Note: Historical linguists reconstruct Proto-Germanic *tredaną from an Indo-European root signifying rhythmic stepping or foot-movement, with cognates in Celtic (Old Irish *droch 'wheel') and Balto-Slavic.
