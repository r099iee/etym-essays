Proto-Germanic Sound-Imitative Root (expressive / onomatopoeic)
*hwis- / *hwez-
"to hiss, whistle, murmur, rush"

Old Norse Cognate (c. 9th–12th c.)
hvísa / hvæsa
"to hiss, breathe heavily, whistle"
