• Intimidating: Similar because both provoke fear or loss of confidence; differs because intimidating carries a more personal, almost threatening colour, while daunting is measured and impersonal.
  • Formidable: Similar because both describe something that inspires dread; differs because formidable emphasises the respect commanded by sheer power, excellence or magnitude, while daunting stresses the effect of deterring action.
  • Overwhelming: Similar because both describe something too much to handle; differs because overwhelming suggests being flooded or swamped by sheer volume or emotion, not deterred by difficulty.
  • Discouraging: Similar because both describe something that saps motivation; differs because discouraging focuses on draining hope or heart, while daunting couples genuine difficulty with a rational hesitation.
  • Dismaying: Similar because both describe a loss of confidence; differs because dismaying implies a shock or disappointment that demoralises, while daunting implies a sober assessment of size or difficulty.
  • Frightening: Similar because both describe something that makes a person afraid; differs because frightening is blunter and more visceral, producing real fear, while daunting produces a calm pause rather than terror.
  • Fearsome: Similar because both describe something that inspires fear; differs because fearsome describes the object's terrifying quality, while daunting focuses on the effect of making a person hesitate to begin.
  • Alarming: Similar because both describe something unsettling; differs because alarming implies a sudden warning or concern, while daunting implies a steady, acknowledged weight of difficulty.
  • Forbidding: Similar because both describe something hostile or discouraging; differs because forbidding suggests an appearance of unfriendliness or threat, while daunting suggests size and difficulty that deter effort.
  • Arduous: Similar because both describe something requiring great effort; differs because arduous stresses the exhausting difficulty of the labour itself, while daunting stresses the intimidating effect before one begins.
  • Exacting: Similar because both describe something demanding; differs because exacting stresses the stringent requirements and high standards of the task, while daunting stresses the task's power to discourage.
  • Challenging: Similar because both describe something demanding effort; differs because challenging is positive and inviting - a test one welcomes - while daunting is deterring and requires courage or skill to attempt.
