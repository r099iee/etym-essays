Note: PIE *plak- to strike is the standard account (Greek plege blow, Latin plangere to beat the breast, plag- wound). Latin plaga (blow) is distinct from plaga (net, region, from a different base) though medieval spelling confuses them; the disease sense develops via Vulgate plagae Aegypti (blows of Egypt).
