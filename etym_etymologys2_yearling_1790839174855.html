Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*yēro- / *yōr- + *-ling-
"year, season; diminutive/entity suffix"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*jērą + *-lingaz
"year; one belonging to a year"
↓
Old English (c. 9th c.)
ġēarling / ġēarlic
"yearling, creature one year old"
↓
Middle English (c. 1300)
yerling / yeerlyng
"an animal in its second year"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present)
yearling
"an animal between one and two years old"
