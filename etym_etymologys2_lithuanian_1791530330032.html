Note: the prehistory of the name is unknown, and no Proto-Indo-European root can be reconstructed with confidence. The proposal most often cited derives Lietuva from the small river Lietava (or Lietauka) near Kernavė, an early power centre; a second connects it with leitis “free man” or with a tribal name; a third, popular but speculative, links it to lietus “rain.” The first written attestation is Litua in the Quedlinburg Annals for 1009, in the account of the mission of St Bruno of Querfurt.
