Note: oak from PIE *h₂eyǵ- via PGmc *aiks is secure (Greek aigilops, Latin aesculus). Apple from PGmc *aplaz is secure within Germanic, but deeper PIE is debated; Beekes and de Vaan treat *abel- as a north-European substrate Wanderwort shared with Celtic ubull/afal and Slavic ablŭko, not a single PIE root, so no PIE stage is posited for apple beyond the substrate note. Compound oak-apple first attested as okappel in 14th-c. herbals.
