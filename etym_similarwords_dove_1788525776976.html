• Pigeon: Similar because both belong to the bird family Columbidae and share stout bodies, soft plumage, and cooing calls; differs because 'pigeon' is typically applied to larger, heavier species (such as the common city rock pigeon, Columba livia) and often carries urban or utilitarian connotations, whereas 'dove' generally designates smaller, more delicate species and carries exalted cultural, spiritual, and romantic associations.
  • Squab: Similar because it is a member of the columbid family; differs because 'squab' specifically refers to a young, unfledged pigeon or dove bred and harvested as culinary meat, lacking all poetic and symbolic connotations of the mature dove.
  • Columbid: Similar because it encompasses all doves and pigeons; differs because 'columbid' is a formal, scientific taxonomic classification referring to any member of the family Columbidae, without the metaphorical, literary, or political meanings of 'dove'.
  • Turtle Dove (Streptopelia turtur): Similar because it is a classic species of dove celebrated in European literature; differs because it is a specific migratory species known for its distinct purring call and mottled brown and black plumage, historically symbolizing lifelong fidelity and conjugal devotion.
  • Pacifist: Similar because both represent opposition to war and militarism; differs because a pacifist is a human individual adhering to a principled philosophical or moral opposition to all violence and armed conflict, whereas a political 'dove' is often a policymaker advocating negotiation and de-escalation in a specific geopolitical context without necessarily rejecting all defensive warfare.
  • Culver: Similar because it is an archaic and dialectal English synonym for a dove or wood-pigeon; differs because 'culver' (from Old English culfre, via Latin columba) is obsolete in modern standard English and preserved primarily in historical verse and regional place-names.
  • Moderate: Similar because both favor non-aggressive and measured approaches; differs because a moderate is characterized by broad ideological centrism across all political domains, whereas a 'dove' specifically denotes someone favoring peaceful diplomatic resolution over military escalation, or low interest rates in central banking.
