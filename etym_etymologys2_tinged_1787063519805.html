Note on nuance: The reconstructed PIE etymon *teng- rests chiefly on Latin tingere itself; solid cognates outside Italic are scarce, so the pre-Latin stage is regarded as probable rather than certain. English tinge (15th c.) was shaped by the Old French verb teindre and its participle teint, both themselves descendants of Latin tingere, so Latin, Old French, and English stream together rather than forming a simple one-way chain.
