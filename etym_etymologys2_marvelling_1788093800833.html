Note: The English verb may derive directly from Old French merveillier or be formed in English from the noun marvel (< Old French merveille); both routes converge on the same forms. The PIE root *smei- "to smile" (cf. Sanskrit smáyate, Greek meidán) underlies mīrus through the notion of astonished wonder — the open, wondering face.
