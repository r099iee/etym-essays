• Kindling: Similar because both are combustible materials used to start a fire; differs because kindling is small dry sticks and wood shavings laid to feed an already-kindled flame, while tinder is the fine, fluffy material that catches the initial spark itself.
  • Touchwood / Amadou: Similar because both are literally tinder made from dried fungus; differs because touchwood/amadou is a specific historical preparation (the bracket fungus Fomes fomentarius softened and charred), whereas tinder is the general class of spark-catching material.
  • Punk: Similar because it is dry, tinder-like material that smoulders and catches sparks; differs because punk is specifically dry rotten wood, coarser and usually used for smouldering or fuse-like burning, not for catching the first spark.
  • Char-cloth: Similar because it is a prepared tinder that catches a spark from flint and steel; differs because char-cloth is a modern, deliberately charred cotton or linen cloth, a manufactured version of the older natural tinders.
  • Spunk: Similar because in Scottish and northern dialect it means touchwood, tinder, or a spark; differs because spunk is archaic/dialectal and also carries the unrelated sense of courage or pluck, which tinder never does.
  • Fuel: Similar because both are burnable material for a fire; differs because fuel is the bulk substance that sustains burning (wood, coal, oil), while tinder is the tiny, fine, low-ignition material that starts the flame in the first place.
  • Tinder-box: Similar because it is inseparable from tinder historically and even shares the root; differs because the tinder-box is the container holding tinder, flint, and steel — the kit itself — not the combustible substance.
  • Fomite: Similar because the word derives from Latin fomes "tinder" and shares the "carrier" image; differs because a fomite is a medical object that transmits infection, a wholly figurative echo, while tinder remains a literal fire-starter.
  • Firelighter: Similar because it ignites a fire quickly; differs because a firelighter is a manufactured, often paraffin-soaked block or liquid, a modern convenience, whereas tinder is a natural or traditionally prepared dry material.
