Note on Etymology: Formed from Latin prefix sub- (which underwent regressive assimilation to suc- before the voiceless velar stop /k/ of cēdere) meaning "from below" or "close behind", and cēdere ("to step, go, yield"). The literal physical sense of stepping up close behind another in rank or march evolved into the temporal sense of following in time and the institutional sense of inheriting an office.
