- 19th c. - Russian literary & philosophical usage: Everyday and literary noun (*переживание*) for deep emotional soul-searching, heartache, or spiritual trials.
- 1898–1930s - Stanislavski & Moscow Art Theatre: Elevated into a foundational technical term for the "Art of Experiencing" in acting theory.
- 1930s - Vygotskian developmental psychology: Lev Vygotsky established *perezhivanie* as the vital unit of analysis linking individual consciousness to culture.
- Mid–Late 20th c. - Global theatrical translation: Adopted into international theatre studies and English scholarship to resolve mistranslations of Russian acting theory.
- 21st c. - Academic & performance renaissance: Extensively studied in contemporary psychology, cognitive science, and performance studies as a holistic model of human emotion.
