Note: prefix be- is Old English be- / bi- "about, around" from Proto-Germanic *bi "by, about" from PIE *h1epi "on, upon". Deeper root *gwet- say is debated: Boutkan rejects proposed cognates (Sanskrit gadati, Welsh guetid, Latin vetare) on formal grounds and marks it of no certain IE etymology. Simple verb survives only as fossil quoth "said" from OE cwæth past tense.
