• SPEED: Similar because both denote rapid movement or rapid action; differs because speed is a neutral measure of rate with no implication of competence or completion, while despatch adds decisiveness, finality, and the finishing of business.
  • PROMPTNESS: Similar because both mean acting quickly; differs because promptness means being on time, punctuality, while despatch means executing and finishing with brisk efficiency, not merely meeting a schedule.
  • ALACRITY: Similar because both denote swiftness of action; differs because alacrity adds cheerful eagerness and willingness, while despatch is economical and businesslike, without the note of enthusiasm.
  • EFFICIENCY: Similar because both describe getting work done well; differs because efficiency is about the best use of resources with little waste, while despatch is specifically about speed joined to completion.
  • EXPEDITION: Similar because both mean speed in doing business; differs because expedition is grander, more literary, and emphasizes haste in travelling or transacting, while despatch emphasizes decisive settlement and sending off.
  • HASTE: Similar because both mean quickness; differs because haste implies urgency and often carelessness or recklessness, while despatch implies controlled, economical, efficient speed.
  • CELERITY: Similar because both mean swiftness; differs because celerity is a formal, literary word for pure quickness of movement, without despatch's sense of business settled and sent away.
  • SWIFTNESS: Similar because both denote rapidity; differs because swiftness is purely descriptive of rate, while despatch adds the connotation of prompt, decisive settlement of affairs.
  • BRIEFNESS: Similar because both suggest something done without delay or prolongation; differs because briefness refers to shortness in time or extent, while despatch refers to speed of execution and finality of outcome.
  • READINESS: Similar because both describe being prepared to act at once; differs because readiness is a state of preparedness, while despatch is the actual brisk performance of the act.
