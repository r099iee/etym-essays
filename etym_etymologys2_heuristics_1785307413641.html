Debate: a PIE source for εὑρίσκειν
is proposed by some (Pokorny *uer- "to
find, win") but is not secure; Beekes
suspects a Pre-Greek origin. The noun
heuristics is the plural of the adjective
heuristic, borrowed from Greek εὑριστικός.
