Note: the prefix un- continues PIE negative *n- via Proto-Germanic *un- and Old English un- (not), added in English to covetous; it is not Latin in- but Germanic. Vulgar Latin *cupidietare is reconstructed, not directly attested, but required by Old French coveitier; deeper PIE *kup- is secure via Sanskrit kupyati. The shift from desiring (cupere) to illicit desiring (covet) happens in French and English moral theology.
