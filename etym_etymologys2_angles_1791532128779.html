Note: the tribal name is usually derived from Proto-Germanic *angulaz “hook, angle,” from PIE *h₂enk- “to bend,” with reference to the hook-shaped coastline of Angeln. A rival explanation connects it with the root meaning “narrow” (as in Latin angustus), referring to the narrow Schlei inlet; both are defensible and the question is unresolved. Latin Anglii (Tacitus) and Angloi (Ptolemy) may not designate exactly the same group as the later Angles.
