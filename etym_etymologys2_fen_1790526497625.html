fenn / fen
"marsh, mud, mire, wetland"
↓

fen / fenne

fen

Etymological Note: The word fen is a direct descendant of the Proto-Germanic neuter noun *fanją ("marsh, fen, mire"), which originated from the Proto-Indo-European root *pen- ("swamp, mud, water"). Under Grimm's Law, the initial PIE voiceless stop *p- regularly shifted to the Proto-Germanic voiceless fricative *f-. Cognates are found throughout all historical Germanic branches, including Old Frisian fenne, Old Saxon fenni, Old High German fenna (modern German Fenn / Venn), Dutch veen ("peat, fen"), Old Norse fen, and Gothic fani (translating Greek πηλός, "mud, clay").
