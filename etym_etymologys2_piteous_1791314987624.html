Note: In classical Latin, pietās meant duty toward gods, fatherland, and parents (as embodied by Virgil's pius Aeneas). In Christian Late Latin, the semantic center shifted from ritual and familial duty to divine grace, compassion, and mercy (whence both English piety and pity split as doublets). Michiel de Vaan reconstructs Proto-Italic *pīos from PIE *pewH- (purify, whence also Latin pūrus).
