Verb muse, "to ponder":
↓
Old French (12th c.)
muser
"to muse, ponder; loiter"
↓
English (14th c.)
muse
"to ponder, reflect"

Debate: Two distinct words: the noun descends from Greek Μοῦσα via Latin and Old French, but its deeper origin is debated — possibly PIE *men- "to think" ("the thinker"), or a pre-Greek substrate word. The verb "to ponder" comes from Old French muser, of uncertain origin, perhaps from Latin mūsum "muzzle" via the sense "to sniff, ponder".
