Note: Formed from Latin in- (‘into, toward’, from PIE *en) + ducere (‘to lead’, source of duke, duct, produce, seduce) + the Latin deverbal suffix -mentum, which denotes an instrument, means, or concrete result of an action. In English legal and rhetorical traditions from the 16th century onward, it transitioned from the act of leading into the discrete factors or benefits that induce.
