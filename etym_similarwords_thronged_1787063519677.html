• Crowded: Similar because both describe places full of people; differs because "crowded" is neutral and can be static or oppressive, while "thronged" implies lively, moving multitudes and a more literary register.
  • Packed: Similar because both mean filled with people; differs because "packed" is informal and emphasises tightness and little room to move.
  • Jammed: Similar because both suggest dense crowding; differs because "jammed" implies blockage and obstruction, with no sense of animated movement.
  • Swarming: Similar because both suggest dense, moving masses; differs because "swarming" has insect-like, restless, slightly chaotic associations.
  • Teeming: Similar because both mean abundantly full; differs because "teeming" stresses sheer abundance and can apply to life and things ("teeming with wildlife"), not only crowds of people.
  • Mobbed: Similar because both mean overwhelmed by a crowd; differs because "mobbed" suggests a disorderly, aggressive, or celebrity-hounded crowd and a more negative register.
  • Congested: Similar because both mean overfull; differs because "congested" is clinical and technical (of traffic, streets, noses), lacking vitality.
  • Crammed: Similar because both mean packed in tightly; differs because "crammed" suggests force and pressure to fit, with informal tone.
  • Filled: Similar because both mean made full; differs because "filled" is neutral and general — anything can fill a space, not only people.
  • Busy: Similar because both suggest much activity; differs because "busy" is far weaker and broader, with no necessary sense of a crowd.
