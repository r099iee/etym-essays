Note: the PIE root *bʰreg- "to break, split" is uncertain — the garment may be named as a "split" (two-legged) covering, but a direct derivation is debated; Latin brācae "breeches" (a Gaulish loanword) suggests an independent Continental form. English breech is a back-formation from the plural breeches.
