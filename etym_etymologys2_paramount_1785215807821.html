Note: the word combines Latin per
"through" + ad "to" + montem "mountain"
(accusative of mons); the phrase
per ad montem "up the mountain" is
inferred from Old French par amont.
Debate: the link of Latin mons to PIE
*men- "to project, tower" is standard but
not absolutely certain.
