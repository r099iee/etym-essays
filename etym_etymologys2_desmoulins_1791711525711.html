Note: The French surname Desmoulins is a toponymic and occupational formation compounding the contracted preposition des ('of the', de + les) with moulins (plural of moulin, 'mill'), derived through Late Latin molīnum from PIE *melh₂- ('to grind'). In political philosophy and modern historiography, the name functions as a singular proper noun designating Camille Desmoulins.
