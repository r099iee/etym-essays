• Dashed: Similar because both involve sudden, high-speed movement; differs because dashed emphasizes rushing with great urgency, violent speed, and forceful momentum over a short distance, whereas darted highlights nimble lightness and sudden trajectory shifts.
  • Bolted: Similar because both describe an abrupt, rapid departure; differs because bolted implies a sudden, panic-fueled escape or breaking away from restraint, like a frightened horse.
  • Sprinted: Similar because both denote running at maximum speed; differs because sprinted implies sustained, athletic exertion at top speed over a measured track, while darted is a brief, agile, zig-zagging burst.
  • Scuttled: Similar because both describe rapid movement by small creatures; differs because scuttled evokes hurried, scurrying, short steps close to the ground, often with a furtive or awkward gait.
  • Flashed: Similar because both convey instantaneous speed; differs because flashed emphasizes brilliant visual appearance and vanishing in an instant of light rather than physical locomotion.
  • Shot: Similar because both evoke projectile motion; differs because shot conveys intense, explosive linear velocity like a bullet, whereas darted retains the agile maneuverability of a light javelin.
