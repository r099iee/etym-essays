- Old English flat + fish compounds - descriptive phrase for any broad thin fish
- Middle Ages market term - sole, plaice, flounder sold as flat fish for fast days
- 18th-19th c. natural history order - Linnaean Pleuronectes groups eye-migration marvels under flatfish
- 20th c. fisheries-commercial category - flatfish stocks, quotas, fillets as economic unit
- Modern popular-euphemistic use - probably a flatfish to minimize shark fears and evoke camouflaged bottom life
