TEAMSTERS
↓ PIE
*dewk-
"to lead, pull"
↓ Proto-Germanic
*taumaz
"a team, line, harness"
↓ Old English
tēam
"a team, offspring, line"
↓ Middle English
team
"a team of draught animals"
↓ English (18th c.)
teamster
"one who drives a team of animals"
↓ English (plural)
teamsters
"people who drive teams
of animals"

Note: The sense of team as "a group of draught animals yoked together" goes back to Old English tēam. The suffix -ster marks an agent.
