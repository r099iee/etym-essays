- [Taking by war] - "Conquest" (from Old French and Latin) means the act of taking possession by armed force, from a single capture to a lasting acquisition.
- [Cumulative achievement] - In historiography the plural "conquests" names a people's cumulative annexations — campaigns that end in permanent territorial possession, the instrument of imperial greatness.
- [Extended figurative sense] - Modern use extends "conquest" to winning people or challenges (a romantic conquest, conquering a fear), diluting the military force while retaining the note of overcoming.
