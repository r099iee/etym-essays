Note on the debate: the pre-Germanic etymology is uncertain. The comparison with PIE *dʰrenǵ-, "to draw or pull", rests on the notion of drawing liquid into the mouth and on possible Indo-Iranian cognates, but no single root is universally accepted, and some scholars treat the Germanic verb as having no secure extra-Germanic etymology. The descent from *drinkaną through Old English drincan is, by contrast, fully attested.
