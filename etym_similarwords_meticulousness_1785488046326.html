• CAREFULNESS: Similar because both name the quality of taking care; differs because carefulness is neutral diligence without extremity, while meticulousness means extreme, detail-obsessed, almost anxious exactness.
  • THOROUGHNESS: Similar because both mean covering the whole task; differs because thoroughness names completeness of coverage, while meticulousness names fineness of attention — the difference between reaching every part and inspecting every detail.
  • SCRUPULOUSNESS: Similar because both mean painstaking care; differs because scrupulousness is care governed by conscience and moral duty, while meticulousness is care driven by fear of error and love of exactness.
  • FASTIDIOUSNESS: Similar because both mean extreme attention to detail; differs because fastidiousness stresses finicky taste and disgust at the imperfect and can edge into a vice, while meticulousness is a professional virtue.
  • PUNCTILIOUSNESS: Similar because both mean exacting carefulness; differs because punctiliousness is careful adherence to formal rules, etiquette, or ceremony, while meticulousness is care about the details themselves.
  • EXACTITUDE: Similar because both mean extreme precision; differs because exactitude names the quality of being exactly right in a result, while meticulousness names the painstaking process of attending to details.
  • PRECISION: Similar because both denote exactness; differs because precision is the fineness and accuracy of measurement or expression, while meticulousness is the disposition of the careful worker.
  • CONSCIENTIOUSNESS: Similar because both mean taking one's work seriously and thoroughly; differs because conscientiousness is governed by duty and moral seriousness, while meticulousness emphasizes detailed, exacting attention.
  • PAINSTAKINGNESS: Similar because both mean taking great pains over work; differs because painstakingness emphasizes the effort and labour invested, while meticulousness emphasizes the exactness and detail of the attention.
  • PEDANTRY: Similar because both can mean excessive attention to small details; differs because pedantry is the ostentatious display of learning about trivial points and is always a fault, while meticulousness is a virtue.
