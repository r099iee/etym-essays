Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE)
*pel- + *meik- / *meig-
"to thrust, strike, drive" + "to mix, mingle"
↓
Classical Latin (c. 1st c. BCE)
pāla / pellere + miscēre
"shovel, blade / to drive" + "to mix"
↓
Vulgar Latin (c. 5th c. CE)
*mesculāre
"to mix thoroughly, blend"
↓
Old French (c. 12th c. CE)
pesle-mesle
"mixed in a confused heap, disordered melee"
↓
Middle French (c. 14th–15th c. CE)
pêle-mêle
"in a confused, jumbled heap"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1590s CE)
pell-mell
"in confused, disorderly haste"
