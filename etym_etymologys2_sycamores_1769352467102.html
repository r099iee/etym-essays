Debate: σῦκον "fig" has no clear Indo-European etymology and is likely a Pre-Greek or eastern Mediterranean loan. The name sycamore was later applied to unrelated trees (the American plane tree, the British maple) by superficial resemblance; these are not the fig-mulberry.
