buck

Modern English (present)
buck

Note: beyond Germanic *bukk- no certain PIE root is established; older links to PIE *bhu- or Celtic *bukk- remain debated, so the chain properly begins in Germanic. The dollar, verb and sawhorse senses are inner-English semantic extensions of the animal noun, not separate borrowings.
