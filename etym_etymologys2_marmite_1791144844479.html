Note: the marm- → marmite step is the standard account (onomatopoeic marm-, the pot’s bubbling ‘murmur,’ plus suffix -ite, cf. marmotter, murmurer), but French etymologists flag it as probable rather than certain; an alternative older theory linked the pot to the ‘hypocrite/painted-face’ homonym marmite. The murmur theory is followed here with that caveat.
