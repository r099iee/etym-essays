Etymological Note: Greek aḗr originally denoted the dark, misty lower atmosphere inhabited by mortals, sharply contrasted with aithḗr (the radiant, blazing upper ether of the gods). The verbal suffix -ing was affixed in late Middle English to denote both the process of ventilation and the resulting outdoor excursion.
