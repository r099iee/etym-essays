Note: Greek γῆ (gê) is probably a Pre-Greek substrate word of unknown origin, not an inherited PIE form. The second element, νομός (nomós) “dwelling, pasture,” derives from νέμειν (némein) “to deal out, pasture” (PIE *nem-). The compound *gēnomos is an unattested reconstruction. A rival etymology connects gnomus with Greek gnōmē “thought, judgement,” on the notion that gnomes know the earth’s secrets; the OED treats the earth-dweller derivation as probable but notes Paracelsus’s omission of the ē as a “blunder.”
