Etymological Note on Derivation: Eccentricity is an etymological compound whose primary stem derives from PIE *ḱent- ("to prick, point"), which in Greek yielded κέντρον (the sharp point of a compass used to trace circles, hence "center"), compounded with the PIE directional prefix *eǵʰs ("out of", Greek ἐκ-). The abstract noun was formed via the productive Romance/Latin suffixes -ic (Latin -icus, Greek -ikos) and -ity (Latin -itās, French -ité).
