Note on semantic divergence: Latin abstinēre gave rise to two parallel nouns in English: abstinence (borrowed earlier via Old French astinence, focusing on sustained physical and sensory self-denial) and abstention (borrowed as a formal Latinate action noun, which later became specialized in the 19th century for parliamentary non-voting and judicial recusal).
