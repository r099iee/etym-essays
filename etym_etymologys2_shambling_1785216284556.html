Debate: Latin scamnum is of uncertain
origin, possibly from PIE *skebʰ- "to
split, scrape". Note: the semantic shift
from "butcher's stall" to a shuffling gait
arose because a lame, awkward walk was said
to resemble picking a path through the
rows of stalls in a butcher's shambles.
