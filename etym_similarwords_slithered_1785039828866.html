• Slid: Similar because both mean movement over a surface with little friction; differs because sliding is smooth, continuous and often deliberate and controlled, while slithering is unsteady, grating and imperfectly controlled.
  • Slipped: Similar because both mean loss of secure footing; differs because slipping is sudden, short and over in an instant, while slithering is prolonged and repeated — a series of slips strung together.
  • Skidded: Similar because both mean loss of traction on a surface; differs because skidding is the sliding of a wheel, vehicle or body as a single uncontrolled movement, without slither's writhing, repeated, friction-heavy quality.
  • Glided: Similar because both mean smooth, effortless movement over a surface or through air; differs because gliding is steady, graceful and under control, the opposite of slithering's ungainly, grating instability.
  • Writhed: Similar because both describe a twisting, snaking bodily motion; differs because writhing is twisting and contorting in place (of pain, of a snake's coils), while slithering is a gliding forward motion over a surface.
  • Snaked: Similar because both describe a sinuous, winding course; differs because snaking is following a curving, winding path (a river snakes), while slithering is the friction-losing, skidding slide itself.
  • Slink / Slinky: Similar because both mean moving quietly, smoothly and unobtrusively; differs because slinking is stealthy, dignified withdrawal (of a guilty person or a cat), while slithering is an unstable, grating slide, not a graceful retreat.
  • Skittered: Similar because both mean quick, light movement over a surface; differs because skittering is light, skipping, touch-and-go movement (an insect skitters), while slithering is heavier, longer, grinding and friction-filled.
  • Stumbled: Similar because both mean losing one's footing; differs because stumbling is tripping and lurching while walking upright, while slithering is the low, sliding, prone or crouched loss of grip.
  • Scrambled: Similar because both describe awkward, effortful movement over difficult ground; differs because scrambling is a fast, vigorous climb or clamber using hands and feet, while slithering is a downward or lateral slide without firm footing.
  • Crept: Similar because both mean slow, low, cautious movement; differs because creeping is slow, careful, deliberate progress on hands and knees, while slithering is an uncontrolled slide.
  • Careened: Similar because both mean lurching, uncontrolled movement; differs because careening is a violent sideways lurch or swerve (of a vehicle), while slithering is a low, friction-losing glide.
