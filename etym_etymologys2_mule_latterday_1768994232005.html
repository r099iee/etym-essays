Debate: Latin mulus has no secure Indo-European etymology and may be a loanword; the PIE root *mus- is conjectural. Proto-Germanic *lataz "late" has no certain PIE cognates — the link to *leh₁d- is uncertain. For day, the connection of Proto-Germanic *dagaz to PIE *dʰegʷʰ- "to burn" (the sun) is widely suggested but not entirely secure.
