Note on nuance: Mithril is not a historical word; it is a philologically constructed term invented by J.R.R. Tolkien, a professional linguist, from his own Sindarin (Elvish) roots. Tolkien glossed it as "grey-gleam". The same roots recur elsewhere in his nomenclature: mith in Mithrandir "Grey Pilgrim" (Gandalf) and Mithlond "Grey Havens"; ril in names of glinting things such as Idril and Nimphelos. There is therefore no PIE or historical chain to reconstruct — the derivation is entirely internal to Tolkien's invented language, and the glosses above follow his own published explanations.
