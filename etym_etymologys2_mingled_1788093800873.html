Note: The connection of Germanic *mang- with PIE *mag- "to knead" (cf. Greek mássein "to knead") is widely repeated but not accepted by all authorities; the OED marks the further origin of *mang- as uncertain, so this stage should be read as probable rather than proven. The frequentative suffix -le gives the verb its modern shape by c. 1400.
