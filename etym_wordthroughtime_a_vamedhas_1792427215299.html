- [Vedic Saṃhitā & Brāhmaṇa Era (c. 1500–600 BCE)] — formulated and codified in the *Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa* and *Yajurveda* as the premier royal Śrauta liturgy of cosmic regeneration and imperial suzerainty — [perception: supreme sacrificial rite of sovereign kingship, military dominance, and cosmic alignment]
- [Epic & Purāṇic Period (c. 500 BCE – 400 CE; Rāmāyaṇa & Mahābhārata)] — immortalized as the crowning achievement of legendary emperors (Rāma, Yudhiṣṭhira, Daśaratha, Bharata), serving to atone for war sins and prove global sovereignty — [perception: the absolute gold standard of righteous imperial glory and lavish religious generosity]
- [Upaniṣadic Mystical Age (c. 700–300 BCE)] — sublimated in the opening verses of the *Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad* into an esoteric cosmic meditation, identifying the sacrificial horse with the universe — [perception: allegorical bridge from external blood sacrifice to non-dual spiritual knowledge of Brahman]
- [Classical Historical & Numismatic Era (c. 2nd c. BCE – 6th c. CE)] — enacted historically by paramount kings (Puṣyamitra Śuṅga, Śātakarṇi I, Samudragupta, Kumāragupta) and struck on famed gold coins — [perception: historical political confirmation of undisputed sovereign overlordship across northern and southern India]
- [Modern Political & Metaphorical Age (19th–21st c.)] — transformed in contemporary Indian political discourse into an evocative idiom (*ashwamedha yatra*) for an unstoppable, sweeping electoral campaign or nationwide tour — [perception: potent metaphor for political invincibility, ideological conquest, and nationwide dominance]
