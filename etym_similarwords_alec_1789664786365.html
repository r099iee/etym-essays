• Alexander: Similar because it is the full formal source name sharing the same etymological meaning defender of men; differs because it is longer, more formal and imperial, used officially where Alec is familiar.
  • Alex: Similar because it is the other dominant short form of Alexander used interchangeably; differs because Alex is more international and modern, while Alec retains a specifically British and mid-century flavour.
  • Xander: Similar because it is another hypocoristic of Alexander with identical reference; differs because it is trendy, youthful and American-influenced, where Alec is traditional and understated.
  • Sandy: Similar because it is the older Scottish pet form of Alexander from the same community; differs because Sandy foregrounds Scottishness and is now often perceived as dated or nickname-only.
  • Alistair / Alastair: Similar because it is a Gaelic cognate of Alexander sharing origin and cultural milieu; differs because it is a distinct full name, not a shortening, with Highland associations.
  • Alexei / Alessandro / Alejandro: Similar because they are cross-language cognates of Alexander with the same defender-of-men meaning; differs because they mark Russian, Italian and Spanish identity respectively, while Alec marks Anglophone, especially British, identity.
