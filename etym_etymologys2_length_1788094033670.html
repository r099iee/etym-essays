Note: The reconstruction *del- “long” with variant *dlongʰ- is the standard account (Pokorny; the family is attested in Sanskrit dīrghá-, Greek δολιχός “long”, Latin longus < *dlongʰ-o-, and Germanic *langaz with loss of the initial *d-); the details of grade and laryngeal treatment are debated among specialists, but the lineage itself is secure. The idiom “at length” is Middle English atte lengthe (14th c.).
