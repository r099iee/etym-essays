Note: Inure was formed by compounding Anglo-Norman preposition en ("in") with ure ("work, practice"), a phonetic contraction of Old French uevre (from Latin opera, "work"). The verb originally meant "to put into operation" in law, and later "to accustom through continuous work or exposure" in general usage.
