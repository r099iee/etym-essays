"heavy"

Proto-Italic *gʷrawis
"heavy"

↓

Latin gravis

↓

Latin aggravāre
"to weigh down, burden, make heavy"

↓

Old French agrever
"to burden, oppress, cause grief" (10th c.)

↓

Middle English agreven / aggrieven
"to distress, burden, cause injury" (14th c.)

↓

Modern English AGGRIEVED
"feeling wronged, resentful, injured by injustice"
(also: "having legal standing as a party aggrieved")
