• Demurely: Similar because both describe a restrained, proper, modest manner of behaving; differs because “demurely” stresses shy modesty and self-effacement, especially of a woman, while “primly” stresses self-conscious, neat propriety with a faint hint of self-satisfaction.
  • Sedately: Similar because both describe composed, unhurried, decorous behaviour; differs because “sedately” stresses calm dignity and self-possession without fuss, while “primly” adds an exaggerated, meticulous neatness of dress and deportment.
  • Stiffly: Similar because both describe rigid, unrelaxed, formal movement or manner; differs because “stiffly” stresses physical rigidity and lack of ease, while “primly” foregrounds neatness and demure correctness rather than mere stiffness.
  • Fussily: Similar because both describe an anxious, excessive attention to detail; differs because “fussily” suggests worried, bustling over-arrangement, while “primly” is neatness performed with cool, self-satisfied exactness.
  • Precisely: Similar because both describe exactness of performance; differs because “precisely” is a neutral adverb of accuracy (also “exactly”), while “primly” is specific to socially performed neatness and propriety.
  • Properly: Similar because both describe behaviour that conforms to convention; differs because “properly” is a general adverb meaning “in the right or correct way,” while “primly” is a manner-word loaded with an exaggerated, faintly comic formality.
  • Correctly: Similar because both mean doing something in the right manner; differs because “correctly” stresses factual or procedural accuracy, while “primly” stresses the outward performance of good manners.
  • Affectedly: Similar because both describe behaviour that is not wholly natural; differs because “affectedly” stresses insincerity and pretence, while “primly” stresses an over-neat performance that may still be sincere.
  • Prudishly: Similar because both describe an exaggerated propriety; differs because “prudishly” stresses horror at anything improper or sexual, while “primly” stresses general neatness and formal correctness.
  • Finickily: Similar because both describe petty fastidiousness; differs because “finickily” stresses fussiness over small details of taste, while “primly” stresses formal neatness of manner and deportment.
  • Daintily: Similar because both describe a delicate, careful, refined way of doing something; differs because “daintily” stresses delicacy, smallness, and grace, while “primly” stresses stiff correctness and propriety.
  • Reservedly: Similar because both describe restraint in manner; differs because “reservedly” stresses emotional restraint and keeping one’s feelings in, while “primly” stresses outward neatness and propriety of behaviour.
