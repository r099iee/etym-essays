Latin(archaic; legendary foundation)
Laurentum
"ancient coastal city of Latium"
(traditionally derived from laurus "laurel"; ancient origin debated, possibly Etruscan)

Latin(Republican era)
Laurentius
"a man from Laurentum"
(cognomen; St Lawrence, d. 258 CE, spreads it as a saint’s name)

Latin / Christian onomastics(4th–10th c.)
Laurentius
"martyr-deacon of Rome; given name"

Italian(medieval)
Lorenzo
"Italian form of Laurentius"
↓
Florentine Usage(15th c.)
Lorenzo
"Lorenzo de’ Medici (1449–1492) il Magnifico"
(debated: the link of Laurentum to laurus is a scholarly guess, reinforced by the legend that Aeneas’s sow led him to a laurel grove; an Etruscan source for the city name is possible and cannot be excluded)
