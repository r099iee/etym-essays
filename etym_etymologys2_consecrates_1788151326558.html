Note on the debate: The PIE ancestry of Latin sacer is not securely established. A reconstruction *sak- ‘to sanctify’ has been proposed in older literature, but modern authorities (de Vaan) treat the deeper etymology as unclear; possible cognates in Italic and Celtic are debated, so no confident PIE form is asserted here.
