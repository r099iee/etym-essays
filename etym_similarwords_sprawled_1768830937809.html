• Lolled: Similar because both describe relaxing the body into an undignified posture; differs because lolled implies a languid, self-indulgent recline against a support with lazy pleasure, while sprawled is accidental and careless, limbs flung out without design.
  • Lounged: Similar in sitting or lying at ease; differs because lounged is a deliberate, comfortable, socially acceptable ease, whereas sprawled is ungainly and indifferent to elegance.
  • Stretched out: Similar in an extended, full-length position; differs because stretched out is generally intentional and orderly, while sprawled emphasizes disorder and awkward angles.
  • Splayed: Similar in spreading limbs or digits wide apart; differs because splayed specifically highlights outward turning or fanning of parts, whereas sprawled describes whole-body posture and spatial dispersion.
  • Prostrated: Similar in lying flat on the ground; differs because prostrated implies submission, exhaustion, or worship in an aligned position, while sprawled is chaotic and misaligned.
  • Recumbent: Similar in lying down; differs because recumbent is a formal, restful posture without ungainly disorder.
  • Floundered: Similar in violent, awkward limb movement during a fall; differs because floundered emphasizes struggling movement in mud or water, whereas sprawled emphasizes the static spread posture resulting from a fall.
  • Slouched: Similar in a careless, collapsed posture; differs because slouched is a seated, hunched droop of the shoulders, while sprawled is a full outward spreading of the whole body.
  • Draped: Similar in the body being spread across a surface; differs because draped implies a decorative or artful arrangement, whereas sprawled is careless and unarranged.
  • Spilled: Similar in matter flowing outward over a surface; differs because spilled is used of liquids or loose contents, while sprawled applies to a body or mass that keeps its shape while spreading.
  • Slumped: Similar in a heavy, limp loss of posture; differs because slumped implies a sudden collapse downward into a heap, while sprawled implies limbs thrown outward across the area.
  • Collapsed: Similar in giving way and losing structure; differs because collapsed stresses the failure of support, whereas sprawled stresses the resulting spread of parts.
  • Tumbled: Similar in falling with limbs thrown about; differs because tumbled stresses rolling or pitching motion in the fall itself, while sprawled stresses the final splayed position.
  • Rambled: Similar in extending without order; differs because rambled is used of buildings, plants, or speech spreading in an irregular path, while sprawled implies a wide, flat outward scatter.
  • Spread: Similar in extension over area; differs because spread is neutral and can be deliberate, whereas sprawled adds carelessness and ungainliness.
  • Sprawling (adjective): the adjectival twin; differs in that sprawling describes the quality of ongoing extension ("a sprawling city"), while sprawled records a completed state.
  • Lazed / Idled: Similar in relaxed inactivity; differs because these name the lack of effort rather than the bodily position, while sprawled is specifically a posture of spread limbs.
