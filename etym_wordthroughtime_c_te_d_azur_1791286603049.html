- Obscure poetic coinage — 1887 Liégeard title La Côte d'Azur; perceived as literary branding, imitating Côte-d'Or, to sell the south to northerners.
- Aristocratic wintering ground — 1890s–1910s English lords and Russian grand dukes winter at Nice and Cannes; connotation of invalids seeking sun, grand hotels, promenades.
- Jazz-age playground — 1920s Fitzgerald, Picasso and Wooster set take the Riviera for summer; perception shifts to youthful, artistic, slightly scandalous glamour and Anglo-French slang (smoking for dinner jacket).
- Mass tourism and cinema — post-1945 Cannes festival, yachting, package holidays; dual image of star-studded exclusivity and crowded beaches.
- Modern heritage brand — contemporary administrative region and luxury trademark; seen as both authentic Provençal littoral and global shorthand for sun, blue sea and chic.
