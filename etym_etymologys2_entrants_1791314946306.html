Note: The base verb derives from Latin intrāre, an ancient denominative from the adverb/preposition intrā, formed from the PIE comparative *h₁én-teros (inside, within), which also yielded English interior and Sanskrit ántara-. Suffix -ant reflects the Latin present participial ending -antem, preserved through French nominalisation.
