• Formalists: Similar because both analyse literature by internal devices rather than biography; differs because formalists are the earlier Russian school of defamiliarisation and plot devices, while Structuralists are the later French school of system-wide oppositions.
  • Semioticians: Similar because both study signs and codes across culture; differs because semioticians follow Peirce and Morris into all sign-action including icons and indices, while Structuralists follow Saussure into closed systems of differences.
  • Post-structuralists: Similar because they share the same Paris names and texts; differs because post-structuralists undo stable structure through différance and power, while Structuralists seek the stable architecture beneath variation.
  • Functionalists: Similar because both dominated mid-century anthropology with grand comparison; differs because functionalists ask what institutions do for social cohesion, while Structuralists ask what hidden logic generates their forms.
  • Marxists: Similar because Althusserian Structuralists fused the two; differs because Marxists explain culture by material base and class struggle, while Structuralists explain it by relational codes that cut across economics.
  • Psychoanalysts: Similar because Lacan famous dictum joins them, the unconscious is structured like a language; differs because psychoanalysts treat patients through transference, while Structuralists chart myths and texts without a couch.
  • Mythographers: Similar because both catalogue and compare myths worldwide; differs because mythographers collect and retell stories, while Structuralists reduce them to mythemes and transformation rules.
  • Narratologists: Similar because both dissect story grammar and functions; differs because narratologists are the specialised heirs studying plot mechanics, while Structuralists are the broader movement claiming all culture works that way.
