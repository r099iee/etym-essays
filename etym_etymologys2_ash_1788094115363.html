Note: Cognates such as Latin ornus ('mountain ash') and Greek oxya support a very ancient shared name for the tree; the immediate Germanic and Old English line is firmly attested. The modern spelling 'ash' lost the original initial vowel-colouring that distinguished Old English æsc from the unrelated 'ash' (cinders).
