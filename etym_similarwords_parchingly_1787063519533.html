• Scorching: Similar because both involve intense heat that dries out surfaces; differs because scorching emphasizes burning, surface charring, and extreme heat rather than the specific extraction of moisture and thirst.
  • Aridly: Similar because both describe extreme lack of moisture; differs because aridly is formal, geographic, and scientific (e.g., arid climates), lacking the intense somatic suffering implied by parchingly.
  • Searingly: Similar because both convey intense, blistering thermal power; differs because searingly highlights sudden, intense, agonizing heat that cauterizes or burns instantly (like searing meat).
  • Desiccatingly: Similar because both denote total removal of moisture; differs because desiccatingly is a technical, scientific, or biochemical term describing complete moisture extraction from matter.
  • Blisteringly: Similar because both describe oppressive heat; differs because blisteringly emphasizes the formation of skin blisters or blistering speed/intensity.
  • Torridly: Similar because both denote tropical, suffocating heat; differs because torridly evokes tropical climatic zones and passionate emotional intensity (e.g., a torrid affair).
  • Thirstily: Similar because both relate to the craving for liquid; differs because thirstily describes the eager, gulping manner of drinking or desiring drink, whereas parchingly describes the agonizing state of drying heat itself.
  • Witheringly: Similar because both describe heat causing loss of vitality; differs because witheringly emphasizes wilting, decay, or crushing, humiliating verbal scorn (e.g., a withering glance).
  • Bakingly: Similar because both evoke enclosed dry heat; differs because bakingly is colloquial and milder, comparing the atmosphere to an oven.
  • Stiflingly: Similar because both describe oppressive atmospheric conditions; differs because stiflingly highlights suffocating air and inability to breathe rather than severe dehydration.
