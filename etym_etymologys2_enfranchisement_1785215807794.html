Debate note: The Germanic ethnic name *frankō has no secure deeper etymology; a proposed link to PIE *per- "forward" (hence "bold, free") is speculative. The sense "free" arose because the Franks, as the ruling class, were the free men of the realm. "Enfranchisement" later shifted from "admission to the freedom of a city or corporation" (17th c.) to "right to vote" (19th c.).
