Note: rattle is unanimously judged echoic or imitative by the dictionaries (OED, Etymonline): its consonants perform the knocks they name, so there is no PIE root to reconstruct and the chain honestly begins at the imitated sound; cognates such as German rasseln are parallel imitations rather than inherited forms.
