- Iron Age narrative — eloquent brother: Exodus presents Aaron as he who can speak well, glad in heart; warm fraternal perception.
- Priestly redaction — founder of cult: Leviticus and Numbers elevate Aaron to anointed high priest despite Golden Calf lapse; reverent-institutional perception.
- Second Temple to Rabbinic — lover of peace: Hillel tradition makes Aharon pursuer of peace who loves people; beloved moral exemplar.
- Christian and Islamic reception — type and prophet: Hebrews contrasts Aaronic with Melchizedek priesthood, Qur'an honours Harun as helper; respectful but subordinated to Moses/Musa.
- Modern — given name and garden: Aaron as common first name and Aaron's beard/rod for plants; familiar-domestic perception far from altar.
