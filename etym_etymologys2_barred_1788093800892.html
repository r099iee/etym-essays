Note: The origin of Vulgar Latin *barra is genuinely uncertain — the word is absent from classical Latin, which used vectis, sera and obex for bars and bolts; the Celtic (Gaulish *barros) hypothesis is the most favoured, but the standard dictionaries record the origin as unknown.
