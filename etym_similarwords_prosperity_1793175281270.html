• Wealth: Similar because both name abundant material goods; differs because wealth stresses possession, the stored pile, while prosperity stresses the thriving condition, fortune in motion.
  • Affluence: Similar because both describe flowing riches and comfortable plenty; differs because affluence is static abundance, literally flowing-toward, while prosperity adds success and good fortune beyond money.
  • Success: Similar because both name the favourable outcome of endeavour; differs because success is general and event-shaped, while prosperity is specifically flourishing fortune, sustained and blessed.
  • Flourishing: Similar because both picture vigorous healthy growth; differs because flourishing is the botanical metaphor itself, while prosperity is the established noun of economic and personal thriving.
  • Well-being: Similar because both cover health, comfort, and happiness together; differs because well-being is modern policy language, measurable and managerial, while prosperity is older, warmer, and toast-shaped.
  • Fortune: Similar because both unite luck and riches in one word; differs because fortune keeps the gambler's sense of chance and the heiress's pile, while prosperity suggests earned, steady, deserved thriving.
  • Boom: Similar because both name periods of rapid economic growth; differs because boom is sudden, noisy, and liable to bust, while prosperity is broad, settled, and respectable.
  • Opulence: Similar because both evoke rich, splendid plenty; differs because opulence is display — silk, marble, excess — while prosperity is condition, the quiet fact of doing well.
  • Good luck: Similar because the tale's greeting means exactly this; differs because good luck is casual and everyday, while prosperity is the formal, capital-letter blessing.
