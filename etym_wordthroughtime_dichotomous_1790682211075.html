- Geometric description, neutral - in late eighteenth-century botany dichotomous simply means forking in pairs; connotation technical, admiring of nature’s regularity.
- Logical ideal, prestigious - through the nineteenth century it hardens into exhaustive binary division; perception rigorous, scientific, the mark of clear thought.
- Structuralist method, fashionable - in mid-twentieth-century theory binary opposition becomes the engine of analysis; connotation chic and powerful, from phonology to mythology.
- False-binary accusation, wary - from the late twentieth century calling something dichotomous often criticises it as reductively either-or; perception sceptical, deconstructionist.
- Everyday shorthand for stark division, mildly pejorative - today dichotomous thinking names black-and-white cognition; connotation cautionary, urging continua and spectra instead of cuts.
