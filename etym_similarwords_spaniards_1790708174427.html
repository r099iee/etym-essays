• Hispanics: Similar because both relate to the culture and lineage originating from the Iberian Peninsula; differs because Hispanics is a broad ethno-linguistic category referring to all Spanish-speaking peoples globally (especially including Latin Americans), whereas Spaniards refers specifically to people from the nation of Spain.
  • Iberians: Similar because both refer to inhabitants of the same geographic territory; differs because Iberians is a broader geographic and historical term encompassing both Spaniards and Portuguese (as well as ancient pre-Roman tribes of the peninsula), rather than a modern national demonym.
  • Castilians: Similar because Castilian language and culture form the central core of Spanish national identity; differs because Castilians refers specifically to inhabitants of the historical region of Castile within central Spain, distinguishing them from other Spanish nationalities like Catalans or Basques.
  • Peninsulares: Similar because both denote individuals from Spain; differs because peninsulares is a specialized historical colonial term applied specifically to Iberian-born Spaniards residing in the Spanish American colonies, held in contrast to American-born criollos.
  • Latinos: Similar because both share cultural connections to Romance-speaking civilizations; differs because Latinos refers specifically to people of Latin American origin or descent residing in the Americas, regardless of whether they have direct Spanish ancestry.
