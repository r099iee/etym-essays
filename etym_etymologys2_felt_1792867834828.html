Cloth sense — the source meaning:
PIE (reconstructed, c. 4000 BCE)
*pel-
‘to beat, strike, drive’
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*filtiz
‘felt, beaten wool’
↓
Old English (9th c.)
felt
‘felted wool cloth’
↓
Modern English (current)
felt
‘matted woollen cloth’

Note: the link between Germanic *filtiz and PIE *pel- is the standard handbook connection through the beating of the fulling process, though some scholars leave the Germanic noun without a certain deeper root.

*pal-

*foljan
