Note: English "Gaul" reaches us through the Germanic branch, not directly from Latin Gallia. The Latin names Gallia and Gallus, whence English "Gallic", form a parallel tradition borrowed from Greek Galates; the ultimate origin of the tribal name Volcae is unknown, and the older derivation from a Celtic root meaning "power" is not securely established.
