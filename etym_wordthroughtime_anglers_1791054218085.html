- Old English hook-tool - neutral implement angel for subsistence fishing
- Middle English agent - neutral occupational label anglere distinct from netters
- 1496 Treatyse - elevation to craft with manuals, tackle lore and gentle status
- 1653 Walton - moralised idealisation as contemplative, virtuous sportsman
- 19th c. sport codification - prestige competitive figure with clubs, matches and records
- 20th c. tourism - commercialised leisure identity, charter and holiday angler
- Present conservation era - recast as ecological stakeholder, catch-and-release ethic, still haunted by exaggeration jokes
