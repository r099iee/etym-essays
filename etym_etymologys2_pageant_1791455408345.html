Note: the link between classical Latin pagina “page” and Medieval Latin pagina “stage, scene” is uncertain. One theory derives the theatrical sense from the page or manuscript of a play; another connects it with pangere “to fasten” and a movable scaffold or framework. The OED cautions that the evidence is insufficient to decide. The PIE *peh₂ǵ- stage belongs to the pangere theory and is therefore probable rather than certain.
