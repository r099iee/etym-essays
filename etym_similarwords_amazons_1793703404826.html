• Warrior: Similar because it denotes a person engaged in battle or conflict; differs because "warrior" is gender-neutral and doesn't carry the specific mythological or gender-specific connotations of strength and independence inherent in "amazon."
  • Valkyrie: Similar because it refers to powerful, mythical female figures associated with battle and heroism (from Norse mythology); differs because Valkyries are divine beings who choose who lives and dies in battle, rather than mortal warriors, and their cultural origin is distinct.
  • Heroine: Similar because it denotes a woman admired for her courage, outstanding achievements, or noble qualities; differs because "heroine" is broader and doesn't necessarily imply physical strength or martial prowess, focusing more on moral or narrative significance.
  • Giantess: Similar because it implies a woman of extraordinary size; differs because "giantess" solely focuses on physical stature, whereas "amazon" implies strength, martial skill, and independence, not just height.
  • Virago: Similar because it refers to a strong, often aggressive or domineering woman; differs because "virago" often carries negative connotations of being shrewish or unfeminine, while "amazon" is generally more positive or admiring of strength.
  • Champion: Similar because it denotes a person who has defeated all rivals in a competition or who fights for a cause; differs because "champion" is gender-neutral and focuses on achievement or advocacy, not necessarily the inherent physical or independent qualities of an "amazon."
