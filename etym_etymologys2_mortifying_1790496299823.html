Note: mortificare joins mors, mort- (death, from PIE *mer-) to -ficare (to make, from facere, from PIE *dheh1- to do, put). Both halves are secure; the Late Latin coinage is patristic (mortificatio of the flesh after St Paul), and the French-English transmission is continuously attested.
