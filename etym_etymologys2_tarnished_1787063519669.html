Note on the debate: the pre-Germanic stage is disputed.
Some connect the root to PIE *dʰer- "to hold, support";
others to a root meaning "dark, gloomy"; and a minority
derive French ternir from an unattested Latin *ternere.
The Frankish-to-French route is the accepted standard account.
