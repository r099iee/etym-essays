• Passy: Similar because both were historic rural villages annexed in 1860 into the 16th arrondissement of Paris, forming the premier bourgeois residential district bordering the Bois de Boulogne; differs because Passy sits closer to the Seine and the Trocadéro with deep associations with Benjamin Franklin, Honoré de Balzac, and Georges Clemenceau, whereas Auteuil developed a distinct legacy as an intimate literary salon retreat for Molière, Madame Helvétius, and the birthplace of Marcel Proust.
  • Montmartre: Similar because both were independent hillside villages outside the historic walls of Paris absorbed during Baron Haussmann's 1860 municipal expansion; differs because Montmartre transformed into a dense, bohemian, working-class cradle of cabarets and avant-garde painting (Picasso, Toulouse-Lautrec), whereas Auteuil retained an aristocratic, sedate, and wealthy residential garden atmosphere.
  • Neuilly-sur-Seine: Similar because both are affluent, prestigious residential havens located on the western periphery of Paris favored by the French economic and diplomatic elite; differs because Neuilly-sur-Seine remains an autonomous commune in the Hauts-de-Seine department outside Paris proper, whereas Auteuil was fully integrated into the administrative fabric of Paris as the 64th administrative quarter.
  • Saint-Germain-des-Prés: Similar because both quarters possess iconic reputations as historic centers of French intellectual and literary culture; differs because Saint-Germain-des-Prés is an energetic central Left Bank quarter centered on bustling sidewalk cafés, publishing houses, and existentialist debate, whereas Auteuil represents suburban retreat, leafy seclusion, and discrete domestic elegance.
  • Belleville: Similar because both were historic peripheral villages annexed into Paris in 1860; differs because Belleville became a vibrant working-class, immigrant, and politically radical district in eastern Paris, whereas Auteuil established itself as the ultra-conservative, affluent bastion of the upper-middle class in western Paris.
