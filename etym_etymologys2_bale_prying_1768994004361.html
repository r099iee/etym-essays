BALE
↓
PIE (uncertain)
*bʰel-
"to strike, harm?"
↓
Proto-Germanic (prehistoric)
*balwą
"evil, harm, woe"
↓
Old English (before 900)
bealu
"evil, harm, mischief"
↓
English (archaic)
bale
"evil, woe, mischief"
Note: the PIE root *bʰel- link
is uncertain. Also, bale 'a large
bundle of goods' (the sense in the
card) is a separate word from Old
French bale, unrelated to this chain.
