• Stored: Similar because both mean put away for later use; differs because stored is general safekeeping, while stowed implies deliberate placement into a designated, often tight space.
  • Packed: Similar because both mean put away compactly; differs because packed stresses compression into containers or bundles, while stowed stresses placement into the right place.
  • Placed: Similar because both mean put in a position; differs because placed is neutral positioning anywhere, while stowed implies tidiness and a designated space.
  • Lodged: Similar because both can describe people given a place to stay; differs because lodged is formal and about accommodation, while stowed (of people) is informal and comic.
  • Housed: Similar because both can mean given shelter or room; differs because housed stresses shelter or accommodation, while stowed stresses tidy arrangement into available space.
  • Stashed: Similar because both mean put away; differs because stashed is informal and implies secrecy or hiding, while stowed is neat and open.
  • Tucked away: Similar because both mean put into a snug or out-of-the-way place; differs because tucked away is cozier and softer, while stowed is practical and sailor-like.
  • Cached: Similar because both mean stored for later use; differs because cached is technical (computing, treasure) and implies a hidden reserve, while stowed is everyday and visible placement.
  • Secured: Similar because both can mean made safe or fast; differs because secured stresses protection from movement or theft, while stowed stresses arrangement.
  • Arranged: Similar because both mean put in order; differs because arranged is general ordering of anything, while stowed means fitting into a designated space.
  • Deposited: Similar because both mean put down in a place; differs because deposited is formal-neutral (banking, geology), while stowed is hands-on and practical.
