English (c. 1550)

Note: Latin auscultare is a compound of aus- "ear" (PIE *h₂ews-) and a hear-root related to Latin cluere and Greek klúein "to hear," but its exact inner analysis is debated. The unrelated English verb scout "to sneer, reject with scorn" comes from Old Norse skúta "a taunt" and is a homograph, not a relative.
