Note: the Latin noun is of uncertain origin. The link to a PIE root *kup- "hollow" is a scholarly proposal supported by Sanskrit kūpa- "pit, well" and Greek kypellon "cup", but it is not certain. The Latin variant cuppa is the immediate source of the Germanic borrowing; Modern English cup continues the Old English form.
