• Nose: Similar because it denotes the smelling organ; differs because it is neutral standard, while smeller is slangy and comic.
  • Snout: Similar because it is slang for nose; differs because snout stresses animal-like protrusion, smeller stresses function.
  • Conk: Similar because it is comic slang for nose; differs because conk stresses size and shape, smeller stresses smelling.
  • Beak: Similar because it jokes about a prominent nose; differs because beak stresses hooked shape, smeller does not.
  • Snoot: Similar because it means nose or snob; differs because it adds snobbish attitude, smeller is purely physical.
  • Cropper: Similar because to come a cropper means to fall heavily; differs because cropper is the standard idiom, smeller is the rarer comic variant.
  • Purler: Similar because to come a purler means a headlong fall; differs because purler stresses head-over-heels motion, smeller stresses impact.
  • Tumble: Similar because it means a fall; differs because tumble is neutral, smeller is slangy and implies a thump.
  • Spill: Similar because it means an accidental fall; differs because spill is common for bikes and horses, smeller is dated comic.
  • Nostrils: Similar because they are the smelling openings; differs because it is anatomical, while smeller is the whole nose or person.
  • Sniffer: Similar because it means one who sniffs or nose; differs because sniffer stresses active sniffing, often of dogs or drugs, smeller is broader.
