↓
Medieval Latin (4th–10th c.)
scarpsus

↓
Old Northern French (12th c.)
escars
"scarce, rare"
↓
English (13th c.)
scarce
"rare, few"

Note: the shift "picked out → few" — what has been picked over is left in short supply.
