Debate: the link to PIE *stebʰ- is uncertain; the noun may instead come from Middle Dutch stomp. The "baffle, perplex" verb sense (19th c.) arose from being brought to a standstill, as if stopped by a stump; the "walk stiffly" sense in the source text comes separately from walking as if on stumps.
