Note: Mainstream guides derive Canua from Latin canna reed for the reedy shore. A debated alternative derives Cannes from Ligurian / Vasconic kan- height, peak, referring to Le Suquet hill (cf. Le Cannet, Canossa), and Greek kanna itself is a Semitic loan (Akkadian qanu reed). The reed etymology remains the conventional explanation.
