Debate: the reconstructed Vulgar Latin *repropiare (from re- + prope "near") is not directly attested; an alternative derives reproach from Latin reprobare "to disapprove, reject" (re- + probare "to test, approve"). The prope-based chain is commonly cited but uncertain.
