- Medieval container word - basket names a humble farm hamper; practical, low-status, no artistic aura.
- Early modern craft abstraction - Adding -ry in 1640s lifts it to an art like pottery, joinery; traders speak of basketry ware, still utilitarian.
- Victorian anthropology elevation - Tylor, Mason and museums recast basketry as primeval human ingenuity, mother of pottery; perception shifts to evolutionary milestone and ethnographic treasure.
- Arts and Crafts revival - Morris followers and studio makers celebrate hand basketry against industrial mass goods; connotation becomes authentic, rustic, anti-machine.
- Contemporary art and eco-design - Fine art basketry in galleries and sustainable storage revival; now perceived as heritage skill, ecological choice, and sculptural medium.
