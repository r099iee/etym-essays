[Root Stream 1: DEAL as Timber / Plank (Middle Low German dele)]
PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*tel- / *telh₂-
"flat surface, ground, board, plank"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 1000–500 BCE)
*þiljǭ / *þilją
"floorboard, plank, wooden deck"
↓
Middle Low German / Middle Dutch (c. 12th–14th c.)
dele / deel
"plank, floorboard, fir or pine timber"
Note: Borrowed into Middle English through the Hanseatic League timber trade, denoting imported Baltic pine or fir boards.
↓
Middle English (c. 14th–15th c.)
dele / deele
"plank of pine or fir wood"

[Root Stream 2: DEAL as Share / Transaction (Old English dǣl / dǣlan)]
PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*dʰail- / *deh₂-y-
"to divide, apportion, share out"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 1000–500 BCE)
*dailiz / *dailijaną
"part, portion" / "to divide, distribute"
↓
Old English (c. 8th–11th c.)
dǣl / dǣlan
"part, portion, share" / "to divide, distribute"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–14th c.)
del, deel / delen
"portion, quantity; to distribute, give out"
