• Gloom: Similar in being darkness that oppresses; differs because gloom is darkness with a melancholy mood attached, whereas murk is dense and palpable — a substance, not merely a feeling.
  • Darkness: Similar in the absence of light; differs because darkness is neutral and total, while murk is darkness that has become a dense, obscuring substance.
  • Haze: Similar in obscuring the view; differs because haze is a watery, light-scattering veil, while murk is a darkness thick enough to reach into.
  • Mist: Similar in being an atmospheric obscurer; differs because mist is a light, watery, grayish veil that diffuses rather than darkens, while murk is dense and dark.
  • Fog: Similar in thick obscuring atmosphere; differs because fog is a specific meteorological phenomenon of condensed water vapor, while murk is any heavy, dark obscurity of the air.
  • Smog: Similar in thick, dirty, obscuring air; differs because smog is specifically polluted fog (smoke + fog), a modern environmental term, while murk is the old, general word for darkness thickened by anything.
  • Dusk: Similar in dimness of light; differs because dusk is transitional — dim but not opaque, the fall of evening — whereas murk is total, opaque darkness.
  • Twilight: Similar in dimness; differs because twilight is the brief, poetic half-light of dawn or evening, while murk is a heavy darkness without the promise of dawn.
  • Dimness: Similar in reduced light; differs because dimness is a mere reduction of light that still allows sight, while murk swallows things entirely.
  • Shadow: Similar in darkness; differs because shadow is the darkness cast by an object cutting off light, often discrete and specific, while murk is a general, atmospheric darkness.
  • Obscurity: Similar in the state of being hidden from view or understanding; differs because obscurity is abstract and can mean unknownness (the obscurity of a writer), while murk is physical and sensory.
  • Night: Similar in total darkness; differs because night is the neutral name for the dark hours of the day, while murk is a qualitative darkness with weight and menace.
  • Blackness: Similar in extreme darkness; differs because blackness is the simple absence of color or light, while murk adds thickness, opacity, and atmosphere.
  • Murky (adjective): Similar because it is the adjective form of the same word; differs because murky describes a thing (murky water, murky weather) as being dark, gloomy, or obscure, while murk names the darkness itself.
