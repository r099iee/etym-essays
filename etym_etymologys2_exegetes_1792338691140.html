Note on morphological evolution: English borrowed the word directly from Greek exēgētḗs via Neo-Latin exegeta/exegetes. In English, the singular form was anglicized as exegete (or occasionally exegetist), while the plural regularly forms as exegetes, maintaining the exact visual spelling of the ancient Greek masculine nominative singular ἐξηγητής as transliterated into Latin characters.
