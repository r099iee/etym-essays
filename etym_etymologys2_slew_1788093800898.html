NOTE: The root reconstruction is standard (Pokorny *slak-, with Latin lacerāre “to tear” as cognate) but, like all PIE roots, probabilistic. The -ew spelling of the modern past was aligned analogically with crew, drew, flew once the Middle English vowel had raised; the strong past has outlived the rare medieval weak form “slayed”.
