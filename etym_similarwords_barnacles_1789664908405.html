• Limpets: Similar because they are small tenacious intertidal shellfish clinging to rocks; differs because limpets are molluscs that can move slightly, while barnacles are sessile crustaceans cemented headfirst.
  • Mussels: Similar because they form dense encrusting beds on hulls and piers; differs because mussels are bivalve molluscs attached by byssus threads, not calcareous wall plates.
  • Encrustations: Similar because it names any hard accretion on a surface; differs because it is generic and inanimate, while barnacles are living animals with a lifecycle.
  • Parasites: Similar because barnacles in metaphor function as freeloading hangers-on; differs because true barnacles are mostly commensal foulers, not feeders on host tissue.
  • Clingers-on: Similar because it captures human hangers-on like myth-barnacles; differs because it is colloquial for people, lacking zoological precision.
  • Goose barnacles: Similar because they are a stalked subgroup of barnacles on driftwood; differs because their fleshy peduncle and feathery capitulum look unlike acorn barnacles and gave rise to the goose myth.
  • Acorn barnacles: Similar because they are the prototypical hull-fouling barnacles; differs because the term narrows to sessile Balanomorpha, while barnacles covers all Cirripedia.
