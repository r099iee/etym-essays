*tara
"foot, leg"

Proto-Tarahumaran (c. 1000 CE)
*rarámuri
"people, runners"
"those who run well"

rarámuri

tarahumara

Modern English (1870–present)
tarahumara

↓
Modern English (plural) (19th c.–present)
Tarahumaras

"plural with -s"

Note: The exonym tarahumara is a Spanish corruption of endonym rarámuri; the gloss "runners, light feet" follows Lumholtz and Bennett-Zingg but remains debated, with some linguists glossing rarámuri simply as "people, humans" and tarahumara folk-etymologised as "where night is day of the moon". No PIE root is involved; the family is Uto-Aztecan.
