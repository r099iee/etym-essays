Note on the debate (zero guesswork): The Greek lēth- cluster is usually traced to a PIE root meaning "to conceal, escape notice" (the source also of Latin lateō "to lie hidden"), but the exact root shape is debated: Beekes allows that parts of the family may conceal a Pre-Greek element, and Pokorny's asterisked forms are reconstructions, not attested words. The compound's second element, argos "idle", is uncontroversial — the same root seen in English argon ("the lazy gas") and ergonomics (ergon, "work").
