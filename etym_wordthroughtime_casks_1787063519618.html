- 16th c. [Naval & Continental Borrowing] - Borrowed from French casque / Spanish casco ("helmet, skull, shard, barrel") into English maritime trade for transport containers.
- 17th–18th c. [Universal Standard of Maritime Freight] - Became the global standard shipping container for wine, rum, flour, salt pork, and gunpowder across transatlantic trade.
- 19th c. [Industrial Distillation & Cooperage Peak] - Standardized within commercial brewing and whiskey aging, establishing distinct flavor-maturation science.
- 20th–21st c. [Artisanal Real Ale & Craft Spirit Prestige] - Reclaimed as a symbol of authentic, unadulterated brewing ("cask-conditioned", "cask strength") against mass industrial pasteurization.
