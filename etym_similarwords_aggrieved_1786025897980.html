• Resentful: Similar because both involve harboring negative feelings about unfair treatment; differs because resentful emphasizes lingering, smoldering bitterness, whereas aggrieved emphasizes the initial sense of injury and the claim to redress.
  • Indignant: Similar because both express strong anger at unjust treatment; differs because indignant is righteous moral anger aimed outward, while aggrieved is hurt pride and a feeling of being personally wronged.
  • Offended: Similar because both involve feeling hurt or insulted by someone's behavior; differs because offended can follow minor breaches of etiquette, while aggrieved implies a substantive wrong or injustice.
  • Disgruntled: Similar because both describe unhappy, dissatisfied people; differs because disgruntled suggests general sulky discontent or bad mood, while aggrieved requires a specific cause of unjust treatment.
  • Slighted: Similar because both involve feeling ignored or disrespected; differs because slighted focuses on injuries to ego and manners, while aggrieved extends to legal and material loss.
  • Afflicted: Similar because both describe suffering hardship; differs because afflicted implies physical disease or misfortune from fate or nature, while aggrieved requires unfair treatment by human agency.
  • Victimized: Similar because both describe having suffered harm at another's hands; differs because victimized stresses helplessness and trauma, while aggrieved stresses indignation and a demand for justice.
  • Hurt: Similar because both describe emotional pain from others' actions; differs because hurt is a broad, passive feeling, while aggrieved adds a moral judgment of injustice and a sense of entitlement.
  • Wronged: Similar because both mean treated unjustly; differs because wronged describes the objective fact of injustice, while aggrieved emphasizes the subjective emotional reaction to it.
  • Wounded (of pride): Similar because both involve damage to dignity; differs because wounded is metaphorical and private, while aggrieved implies a public complaint or legal claim.
