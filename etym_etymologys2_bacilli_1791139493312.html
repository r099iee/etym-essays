PIE (c. 4000-2500 BCE)
*bak-
"staff, stick"
↓
Latin (c. 500 BCE-200 CE)
baculum
"staff, stick, sceptre"
↓
Latin (c. 100 BCE-200 CE)
bacillus
"little staff, rodlet"
↓
Modern Latin (Bacteriology) (c. 1850s-1880s)
bacillus
"rod-shaped bacterium"
↓
Modern English (c. 1880s-present)
bacilli
"rod-shaped bacteria (plural)"

Note: PIE *bak- is a regional European substrate root for stick, not fully secure; Latin baculum / bacillus certain. Modern bacterial sense is 19th-century Neo-Latin coinage by Ehrenberg / Koch, not Classical usage.
