• snuggling: Similar because it means pressing close for warmth and affection; differs because snuggling emphasizes mutual cuddling while nestling emphasizes fitting into a sheltered hollow.
  • cuddling: Similar because it means holding close lovingly; differs because cuddling is usually reciprocal between people while nestling can be solitary against bedding or landscape.
  • nuzzling: Similar because it means pressing the nose or face close affectionately; differs because nuzzling specifies rubbing with nose/face while nestling is whole-body settling.
  • burrowing: Similar because it means pushing into softness for shelter; differs because burrowing implies digging or disappearing inside while nestling implies resting on the surface cozily.
  • huddling: Similar because it means crowding close together; differs because huddling often implies cold, fear, or misery while nestling implies contentment and trust.
  • sheltering: Similar because it means resting in protection; differs because sheltering is broader and more neutral while nestling adds softness and intimacy.
  • ensconcing: Similar because it means settling securely and comfortably; differs because ensconcing is formal and often implies established comfort while nestling is tender and small-scale.
  • nesting: Similar because it shares the nest root and settling sense; differs because nesting usually means making a home or preparing for a baby while nestling means the momentary act of snuggling.
  • curling up: Similar because it describes drawing the body into cozy compactness; differs because it specifies posture while nestling specifies relationship to a shelter or person.
  • tucking: Similar because it means fitting snugly into place; differs because tucking is usually done by another hand while nestling is what the tucked thing itself does.
