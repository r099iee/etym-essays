Late Latin (c. 4th c.)
bonna / abonnis
"a kind of headdress, cap"
↓
Medieval Latin (c. 11th c.)
bonetum
"fine cloth or material used for headdresses"
↓
Old French (c. 12th c.)
bonet
"cap, headdress; cloth for hats"
↓
Middle English (c. 14th c.)
bonet / bonnet
"cap, headdress; extra sail on a ship"
↓
Modern English (c. 1500s–present)
BONNET
"brimless cap; Scottish cap; car engine hood"

Note on Material Transfer: Like *panama* or *tweed*, *bonnet* is an elliptical derivation where the name of the fabric (*étoffe de bonet*) transferred directly to the finished headwear.
