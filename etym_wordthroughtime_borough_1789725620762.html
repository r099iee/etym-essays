- Early medieval fortified place - burh means a walled stronghold against Vikings; perception martial, refuge, Alfred’s network of defences.
- High medieval chartered town - borough gains market, mint and liberties; connotation of freemen, guilds and tolls, privilege earned by walls and wealth.
- Early modern parliamentary borough - sends burgesses to Parliament; splits into proud cities and corrupt rotten boroughs, perception both representative and rotten.
- Victorian municipal borough - reformed corporations with elected councillors, mayors and town halls; image of gas, water, sewers, civic pride and top hats.
- Contemporary administrative borough - London boroughs, metropolitan boroughs, Chelmsford’s city-borough; connotation of bins, planning, schools and local democracy, historic walls remembered only in -borough names.
