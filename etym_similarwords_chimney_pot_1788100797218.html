• Chimney: Similar because both belong to the same smoke-drawing system; differs because chimney names the whole structure — hearth, flue and stack — while the chimney-pot is only the terminal pipe perched on top.
  • Chimney-cowl: Similar because both are fittings on the chimney mouth that control the draught; differs because a cowl is a vented, often rotating metal cap that chiefly weatherproofs the flue, while a chimney-pot is usually fixed, static and made of earthenware, and chiefly boosts the up-draught.
  • Chimney-stack: Similar because both refer to the part of the chimney above the roof; differs because the stack is the masonry body built into the roofline, while the pot is the removable pipe seated upon it.
  • Flue: Similar because both concern the passage of smoke; differs because the flue is the internal channel inside the chimney, invisible and structural, not a discrete object on the roof.
  • Flue terminal: Similar because both name the outlet fitting of a flue; differs because flue terminal is modern technical trade English, covering metal and concrete fittings in new-build work, with none of the Victorian earthenware resonance of chimney-pot.
  • Stovepipe: Similar because both are pipes that conduct smoke; differs because a stovepipe runs from a stove to a flue — often horizontally through a room — while a chimney-pot crowns the masonry chimney outside; both, however, fathered hat-slang (stovepipe hat beside chimney-pot hat).
  • Smokestack: Similar because both are chimney outlets; differs in scale and register: a smokestack is the tall industrial funnel of a factory or ship, American in flavour, while the chimney-pot belongs to the domestic rooftop.
  • Top hat: Similar because the Victorian slang “chimney-pot” could itself denote the tall silk top hat; differs because a top hat is headwear — the pipe and the hat merely share a silhouette, an accidental and satirical resemblance.
  • Pot: Similar because the compound's head is pot, a vessel-like tube; differs because the plain pot is an open container with a base, while the chimney-pot is an open-ended pipe standing on a chimney — shape-family, not function-family.
