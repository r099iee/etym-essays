• accumulated: Similar because it denotes gradual piling up over time; differs because it suggests deliberate collection or heaping rather than natural growth vesting in someone.
  • amassed: Similar because it means gathered into a large total; differs because it implies active, often ambitious gathering of wealth or power and is stronger and less technical.
  • gathered: Similar because it means collected together; differs because it is everyday and physical, lacking the formal financial-legal sense of vesting over time.
  • collected: Similar because it means brought together as due; differs because it implies agency in fetching or receiving, not slow organic increase.
  • built up: Similar because it denotes incremental growth; differs because it is phrasal, informal, and stresses effortful construction rather than automatic accrual.
  • increased: Similar because it denotes becoming larger; differs because it is generic and neutral, without the to-someone beneficiary structure.
  • grew: Similar because it denotes organic enlargement; differs because it is broad and biological, lacking the entitlement and accounting precision of accrued.
  • mounted: Similar because it denotes rising totals, often of debts or problems; differs because it leans negative and dramatic, while accrued is neutral and institutional.
  • piled up: Similar because it denotes heaped accumulation; differs because it is colloquial and often implies disorder or burden.
  • vested: Similar because in law it denotes rights coming to belong to someone; differs because it stresses legal fixity and non-forfeitability rather than gradual growth.
  • earned: Similar because it denotes something coming to one through work or time; differs because it stresses desert and labour, while accrued stresses time-mediated growth whether or not laboured for.
