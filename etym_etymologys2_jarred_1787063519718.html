Note on the debate: the origin of the verb jar is not securely known. Standard dictionaries treat it as probably imitative (echoic) of a harsh grating noise, but no older ancestor — Old English, Romance, or Germanic — has been convincingly demonstrated, and no Indo-European root can be reconstructed. The entirely unrelated noun jar (“a glass container”, from French jarre, ultimately Arabic jarrah) shares the spelling but has a separate history.
