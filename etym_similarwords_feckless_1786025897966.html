• Incompetent: Similar because both describe inability to perform duties effectively; differs because incompetent implies a lack of skill or qualification, whereas feckless points to a defect of will and character rather than of ability.
  • Irresponsible: Similar because both involve failing obligations; differs because irresponsible stresses deliberate carelessness about consequences, while feckless stresses a passive, aimless helplessness.
  • Shiftless: Similar because both signify lack of ambition and resourcefulness; differs because shiftless more strongly implies laziness and refusal to work, while feckless can describe genuine weakness or futility rather than idleness.
  • Ineffectual: Similar because both describe failure to achieve results; differs because ineffectual is a neutral judgement about outcomes, while feckless is a moral judgement about character.
  • Futile: Similar because both describe fruitless actions; differs because futile applies to the effort or attempt, while feckless applies to the person's character or the hopeless quality of their doings.
  • Helpless: Similar because both involve incapacity; differs because helpless is neutral or sympathetic (unable to act), while feckless carries blame for the incapacity.
  • Weak-willed: Similar because both denote lack of resolve; differs because weak-willed names the specific failure of will, while feckless covers initiative, responsibility, and effectiveness together.
  • Lethargic: Similar because both involve a lack of vigorous action; differs because lethargic is physical or mental sluggishness, while feckless is a lack of purpose, spine, and accountability.
  • Aimless: Similar because both describe a lack of clear direction; differs because aimless describes having no goals, while feckless implies being unable to carry out goals even when given them.
  • Good-for-nothing: Similar because both dismiss someone as useless; differs because good-for-nothing is slangy and absolute, while feckless is more formal and can describe actions and efforts, not just people.
