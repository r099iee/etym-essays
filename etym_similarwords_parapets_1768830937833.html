• Ramparts: Similar because both are defensive walls; differs because ramparts are massive broad embankment walls surrounding an entire fort, while parapets are low chest-high guardrails atop walls or edges.
  • Breastworks: Similar because both are chest-high defences; differs because breastworks are temporary field fortifications hastily thrown up, while parapets are permanent stone or masonry structures.
  • Bulwarks: Similar because both are protective barriers; differs because bulwarks are heavy timber-and-earth works or the sides of a ship, while parapets are low walls along elevated edges.
  • Balustrades: Similar because both guard the edge of a roof or balcony; differs because balustrades consist of individual pillars (balusters) supporting a rail, while parapets are solid walls.
  • Railings: Similar because both prevent falls at edges; differs because railings are open bar-and-post barriers, while parapets are solid masonry walls.
  • Battlements: Similar because both crown the tops of walls; differs because battlements are parapets with alternating solid merlons and crenels for shooting, while parapets may be plain.
  • Merlons: Similar because both are parts of high wall defences; differs because merlons are the solid teeth between the openings of a battlement, while parapets are the whole low wall.
  • Bastions: Similar because both are fortification elements; differs because bastions are projecting angled fortification towers, while parapets are low breast-height walls.
  • Embankments: Similar because both are raised defensive earthworks; differs because embankments are broad raised banks of earth, while parapets are relatively thin walls on top.
  • Walls: Similar because both are vertical barriers; differs because walls are general structural barriers, while parapets are specifically the low guarding walls at elevated edges.
  • Barriers: Similar because both obstruct or protect; differs because barriers are any obstruction, while parapets are specifically low architectural safety or defence walls.
  • Fortifications: Similar because both are defensive structures; differs because fortifications are whole defensive systems, while parapets are one component of them.
  • Guardrails: Similar because both protect against falling; differs because guardrails are modern metal or wood rails, while parapets are solid low masonry walls.
  • Banisters: Similar because both guard stair edges; differs because banisters are the handrail and posts of a staircase, while parapets are low walls along roof and rampart edges.
