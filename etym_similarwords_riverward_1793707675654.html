• Towards the river: Similar because it expresses the same direction; differs because it's a phrase, not a single word, and lacks the conciseness of "riverward."
  • Downstream: Similar because it often implies movement along a river; differs because "downstream" specifies movement with the current, while "riverward" simply means towards the river, which could be upstream, downstream, or across.
  • Upstream: Similar because it implies movement along a river; differs because "upstream" specifies movement against the current, while "riverward" is a general direction towards the river.
  • Riverine: Similar because it relates to a river; differs because "riverine" describes something related to or found near a river (e.g., "riverine ecosystem"), while "riverward" is purely directional.
  • Riverside: Similar because it relates to a river; differs because "riverside" denotes a static location beside a river, while "riverward" indicates movement or orientation towards it.
  • Waterward: Similar because it indicates direction towards water; differs because "waterward" is more general, referring to any body of water, whereas "riverward" is specific to a river.
  • Shoreward: Similar because it indicates direction towards a shore/bank; differs because "shoreward" is typically used for larger bodies of water (sea, lake), and while a river has banks, "riverward" specifically points to the river itself.
