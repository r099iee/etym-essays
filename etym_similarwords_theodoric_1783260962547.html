• Alaric: Similar because he is another famous Gothic king and fellow 'zealous Christian' dynasty-founder in the same passage; differs because Alaric was a Visigoth who sacked Rome in 410, whereas Theodoric was an Ostrogoth who built a lasting kingdom in Italy from Ravenna.
  • Odoacer: Similar because he was the Germanic ruler of Italy whom Theodoric displaced; differs because Odoacer was a Herulian/Scirian general rather than a Gothic king, and he was defeated and killed by Theodoric in 493.
  • Dietrich von Bern: Similar because it is the legendary form of the same historical Theodoric; differs because Dietrich is the medieval epic hero of the Nibelungenlied and Þiðreks saga, while Theodoric is the historical king of the chronicles.
  • Clovis: Similar because he is another Germanic dynasty-founder and 'zealous Christian' king who ruled a continental province of the empire; differs because Clovis was a Frank who founded the Merovingian kingdom in Gaul and converted to Catholic Christianity, while Theodoric remained an Arian Goth ruling Italy.
  • Boethius: Similar because he belongs to the same Ravenna-centred, 6th-century Italian world; differs because Boethius was the Roman philosopher and senator employed and later executed by Theodoric, not a ruler.
  • Cassiodorus: Similar because he served Theodoric's Ravenna as minister and Roman civil servant; differs because Cassiodorus was a scholar-administrator who preserved Roman letters for Theodoric's kingdom rather than a Germanic king.
  • Justinian: Similar because he was the 6th-century Roman emperor whose general Belisarius overthrew the Ostrogothic kingdom; differs because Justinian was the Byzantine (eastern Roman) emperor, Theodoric's opposite in a later generation.
  • Ermanaric: Similar because his Gothic royal name shares the same *-reiks 'ruler' element as Theodoric; differs because Ermanaric was a 4th-century king of the Greuthungi (eastern Goths) whose history is largely legendary.
  • Diederik / Dirk: Similar because these are modern Dutch forms of the very same name Theodoric; differs because they are everyday given names in the Low Countries, while English Theodoric remains a learned historical name.
