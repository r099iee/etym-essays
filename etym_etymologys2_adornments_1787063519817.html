ADORNMENTS

Note on the debated root: the connection between Latin ornare "to fit out" and ordo "order" is long-standing but debated; de Vaan judges the *h₂er- base uncertain, and some scholars propose a separate, obscure origin for ornare. The chain above follows the most widely accepted derivation but flags the uncertainty honestly. The suffix -ment comes via Old French -ment from Latin -mentum, making nouns of result.
