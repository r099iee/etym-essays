- 1913 comic-strip joke - the Joneses as the unseen slightly-richer neighbours in Arthur Momand’s Keeping Up with the Joneses
- 1920s–1930s catchphrase - generalised emblem of suburban envy and middle-class status anxiety
- Mid-century sociology - the phrase as Exhibit A for conformity, consumer society, and Veblen-style critique
- Postwar suburbia - giant lawns, cars, and appliances make the race literal; moralists preach against it
- Contemporary ironic idiom - applied to influencers, feeds, and global comparison; rueful, self-aware, still aimed at others
