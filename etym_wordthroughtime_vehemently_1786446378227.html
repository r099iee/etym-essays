- Late 15th to 16th Century - Elemental Violence & Physical Tempest: Borrowed into English describing overwhelming physical forces, raging fires, violent disease symptoms, and ferocious storms.
- 17th Century - Psychological Inward Turn: Shifted to describe internal human passions, intense psychological states, burning zeal, and fiery romantic or religious desires.
- 18th to 19th Century - Rhetorical & Parliamentary Discourse: Became a standard term in political, judicial, and literary debate for impassioned speeches, fierce moral objections, and principled denunciations.
- 20th to 21st Century - Categorical Denial & Public Assertion: Predominantly collocated with verbs of denial and protest (e.g., "vehemently denied," "vehemently opposed"), signifying unyielding, indignant, and absolute rejection of allegations.
