Bowled is the past tense and past participle of the verb bowl, conveying physical impact, rotational propulsion, athletic delivery, or overwhelming emotional astonishment. Depending on its context and prepositional pairing, it ranges from the violent kinetic overturning of bodies in physical collisions to the graceful delivery of a cricket ball or the psychological state of being utterly flabbergasted.

The extensive polysemy and semantic development of bowled encompass several distinct spheres of physical action, competitive sport, and metaphorical idiom:

  • Physical Collision and Overturning (Bowling Over): When used with the particle over (or in transitive physical contexts), to have bowled someone over means to collide with them forcefully, knocking them off their feet and sending them tumbling to the ground like pins in a bowling alley. This usage emphasizes kinetic momentum, sudden impact, and the victim’s total loss of equilibrium under the momentum of an oncoming person, animal, or heavy projectile.
  • Sporting Delivery and Propulsion: In the context of games and sports:
    • Cricket: To have bowled refers specifically to propelling the leather ball toward the batsman’s wicket with a straight, unbent arm. It also refers to dismissing a batsman directly by hitting the stumps with the delivery (e.g., "he bowled the opener with an inswinging yorker").
    • Lawn Bowls, Ten-Pin, and Bocce: To have rolled, pitched, or propelled a heavy wooden, composite, or stone ball across a flat green, alley, or pitch toward a designated target (the jack or pins) with calculated bias, speed, and spin.
  • Rapid, Smooth, and Effortless Locomotion ("Bowling Along"): In vehicular, equestrian, and pedestrian movement, the phrase bowling along describes traveling forward smoothly, rapidly, and without noticeable resistance. A carriage, sports car, or cyclist bowling along an open highway conveys a sense of easy, buoyant, and frictionless momentum, mirroring the uninhibited roll of a spherical ball over a smooth surface.
  • Psychological Shock, Amazement, and Overwhelming Gratitude: Idiomatically and metaphorically, the passive construction bowled over (e.g., "I was completely bowled over by her generosity") describes being incapacitated by shock, delight, awe, or utter astonishment. The mental faculties are figuratively knocked flat, stripped of composure and critical skepticism in the face of unexpected news, immense kindness, or stunning artistic beauty.
  • Craft, Domestic, and Industrial Hollow Forms: While the verbal form primarily derives from the spherical toy/game (Old French boule), it occasionally interacts historically with the Germanic noun bowl (a deep circular dish or concave vessel, from Old English bolla), appearing in archaic or technical crafts where materials are hollowed, cupped, or curved into a concave basin shape.

The unifying conceptual thread across these varied manifestations is the dynamics of the sphere: whether rolling across turf, striking targets like wooden skittles, cruising effortlessly across open terrain, or metaphorically knocking a person off their psychological balance, to have bowled reflects the unyielding kinetic power and continuous motion of a rolling globe.
