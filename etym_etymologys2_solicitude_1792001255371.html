PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500 BCE)
*sol-
"whole, intact, entire, well-kept"
[Compounded with *kʷi- / *kʷei- "to move, stir, excite"]

Proto-Italic (c. 1000 BCE)
*soll-i-ki-to-
"entirely stirred up, completely moved"

sollicitus
"wholly agitated, disturbed, deeply anxious"
[From sollus "whole" + citus "moved, aroused"]
↓
Classical Latin (c. 1st c. BCE)
sollicitūdō
"state of anxiety, mental distress, troubled care"

↓
Late Latin / Church Latin (c. 4th c.)
sollicitudo
"pastoral care, watchful spiritual oversight"
↓
Old French (c. 12th c.)
solicitude

↓
Middle English (c. 1380s)
solicitude
"anxious care, spiritual watchfulness, trouble of mind"
↓
Modern English (present)
solicitude
