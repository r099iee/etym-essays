- 14th century - from Old French 'covre-lit' ('cover the bed'): the outer covering of a bed
- 15th-18th century - standard household-inventory word for a decorative top cloth, often quilted or embroidered
- 19th century - competes with 'counterpane'; the ornamental upper layer of a bed in domestic fiction and furnishing lists
- Contemporary - slightly literary and old-fashioned; everyday speech prefers 'bedspread', 'duvet', or 'comforter', while 'coverlet' suggests a lighter, decorative top covering
