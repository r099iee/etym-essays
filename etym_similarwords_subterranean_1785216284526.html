• underground: Similar because both mean below the earth's surface; differs because underground is the plain, everyday, neutral synonym — practical and unmarked — whereas subterranean carries a learned, Latinate, slightly ominous weight.
  • subterraneous: Similar because it is a near-synonym meaning "under the earth"; differs because it is a rare, purely technical variant, almost extinct in ordinary usage where subterranean has prevailed.
  • subsurface: Similar because it means below a surface; differs because it is a scientific, value-neutral term (subsurface water) with no overtones of depth, darkness, or the mythic underworld.
  • hypogeal: Similar because it means under or below the earth; differs because it is a technical botanical term for growth underground, unknown outside specialist usage.
  • chthonic: Similar because it belongs to the subterranean world; differs because it is mythic and religious — the underworld and its gods — where subterranean is descriptive and geological rather than divine.
  • buried: Similar because both involve being placed under the earth; differs because buried implies a deliberate act of covering, whereas subterranean describes a natural or structural state of being beneath the surface.
  • nether: Similar because it means lower or under (the nether world); differs because it is archaic, poetic, and vaguely menacing, lacking subterranean's scientific precision.
  • underlying: Similar because it means lying beneath something; differs because it is abstract and figurative — a principle or cause beneath a phenomenon — with no sense of earth or physical depth.
