↓

NOTE: The pre-Latin origin of cattus is unknown; proposed sources include Egyptian čaute, Nubian kadîs, and Berber kaddîska, but none is proven. The pairing of Tom with cat is popularly credited to the 1760 novella “The Life and Adventures of a Cat”; the compound is first recorded in 1809.
