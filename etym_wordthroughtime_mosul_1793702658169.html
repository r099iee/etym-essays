- Ancient Settlement (pre-Islamic) - Strategic location near Nineveh, a regional hub.
- Medieval Islamic Golden Age (9th-13th centuries) - Flourishing center of trade, scholarship, and textile production (origin of 'muslin'), highly regarded.
- Ottoman Era (16th-20th centuries) - Important provincial capital, continued commercial significance, known for diverse communities.
- 20th Century (Post-Ottoman) - Geopolitical importance due to oil reserves, incorporated into Iraq, symbol of national identity.
- Early 21st Century (Post-2003 Invasion) - Period of instability and conflict, symbol of Iraqi resilience.
- ISIS Occupation (2014-2017) - Global notoriety as a stronghold of extremism, associated with destruction and human suffering.
- Post-ISIS Liberation (2017-Present) - Symbol of recovery, reconstruction, and the enduring spirit of its people, still grappling with challenges.
