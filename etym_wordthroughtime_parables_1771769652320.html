- [Ancient Hebrew scripture] - ['Parable' renders the Hebrew 'mashal', a comparison or proverb of wisdom, as in the Old Testament]
- [Gospel teaching stories] - [The word fixes on Jesus' vivid stories — the sower, the prodigal son — that hide a moral inside a likeness]
- [Medieval religious instruction] - [A stock form of sermon and moral teaching; the parable becomes a formal genre of religious narrative]
- [18th century to present broad instructive tale] - [Widened to any simple story that teaches a lesson; Gibbon even 'translates catastrophes into parables of human nature', carrying the religious genre into secular instruction]
