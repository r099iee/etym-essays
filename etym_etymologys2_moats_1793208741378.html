mote

Modern English (plural)
moats

Note: the deeper origin of Medieval Latin mota is uncertain; older handbooks suggest Gaulish *motta meaning 'clod of turf', but no secure Celtic attestation exists, so the chain honestly begins at the first fully attested stage. The shift from 'mound' to 'ditch' reflects castle engineering itself: the ditch was dug to heap up the mound of the motte, and the name of the heap passed to the hollow that made it.
