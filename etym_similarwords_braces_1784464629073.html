• Suspenders: Similar because they are the shoulder straps that hold up trousers; differs because suspenders is the usual American term (in Britain it can mean stocking-supporters), while braces is the British word.
  • Galluses: Similar because they are the same paired shoulder straps; differs because galluses is a rustic or dialectal American word, while braces is the standard British term.
  • Belts: Similar because they hold up trousers; differs because a belt circles the waist, while braces hang from the shoulders over both shoulders.
  • Straps: Similar because they are supporting bands of material; differs because a strap is the general band of any kind, while braces are specifically the paired trouser straps.
  • Garters: Similar because they are supporting bands of dress; differs because garters support stockings (or sleeves), while braces support trousers.
  • Shoulder-straps: Similar because they are straps worn over the shoulders; differs because shoulder-straps is a generic descriptive term (also on rucksacks, dresses), while braces is the fixed garment word.
  • Fasteners: Similar because they secure clothing; differs because fasteners are the small closures (buttons, clips, hooks), while braces are the supporting straps themselves.
