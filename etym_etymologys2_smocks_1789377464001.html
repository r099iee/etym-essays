Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*smewg- / *smuk-
"to creep, slip into, slide through, sneak"
Note: Germanic verbal base describing slipping into a narrow opening or garment, cognate with Proto-Germanic *smeukaną.

Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE–1st c. CE)
*smukkaz
"garment slipped into or pulled over the head; a shirt, chemise"
Note: Nominal derivative of the verb *smuganą ('to creep through, slip into'); cognate with Old Norse smokkr ('chemise, smock').
