• Conjecture: Similar because both form opinions without firm evidence; differs because conjecture emphasises guesswork built on incomplete clues and is often forensic or scholarly, while speculate is more systematic, reflective, and can also carry the dignified philosophical sense of contemplation.
  • Guess: Similar because both answer without certainty; differs because guessing is casual, immediate, and unreflective, whereas speculating involves deliberate, ranging thought about what might be — or, in finance, deliberate action on it.
  • Hypothesize: Similar because both frame a tentative proposition; differs because hypothesize names the scientific act of framing a testable claim, while speculate is freer and not constrained by testability — as Mill's "idle to speculate" shows.
  • Theorize: Similar because both construct reasoned explanation; differs because theorize implies building a systematic explanatory framework, whereas speculate ranges beyond the evidence without pretending to settle anything.
  • Suppose: Similar because both assume something without proof; differs because suppose is a lighter, more ordinary assumption or working hypothesis, while speculate implies active inquiry and watching for what might come.
  • Surmise: Similar because both infer without full evidence; differs because surmise is a quiet, tentative inference from partial signs, whereas speculate is a broader and often more sustained process of conjecture or reflection.
  • Postulate: Similar because both put forward an unproven proposition; differs because postulate is the formal, mathematical or logical laying-down of a basis for reasoning, while speculate is exploratory and free.
  • Invest / Trade (financial sense): Similar because both commit money in expectation of return; differs because investment is calculated and long-term, while speculation is the riskier dealing on short-term price movements that the word specifically names.
