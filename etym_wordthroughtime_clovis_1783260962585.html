- Frankish foundation - Clovis (c. 466-511), the first king to unite all the Frankish tribes, founded the Merovingian dynasty and conquered most of Gaul, and his victory at Vouillé (507) over the Visigoths secured Frankish dominion.
- The crucial conversion - by embracing Nicene (Catholic) orthodoxy at his baptism around 496, unlike the Arian Germanic kings, Clovis gained the alliance of the papacy and the loyalty of the Romanized population of Gaul.
- Later historiography - Macaulay ranked him among the chiefs who founded Teutonic dynasties and were zealous Christians, and historians since have seen his conversion as the reason the Franks alone, of all Germanic peoples, founded a lasting realm.
- Contemporary sense - Clovis stands for the moment a barbarian kingdom entered Latin Christendom and for the birth of France, and his name survives in history, in the surname of the Merovingian dynasty, and as the old form of Louis.
