Note: The PIE etymology of Latin vitium is historically debated. While traditional lexicography connected it to roots denoting crookedness or fault (cf. Sanskrit vyathate ‘wavers, stumbles’), modern comparative linguists (such as Michiel de Vaan) note that vitium lacks unambiguous cognates outside Italic, suggesting it could be an Italic innovation or substratum term.
