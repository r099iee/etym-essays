Etymological Note: Lexicographers generally agree that bally originated as a deliberate phonetic corruption of bloody, reinforced and popularized by Robert Martin's smash-hit 1887 music-hall song 'The Ballyhooly Dooley' (referencing Ballyhooly, a village in County Cork, Ireland).
