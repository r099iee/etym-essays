Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE) *eti "above, beyond, also; back, again"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE) *idi- / *iþi- "back, backward, again, in return"
↓
Old Norse (c. 9th–11th c.) iða "a whirlpool, eddy, reverse current"
↓
Middle English (c. 15th c.) edy / yde "a circular backwater current"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present) EDDYING "swirling in circular countercurrents"
