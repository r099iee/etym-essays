ESGAROTH
“the Elvish name of the Lake-town”

Note: no Indo-European root applies — the name is a constructed Sindarin compound invented by J.R.R. Tolkien. The gloss “Reedlake” is Tolkien's own, but the analysis is partly reconstructed: esgar is attested nowhere else in Sindarin, and loth elsewhere in Sindarin most usually means “flower” (as in Lothlórien); whether Tolkien intended a homonymous loth “pool” is not fully documented and is debated among scholars. No stages are missing — the record is complete and entirely modern.
