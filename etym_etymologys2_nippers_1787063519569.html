Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gnebʰ- / *kneip-
"to pinch, nip, snap, press together"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*knīpaną / *neupaną
"to pinch, squeeze tightly with tongs or claws"
↓
Middle Low German / Middle Dutch (c. 13th c.)
nīpen / knīpen
"to pinch, squeeze, nip"
↓
Middle English (c. 1380)
nippen
"to pinch, bite sharply, catch tightly"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1570)
nipper
"one who nips; a pinching tool; a crab's claw; a cutpurse"
↓
Modern English (plural)
nippers
"pincer claws; cutting pliers; small children (plural)"
