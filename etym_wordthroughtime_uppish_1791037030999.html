- 1728 coinage - pert, saucy; masters scold servants for forgetting place; class discipline word.
- 18th-19th c. service - maids, footmen, clerks getting uppish; upward mobility framed as rudeness.
- Late 19th c. empire - colonial and gendered use; natives, women, workers uppish for asserting rights.
- Early 20th c. Wodehouse - comic general snobbery; grovelling breeds uppishness; light, ironic, upper-class banter.
- Late 20th c. to present - any arrogant manner or venue is uppish; class sting faded, British informal for stuck-up remains.
