• Lolled: Similar in relaxing the body into an undignified posture; differs because lolled implies a languid, self-indulgent recline against a support with lazy pleasure, while sprawled is accidental and careless, limbs flung out without design.
  • Lounged: Similar in sitting or lying at ease; differs because lounged is a deliberate, comfortable, socially acceptable ease, whereas sprawled is ungainly and indifferent to elegance.
  • Stretched out: Similar in an extended, full-length position; differs because stretched out is generally intentional and orderly, while sprawled emphasizes disorder and awkward angles.
  • Splayed: Similar in spreading limbs or digits wide apart; differs because splayed specifically highlights outward turning or fanning of parts, whereas sprawled describes whole-body posture and spatial dispersion.
  • Prostrated: Similar in lying flat on the ground; differs because prostrated implies submission, exhaustion, or worship in an aligned position, while sprawled is chaotic and misaligned.
  • Recumbent: Similar in lying down; differs because recumbent is a formal, restful posture without ungainly disorder.
  • Floundered: Similar in violent, awkward limb movement during a fall; differs because floundered emphasizes struggling movement in mud or water, whereas sprawled emphasizes the static spread posture resulting from a fall.
  • Slouched: Similar in a careless, collapsed posture; differs because slouched is a seated, hunched droop of the shoulders, while sprawled is a full outward spreading of the whole body.
  • Draped: Similar in the body being spread across a surface; differs because draped implies a decorative or artful arrangement, whereas sprawled is artless and accidental.
  • Spilled: Similar in occupying space carelessly; differs because spilled is used of liquids or loose contents, while sprawled applies to a solid, animate body.
  • Overspread: Similar in extending over a large area; differs because overspread is neutral covering, whereas sprawled carries the connotation of ungainly looseness.
  • Scattered: Similar in dispersion over a wide area; differs because scattered applies to separated discrete objects, while sprawled describes one continuous body spread out.
  • Sprawling (adjective): Similar in the same root image; differs because the adjective directly modifies a noun (a sprawling city) while sprawled names a finished state or result.
