Proto-Indo-European / Mediterranean substrate (c. 2000–1000 BCE)
*kol-omb- / *columba
"dove, pigeon; dark/slate-colored bird"
Note: Cognate with Ancient Greek kólymbos ('diver, seabird') or derived from a pre-Indo-European Mediterranean substrate.

columbīnus

Late Medieval Latin (c. 12th–13th c.)
aquilegia columbīna / herba columbīna
"dove-like aquilegia, dove-plant"
