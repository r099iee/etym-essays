Note: origin of Tudeus / Tydeus itself is debated. Beekes treats names in -eus as likely Pre-Greek substrate with no secure PIE etymology; ancient folk-etymology sometimes linked it to tupto "to strike", which is not accepted by modern scholars. The patronymic suffix -ides itself is regular Greek -ides "son / descendant of", from -i- + -des, well attested in Homer.
