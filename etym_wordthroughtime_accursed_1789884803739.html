- Old English curs as evil-prayer and church ban - imprecation plus excommunication
- Late 12c. acursen as to curse thoroughly - intensive prefix deepens the ban
- Early 13c. acursede as being under a curse - outcast spiritual state
- 15c. accursed with false cc - Renaissance Latin look, same venom
- 1590s weakened damnable plus modern comic - from anathema to annoying printer and goose
