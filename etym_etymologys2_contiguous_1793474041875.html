Note: Formed from Latin con- (‘together, with’) + tangere (‘to touch’, with root vowel weakening from -a- to -i- in unstressed medial syllables, producing contingere) + the verbal adjectival suffix -uus denoting continuous state or propensity. English anglicized the Latin adjectival suffix -uus directly into -ous, influenced by parallel Middle French forms like contigu.
