• Diligence: Similar because both denote steady, careful application to work; differs because diligence stresses painstaking attention to detail and is narrower in scope, while industry also implies large-scale, sustained productivity and, in its modern sense, the economic sector.
  • Assiduity: Similar because both denote constant, devoted application to a task; differs because assiduity is a more formal, even technical word emphasising constancy of attendance, while industry emphasises productive output and has developed the separate economic sense.
  • Application: Similar because both denote focused, continued effort; differs because application names the act of directing oneself to a task, while industry names the settled habit and its fruits, and also the economic sector.
  • Labour / Toil: Similar because both denote work, especially productive physical work; differ because labour and toil emphasise the effort and difficulty of work itself, while industry stresses disciplined, systematic, productive application and its organisation.
  • Perseverance / Persistence: Similar because both denote continued effort over time; differ because perseverance stresses endurance through difficulty, while industry stresses steady, habitual, systematic application and, in its modern sense, organised production.
  • Enterprise: Similar because both denote energetic, purposeful activity in a productive sphere; differs because enterprise stresses initiative, risk, and the launching of new ventures, while industry stresses the sustained execution of work — and 'industry' is the older, more settled virtue.
  • Business / Manufacturing / Trade (economic cluster): Similar because industry in its modern sense denotes organised economic activity in these spheres; differ because business and trade are broader (all commerce), manufacturing names one specific sector, while industry as a sector is the organised production of goods and services.
  • Work / Effort: Similar because both denote activity directed to a purpose; differ because work and effort are generic and neutral, while industry adds the qualities of diligence, system, and sustained productivity — and, in the modern sense, institutional organisation.
