• Skeleton: Similar because it is the bony framework of the body; differs because skeleton is the whole articulated system, bone is one piece or the substance.
  • Ossein: Similar because it is the technical term for bone substance; differs because it is laboratory-formal, while bone is everyday.
  • Utterly: Similar because bone idle means utterly idle; differs because utterly is a pure degree adverb, bone is a metaphorical intensifier limited to fixed idioms.
  • Completely: Similar because it marks totality; differs because completely is neutral-general, bone adds bodily-hyperbolic colour.
  • Stone: Similar because stone cold parallels bone dry as hard-noun intensifier; differs because stone stresses cold hardness, bone stresses innermost residue.
  • Stark: Similar because stark naked parallels bone idle in totality; differs because stark is Germanic-bare, bone is anatomical.
  • Thoroughly: Similar because it means through and through; differs because thoroughly is procedural, bone is visceral.
  • Remains: Similar because bones mean mortal remains; differs because remains is euphemistic-funereal, bones is blunt-physical.
  • Marrow: Similar because marrow fills bones and stands for innermost essence; differs because marrow is soft interior, bone is hard casing.
  • Framework: Similar because bones frame the body as frameworks frame buildings; differs because framework is abstract-structural, bone is fleshly-literal.
