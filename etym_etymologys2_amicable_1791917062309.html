amīcitia

amīcābilis

↓

amicable

Note: Latin amāre has no agreed PIE etymology. Proposed links to a nursery root for “mother, aunt” or to a root meaning “to grasp” remain speculative, so the chain begins at the reconstructed Proto-Italic stage. The same Late Latin amīcābilis also reached English through Old French as amiable, giving the modern doublet.
