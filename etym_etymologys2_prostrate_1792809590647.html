Note: the prefix pro- (forward, before) comes from PIE *per-, forth, forward; the stem is *sterh3-, to strew — the same root that gives strew, straw, stratum, and strategy (the strewing of armies). The adjective–verb stress split (PROS-trate vs pros-TRATE) is regular English noun-verb patterning.
