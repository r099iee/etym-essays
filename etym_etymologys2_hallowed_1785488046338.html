Debate: the PIE source of Proto-Germanic *hailaz ("whole, sound") is uncertain; *kelh₁- ("to hide, cover") is only a debated proposal, and many reconstruct a root *kailo- with no secure non-Germanic cognates. holy and hallow both rest on the same stem hāl ("whole").
