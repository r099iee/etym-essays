Note: The base verb shed originally meant "to divide or separate" (cognate with German scheiden). The specialization of meaning in English—from "dividing" to "scattering in drops" and "casting off hair or tears"—is an insular English semantic development of the early Middle English period, absent in continental Germanic relatives.
