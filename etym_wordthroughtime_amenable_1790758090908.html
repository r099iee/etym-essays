- late 1500s–1650s: Anglo-French legal term meaning 'liable to be brought to answer before a court of justice' - Formal juridical accountability.
- 1650s–1750s: Semantic softening toward personal disposition: 'tractable, manageable, submissive to advice or governance' - Moral and personal tractability.
- 1800s–Present: Broadened into science, medicine, and academic literary theory: 'open to treatment, analysis, or specific methodology' - Highly respected formal and intellectual adjective.
- Contemporary: Common across academic papers, corporate negotiations, and clinical medicine.
