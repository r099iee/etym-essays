Note: Triarnum is the earliest attested form, in a papal bull of Alexander III (1163). Its origin is disputed; the most cited explanation takes it as a Latinised name meaning “junction of three domains”, comparable to Latin trifinium, but this is a hypothesis, and no pre-1163 form is known.
