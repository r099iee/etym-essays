Etymological Note: The base verb bouder is an expressive formation imitating the physical act of puffing out one's cheeks or pouting the lower lip in a fit of petulance (cognate with French boudin and English pout). The French locative suffix -oir derives from Latin -orium (as in dormitorium / dormitory), yielding the original literal meaning: "a room for pouting."
