NOTE: Latin nota was the mark of recognition — including the censor's mark of disgrace (nota censoria) — and its feminine form was built on the participle nōtus "known"; the same family gives English notice, notable, notation, notary, notify, and (via Italian) notoriety. The PIE root *ǵneh₃- "to know" is also the root of English know and can ("to know how"), Greek gignṓskein, and Sanskrit jñā-.
