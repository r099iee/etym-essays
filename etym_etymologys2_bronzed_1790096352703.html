Note: the origin of Italian bronzo is famously disputed. Competing theories derive it from Medieval Latin aes brundusinum, brass of Brindisi, from Persian birinj via Venetian trade, or from Late Latin *brunitius, brownish. Most handbooks call the Persian link plausible but unproven and the Brindisi link ingenious but phonologically strained; the Italian stage onward into French bronze and English is fully secure.
