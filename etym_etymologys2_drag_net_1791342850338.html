Compound Formation:
Late Middle English / Early Modern English (late 15th c.)
drag-net
"net drawn along bottom of water"
↓
Modern English (19th–20th c. figurative)
drag-net
"sweeping net; coordinated police search"
Note: Middle English draggen reflects a complex convergence: Old English possessed the strong verb dragan (which developed into modern draw), while Scandinavian settlers in the Danelaw introduced Old Norse draga, retaining the voiced velar plosive /g/. The compound drag-net was formed in late medieval fishing culture, originally hyphenated, and later established in 19th-century law enforcement before spawning the solid American spelling dragnet.
