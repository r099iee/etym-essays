Note on morphological formation: Sanskrit sārasa is formed through standard vṛddhi lengthening (strengthening the initial vowel a to ā) plus the taddhita suffix -a, meaning "pertaining to, originating in, or dwelling within a saras (lake/marsh)". In Indian literary commentary, the bird is frequently treated as synonymous with or closely allied to the krauñca bird of the inaugural Rāmāyaṇa episode.
