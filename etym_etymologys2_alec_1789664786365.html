Note: the first element alexein is of disputed PIE origin; Beekes treats it as possibly Pre-Greek substrate rather than secure *h2lek-, while Pokorny lists *alek- to protect. The *h2ner- man root is secure (cf. Sanskrit nara, Welsh ner). The Mycenaean form a-re-ka-sa-da-ra shows Alexandros already in use c. 1300 BCE.
