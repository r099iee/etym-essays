- Small wood in Old English - From Old English graf, grove has since the medieval period meant a small wood or company of trees, a discreet, walkable cluster rather than a vast forest.
- Sacred and poetic associations - Through classical and biblical tradition the 'sacred grove' makes the word a natural temple, a place of shade, shelter, and quiet devotion.
- Modern neutral-literary use - Today grove remains neutral yet gently literary, equally at home in botanical guides, orchard names (orange grove), and Romantic poetry, where it names the smallest, dearest home in nature.
