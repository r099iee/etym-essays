Note: This Tahitian hora is a homograph of, and wholly distinct from, Latin hora "hour, season" from Greek hōra from PIE *yeh₁r- "year, season". The plant name is Austronesian, not Indo-European, and its exact Proto-Polynesian vowel reconstruction (*hutu / *futu / *hora variants huteo, hotu, hutu) remains debated among Polynesian lexicographers.
