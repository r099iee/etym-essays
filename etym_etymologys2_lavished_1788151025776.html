Note on nuance: the sense bridge is a storm metaphor: what is lavished is poured out like a violent rain. The original pejorative force (wastefulness) softened over the centuries; modern use is admiring or neutral depending on the object (praise vs. money). The -ish ending of lavish is a spelling assimilation to the common English suffix, not the suffix itself etymologically.
