PIE(c. 4500–2500 BCE)
*trewd-
"to thrust, push"
↓
Latin(3rd–2nd c. BCE)
trūdere
"to thrust, press forward"
↓
Latin(1st c. BCE)
abstrūdere
"to thrust away,
conceal, hide"
(ab- "away" + trūdere)
↓
Latin(1st c. BCE)
abstrūsus
"hidden, concealed"
(past participle)
↓
English borrowing(1520s)
abstruse
"hidden from view;
hard to understand"
↓
Abstruse
"recondite, obscure,
difficult to comprehend"
Note on nuance: trūdere belongs to PIE *trewd-; the Latin compound and participle are secure. The only debated point is the degree of metaphoricity in the English borrowing — a semantic, not a formal, question.
