• scattered: Similar because both involve entities distributed irregularly over an area; differs because scattered often implies haphazard, chaotic throwing or accidental dropping, whereas dispersed implies the systematic dissolution of a previously unified or concentrated body.
  • dissipated: Similar because both denote the breaking up and vanishing of something; differs because dissipated emphasizes loss of energy, gradual thinning out to the point of complete disappearance (e.g., fog dissipating, fortunes dissipated), whereas dispersed can describe enduring particles distributed throughout a medium.
  • diffused: Similar because both refer to spreading outward; differs because diffused focuses on slow, permeating spread through a barrier or medium to achieve uniform concentration (such as a gas diffusing through a room or soft diffused light), while dispersed emphasizes the act of breaking apart from a central group.
  • disbanded: Similar because both describe the breakup of an organized group; differs because disbanded applies strictly to organizations, military units, committees, or teams that officially dissolve their formal union, whereas dispersed applies to physical matter, light, sound, and spontaneous crowds.
  • disseminated: Similar because both involve spreading things far and wide; differs because disseminated is used almost exclusively for information, doctrines, ideas, seeds, or pathogens intentionally broadcast across a population or territory.
  • routed: Similar because both describe an assembled body being forced apart; differs because routed implies a panicked, chaotic, and total military defeat where troops flee in terrified disorder from an overwhelming enemy.
  • fragmented: Similar because both describe a whole breaking into parts; differs because fragmented emphasizes physical breakage, shattered brittle shards, and structural disruption, whereas dispersed emphasizes the separation and distance between the components.
  • strewn: Similar because both involve objects lying separated across a surface; differs because strewn is a passive, messy resting state across a floor or ground (e.g., toys strewn across the room), lacking the dynamic physical or wave-based implications of dispersed.
