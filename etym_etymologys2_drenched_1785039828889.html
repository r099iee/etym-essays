Note: The ultimate PIE root behind the Germanic "drink / drench" family is uncertain: the proposed set *dʰreg- "to drink" is not firmly established. OE drencan is the old causative of drincan — literally "to make (someone) drink" — which is why the two verbs differ in vowel and in sense.
