• Walked: Similar because both denote bipedal pedestrian locomotion across a surface; differs because 'walked' is the neutral, standard term lacking the physical weight, rhythmic cadence, or deliberate footfall conveyed by 'trod'.
  • Paced: Similar because both involve deliberate stepping; differs because 'paced' implies measured, repetitive, often anxious movement back and forth within a confined boundary rather than traversing ground.
  • Trampled: Similar because both involve placing the feet upon a surface; differs because 'trampled' carries an explicitly destructive, chaotic, or violent force that ruins or crushes the object beneath.
  • Traversed: Similar because both describe crossing or travelling across a physical space; differs because 'traversed' is a formal, spatial term that does not inherently specify bodily bipedal footfalls.
  • Marched: Similar because both describe foot movement; differs because 'marched' entails rigid, rhythmic, disciplined cadence, typically associated with military formations or collective protests.
  • Trudged: Similar because both describe heavy footfalls; differs because 'trudged' specifically highlights arduous, weary, slow, and laboured movement through heavy mud, snow, or fatigue.
  • Stepped: Similar because both indicate setting the foot down; differs because 'stepped' is punctual, light, and non-committal, lacking the sustained contact or furrowing of 'trod'.
