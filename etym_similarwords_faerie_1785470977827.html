• Fairy: Similar because both descend from the same root and folklore; differs because in modern usage "fairy" names an individual small winged creature of children's literature, while "faerie" names the enchanted realm itself with a consciously archaic, elevated tone.
  • Fay: Similar because both come from Old French fae and name a supernatural being; differs because "fay" is a single spirit or enchantress, while "faerie" is the realm or the collective folk.
  • Sprite: Similar because both belong to the world of supernatural beings; differs because a "sprite" is one elemental spirit, often of air or water, while "faerie" is the collective domain or species.
  • Elf: Similar because both name beings of Germanic/Northern folklore; differs because "elf" is the individual creature of Teutonic tradition, while "faerie" is the Celtic-influenced realm and people; in modern fantasy the two realms are sometimes blended.
  • Elfland: Similar because both name the otherworld of supernatural folk; differs because "elfland" is specifically the elvish domain of Northern tradition, while "faerie" retains the Old French and Celtic associations of enchantment and fate.
  • Goblin: Similar because both name supernatural denizens of folklore; differs because goblins are grotesque, malicious cave-dwellers, whereas the beings of faerie range from noble and beautiful to mysterious and mischievous.
  • Otherworld: Similar because both name a realm beyond ordinary reality; differs because "otherworld" is a broad comparative term used of afterlife or underworld realms in many cultures, while "faerie" is specific to the Celtic-influenced fairy world.
  • Fairyland: Similar because both denote the land of fairies; differs because "fairyland" is the modern, more literal compound and can carry a childish tone, while "faerie" is the archaic, literary, and mythically serious form.
  • Enchantment: Similar because both can denote the magical power of the fairy folk; differs because "enchantment" is the abstract state or act of being magically charmed, while "faerie" is the realm and people from which that enchantment issues.
