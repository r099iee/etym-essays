• Vigorously: Similar because both mean with great physical energy and drive; differs because vigorously stresses raw force and action but implies nothing about persistence or a moral posture, whereas strenuously suggests sustained, effortful exertion against resistance.
  • Earnestly: Similar because both mean with serious commitment and sincerity; differs because earnestly appeals to heartfelt conviction rather than effort, while strenuously names the full expenditure of energy.
  • Laboriously: Similar because both mean with great effort; differs because laboriously suggests slow, grinding, even tedious work, whereas strenuously implies active, vigorous, determined exertion.
  • Forcefully: Similar because both mean with strong force; differs because forcefully emphasises the power applied to the outcome, while strenuously focuses on the effort and vigour poured into the act itself.
  • Energetically: Similar because both describe spirited, vigorous action; differs because energetically is the everyday word for lively activity, with none of strenuously’s formal, ceremonious strain.
  • Ardently: Similar because both mean with intense zeal; differs because ardently stresses burning passion and desire, while strenuously stresses disciplined effort and exertion.
  • Intensely: Similar because both mean to a high degree; differs because intensely describes degree of feeling or quality, not necessarily effort, while strenuously always implies exertion and strain.
  • Determinedly: Similar because both mean with resolve; differs because determinedly stresses fixed intention and resolve of purpose, while strenuously stresses the labour and exertion of the doing.
  • Resolutely / Adamantly / Firmly: Similar because all describe an unyielding refusal or stance; differs because these are static—they picture an immovable finality—while strenuously is dynamic, picturing active, effortful pushing against resistance.
  • Assiduously / Diligently / Industriously: Similar because all mean with careful, persistent application; differs because these describe steady, careful, methodical work, whereas strenuously foregrounds vigour and strain rather than care or method.
