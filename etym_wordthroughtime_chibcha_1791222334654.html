- Muisca self-identity - Neutral endonym Muisca (people) and Muysccubun (our speech) for highland farmers and salt traders
- Spanish colonial label - Administrative exonym Chibcha for tribute, mission and chronicle; connotes civilized heathens with gold and salt to be conquered
- Extinct book-language - After 1770 ban, Chibcha becomes a philological relic in grammars and word-lists; nostalgic and scholarly perception
- 19th-c. stock name - Comparative linguists elevate Chibcha to family name for dozens of Isthmo-Colombian languages; neutral scientific term
- Contemporary revival symbol - Positive heritage emblem in Colombia: Muisca councils, school revival, El Dorado myth, and colloquial pride in chibcha culture
