Note: The Sabine origin is explicitly attested by ancient Roman historians. Suetonius (Life of Tiberius 1.2) writes: inter cognomina autem et Neronis assumpsit, quo significatur lingua Sabina fortis ac strenuus (‘among its cognomina it assumed that of Nero, which in the Sabine tongue signifies strong and valiant’). It is cognate with Ancient Greek anḗr (ἀνήρ, stem andr-, ‘man’), Sanskrit nár (‘man, hero’), and Old Irish nert (‘strength’).
