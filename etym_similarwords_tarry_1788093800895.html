• Linger: Similar because both mean staying longer than intended. Differs because linger suggests reluctance to leave and wistful emotion; tarry suggests deliberate, patient waiting.
  • Loiter: Similar because both mean remaining in place idly. Differs because loiter has suspicious, purposeless, even legal overtones (loitering with intent); tarry is respectable and intentional.
  • Dally: Similar because both mean wasting time. Differs because dally is frivolous and flirtatious trifling; tarry is serious and patient.
  • Dawdle: Similar because both mean being slow. Differs because dawdle is about slowness in moving or doing, mildly reproachful; tarry is about staying and waiting.
  • Delay: Similar because both mean to make late or put off. Differs because delay is neutral, often transitive and imposed from outside (delayed by weather); tarry is voluntary and intransitive.
  • Wait: Similar because both mean remaining in expectation. Differs because wait is the everyday word and implies expecting something specific; tarry implies patient duration and an archaic flavour.
  • Stay: Similar because both mean remaining. Differs because stay is broad and neutral, with no sense of extended or deliberate delay.
  • Remain: Similar because both mean continuing in a place. Differs because remain is formal-neutral and carries no temporal colour of delay.
  • Abide: Similar because both mean to stay (literary). Differs because abide often implies permanent or habitual habitation (abide in a land); tarry is temporary waiting.
  • Sojourn: Similar because both mean staying temporarily. Differs because sojourn is a noun-like residence for a defined visit; tarry is a pure verb of waiting.
  • Pause: Similar because both mean stopping briefly. Differs because pause is a short interruption; tarry implies a long duration.
  • Procrastinate: Similar because both mean putting off action. Differs because procrastinate applies to tasks and is self-critical; tarry applies to presence and is patient, even noble.
  • Hang about / hang around: Similar because both mean staying idly. Differs because these are colloquial and aimless; tarry is literary and implies purposeful patience.
  • Rest: Similar because both mean ceasing activity. Differs because rest is for recuperation or sleep; tarry is not relaxing.
