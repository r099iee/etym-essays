Note on Homographic Streams: Sense 1 (botanical branch/twig in the reading context) is an indigenous Old/Middle English Germanic noun derived from PIE **spereg-* ("to burst forth, sprout"). Sense 2 (water droplets/mist) was borrowed centuries later in the 16th century from Middle Dutch/Middle Low German *sproyen* / *sprāyen* ("to sprinkle, spray, disperse liquid"), which ultimately traces back to the related PIE root **sper-* ("to strew, scatter"). The two words converged in spelling and pronunciation in Early Modern English.
