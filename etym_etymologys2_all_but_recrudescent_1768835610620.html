ALL-BUT — ELEMENT 2: BUT

(c. 4500–2500 BCE)

(uncertain)
↓

(be- "by" + ūtan "outside")
↓
English
(13th c.)

"except, only"
↓
English
(compound)
all-but
"very nearly, almost"
Note: the PIE root of all (*h₂el-) is uncertain; but began as a preposition meaning "outside" — "all but" is literally "all except", hence "almost, nearly".
