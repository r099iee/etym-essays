Note: The Proto-Germanic 3rd-person singular present suffix *-aþi evolved into Old English -eþ / -þ. Through regular syncopation and consonant assimilation in Middle English, the disyllabic form haveth contracted into monosyllabic hath, which remained standard in southern England until the northern -s inflection (has) triumphed in the 17th–18th centuries.
