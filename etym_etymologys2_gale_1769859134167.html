Old Norse gala "to sing, crow, chant" ↓ Old Norse galinn "mad, frantic, bewitched" ↓ English (16th c.) gale "a very strong wind"
Note: origin uncertain. The leading theory borrows it from Old Norse galinn 'mad, frantic' (the wind conceived of as 'mad'), related to Norwegian galen 'bad, mad'; but no direct Old/Middle English continuation is attested, so a Scandinavian origin stays unproven.
