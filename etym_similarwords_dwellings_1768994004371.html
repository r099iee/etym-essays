• Home: Similar because both are places where one lives; differs because home is emotional and possessive, warm and centred on belonging, while a dwelling is cold by comparison — a mere place of residence.
  • Residence: Similar because both name a place of settled living; differs because residence is formal and official, suggesting status and permanence, while dwelling is plainer and can be any shelter where beings live, from a house to a cave.
  • Habitation: Similar because both name the fact and place of being inhabited; differs because habitation is the archaeological and technical word, stressing occupation and often abstract, while a dwelling is a concrete structure where someone lives.
  • Abode: Similar because both name a place where one dwells; differs because abode is literary and somewhat archaic, chosen for atmosphere, while dwelling is the everyday functional noun of habitation.
  • House: Similar because both name a structure where people live; differs because house names the physical structure, while dwelling names the act of living in it — and so dwelling can stretch to cliff dwellings or Martian dwellings that no house could fit.
  • Domicile: Similar because both name a place of residence; differs because domicile is the legal and technical word, tied to jurisdiction and official records, while dwelling is the ordinary noun of settlement.
  • Shelter: Similar because both can name a place that houses living beings; differs because shelter stresses protection from the elements or danger and need not be permanent, while a dwelling implies an ongoing act of living in a place.
  • Lodgings / quarters: Similar because both name places where people stay; differs because lodgings and quarters are temporary, rented, or assigned accommodation, while a dwelling is where one settles and resides.
  • Den / lair / nest: Similar because all name animal homes; differs because they belong to beasts, not beings presumed to have homes in the human sense — precisely the contrast the passage draws between "animal dens" and "Martian dwellings."
