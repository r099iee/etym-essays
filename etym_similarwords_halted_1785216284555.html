• stopped: Similar in the shared core of ceasing to move; differs because it is the neutral general term and may allow a gradual slowdown, whereas halted is abrupt and commanded.
  • paused: Similar in a suspension of movement; differs because a pause is brief, temporary, and easily reversed, without halted’s tension and decisiveness.
  • froze: Similar in sudden immobilization; differs because freezing is involuntary — the body stops through fear or shock rather than by an act of will — losing halted’s deliberate discipline.
  • stood still: Similar in being without motion; differs because it describes a state of stillness already achieved, not the decisive act of arriving at it.
  • came to a standstill: Similar in full cessation; differs because it belongs to machines, traffic, and processes grinding down, not to a living body stopping itself at will.
  • arrested: Similar in a sudden, complete stop; differs because arrest is formal, legal, or technical (arrested development), carrying a passive, imposed quality absent from halted.
  • ceased: Similar in coming to an end; differs because ceasing applies to activities and processes ending over time, not to a body freezing in mid-motion.
  • discontinued: Similar in bringing something to a stop; differs because discontinuing is a deliberate termination of an ongoing activity or product line, not an instant physical stop.
  • suspended: Similar in stopping temporarily; differs because suspension implies a holding in abeyance that may be lifted, while halting is a decisive stop.
  • checked: Similar in restraining or stopping motion; differs because to check is to slow or curb (often briefly), a restraint of momentum rather than a clean full stop.
  • pulled up: Similar in coming to a stop; differs because pulling up is the informal idiom of horses and vehicles reining in, without the military formality of halted.
  • drew up: Similar in arriving at a stop; differs because drawing up suggests a vehicle coming to rest at a point of arrival, lacking halted’s sudden, commanded quality.
