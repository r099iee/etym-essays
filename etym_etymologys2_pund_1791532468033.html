Note: the Latin loan into Germanic is early and its exact route is not recorded; Latin o became Germanic u and the case ending was lost. Modern English pound continues this form, with Middle English diphthongisation of /u/ before /nd/ and the later Great Vowel Shift. The verb to pound and the noun pound "enclosure" are unrelated homographs.
