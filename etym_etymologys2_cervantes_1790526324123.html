Cervantes

Etymological Note: The surname Cervantes is habitational in origin, derived from the mountainous municipality of Cervantes in the province of Lugo, Galicia (Northwestern Spain), or related Castilian localities named after stag habitats (Latin cervus, from PIE *ḱerh₂-, "horn"). An alternative, minority philological hypothesis connects the toponym to pre-Roman Iberian roots referring to rocky heights (*serb- / *cerb-), though the zoological derivation from cervus remains the standard consensus among Iberian onomastic scholars.
