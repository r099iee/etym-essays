Gaulish (Continental Celtic) (c. 3rd c. BCE)
*Parisiī
"the Parisii tribe (possibly 'the craftsmen' or 'the boat-people')"
Note: Gaulish tribal ethnonym; Celticists connect it to Celtic *kʷar- 'to make/craft' or *par- 'boat'.

Classical Latin (c. 1st c. BCE – 4th c. CE)
Parisiī / Lutetia Parisiorum

Late Latin (c. 4th c. CE)
Parisium / Parisii
"the city of Paris (replacing Lutetia)"
