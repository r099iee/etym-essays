- 1st c. Pliny's boa - huge Calabrian serpent, half-hearsay; learned and marvellous
- 1758 Linnaean Boa constrictor - American genus and species; taxonomic and colonial
- Early 19th c. menagerie monster - alive! twenty feet! crushing goats; sensational and popular
- 1820s-1880s domestic simile - hoses, pipes, and feather stoles like boas; visual and Victorian
- Modern pet and metaphor - red-tails in vivaria, debts and monopolies as constrictors; familiar and figurative
- Modern physiology corrected - death by circulatory arrest, not bone-crushing; scientific and precise
