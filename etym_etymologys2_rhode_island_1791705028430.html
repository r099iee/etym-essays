Note: Two converging historical theories explain the name: (1) In 1524, Giovanni da Verrazzano compared an island near Narragansett Bay to the Greek island of Rhodes (Ancient Greek Ῥόδος); (2) In 1614, Dutch explorer Adriaen Block described the clay cliffs of Aquidneck as 't Roode Eylandt ('the red island', from Dutch rood 'red'). In 1644, the colonial court officially adopted the spelling Rhode-Island.
