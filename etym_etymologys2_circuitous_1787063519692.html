Note on debate: Greek kýklos is the usual link between the PIE root *kʷel- “to turn” and Latin circus “ring”; Latin borrowed the word from the Greek sphere (possibly via Etruscan) for its race-tracks and circles. The formation of circuitus is uncontroversial (circum + īre). Some scholars reconstruct *kʷel- with a laryngeal variant *kʷelh₁-; the exact grade is debated, but the meaning “to turn, to move around” is secure.
