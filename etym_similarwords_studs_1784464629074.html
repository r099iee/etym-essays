• Buttons: Similar because they are clothing fasteners; differs because a button is usually sewn on and purely functional, while a stud is a removable decorative fastener with a shank and eyelet.
  • Cufflinks: Similar because they are decorative fasteners of formal dress; differs because a cufflink joins two halves through a double cuff, while a stud is the single shank fastener for shirt fronts and collars.
  • Brooches: Similar because they are decorative fasteners; differs because a brooch pins fabric openly from the front, while a stud passes through an eyelet and locks at the back.
  • Clasps: Similar because they secure things; differs because a clasp joins two separate parts together, while a stud is the knob fastener that holds a collar or shirt in place.
  • Pins: Similar because they are small pointed fasteners; differs because a pin pierces and holds by point pressure, while a stud locks through an eyelet with a back-piece.
  • Bosses: Similar because they are projecting ornamental knobs; differs because a boss is a fixed raised ornament on metal or leather, while a stud is also a removable clothing fastener.
  • Knobs: Similar because they are small round projections; differs because a knob is a projecting handle or lump, while a stud specifically fastens and can be detached.
  • Fasteners: Similar because they secure clothing or materials; differs because fastener is the generic class term, while a stud is a specific removable knob-type fastener.
