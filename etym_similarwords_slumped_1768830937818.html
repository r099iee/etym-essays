• Collapsed: Similar because both mean falling or giving way suddenly and heavily; differs because collapsed implies complete disintegration or caving in (a building, a system, a person's health), while slumped is a heavy sagging drop that may not be total.
  • Sank: Similar because both mean dropping downward; differs because sank is gradual or through a medium (sank into the mud, a ship sank), while slumped is sudden, heavy, and graceless.
  • Sagged: Similar because both describe drooping under weight; differs because sagged is a slow, partial bowing downward (a sagging shelf, sagging spirits), while slumped is a sudden, more dramatic collapse.
  • Drooped: Similar because both mean bending or hanging down; differs because drooped is gentle and gradual (drooping flowers, drooping shoulders) and lacks the heaviness and suddenness of slumped.
  • Caved (in): Similar because both mean giving way under pressure; differs because caved implies a hollow structure collapsing inward, while slumped is a body or mass sagging under its own weight.
  • Crumpled: Similar because both describe a body giving way and folding up; differs because crumpled implies folding and creasing like paper, while slumped is a single heavy drop without folding.
  • Pitched: Similar because both mean falling suddenly; differs because pitched implies being thrown or tossed forward (pitched forward, pitched overboard), while slumped is a limp, heavy sinking rather than a throw.
  • Tumbled: Similar because both mean falling; differs because tumbled implies rolling or turning over repeatedly, while slumped is a single heavy, graceless drop.
  • Subsided: Similar because both mean sinking downward; differs because subsided is slower and geological (the ground subsided, the flood subsided), while slumped is sudden bodily collapse.
  • Flopped: Similar because both describe a limp, graceless fall; differs because flopped is informal and implies flailing or splatting (flopped onto the bed), while slumped is heavy and inert.
  • Wilted: Similar because both describe loss of vigor and erectness; differs because wilted is gradual (a wilting plant, wilting resolve) and lighter, while slumped is sudden and heavy.
  • Folded: Similar because both can mean a body giving way (his legs folded); differs because folded implies bending at the joints, while slumped is a whole sagging collapse.
  • Gave way: Similar because both mean losing support and falling; differs because "gave way" is general and can be gradual or sudden, while slumped specifies the heavy, limp quality.
