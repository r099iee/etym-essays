• Modestly: Similar because both describe unassuming, humble self-presentation; differs because modestly carries no hint of disapproval or criticism, while deprecatingly may include gentle reproach.
  • Humbly: Similar because both lower self-importance; differs because humbly stresses submissive deference to authority or fortune, while deprecatingly may be playful or ironic.
  • Self-deprecatingly: Similar because this is the core modern sense of the word; differs because self-deprecatingly specifies the target (oneself), while deprecatingly can also be aimed at external things or expressed as mild protest.
  • Apologetically: Similar because both soften a statement with regret or concession; differs because apologetically expresses regret for an offence or failure, whereas deprecatingly expresses disparagement or modest dismissal.
  • Disapprovingly: Similar because both can express objection; differs because disapprovingly is direct and critical with no self-effacement, while deprecatingly softens the criticism and often turns it inward.
  • Disparagingly: Similar because both belittle or lower worth; differs because disparagingly is aggressive and damaging to its target, while deprecatingly is gentle, apologetic, and usually self-directed.
  • Derogatorily: Similar because both detract from value or dignity; differs because derogatory is openly insulting and carries a formal, almost legalistic flavour, while deprecatingly is understated and polite.
  • Contemptuously: Similar because both look down on something; differs because contempt is haughty and hostile, whereas deprecatingly is self-lowering and apologetic.
  • Belittlingly: Similar because both diminish worth; differs because belittlingly is condescending and aimed at others, not humble or self-directed.
  • Hesitantly: Similar because both show reluctance to assert oneself; differs because hesitantly signals uncertainty or fear, not humility or mild reproach.
  • Sarcastically: Similar in ironic self-diminishment; differs because sarcasm intends mockery and is sharp-edged, while deprecatingly is soft and polite.
  • Self-effacingly: Similar because both remove the self from the centre of attention; differs because self-effacingly stresses shrinking from notice with no implication of objection or reproach.
