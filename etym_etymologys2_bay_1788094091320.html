NOTE: No PIE root exists for this chain: the Late Latin word is of unknown, probably non-Indo-European (Mediterranean substrate) origin, and any reconstruction would be invention. The origin of baia is debated: (a) from Latin Baiae, the famed bathing resort on the Bay of Naples — the semantic leap "resort town" → "any bay" is possible but unproven; (b) from a pre-Roman Iberian or Ligurian substrate word (cf. Spanish bahía, Portuguese baía); (c) from the "gape" verb batare — the sea "opening" into the land — attractive and certainly the source of the separate architectural "bay" (Old French baee "opening"), but unproven for the water sense. The chain above follows the attested record and stops where certainty stops.
