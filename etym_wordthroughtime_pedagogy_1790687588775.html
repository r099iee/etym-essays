- Slave’s escort duty, humble - in classical Athens the paidagōgós is a servant walking boys to school; connotation servile and custodial, no scholarly prestige.
- Classroom drudge, comic - in Rome and early modern Europe the pedagogue becomes the schoolmaster as butt of jokes; perception petty, rod-wielding, pedantic.
- Humanist art, earnest - from Erasmus through Comenius teaching is dignified as formation of princes and citizens; connotation serious, moral, reforming.
- Science of education, professional - in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries pedagogy gains chairs, journals, and degrees; perception expert, progressive, institutional.
- Liberatory craft, hopeful - from Freire onward pedagogies pluralise into movements for justice; connotation emancipatory, reflective, the guide recast as ally rather than overseer.
