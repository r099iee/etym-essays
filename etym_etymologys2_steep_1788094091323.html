NOTE: The pre-Germanic stage is debated: the connecting idea is “projecting, jutting up” (cf. Middle Low German stōp “projecting, protruding”), and Watkins/Etymonline derive the family from PIE *steu- “to push, stick, knock,” but the Oxford English Dictionary marks the further etymology of Germanic *staupa- as uncertain. The homograph verb steep “to soak” (Old English stēpan) is a distinct word, possibly built on the same PIE base through the sense “to plunge, dip.”
