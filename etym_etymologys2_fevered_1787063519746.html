NOTE: The PIE origin of Latin febris is debated. Some scholars connect febris to the Latin verb ferveō "to boil, to be hot" and thus to a PIE root meaning "to boil, to seethe" (fever as the body boiling); others (notably de Vaan) derive it from PIE *dʰegʷh- "to burn", making febris literally "the burning". Both theories agree that the fever was conceived as an inner heat. English borrowed the noun from Latin already in the Old English period.
