Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE - 200 CE)
*tipp-
"to tap, touch lightly"
[onomatopoeic root for light contact]
↓
Low German (c. 1200 CE)
tippen
"to tap, touch lightly"
[attested in various Germanic dialects]
↓
English (16th Century)
tip (v.1)
"to strike lightly, tap"
[first recorded in English with this sense]
↓
English (Late 16th Century)
tip (v.3)
"to overturn, tilt, cause to lean or fall"
[semantic extension from light touch to causing instability/movement; origin debated, possibly related to *top* or *tup*]
