candēre

↓

candēla

↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
candelābrum
"a candlestick"
↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
candelābra
"candlesticks, branched candle holders"
↓
Modern English (19th c.)
candelabra
"a large branched candlestick"

Debate: the PIE root *kand- "to shine, glow" is uncertain, and candēre may be of non-PIE origin. English candelabra is a Latin plural now often used as a singular.
