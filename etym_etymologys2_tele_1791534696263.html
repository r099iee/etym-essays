Note: Ancient Greek τῆλε (tēle) is an old locative fossil from a nominal stem related to τέλος (telos) "end, goal, border", ultimately derived from PIE *kʷel-es-. It must be carefully distinguished in modern English from combining forms derived from telos (such as teleology, the philosophical study of purpose and ends).
