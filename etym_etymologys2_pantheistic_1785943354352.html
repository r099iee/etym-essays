Note: Coined 1705 by John Toland as pantheist from pan- + theós. The PIE origin of Greek theós is disputed: the traditional link to *dhes- (cf. Latin fānum, fēriae) is not universally accepted, and Beekes suggests a Pre-Greek origin. Greek pâs likewise has no secure etymology.
