• Relate: Similar because both mean to tell a story; differs because relate tends toward the personal and spoken (relating an anecdote at table) and lacks the deliberate, ordered completeness that recount implies.
  • Narrate: Similar because both mean to tell a connected story; differs because narrate is the cooler, professional term of the theorist or of the narrative craftsman, while recount is warmer and more solemn and can carry a moral or political programme.
  • Recite: Similar because both mean to produce words aloud; differs because recite implies reproduction from memory, verbatim and often in public (reciting a poem), while recount is an ordered telling in one's own words.
  • Tell: Similar because both are verbs of communicating a story; differs because tell is the plain, everyday word, casual and oral, with no commitment to order or completeness.
  • Describe: Similar because both give an account of something; differs because describe focuses on visual and sensory detail rather than on the ordered sequence of events that recount promises.
  • Enumerate / detail: Similar because both suggest an item-by-item account; differs because enumerate stresses the bare listing of items (the counting side of the family), while recount integrates the items into a connected narrative.
