Root word 1: peri (περί) "around, beyond"

PIE(reconstructed, 4th–3rd millennium BCE)
*per(i)
"through, across, beyond; exceedingly"
(cf. Lat. per, Skt. pári, Germ. ver-, Eng. far)
↓
Ancient Greek(attested 8th c. BCE, Homer)
περί (peri)
"around, about; beyond measure"
