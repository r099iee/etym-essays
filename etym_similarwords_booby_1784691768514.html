• Simpleton: Similar because both name a foolish, gullible person; differs because simpleton stresses a mild, innocent lack of intelligence, while booby stresses clumsy blundering and is more playful and bird-like.
  • Dunce: Similar because both name a slow, foolish person; differs because dunce has scholastic origins (the followers of Duns Scotus) and names one slow to learn, while booby is about general clumsy foolishness.
  • Fool: Similar because both name a person lacking sense; differs because fool is the broadest and oldest term, covering all degrees, while booby is lighter, more comic, and distinctly childish in register.
  • Blockhead: Similar because both are insults for a stupid person; differs because blockhead imagines the head as a solid, impenetrable block — a harder, more contemptuous insult — while booby is lighter and more mock-affectionate.
  • Numskull: Similar because both are folk-metaphor insults for a dull person; differs because numskull pictures a “numb skull” (an empty or senseless head) and is blunter, while booby is the word of the nursery and schoolyard.
  • Nincompoop: Similar because both are jocular words for a foolish person; differs because nincompoop is a fanciful, unexplained coinage of comic sound, while booby has the documented seabird origin and the compounds booby-trap and booby prize.
  • Halfwit / Nitwit / Dimwit: Similar because all name a person of limited intelligence; differs because the -wit words measure intelligence directly and can sound clinical or cruel, while booby emphasises clumsy, laughable behaviour rather than measured intelligence.
  • Ninny: Similar because both are childish, comic words for a fool; differs because ninny is built on an innocent’s name (from “an innocent”) and is milder still, while booby carries the seabird and trap imagery.
