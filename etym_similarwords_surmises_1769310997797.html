• Conjectures: Similar because both are unproven conclusions; differs because conjectures are formed by piecing together scattered evidence and reasoning, while surmises are lighter, more intuitive guesses.
  • Suppositions: Similar because both are assumptions without certain proof; differs because suppositions are set down as a base for argument, while surmises are tentative guesses cast at the facts.
  • Speculations: Similar because both are thoughts beyond the facts; differs because speculations explore possibilities at length (and in finance, risk), while surmises are single, cautious guesses.
  • Guesses: Similar because both are answers given without evidence; differs because guesses are everyday and can be careless, while surmises are literary and imply a thoughtful, hesitant inference.
  • Hunches: Similar because both are unproven feelings about something; differs because hunches are purely instinctive, while surmises rest on a few perceived clues.
  • Suspicions: Similar because both are doubtful impressions; differs because suspicions imply distrust or suspected wrongdoing, while surmises are neutral conjectures.
  • Assumptions: Similar because both are things taken as true; differs because assumptions are usually taken for granted unreflectively, while surmises are conscious, tentative projections.
  • Presumptions: Similar because both are beliefs formed without full proof; differs because presumptions rely on probability, precedent, or legal standing, while surmises do not.
  • Hypotheses: Similar because both are proposed explanations; differs because hypotheses are formal, testable propositions in science and logic, while surmises are informal guesses.
  • Notions: Similar because both are vague ideas; differs because notions are broad and unfocused, while surmises are directed at a specific truth.
  • Inklings: Similar because both are faint hints of something; differs because inklings are mere traces of awareness, while surmises are fuller, though still unproven, conjectures.
  • Fancies: Similar because both are imaginative projections; differs because fancies are whimsical and playful, while surmises claim some grounding in clues.
