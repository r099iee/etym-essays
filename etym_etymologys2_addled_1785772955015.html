Debate: no PIE ancestor can be reconstructed; the word is only Germanic (cf. East Frisian adel "dung", Middle Low German adel "mud", Dutch aal "puddle"). The phrase addle egg is a loan-translation of Latin ovum urinum "urine egg", itself an erroneous calque of Greek ourión ōion "wind egg". The sense "confused, muddled" (1706) arose from the rotten-egg sense.
