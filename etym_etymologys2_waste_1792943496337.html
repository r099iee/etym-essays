PIE (reconstructed, c. 4000 BCE)
*h₁weh₂-
‘empty, lacking, abandoned’
↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
vastus
‘empty, desolate, vast’
↓
Old Northern French (11th c.)
wast
‘desolate, untilled’
↓
Middle English (13th c.)
wast, waste
‘wild land; to ravage’
↓
Modern English (current)
waste
‘barren land; refuse’

Note: Latin vastus is securely the parent of French wast, but its deeper PIE pedigree is debated — de Vaan treats the connection to *h₁weh₂- and kin like vanus as plausible rather than certain.
