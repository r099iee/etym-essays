- Pre-record obscure water-name - burn-name of unknown (possibly Pictish or Gaelic) origin; local, hydronymic, unexplained
- 1700s mapped Farg Water - military and estate maps fix the burn's name; cartographic, coaching, turnpike
- 1820s child's Glendearg - boy reading Scott rhymes Farg with fictional dearg; romantic, confused, consecrated
- Victorian railway Glenfarg - viaducts and station spread the name; engineering, timetabled, famous
- 1970s motorway glen - M90 buries track but keeps signs; fast, commuter, overlooked
- Present walkers' valley - reservoir, woods, dippers; recreational, conserved, still haunted by the White Lady
