- Medieval siege and building bar (500-1500) - Guild craft and war engine; handspike and crowbar as mason's and gunner's mate, workmanlike respect.

- Mid-century slot-machine arm (1900-1980) - Playful chance; one-armed bandit lever as hope gesture.
