Note: The OED judges the English verb to be of probable Scandinavian origin; the PIE root *swer- is widely accepted (Etymonline), but the intermediate Germanic form is not directly attested, so the Scandinavian stage is inferred from dialect cognates such as Norwegian svirla and Dutch zwirrelen. This gap is real and is flagged rather than filled.
