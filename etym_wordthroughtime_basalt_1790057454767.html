- Greek basanos touchstone - dark stone testing gold by its streak; test, then torment - craft-judicial, grave
- Hellenistic basanites hard dark stone - Egyptian/Ethiopian statues and mortars - exotic-luxury, imperial
- Pliny's basaltes (1st c. CE) - misspelt learned name for hard black stone, copied for 1400 years - bookish, erroneous yet authoritative
- c.1600 English basalt - Saxon mines to Antrim cliffs, any hard black rock - mineralogical, descriptive
- Late 1700s Basalt Controversy - Neptunists (ocean precipitate) vs Plutonists (frozen lava); lava wins, geology founded - scientific-heroic, fiercely argued
- 1800s scenic and imperial basalt - columnar causeways, Deccan Traps, breakwaters and setts; islands announce themselves in black walls - romantic-industrial, your sentence's register
- Modern basalt - ocean crust, lunar maria, Martian plains, aggregate and fibre - planetary-technical, ubiquitous
