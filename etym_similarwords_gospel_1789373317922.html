• Scripture: Similar because both refer to sacred, authoritative religious texts containing divine truth and moral guidance; differs because scripture refers broadly to the entire written body of holy writings recognized by a religious faith (including the Old Testament, the Quran, or the Vedas), whereas gospel refers specifically to the Christian proclamation of salvation through Christ, the four Evangelists' accounts, or a singular transformative text.
  • Doctrine: Similar because both represent foundational teachings and authoritative systems of belief; differs because doctrine refers to the formalized, intellectual, and codified theological or political tenets established by an institution, whereas gospel conveys a living, impassioned message of good news, redemption, and spiritual awakening.
  • Creed: Similar because both articulate essential articles of religious or ideological faith; differs because a creed is a concise, formulaic, and structured confession of dogma (such as the Apostles' Creed or Nicene Creed) recited liturgically, whereas gospel is an expansive, living narrative and revelatory message.
  • Dogma: Similar because both can refer colloquially to an unquestioned truth held with absolute conviction; differs because dogma carries a prominent pejorative modern connotation of rigid, authoritarian, and uncritical enforcement by power, whereas gospel retains connotations of life-giving hope, spiritual illumination, and profound inspiration.
  • Manifesto: Similar because both declare a radical, revolutionary vision designed to convert hearers and reshape human society; differs because a manifesto is an explicitly political, economic, or artistic declaration of programmatic action (e.g., *The Communist Manifesto*), whereas gospel carries sacred existential gravity, metaphysical depth, and spiritual redemption.
  • Revelation: Similar because both signify the disclosure of ultimate or divine truth; differs because revelation focuses on the visionary act or process whereby hidden divine mysteries are unveiled to human prophets, whereas gospel denotes the concrete historical tidings, teachings, and narratives proclaimed to redeem humanity.
