• Helmets: Similar because both denote protective headgear; differs because helmet is the modern everyday word, while helm is archaic or literary and can also mean protection generally.
  • Headpieces: Similar because both are head coverings; differs because headpiece is generic and not specifically protective combat armor.
  • Crests: Similar because both relate to the head; differs because a crest is an ornamental emblem, family badge, or bird tuft, not protective armor.
  • Visors: Similar because both are parts of head armor; differs because a visor is only the movable front face-piece of a helmet, not the whole helmet.
  • Casques: Similar because both mean a helmet, especially a historical one; differs because casque is a specialized or technical term for ancient helmets or anatomical structures, and has no steering sense.
  • Basinets: Similar because both are medieval head armor; differs because a basinet is a specific helmet type with a pointed top, not a general synonym.
  • Tiller: Similar because both can denote the ship's steering gear; differs because tiller is the specific nautical bar for steering, while helm is the broader mechanism or position.
  • Guard: Similar because both suggest protection; differs because guard is generic (protection or watching) and covers the body, while helm specifically covers the head or means command.
