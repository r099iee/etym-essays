- 14th c. - Entered Middle English (*darten*) from Old French (*darter*), initially meaning to pierce with a javelin or cast a missile.
- 15th–16th c. - Shifted into an intransitive verb of motion, describing moving with the speed and agility of a thrown missile.
- 16th–17th c. - Expanded into visual and psychological realms (Shakespeare), describing *darting glances* or lightning flashing from clouds.
- 18th–19th c. - Established in natural history writing for the rapid, zig-zagging movements of birds, fish, and insects.
- 20th–21st c. - Broadened to include tranquilizer projectile firing (veterinary medicine) while remaining the standard term for swift, agile sprints.
