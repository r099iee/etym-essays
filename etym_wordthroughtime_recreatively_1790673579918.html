- A Roman’s refreshment - recreare as revival after fatigue, mind and body restored; wholly restorative, no epistemology
- A Christian’s leisure - medieval recreatio as licit rest from study, walks and games that re-create strength for prayer
- A humanist’s restoration - Renaissance recreate as to revive letters, constitutions, health; making whole again
- A modernist’s remaking - 19th–20th c. re-create as create anew, translations and stagings that remake originals; aesthetics absorbs the prefix
- A constructivist’s perception - contemporary cognitive sense in the source sentence, perceiving as composing; the mind’s workshop replaces the mirror
