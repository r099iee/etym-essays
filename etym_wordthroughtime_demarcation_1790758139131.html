- 1490s–1600s: Spanish imperial and papal treaty term (*la línea de demarcación*) dividing global spheres of exploration between Spain and Portugal.
- 1720s–1850s: Adopted into English diplomacy and cartography for establishing physical borders and frontier posts - Formal treaty and surveying term.
- 1900s–1950s: Specialized into British industrial relations and trade union 'demarcation disputes' regarding trade jurisdiction.
- 1930s–Present: Cemented in the philosophy of science via Karl Popper's 'demarcation problem' (science vs. pseudoscience) and academic stylistics.
