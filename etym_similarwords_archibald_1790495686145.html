• Archie / Archy: Similar because they are the familiar short forms of the same name; differs because Archie is intimate and modern, while Archibald is formal and historic.
  • Archambault: Similar because it is the Old French source-form; differs because Archambault is French surname and place (Quebec), while Archibald is the Anglicised Christian name.
  • Theobald: Similar because it shares the second element -bald meaning bold; differs because Theobald means people-bold, while Archibald means precious-bold.
  • Leopold: Similar because it is another bald-name (people-bold) of German stock; differs because Leopold is continental-royal (Belgian kings), while Archibald is Scottish-baronial.
  • Baldwin: Similar because it joins bald (bold) to wine (friend); differs because Baldwin means bold-friend and belongs to Flanders and Jerusalem, while Archibald belongs to the Borders.
  • Douglas: Similar because Ruskin pairs Archibald with Douglas as one lord; differs because Douglas is the surname and house, while Archibald is the Christian name borne by its earls.
  • Angus (Earl of Angus): Similar because Archibald of Angus names the Red Douglas earldom; differs because Angus is the title and territory, while Archibald is the man holding it.
  • Tantallon: Similar because it is Archibald's castle in the source sentence; differs because Tantallon is the stones, while Archibald is the lord missing from them.
  • Bell-the-Cat: Similar because it is the byname of Archibald, 5th Earl of Angus; differs because Bell-the-Cat names one deed (hanging Cochrane), while Archibald names the man lifelong.
  • Argyll (Archibald Campbell): Similar because Campbell earls repeat the same Christian name; differs because Argyll Archibalds are western Highland power, while Angus Archibalds are eastern border power.
