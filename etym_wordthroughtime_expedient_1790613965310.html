- Roman expedire to free feet from snare, make ready for marching - practical military perception of disentanglement and speed
- Late Latin expediens useful, profitable - generalized perception of whatever clears the way
- Medieval scholastic expedient as what conduces to salvation or governance - moral-theological perception, still largely positive
- Modern English expedient as convenient but dubious - split perception: prudent statesmanship versus unprincipled opportunism and satirical makeshift
