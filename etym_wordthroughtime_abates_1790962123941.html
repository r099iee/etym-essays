- 13th–14th c. - Literal violent physical destruction: felling trees, knocking down ramparts, or striking down foes in martial combat.
- 14th–15th c. - Anglo-Norman legal codification: striking out writs, nullifying improper court actions, and forcibly removing physical nuisances.
- 16th–17th c. - Renaissance literary expansion: softening of intense human emotions, tempests, pride, and raging fevers in dramatic prose.
- 18th–19th c. - Maritime, meteorological, and economic entrenchment: standardized nautical descriptions of dying gales and mercantile discounts.
- 20th–21st c. - Modern technical, medical, environmental, and legal refinement: pain alleviation, carbon/noise abatement policy, and tax concessions.
