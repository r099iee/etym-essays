• Persists: Similar because both mean continuing firmly; differs because persists is neutral or admiring continuance toward a goal, while perseverates is stuck repetition past usefulness, with a clinical colour.
  • Repeats: Similar because both describe saying or doing again; differs because repeats is general and neutral, while perseverates specifies inappropriate, involuntary, or purposeless repetition.
  • Loops: Similar because both evoke going round the same track; differs because loops is vivid and modern, said of thoughts, tapes, and GIFs, while perseverates is the technical term of psychology.
  • Fixates: Similar because both describe the mind stuck on one thing; differs because fixates stresses the locked attention itself, often with desire or dread, while perseverates stresses the repeated output the fixation produces.
  • Ruminates: Similar because both name thoughts chewed over and over; differs because ruminates is slow brooding, often melancholy, while perseverates is mechanical sticking, often neurological.
  • Echoes: Similar because both repeat what was just said or done; differs because echoes mirrors another's words, often faithfully, while perseverates replays one's own response when a fresh one is wanted.
  • Perseveres: Similar because the two verbs share the Latin root of persistence; differs because perseveres honours admirable endurance, while perseverates diagnoses repetition that has lost its purpose.
  • Stagnates: Similar because both describe failure to move forward; differs because stagnates is passive standstill, like pond water, while perseverates is active repetition, the same stroke swum again.
