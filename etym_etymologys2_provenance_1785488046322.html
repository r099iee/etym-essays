PROVENANCE
"The place of origin, source, or
documented history of something (here,
of quoted texts); the record of where
something comes from."
Note: prōvenīre and the macronless prōvenire are one and the same verb; the French noun provenance derives from the present participle provenant of provenir "to come forth," not directly from the infinitive.
