NOTE: Two independent roots converge on the spelling STOLE. For the verb, the modern vowel /əʊ/ in "stole" reflects a documented morphological levelling: the ō-form of the Old English past plural and past participle spread to the past tense (compare "bore" from "bear"), rather than a direct descent from the past singular stæl. The PIE base is standardly reconstructed as *stel-/ *ster- "to steal", though a connection to *stel- "to place, set" (stealing = carrying away secretly) has also been proposed. The noun stole descends from a completely separate Greek-Latin line.
