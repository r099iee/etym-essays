- 1640s learned borrowing - quadruped as natural-history term for four-footed beast; scholarly, Latinate prestige
- 1700s Linnaean class - Quadrupedia as order of animals; systematic, museum-cabinet perception
- 1800s evolutionary word - quadruped vs biped becomes evidence of descent; Darwinian overtone, man as modified quadruped
- Early 1900s literary irony - chattering quadrupeds as satirical equals/rivals of man; misanthropic, apocalyptic tone
- Contemporary technical revival - quadruped gait, quadruped robot; neutral biomechanical and engineering term, positive for stability
