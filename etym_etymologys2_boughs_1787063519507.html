Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*bʰā́ǵʰus
"arm, shoulder, foreleg"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*bōguz
"shoulder, upper arm, foreleg; tree limb"
Note: Developed dual anatomical and arboreal sense in Northern Europe.
↓
Old English (c. 8th c. CE)
bōg / bōh
"shoulder, arm, bough of a tree"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–15th c.)
bough / bowe / bogh
"main branch of a tree, tree limb"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present)
boughs
"large primary branches of a tree"
