Debate note: The origin of sturdy is uncertain. The traditional chain estourdi ← *exturdīre ← turdus "thrush" (the thrush being thought dazed) is widely repeated but doubted, since *exturdīre is unattested and may be an invention; other proposals link Old French estourdir to Latin sturnus "starling" or to a Germanic root. The sense shift from "dazed" to "bold, strong" (16th c.) is likewise debated.
