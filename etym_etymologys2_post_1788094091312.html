Note: the three chains are historically unconnected — the single English form POST is a triple convergence of Latin postis "doorpost", Latin posita "placed" (via the Italian relay-station word), and the Latin preposition post "after". The traditional PIE analysis of postis (*por- "before" + *steh₂- "to stand") is plausible but debated, and the Latin verb sinere behind ponere has no securely recoverable PIE root; where the record stops, this chain stops with an explicit statement rather than an invented stage.
