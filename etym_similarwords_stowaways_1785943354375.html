• Passage-stealer: Similar because both name someone who travels without paying or without entitlement; differs because passage-stealer is a descriptive, non-technical phrase, while stowaway is the fixed legal and nautical noun.
  • Fugitive: Similar because both may hide aboard transport to escape; differs because a fugitive is defined by fleeing law enforcement or capture, whereas a stowaway is defined by the covert method of travel itself, whatever the motive.
  • Intruder: Similar because both are unauthorized presences; differs because an intruder breaks into a building or enclosed space, while a stowaway specifically conceals himself inside a moving vehicle's cargo or workings.
  • Trespasser: Similar because both are illegally present on someone else's property or conveyance; differs because a trespasser steps onto private land, whereas a stowaway boards a moving conveyance covertly — the act is about passage, not land.
  • Hitchhiker: Similar because both obtain transport without paying for it; differs because a hitchhiker travels openly, by request and by the roadside, while a stowaway travels hidden and unauthorized.
  • Free-rider / freeloader: Similar because both take a benefit without bearing the cost; differs because free-rider is a moral or economic term for one who benefits from a collective good without contributing, carrying no implication of concealment aboard a vehicle.
  • Refugee / asylum seeker: Similar because many stowaways are in fact refugees fleeing danger; differs because refugee and asylum seeker are formal legal statuses conferred by law, while stowaway names only the mode of travel and carries no protected status of its own.
  • Clandestine passenger / illegal passenger: Similar because both name covert, unauthorized travellers; differs because these are administrative phrasings used in shipping manifests and port reports, less fixed and idiomatic than the single noun stowaway.
  • Parasite: Similar as a metaphoric label for one who takes without giving; differs because parasite is a biological insult implying exploitative dependence, far more condemnatory than the neutral-to-romantic stowaway.
