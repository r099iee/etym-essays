Note (debated): the PIE root is usually reconstructed as *plāk- "flat" (cf. Greek pláx "flat surface"), but the laryngeal detailing (*pleh₂-k-) is conjectural; the chain "flat/smooth → calm → pleasing" is widely accepted yet not directly attested, and a minority treats placēre as an independent formation. The Latin link placēre → placidus is secure.
