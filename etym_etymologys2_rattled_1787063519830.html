Note: The word is onomatopoeic — its sound imitates its sense — and similar forms occur across Germanic languages (German rasseln, Dutch ratelen), so no single root language can be privileged and no PIE root is reconstructible. The Middle English form was reinforced by Middle Dutch/Low German loans.
