PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*gʰan-dʰ- / *gā-
"to yawn, chatter, screech" (or substrate / Old Persian *ganza- "treasure")
Note: The coin name gazeta derives either from Venetian gaza ("magpie", from Late Latin gāia, imitative of chattering; alluding to the magpie stamped on the coin or its chatter) or directly from Latin gāza ("royal treasury", borrowed from Old Persian ganza-). Romance lexicographers strongly favor the magpie diminutive derivation.
