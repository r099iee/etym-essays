- Mythological Figure (Ancient Greece) - Object of divine love and tragic fate, embodying ideal male beauty.
- Literary Allusion (Renaissance onwards) - Used as a proper noun to evoke the mythological figure, often in poetry and drama, emphasizing beauty and tragedy.
- Common Noun (17th Century onwards) - Shifted to a common noun, 'an adonis,' referring to any exceptionally handsome young man, retaining connotations of classical beauty and sometimes a hint of vanity or tragic potential.
- Modern Usage (20th-21st Century) - Continues as a descriptor for a handsome man, sometimes with ironic undertones or to describe a man with a sculpted physique, maintaining its link to idealized male aesthetics.
