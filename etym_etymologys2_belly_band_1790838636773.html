[ ROOT WORD 1: belly ]
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*bʰelǵʰ-
"to swell, puff up, inflate"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*balgiz
"bag, leather pouch, bellows"
↓
Old English (c. 9th c.)
bælg / belg (plural: belgas)
"leather bag, pouch, stomach, belly"
↓
Middle English (c. 1250)
beli / bely
"abdomen, stomach, bellows"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present)
belly
"abdomen or underside of an animal"

↓

↓

bande / band

↓
Middle English (c. 1300)

↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present)
band
