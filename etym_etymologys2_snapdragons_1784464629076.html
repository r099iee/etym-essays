Debate note: The traditional derivation of Greek δράκων from δέρκεσθαι "to see clearly" (the serpent as "the seeing one") is accepted, but the PIE root *derḱ- "to see" is only indirectly linked; the flower name, first recorded in the 16th c., alludes to its "snapping" dragon-mouth blooms.
