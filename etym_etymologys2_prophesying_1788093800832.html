Note: The force of pro- in prophḗtēs is debated: "beforehand" (hence foreteller) or "on behalf of" (hence spokesman for the god); both readings are reflected in ancient usage. The English spelling split — verb prophesy with -s-, noun prophecy with -c- — was regularised in the 16th–17th centuries; Middle English used the forms interchangeably.
