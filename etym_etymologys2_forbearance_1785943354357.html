*beraną
"to carry, bear"
↓
Old English (c. 700)
beran

↓
Old English (c. 900)
forberan
"to bear, endure;
abstain from"
(for- "away" + beran)
↓
Middle English (12th c.)
forberen
"to endure, forbear"
↓
English (16th c.)
forbearance
"patience, self-control,
restraint"
(forbear + -ance)
Note: the noun forbearance is formed directly in English on the pattern of French -ance nouns.
