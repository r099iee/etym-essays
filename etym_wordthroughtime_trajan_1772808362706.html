- [Spanish family name] - "Trajan" is the English form of the cognomen Traianus, born of a family from Italica in Spain; it names the emperor who ruled 98-117.
- [Emperor of maximum extent] - Trajan becomes the byword for Roman expansion — the Dacian and Parthian wars carried the empire to its greatest territorial extent, earning the title Optimus Princeps.
- [Modern scholarly label] - Today the name is fixed through the Column and Forum of Trajan and "Trajanic" style; it metonymises the empire at its maximum reach.
