↓
Middle English

wellen

↓
Modern English

well
"to rise to the surface and gush out

↓
English
(past tense)
welled
"rose to the surface and gushed

Debate: the PIE base *wel- is also glossed "to roll, turn"; the "boil, bubble" sense behind the spring-word well is debated, and some link it instead to the rolling movement of surging water.
