- Latin factio - a making, party, company; political and artisanal
- Old French façon - shape, make, manner, kind; chivalric and craft
- 1300s Middle English fasoun - shape, form, manner; what is well-shaped pleases
- 1500s fashion as custom - the prevailing manner of dress and speech; courtly and imitative
- 1600s fashionable coined - fit for the fashion, kept by the gentry; elegant and exclusive
- 1700s fashionable world - Bath, London seasons, gazettes of fashion; powerful, codified, satirised
- 1820s fashionablest in Market Street - a child's superlative for a baker's house; provincial, proud, loving
- Modern most fashionable - analytic superlative wins; fashionablest survives as archaism, joke, nursery voice
