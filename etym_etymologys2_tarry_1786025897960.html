Old English / Latin blend (c. 13th c.)
tergan ("to vex") + tardare ("to delay")

↓
Middle English (c. 14th c.)
tarien
"to delay, loiter, stay behind"
↓
Modern English (16th c.)
TARRY
"to linger, delay, stay temporarily"

Note on etymology: Middle English "tarien" represents a historical convergence of Anglo-French "tarier" (to harass/provoke) and Latin-derived "tardare" (to delay, from tardus).
