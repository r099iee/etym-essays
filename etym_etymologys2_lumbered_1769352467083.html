(Debate:) origin is uncertain. The noun lumber
"useless goods" is traditionally linked to lombard
"pawnbroker" (a pawnshop was a "lombard-house"),
but the verb "to move clumsily" may instead be
echoic in origin; the American sense "timber"
has a separate, disputed derivation.
