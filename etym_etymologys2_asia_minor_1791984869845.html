Note: the identification of Greek Ἀσία with Hittite Aššuwa is widely repeated but not certain; the phonology is not exact, and Beekes treats the name as Pre-Greek without committing to the Hittite link. The derivation of minor from *mey- is secure, but Latin minor is a suppletive comparative of parvus, so no positive adjective *minos ever existed. The first attestation of the Latin phrase Asia Minor is late and its exact coinage is uncertain.
