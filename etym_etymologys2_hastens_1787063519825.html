Note on the debate: the deeper Indo-European ancestry of Germanic *haifstiz is uncertain; no secure PIE etymon has been established, so the chain begins at the Proto-Germanic stage. The semantic shift from "strife, contention" to "haste, speed" took place in the Frankish–Old French transfer, where the urgent effort of conflict was generalised to any quick action.
