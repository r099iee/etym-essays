Note: Latin iudicium joins ius (right, law, from PIE *yewes- custom, law) with dicere (to say, from PIE *deyk- show). Prae- continues PIE *per- before. The fusion into praeiudicium is Latin; the PIE stages are therefore two converging roots, standardly cited (de Vaan for ius, Rix for dicere).
