Debate: the link of Old English ficol to PIE *peik- rests on the Germanic noun *faikna- "deceit" (cf. Old Norse feikn "baleful", Sanskrit píśuna- "malicious") and is not beyond dispute. The sense shifted from "deceitful" to "changeable, inconstant" during Middle English (14th c.).
