• Subsumes: Similar because both denote incorporating specific instances or sub-steps under a broader category; differs because subsumes is a strictly logical and taxonomic term implying that the subordinate items lose their distinct individual standing by being absorbed into the higher-order rule, whereas encompasses emphasizes broad, protective boundary-drawing that retains the richness of internal components.
  • Comprises: Similar because both describe the relationship between a whole entity and its constituent parts; differs because comprises formally means "is composed of" or "contains" in an inventory-like fashion, whereas encompasses carries a vivid spatial and dynamic metaphor of surrounding, encircling, or spanning.
  • Encloses: Similar because both involve forming a physical or spatial perimeter around an object; differs because encloses is almost exclusively physical and often implies shutting in, trapping, or sealing off within walls or fences, whereas encompasses readily extends to abstract, intellectual, and temporal domains.
  • Circumscribes: Similar because both involve drawing boundaries around something; differs because circumscribes emphasizes severe restriction, limitation, and confinement of freedom or movement (drawing a line beyond which one cannot pass), whereas encompasses conveys broad, generous, and comprehensive inclusion.
  • Spans: Similar because both describe covering a wide range of topics, times, or distances; differs because spans emphasizes reaching across a gap or extending linearly from one end to another (like a bridge), whereas encompasses suggests a circular, 360-degree perimeter holding everything inside.
  • Incorporates: Similar because both mean integrating multiple elements into a unified whole; differs because incorporates highlights the blending or merging of elements into a single collective body (Latin corpus), whereas encompasses focuses on defining an overarching scope that holds diverse parts within its purview.
