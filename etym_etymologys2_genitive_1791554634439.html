Note: Roman grammarians (notably Marcus Terentius Varro) famously mistranslated the Stoic Greek grammatical term genikē ptōsis (from genos 'class, kind, genus'—originally signifying 'the case indicating the general class or category') as casus genetivus ('the case of birth or generation', erroneously associating it with gignere 'to beget'), permanently anchoring its Western linguistic designation in the concept of origin and generation.
