• Salon: Similar because both refer to refined social and intellectual gatherings in private residences; differs because a salon is a regular, recurring intellectual circle or institution, whereas a soirée is a specific single evening event.
  • Reception: Similar because both involve hosting invited guests for social interaction; differs because a reception is often formal, official, or ceremonial (e.g., diplomatic, wedding reception) and may occur at any hour, whereas a soirée is exclusively an evening affair centered on leisure and conversation.
  • Party: Similar because both describe a gathering of people for socializing; differs because party is a broad, generic term encompassing casual and lively bashes, whereas soirée denotes a sophisticated, elegant evening gathering with higher social polish.
  • Gala: Similar because both are celebratory social occasions; differs because a gala is a lavish, large-scale, often public or philanthropic event with formal entertainment, whereas a soirée is typically more intimate and domestic.
  • Matinée: Similar because both refer to scheduled cultural or social entertainment sessions; differs because a matinée takes place in the afternoon or daytime, whereas a soirée takes place strictly during the evening.
  • Conversazione: Similar because it denotes an evening gathering devoted to polite conversation and the arts; differs because conversazione is an older Italianate/Victorian term emphasizing scholarly or antiquarian discussion, whereas soirée is broader and more fashionable.
  • At-home: Similar because it is a private domestic reception; differs because an at-home was traditionally a daytime or tea-hour reception where a hostess received callers on fixed days, unlike the night-time entertainment of a soirée.
  • Shindig: Similar because it is a social party; differs because shindig is informal, boisterous, noisy slang, representing the polar opposite register of an elegant soirée.
