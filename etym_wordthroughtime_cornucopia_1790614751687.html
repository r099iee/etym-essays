- Greek myth of Amalthea’s goat-horn blessed to overflow - sacred perception of divine nursing, miraculous nourishment
- Roman cornu copiae as coin and goddess attribute - imperial propaganda perception of endless prosperity under Fortuna and Pax
- Renaissance revival as decorative arts motif - humanist perception of classical plenty adorning palaces, maps, and title-pages
- Modern English cornucopia as Thanksgiving basket and metaphor for any rich collection - festive then editorial perception of celebratory abundance
