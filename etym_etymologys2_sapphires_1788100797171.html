Note: the ultimate origin is debated. A Semitic source (Hebrew sappîr, Arabic safīr) is generally preferred; a widely repeated link to Sanskrit śanipriya (“dear to Saturn”, Śani “Saturn” + priya “beloved”) is treated as doubtful by modern dictionaries because no attested path from Sanskrit to Greek exists. In Greek and Roman antiquity the word denoted a blue stone — very probably lapis lazuli — and the transfer of the name to blue corundum belongs to the medieval period.
