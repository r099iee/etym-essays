• Quack: Similar because both falsely claim medical or scientific expertise; differs because 'quack' (from *quacksalver*) focuses almost exclusively on bogus medicine, treatments, and health remedies, whereas 'charlatan' extends broadly across the arts, academia, finance, and philosophy.
  • Mountebank: Similar because both use flamboyant showmanship to deceive the public; differs because 'mountebank' specifically evokes an itinerant street performer hawking wares from an elevated platform (*monta in banco*), carrying a more archaic, theatrical connotation.
  • Impostor: Similar because both assume a false identity; differs because an 'impostor' pretends to be a specific person or hold a specific office, whereas a 'charlatan' primarily exaggerates or fabricates their skills, knowledge, and wisdom.
  • Swindler / Con artist: Similar because both defraud others for financial gain; differs because a 'swindler' focuses directly on theft and financial scams, whereas a 'charlatan' trades specifically on pretensions to superior intellect, art, science, or healing.
  • Fraud: Similar because both deceive others under false pretenses; differs because 'fraud' is a broad legal and general hypernym, whereas 'charlatan' emphasizes noisy, pretentious self-promotion.
