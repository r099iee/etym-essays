Note on Germanic compound morphology: The formation onlooker represents a characteristic Germanic structural innovation: particle-verb inversion in nominal derivation. In standard spoken syntax, the phrasal verb is post-positioned (to look on). However, when generating agent nouns, Germanic syntax routinely preposes the prepositional/adverbial particle to head the noun stem, creating an inverted compound (compare bystander from *stand by*, passerby from *pass by*, outcast from *cast out*, and German Zuschauer from *zu-schauen*). The plural inflection -s descends from the Old English nominative/accusative plural masculine ending -as.
