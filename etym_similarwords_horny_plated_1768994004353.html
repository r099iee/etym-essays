• horned: Similar in suggesting an animal with horn-like bodily features; differs because horned merely states that horns are present, while horny-plated names the horn-like material of a segmented body covering, not the horns themselves.
  • horny: Similar in naming the hard keratin material of a covering; differs because horny names the substance alone, while horny-plated adds the arrangement of that substance into fitted sheets.
  • plated: Similar in naming a covering of flat sheets; differs because plated alone says nothing about the material (it could be metal), while horny-plated fixes the material as keratin.
  • scaly: Similar in describing a covering of hardened body plates; differs because scaly implies many small overlapping flakes, while horny-plated implies fewer, larger, fitted sheets jointed edge to edge like armor.
  • armor-plated: Similar in naming a rigid protective body covering; differs because armor-plated foregrounds defensive function and evokes tanks and medieval knights, while horny-plated stays within the biological realm of integument and adaptation.
  • scute-covered: Similar in describing segmented keratinous plates (zoological register); differs because scute-covered is the technical herpetological term for reptiles, while horny-plated is the literary compound that fuses horn and plate.
  • calloused: Similar in describing hardened, thickened skin; differs because calloused implies the skin's own protective thickening, without the segmentation of fitted plates.
  • cornified: Similar in describing tissue hardened into horn-like keratin; differs because cornified is a physiological term for cell-level keratinization, not a description of visible armor plates.
  • armored: Similar in describing a protective body covering; differs because armored is general and function-focused, while horny-plated specifies the material and the sheet-like arrangement.
  • shelled: Similar in describing a hard outer casing; differs because shelled implies a single continuous case (turtle, mollusk), while horny-plated implies many separate plates fitted together.
  • keratinous: Similar in naming the horn-substance material; differs because keratinous is purely material and scientific, while horny-plated is a vivid literary compound naming both material and form.
