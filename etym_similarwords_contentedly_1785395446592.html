• Happily: Similar because it also describes doing something in a pleasant, positive frame of mind; differs because happily is livelier, more outward, and expresses active joy, whereas contentedly is a quieter, inward peace of settled sufficiency.
  • Gladly: Similar because it denotes a willing, pleased manner of acting; differs because gladly implies active willingness and delight in the doing, whereas contentedly describes a passive, settled ease without craving.
  • Satisfactorily: Similar because both can describe how a task or state is judged; differs because satisfactorily assesses an external result against a standard, whereas contentedly describes an inner feeling of peace.
  • Serenely: Similar because both describe a composed, untroubled manner; differs because serenity can be a cold or detached calm, whereas contentedly is gentle, warm, and domestic.
  • Complacently: Similar because both describe acting in self-satisfied ease; differs because complacently is the pejorative form carrying smug self-approval, whereas contentedly carries no self-congratulation.
  • Peacefully: Similar because both imply freedom from disturbance; differs because peacefully stresses the absence of external conflict or noise, whereas contentedly stresses the inner satisfaction of desire.
  • Comfortably: Similar because both evoke ease and the absence of strain; differs because comfortably is chiefly physical and environmental (a comfortable chair, comfortable circumstances), whereas contentedly is psychological and emotional.
  • Resignedly: Similar because both can describe acceptance of a state; differs because resignedly implies surrender to something one cannot change, often with reluctance, whereas contentedly implies genuine satisfaction with what one has.
  • Blissfully: Similar because both describe an intensely pleasant state; differs because blissfully implies ecstatic, rapturous joy of higher intensity and shorter duration, whereas contentedly is a steady, undramatic peace.
  • Pleasantly: Similar because both describe an agreeable manner; differs because pleasantly can be merely polite or mildly agreeable on the surface, whereas contentedly commits to a genuine inner state of satisfaction.
