• podiatrist: Similar because both refer to licensed health professionals dedicated to the diagnosis, medical management, and rehabilitation of disorders of the human foot and lower leg; differs because 'podiatrist' is the modern international clinical standard encompassing advanced biomechanics, pharmacology, and invasive surgical procedures, whereas 'chiropodist' is the older Commonwealth term traditionally emphasizing routine, palliative, and conservative care (such as nail trimming and hyperkeratosis debridement).
  • pedicurist: Similar because both work directly on the human foot, nails, and surrounding skin; differs because a pedicurist is a cosmetic beauty technician who focuses on aesthetic grooming, foot baths, exfoliating scrubs, and nail polish without medical licensing or authority to treat pathology, whereas a chiropodist is a healthcare practitioner who treats clinical disorders such as ulcers, fungal infections, verrucae, and structural lesions.
  • corn-cutter: Similar because both historically earned their living by cutting and removing painful hyperkeratotic corns from sufferers' feet; differs because 'corn-cutter' was an uncredentialed, itinerant trade in the 17th and 18th centuries, often operating on street corners or alongside barber-surgeons with rudimentary hygiene, whereas a chiropodist possesses rigorous anatomical training, aseptic instruments, and diagnostic expertise.
  • orthotist: Similar because both frequently design and dispense customized insoles, orthotics, and footwear adaptations to correct pathological gait patterns; differs because an orthotist manufactures mechanical support devices for all neuromuscular and skeletal impairments throughout the entire human body (including spines and limbs), whereas a chiropodist focuses specifically on foot health and comprehensive lower-limb care.
  • manicurist: Similar because both provide specialized extremity care for nails and digits, and the historical Greek etymology of chiropody originally encompassed manual treatment; differs because a modern manicurist operates exclusively in cosmetic salons treating hands and fingernails, whereas modern chiropody is exclusively focused on foot pathology.
  • farrier: Similar because both specialize in trimming, balancing, and attending to the load-bearing extremities of walking organisms; differs because a farrier works exclusively with equines (horses and donkeys) shaping hooves and fitting metal shoes, whereas a chiropodist treats human feet.
