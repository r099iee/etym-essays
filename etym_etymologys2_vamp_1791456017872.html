Note: the two Latin elements are shown together as parallel components; they converge in the Old French compound avant-pied. The English intermediate vampay is reported in etymological accounts as the seventeenth-century form that was then clipped to vamp; it is not securely attested in ordinary running text, so it is marked as inferred. The musical sense develops from the cobbling verb to vamp "to patch, piece together"; the femme fatale sense is a separate twentieth-century clipping of vampire and has no connection with the shoe word.
