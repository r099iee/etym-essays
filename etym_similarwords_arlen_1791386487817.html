• Michael Arlen: the full pen name of Dikran Kouyoumdjian; differs from the bare surname by identifying the specific person and his public persona.
  • Kouyoumdjian: Arlen's birth surname, Armenian in form; differs because it is inherited and ethnically marked, whereas Arlen was invented for English readability.
  • Arlenesque: the adjective derived from the surname, similar in referring to his style; differs by describing a manner of writing rather than the man.
  • Arlene: a given name of similar sound, often derived from Irish or Celtic sources; differs by being a female personal name and not the novelist's coinage.
  • Arlan: a variant given name and surname, similar in sound; differs by its separate Turkic, Irish or English etymologies.
  • Harlan: an English surname and given name meaning "hare land"; differs by its distinct habitational origin and by not being a pen name.
  • Harold Arlen: the American composer, similar in bearing the surname; differs because his name is an anglicisation of Arluck and has no connection with Michael Arlen.
  • Armenia: the country from whose name Arlen coined his surname; differs by being a place name and the source, not the result.
  • Armenian: the ethnic and linguistic adjective, similar in root; differs by referring to a people and language rather than an individual's assumed name.
  • Pseudonym: a general term for an assumed name, similar in function; differs because it names the category, not this particular invented surname.
  • Nom de plume: a literary pen name, similar in being adopted for publication; differs by its French register and its narrower literary application.
  • Green Hat: the title of Arlen's most famous novel, similar in being associated with him; differs because it names a work rather than the author.
