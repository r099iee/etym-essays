Compound — coastwise:
↓
English (19th c.)
coastwise
"along the coast"

Note: coast is from Old French coste "a coast, side", from Latin costa "a rib, side"; the PIE root *kost- is uncertain. The suffix -wise is from Old English wīse "a manner, way", from Proto-Germanic *wīsō, related to PIE *weyd- "to see, know" — a "way" as a manner of seeing.
