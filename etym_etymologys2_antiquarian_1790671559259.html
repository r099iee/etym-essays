Note: Latin antiquus was earlier anticus (in front, former), from ante (before) + -icus; ante itself continues PIE *h₂ent- (front, forehead). The *al- connection via alius reflects the old comparative sense of otherness and formerness; some handbooks derive ante directly from *h₂ent- rather than *al-, a nuance noted here without inventing unattested links.
