• Lettish: The older English name for the language and people, from German Lettisch; identical referent, but archaic and now often perceived as a German-imposed label.
  • Latvija: The Latvian endonym for the country; identical referent, but native and untranslated.
  • Latgalian: The eastern variety of Latvian; similar and partly intelligible, but often treated as a separate language with its own standard.
  • Lithuanian: The sister East Baltic language; similar in structure and history, but not mutually intelligible and attached to a different nation.
  • Livonian: The Finnic language once spoken on the Latvian coast; similar only as a regional language, but unrelated and now effectively extinct.
  • Baltic: The wider family and regional adjective; similar as a classifier, but broader than Latvian alone.
  • Latvian Soviet Socialist Republic: The Soviet-era administrative name; similar territory, but a communist and non-sovereign frame.
  • Courlander: An inhabitant of Courland, one of the historical regions; similar regional reference, but sub-national and German-influenced.
  • Rigan: An inhabitant of Riga; similar urban reference, but narrower than the national term.
  • Letto-Slavic: An older philological grouping of Baltic and Slavic; similar as a classification, but broader and now largely superseded by Balto-Slavic.
