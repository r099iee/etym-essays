Notes: (1) The origin of Germanic *lauda- is uncertain (OED); the leading hypothesis is a loan from Celtic (Old Irish lúaide "lead"), pointing to ore trade, though a shared pre-Indo-European substrate source is also proposed. (2) German Blei "lead" comes from a different Germanic root, *blīwą — the two words for the metal were dialectal variants.
