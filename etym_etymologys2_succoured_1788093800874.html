Note: The prefix sub- continues the PIE preposition *upo "under, up from under" (cf. Greek hypó), with the unexplained Latin s- also seen in super. The French-style -our and the doubled -cc- are restorations from Latin succurrere during the 16th century. The PIE equation for currere (*ḱers- "to run") is standard but rests on thin comparanda.
