*(s)kewt- / *skew-
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
"to shake, push, jolt, thrust"
↓
*skud- / *skudaną
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
"to shake, vibrate, agitate"
↓
schudden
Middle Dutch / Middle Low German (c. 1200 CE)
"to shake, jolt violently"
↓
schuderen
Middle Low German / Middle Dutch (frequentative) (c. 1300 CE)
"to shake repeatedly, tremble, shudder"
↓
shuderen / schoderen
Middle English (c. 1380 CE)
"to shake with fear, shiver with horror"
↓
shuddered
Early Modern to Modern English (c. 1500 CE–present)
"trembled violently from horror, fear, or cold"
