• Sickle: Similar because both are traditional curved-blade hand tools used to harvest grass and crops; differs because a sickle has a short handle and small, tightly curved blade operated with one hand while the other hand grasps the grain stalks, whereas a scythe has a long shaft (snath) operated standing up with both hands in sweeping strokes.
  • Billhook: Similar because both are bladed agricultural cutting tools; differs because a billhook is a thick, heavy, short-handled chopping blade designed for hedging, coppicing, and lopping woody branches, rather than slicing soft grass and grain.
  • Machete: Similar because both feature a long steel cutting blade used for clearing vegetation; differs because a machete is a straight or slightly curved single-handed cleaver swung with hacking, chopping strikes against thick brush, unlike the balanced two-handed slicing motion of a scythe.
  • Scythe-Cradle (Cradle Scythe): Similar because it uses a scythe blade as its primary cutting element; differs because a cradle scythe is specially fitted with a framework of light wooden fingers (a cradle) attached to the snath to catch cut grain stalks and deposit them neatly in windrows for bundling.
  • Kama: Similar because it is an agricultural curved bladed implement used for reaping crops; differs because the kama is a traditional Japanese mini-sickle with a straight wooden handle held in one hand, often adapted as a martial arts weapon, lacking the large two-handed snath of a Western scythe.
  • Brush Hook (Slash Hook): Similar because it has a long handle and a curved cutting blade for clearing growth; differs because a brush hook possesses a heavier, hooked blade meant for forceful hacking through woody briars and saplings rather than fluid meadow mowing.
  • Flail: Similar because both are traditional wooden agricultural hand tools used in the seasonal harvest cycle; differs because a flail consists of two hinged wooden sticks used for threshing harvested grain to separate wheat kernels from chaff, having no cutting blade.
