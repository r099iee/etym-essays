NOTE — DEBATED / NUANCED STAGES: the PIE root *skeubʰ- is itself an extension (with the -bʰ- suffix) of the root *skeu- "to bend, curve, press"; cognates include Sanskrit kṣubhyati "to shake, be agitated" and, possibly, Greek skýptō "to stoop" — the Greek connection is debated. The Old English verb was strong (past scēaf, plural scufon, participle scofen); the strong forms were abandoned in the 14th–15th centuries in favour of the regular weak past shoved, and no fossil strong forms survive. Only attested forms appear above; nothing is guessed.
