Debate note: An older idea linking ripe to a root *h₁reyb- "to reap" is rejected. The link to *h₁rebʰ- "to tear, break, pluck" rests on ripe fruit being "plucked/torn" from the plant for harvest (cf. reap, OE rīpan "to reap"); it remains debated, and Proto-Germanic *rīpijaz is the earliest secure reconstruction.
