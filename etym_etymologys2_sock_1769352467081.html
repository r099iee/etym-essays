Note: This card's sense — the verb to sock "to hit forcefully; a punch" — is etymologically separate from the foot-covering word. It is of uncertain origin (late 17th c.), possibly from the phrase sock in the eye "a hard blow", and is not derived from Old English socc. The Greek σύκχος is likewise of uncertain origin.
