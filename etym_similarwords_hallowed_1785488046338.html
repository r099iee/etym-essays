• Sacred: Similar because [both are set apart as holy]; differs because [sacred denotes an inherent connection to the divine, while hallowed stresses sanctity conferred by reverence and long tradition].
  • Consecrated: Similar because [both are dedicated as holy]; differs because [consecrated emphasises the specific rite of dedication, while hallowed emphasises the reverence and customary awe that surround the object].
  • Blessed: Similar because [both are favoured or sanctified]; differs because [blessed implies a gift of divine favour, while hallowed implies being made the object of reverential awe or regard].
  • Sanctified: Similar because [both mean made holy]; differs because [sanctified sits closer to consecrated — an act of making holy — while hallowed stresses the state of being reverently regarded].
  • Revered: Similar because [both are deeply respected]; differs because [revered stresses the honour paid, while hallowed adds a layer of superstitious or religious awe].
  • Venerated: Similar because [both mean held in profound respect]; differs because [veneration may be entirely secular — venerated scholars, venerated traditions — while hallowed keeps a religious or quasi-religious aura].
  • Holy: Similar because [both denote sanctity]; differs because [holy names the quality of being divine or devoted to God, while hallowed is the result of an act of consecration or of reverent tradition].
  • Canonical: Similar because [both can describe an honoured body of works or authority]; differs because [canonical stresses official listing and doctrinal authority, while hallowed stresses quasi-religious reverence by custom].
