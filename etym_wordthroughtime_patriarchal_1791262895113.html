- Biblical-classical sense: of the Patriarchs Abraham, Isaac, Jacob - venerable, sacred father-founders of a people.
- 17th c. political theory: patriarchal kingship - Filmer's divine-right father-king, contested by Locke; perception splits between natural order and tyranny.
- 19th c. anthropology: patriarchal stage of social evolution - Maine, Morgan, Engels; seen as progressive step from promiscuity to civilization, yet seed of private property.
- Early 20th c. neutral kinship term: patriarchal vs matriarchal family - textbook classification, mildly respectful of elder authority.
- Late 20th c.–present feminist critique: patriarchal as systemic male oppression - pejorative, structural, demanding dismantling; simultaneously nostalgic benevolent patriarch in popular culture.
