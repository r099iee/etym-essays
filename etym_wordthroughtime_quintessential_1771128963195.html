- Alchemical fifth essence - Descended from medieval alchemy’s “quintessence” (the fifth element above earth, air, fire, and water), it first referred to the purest substance from which all things were made.
- Metaphorical purification - Broadened from physical element to abstract character, coming to mean the purest or most perfect embodiment of a quality, moving into moral and spiritual vocabulary.
- Contemporary Sense - Today it means representing the most perfect or typical example of a quality or class; it signals archetypal, unqualified authenticity, elevated far above the merely average.
