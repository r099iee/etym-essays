Note on the debate (zero guesswork): The etymology of whim is genuinely uncertain. The OED records whim-wham from the 1530s as the earlier word and treats whim as a later shortening. A Scandinavian origin has long been suggested (compare Norwegian hvimsa / kvimsa "to flutter about", hvimsa "whimsical"), but no continuous attestation proves it, and a comic reduplicative invention for the sake of a fanciful word cannot be excluded. The chain above therefore marks the Scandinavian stage as proposed, not established.
