Note: The Latin base annus descends from the Proto-Indo-European root *h₁et-no- (or *at-no-), literally denoting the passing or going of time (cognate with Sanskrit atati 'walks, wanders' and Gothic aþnam 'year'). In early Republican Rome, the adjective annālis was nominalized in the masculine plural phrase annālēs librī, designating the official public records maintained annually on a whitewashed board (the tabula dealbata) by the Pontifex Maximus, which established the prototypical meaning of the term in Western historiography.
