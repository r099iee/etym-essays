- Old English wicce/wicca as sorcerer and wiccian to practise sorcery - feared craft with real courts and charms
- c. 1225 bewicchen as to cast a spell upon - be- makes the magic fall upon the victim
- 1500s-1600s bewitched as witch-trial diagnosis - fits, milk spoiled, pins vomited prove the spell
- 1600s literary bewitched as enraptured - Shakespeare and songs turn curse into allure and love
- Modern comic and romantic bewitched - from accursed goose to bewitched lover and glitching computer
