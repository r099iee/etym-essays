Note: the ultimate origin of Sibir is famously debated. Proposals include Mongolian sibir or shibir meaning marshy thicket or sleeping land, Turkic su-bir, and derivation from the Sipyrt / Sabir nomads. No single etymology commands consensus, so the chain above begins at the securely attested ethnonym-toponym and flags deeper origins as disputed.
