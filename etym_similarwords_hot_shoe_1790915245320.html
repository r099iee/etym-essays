• Cold shoe: Similar because both are standardized mounting brackets atop a camera designed to hold external accessories; differs because a cold shoe lacks electrical wiring and contact pins, functioning solely as a passive mechanical bracket.
  • PC sync port: Similar because both electronically synchronize the camera shutter with an external flash unit; differs because a PC sync port is a small circular coaxial cable connector requiring a physical wire rather than a top-mounted bracket.
  • Accessory shoe: Similar because both describe physical mounting slots on optical devices; differs because 'accessory shoe' is the generic umbrella category that includes both unpowered cold shoes and electronically live hot shoes.
  • Multi-Interface shoe: Similar because both provide camera-top accessory mounting with electrical signaling; differs because a Multi-Interface shoe is a specialized modern variant featuring an expanded high-density array of digital pins for transmitting complex data like digital audio and video feeds.
  • Flash bracket: Similar because both support external lighting units; differs because a flash bracket is an external metal arm attached to the tripod socket to position the flash far off-axis, whereas a hot-shoe is integrated directly on the camera body.
