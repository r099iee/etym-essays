• Bejewelled: Similar because both mean decorated with jewels; differs because bejewelled implies jewels set here and there as ornament — the base material still visible — while jewel-encrusted implies far greater density, the gems forming a continuous, crust-like layer over the whole surface.
  • Studded: Similar because both mean covered with gems or points; differs because studded pictures jewels or studs driven into the surface like nails, with the base still visible between them, whereas encrusted suggests the base has all but disappeared under the gems.
  • Set: Similar because both involve placing stones in metal; differs because set is the jeweller's technical term for one or a few stones seated in a mount (a ring set with rubies), not a dense covering of an entire surface as encrusted implies.
  • Inlaid: Similar because both place decorative material into a surface; differs because inlay sets pieces flush into cut-out beds so the surface stays smooth, while encrusting piles gems onto the surface as a raised, hardened layer.
  • Adorned: Similar because both mean decorated; differs because adorned is the general, neutral word for any decoration, lacking the specific image of a dense gem-crust that encrusted supplies.
  • Embellished: Similar because both mean enhanced with ornament; differs because embellished is broad — it covers embroidery, gilt, carving, and rhetoric — while jewel-encrusted names specifically a covering of gems.
  • Gemmed: Similar because both mean covered with gems; differs because gemmed is a poetic or literary word for studded with gems, lighter and less technical, while jewel-encrusted stresses the hardening, crust-like density.
  • Ornate: Similar because both mean elaborately decorated; differs because ornate covers any rich ornamentation — carving, filigree, gilt — while jewel-encrusted names specifically the crust of actual jewels.
  • Crusted: Similar because both mean covered with a hard layer; differs because crusted is the plain geological word (rust-crusted, ice-crusted) without the jewels, while jewel-encrusted specifies gems as the crusting material.
