Note: PIE *sreu- is secure (Sanskrit sravati flows, Greek rheos stream, Old Norse straumr). The suffix -let comes via Middle French -elet (hamel-et to hamlet, brook to booklet pattern) from Latin -ellus + -ittus; it became productive in English only in the 16th-17th c., so streamlet is an early modern formation, not inherited Old English.
