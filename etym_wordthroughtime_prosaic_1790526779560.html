- Late 1500s-1600s technical literary classification - of or pertaining to prose composition; non-metrical writing; strictly descriptive and neutral
- 1700s-1750s Augustan literary criticism - shift toward pejorative aesthetic critique; writing that resembles dull, uninspired prose rather than heroic poetry; unpoetic and flat
- 1790s-1850s Romanticism and German Idealism - Hegel's 'prose of the world' vs. epic poetry; prosaic reality as the disenchanted, bourgeois, unheroic modern order
- Victorian era to mid-1900s - broadened to everyday domestic life, mundane duties, practical matters; unromantic and workaday
- Modern contemporary usage - descriptive of unglamorous facts, practical business concerns, and matter-of-fact reality vs. idealistic fantasy; polished critical vocabulary
