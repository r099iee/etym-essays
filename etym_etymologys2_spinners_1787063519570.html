Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*(s)pen- / *(s)pend-
"to draw out, stretch, spin thread"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*spinnaną
"to draw out fibers, twist into yarn"
↓
Old English (c. 8th–10th c.)
spinnere
"one who spins thread, yarn maker"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–14th c.)
spinner / spynnere
"spinner of yarn; spider (silk producer)"
↓
Modern English (present)
spinners
"silk organs; rotating devices; spin bowlers"
