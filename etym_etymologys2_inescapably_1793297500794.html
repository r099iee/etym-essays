Note: the negative prefix in- descends from Latin in-, ‘not’, from PIE *n̥-, and the adverbial -ly from Old English -līce; both are among English’s oldest and most productive formatives, while the vivid cape-escape etymology of the base is the standard handbook account though first reconstructed rather than directly attested.
