- Late medieval supply and ordinance - practical, dual sense of victuals for armies and clauses in statutes
- Tudor-Stuart naval and colonial sense - heroic-logistic, provisions as survival and imperial reach
- 18th-century legal-formal sense - authoritative, provisions of treaties and wills as rational foresight
- Victorian welfare and household sense - moralized, making provision as thrift, prudence, paternal duty
- Modern administrative sense - bureaucratic-neutral, provision of services, healthcare provision, loan-loss provisions
