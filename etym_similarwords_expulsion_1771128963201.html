• ejection: Similar because both mean being forced out of a space; differs because ejection is mechanical, physical, and often instantaneous (a pilot or a tape ejecting), without the institutional or moral weight of expulsion.
  • ouster: Similar because both describe removal from a position; differs because ouster specifically concerns the removal of someone from power, office, or legal possession, rather than the physical driving out of a space.
  • banishment: Similar because both describe forced removal from a place; differs because banishment implies a decree and permanent exile from a homeland, focusing on the state of being excluded, while expulsion is the act of driving out itself.
  • exile: Similar because both describe being removed from one's country or community; differs because exile names the resulting condition of being banished, not the forceful act, and can even be voluntary.
  • deportation: Similar because both involve a state removing a person from its territory; differs because deportation is specifically the legal removal of an alien to another country, a modern administrative procedure rather than a punitive casting out.
  • eviction: Similar because both are removals from a place; differs because eviction is the legal removal of a tenant from property, narrow, procedural, and aimed at possession rather than membership.
  • dismissal: Similar because both mean being sent away from a position; differs because dismissal is bureaucratic and employment-based, comparatively gentle and procedural, and lacks the violent, forceful 'driving out' of expulsion.
  • discharge: Similar because both describe matter being emitted from a container or body; differs because discharge is a neutral technical term (as of fluids, gases, or military duty), without any judgment or social rupture.
  • purging: Similar because both describe the removal of something undesirable; differs because purging implies cleansing or purification of impurities (often internal and self-directed), while expulsion is a forceful ejection by an external power.
