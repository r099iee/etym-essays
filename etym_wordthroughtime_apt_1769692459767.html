- Fitted & suitable (Old–Early Modern English) - a neutral, factual judgment that something is well-adapted or appropriate to its circumstances.
- Natural tendency & disposition (17th–19th centuries) - extends from things to people; gains an evaluative and often ironic colour (an apt pupil, apt to forget), marking a characteristic inclination.
- Appropriate / quick to learn (Modern English) - retains a sense of natural fitness, now usually positive; connected with admired talent and ability.
