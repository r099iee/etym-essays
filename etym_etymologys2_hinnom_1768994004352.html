Debate: the proper name hinnōm is of uncertain origin (perhaps a personal or tribal name). Greek Γέεννα géenna, from Hebrew gēʾ-hinnōm, is the source of "Gehenna", used in the New Testament for the place of punishment and hence for hell; "Hinnom" is a direct English transliteration of the Hebrew name.
