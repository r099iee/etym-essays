- 1642 Dutch sighting - Van Diemen's Land connotes distant navigational mark on VOC charts, barely peopled in European minds.
- 1803-1853 penal infamy - Van Diemonians connote convicts, floggings and bushrangers, a place of dread in British imagination.
- Mid-19th-century extinction elegy - Tasmanians connote dying race and hollow-tree primitives, objects of phrenology and pity.
- 1856 rebrand - Tasmanians connote respectable free settlers shedding convict stain, orchards and wilderness.
- Late 20th-century pride - Tasmanians connote environmentalists, MONA creatives and resilient Aboriginal community, connoting island distinctiveness.
- Contemporary reclamation - Aboriginal Tasmanians connote survival, language revival and land return, overturning extinction myth.
