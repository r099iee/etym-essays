• Waves: Similar because both describe rising-and-falling motion like water; differs because waves is the plain everyday verb, while undulates stresses smooth, regular, graceful curves and sounds more formal.
  • Ripples: Similar because both name small wave-like motion across a surface; differs because ripples suggests tiny shallow disturbances, while undulates suggests broader, deeper, more stately swells.
  • Flows: Similar because both describe continuous gliding movement; differs because flows stresses forward streaming, while undulates stresses the side-to-side or up-and-down wave shape of the path.
  • Surges: Similar because both describe swelling forward motion; differs because surges is powerful and sudden, a rush of force, while undulates is gentle and rhythmic, without violence.
  • Meanders: Similar because both describe winding, curving paths; differs because meanders wanders sideways with no fixed rhythm, while undulates rises and falls in regular wave-like beats.
  • Oscillates: Similar because both name regular back-and-forth movement; differs because oscillates is the physicist's word for vibration between poles, while undulates paints the visible wave shape.
  • Fluctuates: Similar because both describe rising and falling over time; differs because fluctuates is abstract and statistical, said of prices and moods, while undulates keeps the physical picture of water.
  • Wriggles: Similar because both describe squirming wave-like body motion; differs because wriggles is small, quick, and often comic or effortful, while undulates is smooth, slow, and graceful.
