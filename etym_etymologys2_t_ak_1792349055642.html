Note on historical debate: Sanskrit grammarians and modern comparative philologists discuss two primary etymological threads for the name: (1) an organic derivation from the root tṝ ("to cross over, conquer"), yielding feminine Tārakā ("star, shining deliverer", reflecting her original radiant Yakṣiṇī status), which underwent characteristic Middle Indo-Aryan Prakritic cerebralization (retroflexion of the liquid consonant -r- to -ḍ- or -ṭ-) to yield Tāḍakā and Tāṭakā; and (2) an alternative association with the secondary verbal root taḍ- ("to strike, beat, knock, whip"), signifying "she who smites or beats down her foes", descriptive of her monstrous, violent persona following the curse.
