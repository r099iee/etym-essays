Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*darōþuz
"spear, javelin, dart"
↓
Old French (c. 11th c. CE)
dart
"light throwing spear, missile"
↓
Old French (c. 12th c. CE)
darter
"to pierce with a dart; spring forward"
↓
Middle English (c. 14th c. CE)
darten
"to shoot a missile; spring forward swiftly"
↓
Modern English (16th c. CE–present)
darted
"moved, sprang, or shot suddenly"

Note: Dart traveled from Proto-Germanic (*darōþuz) into Old French (dart / darter) before entering Middle English as darten, replacing the native Old English cognate daroð.
