• Tarried: Similar because both denote staying behind; differs because tarried is an archaic, formal, or biblical verb emphasizing waiting, pausing in a journey, or deliberately delaying travel, whereas lingered emphasizes slow, reluctant, or gentle lingering.
  • Loitered: Similar because both involve staying in a place; differs because loitered carries a negative, suspicious, or illicit connotation of hanging around aimlessly in public spaces with no apparent purpose or lawful business.
  • Persisted: Similar because both mean continuing to exist; differs because persisted emphasizes active, stubborn, or obstinate endurance against resistance, obstacles, or opposing forces (e.g., the storm persisted, he persisted in his demands).
  • Endured: Similar because both denote lasting through time; differs because endured implies surviving hardship, suffering, severe pain, or extreme wear over long durations.
  • Remained: Similar because both describe staying in place; differs because remained is a neutral, factual, and unemotional statement of location or status, lacking the poetic reluctance and slow fade of lingered.
  • Dawdled: Similar because both involve wasting time; differs because dawdled is an informal, trivial term emphasizing childish slowness, fiddling, and pointlessly squandering time when one should be hurrying.
  • Abided: Similar because both describe staying; differs because abided is an elevated, archaic verb denoting steadfast residence, enduring loyalty, or standing firm in faith.
