- Greek grammatical coinage - hypotassein as arranging under, ranking troops and words in hierarchy
- 19th-century comparative grammar - hypotaxis / hypotactic revived by German philologists to classify subordinate vs coordinate syntax
- Rhetorical style label - 20th-century critics use hypotactic for dense, academic, Jamesian prose vs plain paratactic prose
- Jargon and self-parody - fashionable theory-speak where hypotactic nominal style with alliterative isocolon both describes and performs learned obscurity
