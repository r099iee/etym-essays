• Rereading: Similar because both mean reading something again; differs because rereading is the ordinary, unmarked term implying no special intensity or completeness, while re-perusal insists on a thorough, point-by-point second pass.
  • Perusal: Similar because both name careful, attentive reading; differs because perusal alone stresses completeness without repetition — one can peruse a document once, whereas re-perusal is by definition repeated.
  • Reading: Similar because both denote the act of taking in a text; differs because reading is the neutral, general term for any level of attention, while re-perusal is specifically the repeated, complete, careful kind.
  • Review: Similar because both involve re-examining a document; differs because review is a broad reconsideration or an official inspection, often for judgment or appraisal, whereas re-perusal is specifically the repeated thorough reading of written text.
  • Revision: Similar because both involve looking at a text again; differs because revision aims at altering or improving the text, while re-perusal aims only at reading it through again with care.
  • Re-examination: Similar because both mean examining again; differs because re-examination is the general term for any second inspection (of evidence, of a witness, of a question), while re-perusal is confined to the careful reading of written material.
  • Scrutiny: Similar because both denote close examination; differs because scrutiny suggests suspicious or investigative checking of conduct, accounts, or detail, not the repeated careful reading of a literary text that re-perusal names.
  • Study: Similar because both involve close attention to something; differs because study is the general and continuing engagement with a subject, while re-perusal is a single, complete, second reading of a specific text.
  • Inspection: Similar because both mean careful examination; differs because inspection is a formal or official examination of things, premises, or documents, while re-perusal is specifically the repeated reading of written words.
  • Examination: Similar because both name careful attention; differs because examination covers tests, medical checks, and investigations, while re-perusal is limited to the attentive re-reading of a text.
  • Survey: Similar because both involve going over something; differs because a survey is a broad, often superficial overview or stock-taking, while re-perusal is a complete and meticulous second reading.
  • Collation: Similar because both belong to the world of textual scholarship; differs because collation is the technical comparison of copies of a text to establish a correct version, while re-perusal is simply the thorough re-reading itself.
  • Recension: Similar because both are technical scholarly terms for textual work; differs because recension is the critical revision and establishment of a text, while re-perusal is the act of reading it through carefully again.
  • Second reading: Similar because both denote reading a text twice; differs because "second reading" is the ordinary phrase (and a stage of legislative procedure), while re-perusal adds the learned insistence on complete and careful attention.
  • Skimming / scanning: Similar because both are acts of reading; differs because skimming and scanning are hasty, partial passes for gist or keywords — the precise opposites of re-perusal's complete, thorough attention.
