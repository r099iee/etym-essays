Note: The feminine suffix -issa in Late Latin dūcissa was borrowed from Ancient Greek -issa (used to form feminine agent nouns, as in basilissa, “queen”). This suffix passed through Gallo-Romance into Old French as -esse, yielding modern English -ess (found in princess, countess, actress).
