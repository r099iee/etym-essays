Notes: (1) Some authorities derive Old French arche from a Vulgar Latin feminine *arca “arch”; either way the source is Latin arcus. (2) The link of Latin arcus to PIE *h₂erk- is traditional but disputed — modern scholarship (e.g. de Vaan) treats arcus as possibly an old word for “bow/curve” of uncertain pedigree. (3) The prefix arch- in archbishop is NOT from arcus but from Greek arkhē “rule, beginning” — a coincidence.
