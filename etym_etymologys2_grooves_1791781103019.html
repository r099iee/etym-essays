Note on the debate: the immediate source of Middle English grove is disputed between Old Norse gróf and Middle Dutch groeve, two cognates of the same Proto-Germanic noun; the Scandinavian route is favoured by most handbooks, but the Dutch route cannot be excluded. The PIE root *gʰrebh- is a standard reconstruction (Pokorny, Etymonline), though some scholars treat the Germanic noun as of uncertain pre-Germanic origin.
