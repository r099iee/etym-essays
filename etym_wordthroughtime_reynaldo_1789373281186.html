- [Early Middle Ages / Germanic Era] - Arises as the dithematic Germanic name *Raginwaldaz (from ragin 'counsel, decision' + wald 'power, rule') across Frankish, Saxon, and Gothic tribes - [connotation: sovereign majesty, wise governance, judicial authority]
- [Middle Ages – Chivalric Romance] - Enters Romance languages via French (Renaud), Italian (Rinaldo), and Spanish/Portuguese (Reinaldo / Reynaldo); celebrated through heroes in Ariosto's Orlando Furioso - [connotation: knightly valour, chivalric romance, heroic passion]
- [Late 19th c. – Belle Époque] - Embodied by the prodigy Reynaldo Hahn in the aristocratic Parisian salons of Madeleine Lemaire and the Princesse de Polignac - [connotation: supreme salon elegance, exquisite vocal melody, intimate romance with Marcel Proust]
- [20th c. – Modernity] - Recognized globally in musicology as the master of the French mélodie, operetta (Ciboulette), and post-war Director of the Paris Opéra - [connotation: Mozartian clarity, classical restraint, quintessential fin-de-siècle nostalgia]
- [Contemporary Era] - Celebrated in cultural history and LGBTQ+ scholarship as an artistic icon whose relationship with Proust enriched world literature - [connotation: artistic fidelity, lyrical tenderness, romantic muse]
