PIE (uncertain) *h₂rey- "to count, reason" ↓ Latin ratus "reckoned, fixed by calculation" ↓ Latin ratio "reckoning, account, reason" ↓ Old French raison "reason, reasoning, cause" ↓ English reason "the power of reasoning" ↓ English (15th c.) unreason "lack of reasonable or rational thought; irrationality"
Note: the PIE root *h₂rey- 'to count, reason' behind Latin reri/ratus/ratio is uncertain; unreason is a native formation of un- + reason.
