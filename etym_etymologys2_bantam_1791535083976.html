bantam

Note: Toponymic origin from Banten (Dutch Bantam), a historic sultanate and seaport on the northwestern coast of Java, Indonesia. European merchant sailors purchased small, hardy native domestic fowls there as shipboard rations and novelties, leading to the designation "Bantam fowl", later shortened to bantam and transferred metaphorically to spirited small people and the boxing weight class.
