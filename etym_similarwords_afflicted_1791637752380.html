• Beset: Similar because both describe being troubled by recurring or persistent difficulties; differs because beset emphasizes being surrounded, hemmed in, or attacked from all sides (e.g., beset by doubts, beset by creditors), whereas afflicted emphasizes the deep internal suffering, pain, or disability caused by the condition.
  • Plagued: Similar because both denote continuous suffering or torment; differs because plagued carries an active, irritating, or epidemic connotation, implying relentless harassment or widespread pestilence, whereas afflicted suggests solemn distress, chronic debility, or structural impairment.
  • Stricken: Similar because both denote being struck by illness or disaster; differs because stricken emphasizes the sudden, catastrophic impact of a blow or shock (e.g., panic-stricken, grief-stricken), while afflicted more commonly describes an ongoing state of suffering or an enduring flaw.
  • Tormented: Similar because both describe undergoing intense distress; differs because tormented emphasizes acute, active agonizing torture—often mental, emotional, or spiritual—whereas afflicted can be applied dispassionately to chronic medical conditions or metaphorical defects.
  • Burdened: Similar because both involve bearing a heavy load; differs because burdened implies being weighed down by responsibilities, debts, or sorrow without necessarily suffering physical disease, bodily injury, or structural defect.
  • Marred: Similar because both describe something flawed or compromised; differs because marred focuses on external, surface, or aesthetic damage (spoiled beauty), whereas afflicted conveys an intrinsic, organic ailment or active imposition of distress.
  • Cursed: Similar because both describe enduring profound misfortune; differs because cursed attributes the suffering to a supernatural, divine, or malevolent agency, whereas afflicted is primarily medical, experiential, or descriptive.
  • Inconvenienced: Similar because both involve hindrance; differs vastly in severity: inconvenienced is a trivial, mild disruption of routine, whereas afflicted denotes genuine pain, suffering, or debilitating flaw.
