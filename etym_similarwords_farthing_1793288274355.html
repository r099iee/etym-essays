• Penny: Similar because both are small pre-decimal bronze coins of daily life; differs because the penny was four times the farthing and the standard unit of small price, while the farthing was the despised minimum, the coin of beggars and buns.
  • Halfpenny: Similar because both are tiny fractions of the penny now vanished; differs because the halfpenny (ha'penny) survived in sweets and bus fares into living memory, while the farthing died in 1961 and feels a generation older.
  • Cent: Similar because both name the smallest coin of their currency and idioms of worthlessness; differs because the cent is the live hundredth of modern dollars and euros, while the farthing is a dead quarter-penny kept alive only by proverb.
  • Sou: Similar because both are small foreign coppers used for worthlessness (not worth a sou); differs because the sou is French and literary in English, while the farthing is home-grown and agricultural, smelling of English markets.
  • Rap: Similar because not worth a rap matches not worth a farthing exactly; differs because a rap was a counterfeit Irish halfpenny, so the idiom hints at fraud, while the farthing idiom hints at honest littleness.
  • Fig: Similar because not worth a fig is the fruit equivalent of worthlessness; differs because the fig draws on biblical and Mediterranean contempt for trifles, while the farthing draws on the English purse and ledger.
  • Straw: Similar because not worth a straw also prices a thing at field rubbish; differs because straw is agricultural waste imagery, while farthing is exact accounting imagery down to the quarter-penny.
  • Trifle: Similar because both name things of negligible value; differs because a trifle is any small matter in general, while a farthing is specifically the smallest money, with the whole mint behind it.
  • Mite: Similar because the widow's mite is the biblical cousin of the farthing's poverty; differs because a mite is a tiny contribution honoured for generosity, while a farthing in Cobbett is a surplus stolen by rates.
