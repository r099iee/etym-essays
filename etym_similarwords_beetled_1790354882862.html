• Scuttled: Similar because both describe moving rapidly away with short, quick, low-profile steps; differs because scuttled carries stronger connotations of panic, evasion, undignified flight, or abandoning a doomed position, whereas beetled is brisk, matter-of-fact, or lightheartedly purposeful.
  • Scurried: Similar because both denote fast, agitated locomotion in small strides; differs because scurried evokes the scampering nervousness of mice or flustered confusion, while beetled emphasizes a steady, mechanical, head-down momentum.
  • Darted: Similar because both signify sudden, rapid movement; differs because darted indicates a swift, straight, arrow-like rush over a short distance, whereas beetled denotes continuous scurrying transit across space.
  • Bolted: Similar because both indicate sudden departure; differs because bolted conveys explosive, terrified leaping away or fleeing from confinement, whereas beetling off is colloquial, jaunty, or dismissive.
  • Trotted: Similar because both denote brisk pedestrian movement; differs because trotted implies an upright, rhythmic, and composed pace, lacking the insectoid, busy, low-slung quality of beetled.
  • Hustled: Similar because both imply urgent, fast-paced action; differs because hustled emphasizes aggressive striving, jostling, or commercial drive, whereas beetled focuses strictly on physical scurrying locomotion.
  • Jutted: Similar (in the topographical sense of beetling) because both denote projecting outward; differs because jutted is static and neutral, whereas beetled evokes a heavy, brooding, or menacing overhang.
