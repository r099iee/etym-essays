• Shone: Similar because it is the plain general verb for emitting light, as gleamed is for the snow-peaks; differs because 'shine' is neutral and colourless, with no hint of smallness, softness, or distance, whereas 'gleam' implies a clean, subdued point of light seen against gloom.
  • Glinted: Similar because it too names a brief emission of bright light, and the shortDefinition itself offers 'glinted' as an equivalent; differs because a glint is a single sudden reflected flash (of metal, glass, or an eye), while a gleam is steadier and may be light given out, not merely thrown back.
  • Glimmered: Similar because both describe a subdued, non-dazzling light; differs because a glimmer is weak, unsteady, and on the point of going out, while a gleam is brighter and more clearly defined.
  • Glittered: Similar because both are verbs of sparkling brightness; differs because glitter is hard, fractured, many-pointed sparkle like metal or sequins, whereas a gleam is a single soft, clean point of light.
  • Glistened: Similar because both describe the reflection of light; differs because glisten names light lying on a wet, oily, or sweaty surface, a sheen that gleam does not require.
  • Shimmered: Similar because both verbs evoke a delicate, tremulous light; differs because shimmer is a quivering, wavering effect on water or heat-haze, while a gleam is steady and does not tremble.
  • Glowed: Similar because both denote light without flame or harshness; differs because glow is steady, warm, and often sourceless (an ember, a face), whereas gleam is a small, cold, bright point usually seen at a distance.
  • Flashing: Similar because both describe light appearing suddenly; differs because a flash is violent, brief, and dazzling, the opposite of gleam's subdued, lingering softness.
  • Radiated: Similar because both involve the giving out of light; differs because radiate is a formal, technical word for light or heat spreading in all directions from a centre, without gleam's image of a small clear point against darkness.
  • Beamed: Similar because a gleam is like a soft beam of light, and the mnemonic pairs GLEAM with BEAM; differs because a beam is a directed shaft or ray of light, often brighter and more concentrated, while a gleam is a softer, more diffuse brilliance.
