"to dry up, parch, thirst"

Proto-Italic (c. 1000–500 BCE)
*torz-ē-
"to be parched, to dry out"

Classical Latin (c. 3rd c. BCE – 1st c. CE)
torrēre → torrens (stem torrent-)
"to scorch, parch; (of streams) boiling, roaring, rushing"
Present participle of torrēre; originally applied to parched summer wadis that suddenly turned into boiling flash floods.
