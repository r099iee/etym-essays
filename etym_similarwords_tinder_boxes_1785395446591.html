• Tinder: Similar because both involve the flammable material for starting a fire; differs because tinder is only the fuel - the charred cloth or dried fungus that catches the spark - while tinder-box names the whole kit of fuel, striker, and container.
  • Flint and steel: Similar because both are the means of striking a spark; differs because flint and steel names the implements, the tools that make the spark, while tinder-box names the box that holds them together with the tinder.
  • Fire-striker: Similar because both are fire-making tools; differs because a fire-striker is the steel used to strike sparks, a single implement, while a tinder-box is the whole container kit.
  • Matchbox: Similar because both are small boxes for fire-lighting materials; differs because a matchbox holds manufactured friction matches, the modern successor, while a tinder-box predates the match and holds tinder, flint, and steel.
  • Lighter: Similar because both are devices for making fire; differs because a lighter is a twentieth-century self-contained device using fuel and a sparking wheel, while a tinder-box is the pre-modern kit of separate parts.
  • Powder keg: Similar because both are metaphors for something ready to explode into conflict; differs because a powder keg is a literal barrel of gunpowder used as an image of imminent explosion, while a tinderbox is a box of easily kindled material - both mean 'ready to burst into flame', but the tinderbox image is about a spark, the powder-keg about the blast.
  • Flashpoint: Similar because both name the moment or place where conflict ignites; differs because flashpoint is the precise temperature at which something ignites or the precise moment of crisis, while a tinderbox is the situation that is already ready to ignite.
  • Fuse: Similar because both describe readiness to burst into flame; differs because a fuse is a cord that burns toward an explosion, implying a deliberate countdown, while a tinderbox is merely waiting, its ignition depending on a chance spark.
  • Kindling: Similar because both involve dry material for starting fires; differs because kindling is the small dry wood used to build a fire, while tinder is the finer material that catches the spark and the tinder-box is the box that holds it.
  • Sparking device: Similar because both produce the fire-starting spark; differs because a sparking device is a general technical term for any spark-maker, while a tinder-box is the specific historical kit of tinder, flint, and steel in a box.
