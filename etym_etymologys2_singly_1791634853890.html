Modern English (present)
singly
"one by one, individually,

Note: 'Singly' represents a hybrid morphological construction: a Romance lexical stem ('single', from Latin 'singulus' via Old French) combined with the productive native Germanic adverbial suffix '-ly' (from Proto-Germanic '*-līkō', Old English '-līce', meaning 'having the body, form, or manner of'). It is cognate with Latin 'simplex' ('simple') and Ancient Greek 'ἅπαξ' ('hapax', once).
