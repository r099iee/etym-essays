• Swarming: Similar because both depict dense, moving multitudes of living entities; differs because swarming carries an insect-like, chaotic, or agitated connotation of tightly packed, circling bodies, whereas teeming emphasizes generative fertility, organic richness, and overflowing abundance.
  • Abounding: Similar because both indicate existing in large numbers or great plentifulness; differs because abounding is a more formal, static, and peaceful description of wealth or resources (e.g., "abounding in grace"), lacking the raw kinetic writhing and fertile pulse of teeming.
  • Bustling: Similar because both describe lively, energetic human activity in places like markets or streets; differs because bustling emphasizes purposeful human movement, brisk scurrying, and business-like activity, while teeming conveys overwhelming crowd density and biological proliferation.
  • Prolific: Similar because both relate to high reproductive output and intellectual fruitfulness; differs because prolific is an adjective applied to the creator or parent (e.g., a "prolific author" or "prolific tree"), whereas teeming describes the populated environment, womb, or resulting swarm itself.
  • Overflowing: Similar because both involve exceeding the capacity of a container or space; differs because overflowing is primarily a physical or emotional metaphor of fluid spilling over the brim (e.g., "overflowing cup"), whereas teeming is intrinsically tied to living, multiplying organisms and dynamic energy.
  • Crawling: Similar because both describe surfaces densely populated with living creatures; differs because crawling carries an unpleasant, creepy, or vermin-laden connotation (e.g., "the floor was crawling with spiders"), whereas teeming is generally celebratory of boundless ecological and creative vitality.
  • Bristling: Similar because both describe places packed with dynamic elements; differs because bristling implies defensive tension, sharp edges, or aggressive readiness (e.g., "bristling with weapons/anger"), while teeming conveys fertile, fluid abundance.
