• Simpleton: Similar because both denote a foolish, intellectually deficient person; differs because simpleton is a more formal, Latinate term emphasizing gullibility and lack of worldly sophistication, while Tomnoddy has a comedic, rhythmic, and dialectal folk quality.
  • Noddy: Similar because it is the base component meaning fool; differs because noddy alone is older and can also refer to a 16th-century card game, whereas Tomnoddy is an intensified, personalized compound.
  • Blockhead: Similar because both criticize mental slowness; differs because blockhead implies a wooden, dense, and immovable skull incapable of learning.
  • Dunce: Similar because both describe poor learners; differs because dunce historically derives from the followers of philosopher John Duns Scotus and is specifically tied to academic or scholastic failure.
  • Nincompoop: Similar because both are whimsical, non-profane insults for silly people; differs because nincompoop emphasizes foolish behavior and silly antics rather than clumsy physical lumbering.
  • Buffoon: Similar because both involve ridiculousness; differs because a buffoon actively performs comical, undignified antics for attention.
  • Numskull: Similar because both insult intelligence; differs because numskull emphasizes numbness and physical density of the brain.
