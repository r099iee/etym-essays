*snā-

nutricia

↓

norrice

↓
Middle English (13th c.)
norice
"a nurse"
↓
English (14th c.)
nurse

Compound:
↓
English (15th c.)
wet-nurse

Note: The PIE root *snā- behind Latin nutrire is debated (some posit *snewH- "to flow" or *snu- "to suckle").
