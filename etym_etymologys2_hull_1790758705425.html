↓
Modern English (18th c.–present)
Hull

Note on Toponymic Hydronym: The city name *Hull* derives from the River Hull, first recorded c. 10th century as *Hul*, likely rooted in a pre-English Celtic hydronym cognate with Welsh *hullo* ("murky/muddy water"), subsequently assimilated phonetically to the Germanic word *hulu* / *hulle*.
