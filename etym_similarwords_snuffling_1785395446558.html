• Sniffing: Similar because both mean drawing air through the nose; differs because a sniff is short, quiet, and voluntary, usually of curiosity, while snuffling is heavier, wetter, and repetitive — a prolonged, laborious series of inhalations.
  • Snorting: Similar because both are audible nasal sounds; differs because a snort is a sudden, explosive blast of contempt or alarm, while snuffling is a slow, investigative drawing-in of scent with no sudden force.
  • Snoring: Similar because both name audible nasal breathing; differs because snoring is the resonant, unconscious breathing of sleep, while snuffling is the active, awake searching of an animal or a congested person.
  • Snooping: Similar because both can mean prying into hidden things, and snuffling about a place is the animal form of snooping; differs because snooping is specifically human, secretive investigation, while snuffling is physical, nasal scenting.
  • Nosing: Similar because both mean moving about seeking a scent or prying into something; differs because nosing is lighter and can be mere curiosity (nosing into affairs), while snuffling emphasizes the audible, wet, repeated breathing.
  • Sniffling: Similar because both are frequentative nasal words, close in sound and form; differs because sniffling usually names the small, repeated sniffing of a cold or of tears, while snuffling is heavier, wetter, and more often animal.
  • Smelling: Similar because both involve perceiving by the nose; differs because smelling is passive and neutral — the scent reaches you — while snuffling is active, noisy, and investigative, the deliberate drawing-in of air to find a scent.
  • Wheezing: Similar because both describe noisy, effortful breathing; differs because wheezing is the high, whistling breath of a damaged chest or exertion, while snuffling is the wet, congested, nasal sound of the snout and nose.
  • Puffing: Similar because both name audible, repeated breathing; differs because puffing is the breath of exertion, expelled noisily from the mouth, while snuffling is the drawn-in breath of the nose, wet and searching.
  • Mumbling: Similar because both describe a muffled, indistinct utterance or sound; differs because mumbling is confused speech through the mouth, while snuffling is nasal sound, either congested breathing or nasal, affected speech.
