• Marijuana: Similar because both name cannabis used as an intoxicant; differs because marijuana usually means the dried leaves and flowers, while hashish means the pressed resin, stronger and more concentrated.
  • Cannabis: Similar because both refer to the same plant and its preparations; differs because cannabis is the neutral botanical and legal umbrella term, while hashish names one traditional resin product.
  • Ganja: Similar because both are old-world words for potent cannabis flower preparations; differs because ganja belongs to Indian usage for the flowering tops, while hashish belongs to Arabic and Mediterranean usage for sieved or rubbed resin.
  • Charas: Similar because both are hand-made cannabis resins of high potency; differs because charas is specifically the Indian and Himalayan hand-rubbed resin, while hashish is the wider Middle Eastern and North African product.
  • Kif: Similar because both are North African cannabis products smoked for their narcotic effect; differs because kif is the Moroccan mixture of chopped herb with tobacco, while hashish is the pressed resin alone.
  • Bhang: Similar because both are traditional Indian and Islamic-world cannabis edibles and drinks; differs because bhang is a mild drink or paste of leaves and milk, while hashish is concentrated resin, far stronger.
  • Weed: Similar because both are casual names for cannabis as an intoxicant; differs because weed is modern slang for any herbal cannabis, while hashish is the older, more literary word with Oriental associations.
  • Opium: Similar because both are Eastern plant narcotics of 19th-century legend; differs because opium comes from poppies and brings heavy sleep, while hashish comes from cannabis and brings dreamy distortion of the senses.
