Note: the deeper origin of Latin omen (Old Latin osmen) is disputed: ancient grammarians linked it to os (mouth, utterance), while modern scholars often treat it as a word of unknown, possibly non-Indo-European (Etruscan-associated) origin; the chain above therefore starts from the attested Old Latin form rather than a reconstructed PIE root.
