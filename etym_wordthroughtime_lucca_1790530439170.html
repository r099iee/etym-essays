- Antiquity (c. 3rd c. BCE - 5th c. CE) - Ligurian/Etruscan settlement *Luk (marshy light clearing); becomes Roman colony Luca (180 BCE); famous site of the First Triumvirate conference (56 BCE) between Julius Caesar, Pompey, and Crassus.
- Early Middle Ages (6th-10th c.) - Capital of the Lombard Duchy of Tuscia and later the Carolingian Margraviate of Tuscany; strategic fortress along the Via Francigena pilgrimage route.
- Republic of Lucca (1160-1799) - Flourishes as an independent sovereign republic; motto 'Libertas'; European capital of luxury silk manufacture and international merchant banking.
- Napoleonic Era & Duchy (1805-1847) - Republic turned into Principality of Lucca and Piombino for Elisa Bonaparte (opening of Tolstoy's War and Peace); becomes Bourbon Duchy of Lucca (1815-1847) before joining Tuscany and unified Italy (1860).
- Modern & Contemporary Era (1860-present) - Famed worldwide for its intact Renaissance walls, olive oil, and musical legacy as the birthplace of Giacomo Puccini.
