• Patrician: Similar because both denote an aristocratic, high-born lineage possessing established ancestral wealth, pride, and civic dominance; differs because patrician is a universal sociological and historical noun/adjective applicable to Roman senators or European nobility, whereas Pyncheon is a specific Anglo-Norman proper name and a gothic literary archetype.
  • Dynast: Similar because both refer to members of an enduring, influential family line that perpetuates power and wealth across generations; differs because dynast typically designates hereditary political or royal rulers, whereas Pyncheon represents a merchant and magisterial family in colonial and post-colonial New England.
  • Maule: Similar as the opposing ancestral family inextricably intertwined with the Pyncheons throughout Hawthorne's *The House of the Seven Gables*; differs because the Maules represent the plebeian, wronged victims—executed for witchcraft and uttering the blood curse—whereas the Pyncheons represent the covetous, aristocratic persecutors.
  • Brahmin: Similar because both evoke the elite, long-established Anglo-Saxon upper crust of New England (the 'Boston Brahmins'); differs because Brahmin is a sociological label coined by Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr. emphasizing intellectual refinement and upright civic virtue, whereas Pyncheon carries dark gothic connotations of hypocrisy, inherited sin, and physical retribution.
  • Usurper: Similar because the fundamental sin defining the Pyncheon lineage is the wrongful seizure of Matthew Maule's homestead; differs because usurper is an abstract noun of criminal action rather than an ancestral family name.
  • Eponym: Similar because the name Pyncheon became the eponym for the ancestral estate, the 'Pyncheon Elm', and 'Pyncheon Street'; differs as an abstract linguistic category denoting any name giver.
  • Oligarch: Similar because Judge Pyncheon exercises concentrated, unaccountable economic and political influence in his community; differs because oligarch is a modern political science designation lacking the gothic, ancestral, and supernatural resonance of Hawthorne's family.
