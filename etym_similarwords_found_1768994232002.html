• Board: Similar in being provision supplied as part of employment; differs because "board" means meals specifically, while "found" means meals plus a place to sleep — the whole subsistence of the hire.
  • Lodging: Similar in being shelter supplied; differs because "lodging" names the sleeping quarters alone, while "found" bundles shelter together with food into the complete maintenance.
  • Keep: Similar in meaning the provision of everything a person needs; differs because "keep" ("earn one's keep") is general and not tied to a wage bargain, while "found" is the contractual board-and-lodging thrown in with wages.
  • Rations: Similar in being supplied food; differs because "rations" are a fixed, measured allowance with a military flavour, while "found" is the open-ended maintenance of the labour hire.
  • Victuals: Similar in naming food supplies; differs because "victuals" are food alone and old-fashioned, while "found" bundles food with the roof — the whole keep.
  • Subsistence: Similar in naming the minimum needed to live; differs because "subsistence" is the abstract means of staying alive, while "found" is the concrete, contracted food-and-lodging of the wage deal.
  • Maintenance: Similar in being support provided; differs because "maintenance" is the general legal term for support and upkeep, while "found" is the specific, old labour-market word for the hired man's keep.
  • Provisions: Similar in being supplies of food and necessaries; differs because "provisions" are stores laid in (for a voyage, an army), while "found" is the recurring daily maintenance thrown in with wages.
  • Allowance: Similar in being a fixed provision given in addition to wages; differs because an "allowance" is usually money or a set ration granted periodically, while "found" is the in-kind board and lodging of the hire.
  • Emoluments: Similar in being compensation beyond the bare wage; differs because "emoluments" are formal, usually monetary perquisites of office, while "found" is the plain, material keep of the labouring man.
