Note: the PIE stage *leis- is a traditional reconstruction (Pokorny) resting mainly on the Germanic comparative forms; the Germanic branch itself (*laisiz-, Gothic laisiza) is securely attested. The Old English phrase þȳ lǣs þe underwent contraction and reanalysis in Middle English, at which point the conjunction became an independent word; the spelling without final -e was fixed in the 16th century.
