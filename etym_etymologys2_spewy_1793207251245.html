*spīwaną

↓
Old English (9th c.)
spīwan
‘to spew, vomit’
↓
Middle English (14th c.)
spewen
‘to vomit; to emit’
↓
Modern English (dialectal, 19th c.)
spewy
‘oozing, marshy’

Note: the final step adds the productive adjectival suffix -y to the living verb spew; the adjective is first captured in provincial glossaries and Cobbett, so its exact birthday before the 19th c. is unrecorded rather than datable.
