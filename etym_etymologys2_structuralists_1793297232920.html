PIE (reconstructed, c. 4000 BCE)
*ster-
‘to spread, strew, heap up’
↓
Latin (classical, 1st c. BCE)
struere
‘to heap up, build, arrange’
↓
Latin (derived noun) (classical–late)
structura
‘fitting together, building’
↓
French (17th–19th c.)
structural
‘of arrangement, of structure’
↓
Modern English (19th c., -ist added)
Structuralists
‘members of structure-school’

Note: the PIE connection of Latin struere to *ster- is the standard handbook link via the strewn-heap image, though some handbooks phrase the root as extended *streu-; the Latin-to-modern chain itself is fully attested.
