• lauds: similar because both are the dawn offices of the Divine Office; differs because lauds is technically the office of praise at daybreak that followed and fused with matins and is named for its psalms of praise (laudes).
  • Morning Prayer: similar because both are the morning service of the Church; differs because "Morning Prayer" is the modern Anglican parish name while "Matins" retains the traditional office's technical content and night-office history.
  • nocturns: similar because both belong to the night-division of the old office; differs because nocturns are the internal sections of matins sung in the dark hours, not the whole office.
  • vigils: similar because both are night watches of prayer; differs because vigils originally named the whole night office (and wake before feasts) and has a secular sense of keeping watch.
  • prime: similar because both are early offices of the day; differs because prime was the first "little hour" (about 6 a.m.), later and shorter than matins, suppressed by Vatican II.
  • dawn service / sunrise service: similar because both are early-morning worship; differs because these are modern, non-canonical, and lack the fixed psalmody and readings of matins.
  • matutinal (rite): similar because both derive from Latin matutinus "of the morning"; differs because "matutinal" is an adjective for anything of the morning, not a service.
