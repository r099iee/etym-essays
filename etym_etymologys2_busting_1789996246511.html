Note 1 (debated metathesis): bust is traditionally explained as burst with metathesis (transposition of /r/: burst → bust, as in third → thrid, horse → hoss). Some scholars argue for convergent reinforcement from Scandinavian busta or Low German busten, but metathesis remains the standard OED account, first firmly dated 1764 in America.

Note 2 (homograph disambiguation): sculpture bust is unrelated — Latin bustum (funeral pyre, tomb) → Italian busto (torso, trunk, c. 1500s) → French buste → English bust (1690s) "sculpted torso". It must not be confused with bust = burst above.
