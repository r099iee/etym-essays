- [18th c. Enlightenment] - formally codified in scientific taxonomy by Carl Linnaeus in 'Philosophia Botanica' (1751) and Joseph Gaertner (1788) to define any accessory seed envelope distinct from the pericarp - [perception: precise morphological taxon essential for systematic botanical classification]
- [19th c. Victorian Era] - integrated into evolutionary biology by Charles Darwin and botanical anatomists studying animal-mediated seed dispersal (zoochory) and the spice trade - [perception: celebrated evolutionary adaptation linking angiosperm reproduction with animal frugivory]
- [Late 20th c.] - popularized in commercial food processing and global culinary literature to designate pomegranate seeds and tropical fruits (lychee, mangosteen, rambutan) - [perception: technical term transitioning into high-end gastronomy and food packaging]
- [21st c.] - widely recognized in supermarket produce marketing, nutritional science, and agronomy as the edible, antioxidant-rich jewel of the pomegranate - [perception: familiar culinary and nutritional buzzword for healthy, flavorful fruit sacs]
