Note: Latin gnatus is the older participle of nasci “to be born”; the initial g was lost in the simple form natus but preserved after the prefix in cognatus. English keeps both layers: native and natal show the worn-down form, while cognate, agnate and pregnant preserve the older cluster. The PIE root *ǵenh₁- is secure, though its relationship to the similar root *ǵneh₃- “to know” (Greek gignōskō) is debated.
