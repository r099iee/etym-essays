• Loads: Similar because both mean things carried; differs because loads is more neutral and physical, while burdens carries a stronger sense of heaviness, strain, and often figurative weight.
  • Weights: Similar because both involve heaviness; differs because weights emphasises measurable mass (weights on a scale, weights for fitness), while burdens emphasises what is carried and borne.
  • Packs: Similar because both are loads carried by a person or animal; differs because a pack is a specific, organised container (backpack, pack animal's load), while a burden can be any carried weight, including emotional.
  • Responsibilities: Similar because both describe obligations that weigh on a person; differs because responsibilities are neutral duties, while burdens emphasises the oppressiveness and difficulty of those duties.
  • Cares: Similar because both describe worries that weigh on the mind; differs because cares is purely emotional and gentler, while burdens can be physical, financial, or social and is heavier.
  • Cargoes: Similar because both are transported loads; differs because cargoes is a technical commercial term for goods shipped by sea or freight, while burdens is the general word for anything borne.
