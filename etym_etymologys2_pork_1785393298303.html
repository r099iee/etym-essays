Debate: English pork was borrowed from Anglo-Norman/Old French porc, hence the French-derived name for the meat while the living animal keeps native pig/swine — a legacy of the Norman Conquest. The PIE root *pórḱos is confined to Italic, Celtic and Germanic, and some scholars doubt it originally meant "young pig" specifically.
