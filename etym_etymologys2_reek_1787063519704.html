Note: the PIE root *h₁rewg- "to belch out" also yields Greek ereúgesthai "to belch, disgorge" and Latin ructāre "to belch"; the Germanic reflex is limited to the nominal meaning "smoke," and some scholars allow for sound-symbolic influence in the Germanic formation.
