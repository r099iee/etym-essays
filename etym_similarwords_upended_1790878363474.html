• Overturned: Similar because both denote flipping upside down or reversing a decision; differs because overturned is legal and formal (e.g., overturning a court verdict), whereas upended carries a more dynamic, chaotic, and physical kinetic energy.
  • Disrupted: Similar because both involve breaking order; differs because disrupted focuses on interrupting the smooth flow or continuity of a process (e.g., disrupting supply chains), while upended implies a complete structural inversion or toppling.
  • Subverted: Similar because both undermine established power; differs because subverted implies insidious, covert, or gradual undermining from within (subversion), whereas upended is overt, dramatic, and revolutionary.
  • Toppled: Similar because both describe knocking something over; differs because toppled specifically emphasizes tall, top-heavy structures, statues, or political regimes falling from power, while upended can apply to horizontal systems set on their ends.
  • Capsized: Similar because both involve flipping over; differs because capsized is almost exclusively nautical or aquatic (overturning boats), while upended applies universally to objects, concepts, and lives.
  • Revolutionized: Similar because both describe radical transformation; differs because revolutionized is predominantly positive and focused on forward-looking progress, while upended highlights the initial shock, chaos, and total inversion of norms.
