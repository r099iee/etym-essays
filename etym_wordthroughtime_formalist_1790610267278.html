- 16th-17th c. ceremony keeper - formalist as observer of forms, rites, etiquette, often Catholic or courtly
- 19th c. art-philosophy label - formalist versus realist; form over content as aesthetic insult or badge
- 1910s-1930s Russian Formalism - proud scientific school: Shklovsky, Jakobson study literariness and device
- Mid-20th c. New Criticism - Anglo-American formalist close reading as university method and moral discipline
- Modern culture wars - formalist as either rigorous analyst or empty pedant, depending on historicist or creative backlash
