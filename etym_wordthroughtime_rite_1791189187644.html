- Correct customary form in Rome - legalistic and priestly: doing the sacred in exactly the sanctioned way
- Contested ceremony in Reformation England - polemical and sectarian: popish rites versus true worship
- Universal primitive category in Victorian anthropology - scholarly and distancing: savage rites as data for evolution of religion
- Dignified heritage or personal routine today - dual and reflexive: my Rite as identity, my morning rite as affectionate irony
