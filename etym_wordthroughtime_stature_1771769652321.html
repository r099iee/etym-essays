- [13th-14th century physical height] - [Names natural bodily height and upright build; neutral and measurable]
- [17th century dignified presence] - [Gains the idea of a commanding or imposing bodily presence; 'of great stature' flatters the person, not just the measurement]
- [18th-19th century moral and intellectual eminence] - [Goes metaphorical: importance and reputation achieved by ability — one's standing in the world]
- [20th century to present dual life] - [Both senses thrive; the physical stays objective, while the metaphorical (moral stature, professional stature) carries the weight of earned reputation]
