Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*kōw- / *kaw- / *kewh₂-
"to strike, beat, chop, forge"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*hawwaną (p.p. *hawwanaz)
"to chop, cut, strike forcefully"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
hēawan (p.p. ġehēawen)
"to chop down, hack, dress stone"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–14th c.)
hewen (p.p. hewen / y-hewen)
"cut, shaped with axe or chisel"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c. to present)
hewn
"shaped or felled by striking blows"
