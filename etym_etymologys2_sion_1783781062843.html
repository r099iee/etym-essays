Debate: The Hebrew origin of צִיּוֹן is uncertain: possibly from a root meaning "dry, parched", possibly an old Jebusite fortress-name, possibly a pre-Hebrew name; none is established. The Septuagint vocalization Σιών fixed the Greek and Latin forms.
