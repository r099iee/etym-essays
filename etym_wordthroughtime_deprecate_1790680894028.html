- Latin deprecari - to avert by prayer, to beg off punishment, religious and legal intercession
- 17th c. English borrowing - to pray against evil, to plead earnestly against a course, literary and theological
- 18th-19th c. generalization - to express strong disapproval, to deplore, formal moral censure
- Self-deprecation branch - deprecating manner as modest belittling of self to ward off pride, social virtue
- 20th c. confusion with depreciate - to belittle or lower in value, common but criticized overlap
- Late 20th c. computing sense - to mark as deprecated, discouraged but working, warning before removal, now dominant in tech
