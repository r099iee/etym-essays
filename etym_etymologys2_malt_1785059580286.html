MALT
"Grain (usually barley) that has been
germinated in water and then dried, used
especially in brewing ale or beer."

Debate: The PIE root *mel- "to soften, dissolve" fits malt as grain softened in water; some scholars instead derive it from *melh₂- "to grind" or connect the base to PGmc *meltan "to melt", so the precise root remains debated.
