Khuzdul (invented language; Tolkien's notes)
*gund(u)
"underground hall, delve"
(root of the Dwarvish tongue, modelled by Tolkien
on Semitic triliteral patterns; reconstruction per Salo 2004)
↓
Khuzdul compound (before 1937)
Gundabad
"delve-mountain; mountain of the
underground halls"
(second element -bad unpublished,
of unknown sense; the whole behaves
as an opaque proper name)
↓
English (proper noun) (The Hobbit, 1937)
GUNDABAD
"the great mountain of the North,
orc-capital"

Note: Gundabad is a constructed name with no real-world or PIE ancestry - the language itself was invented by Tolkien, so there is no attested pre-history to recover. The chain above reflects Tolkien's own Khuzdul material and scholarly reconstruction; the sense of the second element is honestly unknown, and no stage has been invented to fill it.
