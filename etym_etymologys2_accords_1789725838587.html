Note: PIE *kerd- heart is secure (cf. Greek kardia, Sanskrit hrd, English heart). Vulgar Latin *accordare ad- + cor is reconstructed but universally accepted (OED, Etymonline, de Vaan under cor). Old French acorder already shows both agree and grant senses. Final -s is regular Middle English third-person singular. No serious debate on the chain.
