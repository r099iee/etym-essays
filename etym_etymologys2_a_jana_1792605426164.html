English (Indological translation)
Añjana
"one of the eight cosmic guardian bull elephants supporting the earth"

Note on comparative philology: Sanskrit añjana is an exact morphological cognate of Latin unguentum ("salve, ointment"), both tracing to Proto-Indo-European *h₃engʷ- ("to smear, anoint"). While in the Western Indo-European branch (Latin > Romance > English) the root evolved into Christian sacramental and pharmaceutical terms like anoint, unction, and unguent, in the Indo-Iranian branch it retained both its literal cosmetic sense of eye-salve (añjana) and was mythologically personified in the lustrous, pitch-black hide of the cosmic guardian bull elephant.
