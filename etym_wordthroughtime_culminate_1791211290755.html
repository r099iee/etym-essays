- Architectural summit - Latin culmen means rooftop, ridge-beam, physical top; concrete and honorific but not yet verbal process.
- Astronomical transit - Late Latin to 17th-century culminare means a star reaching meridian; technical, measurable, prestigious science.
- General historical climax - 18th-19th centuries extend to empires, careers, arts culminating in glory; perception becomes narrative and teleological, history as ascent.
- Institutional ritual - Commencements, festivals, campaigns culminate in ceremonies; now marks planned fulfillment and celebratory closure.
- Modern formal closer - Today staple of journalism and academia for decisive endings, sometimes overused for any end; retains elevated tone versus plain end, with purist debate over transitive use.
