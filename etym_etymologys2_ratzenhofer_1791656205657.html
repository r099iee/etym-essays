Note: Folk etymology often erroneously associates the prefix with the southern German dialect word Ratz (meaning "rat" or "dormouse"). In historical Bavarian and Austrian toponymy, however, Ratzen- unequivocally derives from the Old High German personal name Razo (a pet form of compound names based on *rēd-, "counsel"), designating the original medieval estate founder.
