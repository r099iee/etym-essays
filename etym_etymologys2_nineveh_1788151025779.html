Note on the debate: The underlying meaning of Akkadian Ninua is disputed. Scholars commonly connect it (in the form Ninâ) with the Sumerian name of the city’s patron goddess ‘Lady’ (possibly ‘Lady of the Waters’), later identified with Ištar; others argue for a non-Sumerian (Hurrian or substrate) origin. No confident translation of the city’s name can be asserted. The classical story of a legendary founder Ninus is folklore, not etymology, though it shaped Greek and Roman transmission of the name.
