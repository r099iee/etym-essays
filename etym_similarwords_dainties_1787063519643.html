• Delicacies: Similar because both refer to rare, choice, and highly prized foods; differs because delicacies is a broader, formal term encompassing costly or culturally exotic items (such as caviar, escargot, or bird's nest soup) that may not be sweet or miniature, whereas dainties specifically emphasizes smallness, sweetness, delicate crafting, and visual charm.
  • Sweetmeats: Similar because both refer to sugary, confectionary treats; differs because sweetmeats is strictly limited to candied fruits, nuts, marzipan, and sugar-based confectionery, whereas dainties can encompass fine savoury bites, tarts, and rare culinary titbits.
  • Titbits / Tidbits: Similar because both denote small, choice morsels of food; differs because titbits emphasizes small size and casual snacking (and frequently refers metaphorically to small pieces of gossip or news), whereas dainties carries a much stronger connotation of luxury, refinement, and artistic confectionery.
  • Treats: Similar because both refer to enjoyable, special food indulgences; differs because treats is an everyday, informal term applicable to any casual snack, children's reward, or pleasant activity, lacking the vintage, aristocratic refinement of dainties.
  • Confections: Similar because both describe elaborate, sweet culinary creations; differs because confections is a technical pastry term focused on sugar formulation, cake decoration, or elaborate artistic fabrication, while dainties focuses on the pleasurable sensory experience of the eater.
  • Viands: Similar because both refer to choice provisions and articles of food; differs because viands is an archaic, formal term for substantial food provisions, meat dishes, and hearty banquet courses rather than light, sweet, delicate finger foods.
