Note on Etymological Convergence: The modern English noun pallet merges two historical word-families: (1) the straw bed sense detailed in the primary chain above from Latin palea ("straw"); and (2) the flat tool/platform sense borrowed from French palette (diminutive of pale "spade, shovel", from Latin pala "spade, shoulder blade"), which gave rise to the potter's tool, clock escapement pawl, and 20th-century industrial freight platform.
