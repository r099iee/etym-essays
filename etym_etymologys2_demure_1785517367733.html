demure

↓ English (present)
DEMURE
"reserved, modest,
and shy"
Debate: the origin is uncertain; the shift from
"delayed / stayed" to "calm, grave" is obscure, and
an alternative derivation from Old French de meur
"of mature years, sober" (from Latin dē mātūrus)
has also been proposed.
