- 14th century - 'like a dog': base, contemptuous, doggish behavior
- 16th-18th century - settles on stubborn, grim tenacity: a dogged determination that will not let go, still with a hint of obstinacy
- 19th century - acquires approval when paired with effort: 'dogged determination' becomes praise for unbreakable perseverance
- Contemporary - largely admiring; applied to loyalty, pursuit, and persistence, with the faint animal metaphor of not releasing a grip
