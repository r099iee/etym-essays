- Heavy iron nail for a wooden coffin - neutral trade hardware, sombre but practical, associated with undertakers and carpenters
- Dickensian dead metaphor for lifelessness - wry and literary, the coffin-nail as the deadest ironmongery, connoting ultimate inertness
- Late-Victorian slang for a cigarette - jocular-morbid, smokers joking that each smoke drives a nail into their coffin
- Twentieth-century public-health moralism - disapproving and cautionary, coffin nail printed on anti-smoking posters
- Modern figurative extension to any ruinous habit - wry and proverbial, each drink or late night another nail in the coffin
