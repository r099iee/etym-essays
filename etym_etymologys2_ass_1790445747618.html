Second, distinct chain (convergent homograph): PIE *ors- "buttocks" → Old English ears → Middle English ars (arse) → dialectal loss of -r- before -s- → American ass "backside" (1860 nautical slang, popular from 1930). Note: Latin asinus is itself judged a loanword (de Vaan); most Indo-European ass-words are borrowings, likely from an Asia Minor source akin to Sumerian ansu and Greek onos.
