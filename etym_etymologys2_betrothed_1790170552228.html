Note: the prefix be- continues Old English be- from Proto-Germanic *bi- meaning "around, about, thoroughly", making betroth literally "to thoroughly pledge truth". No debated root; the derivation from PIE *deru- via tree / true / troth / trust is universally accepted (OED, Etymonline, Kroonen).
