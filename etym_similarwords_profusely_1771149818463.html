• lavishly: Similar because both mean generously, in large amounts; differs because lavishly specifically implies luxurious expenditure of money or resources, while profusely can describe any outflow (blood, sweat, words, thanks) without wealth implied.
  • copiously: Similar because both mean in large quantity; differs because copiously emphasizes sheer volume or abundance (copious notes, copious tears), while profusely keeps the image of a stream or flood pouring forth.
  • abundantly: Similar because both mean in plentiful supply; differs because abundantly is a neutral, static term for a large amount (crops grow abundantly), while profusely suggests active, unstoppable outflow.
  • generously: Similar because both can mean giving without stinting; differs because generously stresses goodwill and open-handedness in giving, while profusely stresses sheer quantity, regardless of motive.
  • extravagantly: Similar because both suggest going beyond what is needed; differs because extravagantly implies wastefulness or excess, while profusely is often positive or neutral.
  • richly: Similar because both can mean amply or abundantly; differs because richly suggests value, quality, or wealth (richly deserved), while profusely emphasizes volume and flow.
  • plentifully: Similar because both mean in large amounts; differs because plentifully is neutral and static, while profusely is dynamic and fluid.
  • unstintingly: Similar because both mean without holding back; differs because unstintingly emphasizes effort and lack of restriction, while profusely emphasizes quantity and flow.
  • excessively: Similar because both can mean beyond the ordinary amount; differs because excessively is negative and implies blameworthy excess, while profusely is usually neutral or positive.
  • unreservedly: Similar because both suggest expression without holding back; differs because unreservedly is about lack of reservation (emotional openness), while profusely is about sheer quantity.
