- Early borrowed sense (c.1300) - soft, tender, easily bent materials; admired craft quality in leather and boughs
- Courtly-moral sense (14th-17th c.) - submissive fawning flatterer; supple knees kneeling too readily; ambivalent to negative
- Graceful-agile sense (17th-19th c.) - athletic dance-like litheness; positive aesthetic of youth, horsemanship, eloquence
- Industrial-material sense (19th-20th c.) - tempered alloyed metals and plastics stronger yet suppler; engineering virtue
- Modern wellness and systems sense - suppler joints, skin, minds, markets; wholly positive ideal of healthy adaptability
