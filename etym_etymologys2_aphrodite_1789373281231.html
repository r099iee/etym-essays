Pre-Greek / Semitic Substrate (c. 2nd millennium BCE)
*‘Aštart / *Ashtoreth
"Northwest Semitic goddess of love, sex, and war"
Note: Historically adapted via Phoenician seafaring trade into Mycenaean/Archaic Greek; folk-etymologized by ancient Greeks from ἀφρός (aphrós, 'sea foam').
