- Early 16th-century import marvel - rare translucent Chinese vessels for princes, connoting exotic luxury and almost magical thinness.
- Late 16th to 17th-century collector term - cabinets of porcelains signify wealth, global trade reach and connoisseurship among elites.
- Early 18th-century scientific prize - after Meissen cracks hard-paste recipe, porcelains signify European technical mastery and court rivalry.
- 19th-century industrial and domestic term - mass-produced porcelains bring refined tableware to middle classes, shifting from marvel to respectable commodity.
- Modern museum and figurative term - porcelains evoke heritage collections and auction prestige, while porcelain as adjective evokes flawless pale delicacy and brittle elegance.
