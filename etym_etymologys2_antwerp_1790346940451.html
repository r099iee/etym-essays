Antwerp
Note on folk etymology: Popular municipal folklore derives the name from Dutch hand-werpen ("hand-throwing"), celebrating the mythical hero Silvius Brabo who cut off the giant Druon Antigonus's hand and cast it into the Scheldt. Toponymic scholarship confirms the authentic linguistic root as the Germanic compound *anda-werp ("promontory thrown up against the bank").
