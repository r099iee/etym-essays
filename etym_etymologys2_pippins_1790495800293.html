Note: Old French pepin is of uncertain deeper origin. The derivation via Vulgar Latin *pepinus from Latin pepo (from Greek pepon, ripe melon) is traditional but debated; others treat pepin as expressive formation from *pip- for a small round object (cf. pip, pigeon?). The French to English borrowing itself is secure; only the pre-French root is disputed.
