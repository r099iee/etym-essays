Note: overhung is the irregular strong past tense and past participle of overhang.
Debate: the deeper origin of hang is uncertain — PGmc *hanhaną is sometimes linked to PIE *ḱenk- "to hang" (cf. Latin cunctārī), so the *ḱonk- reconstruction is not secure. Modern hang also blends Old English hōn (transitive, strong) with hangian (intransitive, weak), reinforced by Old Norse.
