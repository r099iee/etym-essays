Note: The ultimate etymology of Greek *korállion* is debated among classical philologists. It is widely considered a loanword from a pre-Greek Mediterranean substrate or an ancient Semitic maritime root (compare Hebrew *gōrāl*, "pebble, lot drawn in casting lots", referring to the small smooth gravel-like pieces of polished red coral gathered along Levantine and North African shores). The Latin, Old French, and Middle English transmission chain is direct and undisputed.
