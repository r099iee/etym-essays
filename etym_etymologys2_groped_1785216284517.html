Note: Old English grāpian comes from
the o-grade of *grīpaną, not directly from
a separate *grēpaną.
Debate: the PIE root is also given as
*ǵʰreb- "to seize, grasp" in older
scholarship; the connection between
*ǵʰreyb- and Germanic *grīpaną is
widely accepted but not entirely certain.
