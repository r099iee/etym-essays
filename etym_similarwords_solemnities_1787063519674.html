• Ceremonies: Similar because both denote formal observances and public rites; differs because "solemnities" adds the charge of gravity, dignity, and (often) religious fixing, while "ceremonies" is the neutral, general term.
  • Rites: Similar because both can name religious observances; differs because "rites" points to the prescribed ritual actions themselves, whereas "solemnities" names the whole formal occasion and its celebrations.
  • Rituals: Similar because both involve set ceremonial practice; differs because "rituals" emphasises fixed sequences of actions (even private or habitual ones), while "solemnities" are grand public occasions.
  • Observances: Similar because both mean the keeping of customs or feasts; differs because "observances" is more technical and detached, lacking the festive grandeur of "solemnities."
  • Celebrations: Similar in the festive sense; differs because "celebrations" is broad, cheerful, and light, whereas "solemnities" is grave and dignified (or ironically pompous).
  • Festivities: Similar because both can describe ceremonial merry-making; differs because "festivities" is purely joyful and informal, with none of the religious or sombre weight of "solemnities."
  • Pageantry: Similar because both involve impressive display; differs because "pageantry" stresses spectacle, costume, and show rather than rites and gravity.
  • Formalities: Similar because both describe the procedural apparatus of an occasion; differs because "formalities" is bureaucratic and often mildly dismissive ("mere formalities"), while "solemnities" is imposing and dignified.
  • Pomp: Similar because both involve ceremonial grandeur; differs because "pomp" is an abstract quality of display (often empty), whereas "solemnities" are concrete ceremonies with serious weight.
