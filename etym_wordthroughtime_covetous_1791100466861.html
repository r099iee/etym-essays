- Roman - Cupidus / cupiditas as eager desire, good or bad - neutral to ambitious
- Early Christian - Cupiditas as disordered love vs caritas - sinful, theological
- Medieval England - Coveitise as Deadly Sin, covetous as sinner-label - penitential, dreaded
- 1611 KJV - Thou shalt not covet fixes English moral sense - biblical, absolute, thunderous
- 18th-19th century - Covetous old miser as stock character - literary, Dickensian, miserly
- Present day - Literary word for envious greed - formal, condemnatory, biblical echo
