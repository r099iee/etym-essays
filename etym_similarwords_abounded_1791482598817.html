• Teemed: similar in being full of living things; differs by taking with and implying swarming, moving multitude rather than mere quantity.
  • Swarmed: similar in dense presence; differs by its image of insects or crowds in motion, and it is more concrete and dynamic.
  • Proliferated: similar in increasing abundantly; differs by stressing multiplication over time rather than a state of plenty.
  • Prevailed: similar in being widespread; differs by stressing dominance and influence rather than numerical abundance.
  • Was rife: similar in being widespread, especially of something bad; differs by its strongly negative tone and its fixed association with rumours, corruption and disease.
  • Flourished: similar in thriving plentifully; differs by implying health and success, not merely quantity.
  • Thrived: similar in prospering abundantly; differs by referring to growth and wellbeing of the subject itself.
  • Overflowed: similar in the original liquid image; differs by keeping the physical sense of spilling over a boundary.
  • Brimmed: similar in being full to the edge; differs by its container metaphor and its usual construction with with.
  • Was plentiful: the plain paraphrase; differs by being neutral and unstylised, with no literary or biblical colour.
  • Was abundant: similar in meaning; differs as the adjectival paraphrase, which is more common in modern English than the verb.
  • Was thick with: similar in dense presence; differs as a vivid informal phrase suggesting a physical mass.
  • Was awash with: similar in the water metaphor; differs by suggesting an excess that is hard to control.
  • Pullulated: similar in teeming; differs as a rare biological word for rapid multiplication, almost always of organisms.
  • Superabounded: similar but stronger; differs by explicitly meaning "abounded to excess", and it is archaic.
