hindrian

HINDERED
"past tense & past participle:

Note: The semantic development runs from the spatial "behind" through "to put behind" to "to hold back"; the Old English "injure, harm" sense probably reflects striking from behind and died out in Middle English. The same Germanic stem survives in Gothic hindar, Old High German hintar, German hinter "behind", and Old Norse hindra "to hinder".
