Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*(s)pen- / *pend-
"to pull, stretch, spin;
hence to weigh, hang down"
↓
Proto-Italic (c. 1500 BCE)
*pendō
"to weigh, hang"
↓
Latin (3rd c. BCE)
pendere
"to hang, suspend, weigh out"
↓
Latin (2nd c. BCE)
pondus (gen. ponderis)
"a weight, heaviness, burden"
↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
ponderare
"to weigh, balance, consider"
↓
Latin (1st c. CE)
praeponderare
"to outweigh, outbalance,
surpass in weight or value"
↓
Late Latin (4th c. CE)
praeponderantia
"superiority in weight or power"
↓
Early Modern English (1630s)
preponderance
"superiority in weight, numbers,
importance, or legal evidence"
