Note: the exact Germanic proto-form behind glint is not securely reconstructable; it sits in a family of similar formations (*glent-, *glant-, *glint-) all expressing a glancing light. The derivation from *ǵʰel- through a Scandinavian verb is widely accepted, but the intermediate form is inferred, not attested.
