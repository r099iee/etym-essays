Latin (c. 1st c. CE)
barbarus / pravus
"foreign, fierce" / "crooked, unruly, wild"
↓
Vulgar Latin / Old Italian (c. 12th–13th c.)
bravo
"wild, bold, courageous, fierce"
↓
Italian (c. 16th–17th c.)
bravura
"bravery, spirited dash, virtuosity, daring skill"
↓
Modern English (18th c.–present)
bravura
"brilliant virtuoso display; dazzling artistic execution"
Note: Romance 'bravo' blends Latin 'barbarus' (untamed) and 'pravus' (fierce/wild).
