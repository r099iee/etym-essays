• Predicting: Similar because both mean "stating a future event in advance"; differs because predicting is neutral, everyday and secular, while prophesying claims (or mock-claims) inspired authority.
  • Foretelling: Similar because both mean "telling beforehand"; differs because foretelling is archaic-literary and neutral about the source of knowledge, while prophesying implies divine or prophetic inspiration.
  • Forecasting: Similar because both mean "estimating a future outcome"; differs because forecasting is technical and data-based (weather, economics), the opposite pole from inspired prophecy.
  • Foreseeing: Similar because both involve knowing the future; differs because foreseeing is about perception (seeing ahead), not utterance — one may foresee without prophesying.
  • Auguring: Similar because both mean "predicting from signs"; differs because augury is specifically reading omens (bird flight, entrails) in ancient religion, while prophesying is direct inspired speech.
  • Divining: Similar because both mean "discovering hidden or future knowledge"; differs because divining implies technique (rods, cards, intuition), while prophesying implies inspiration from beyond.
  • Prognosticating: Similar because both mean "predicting, especially outcomes"; differs because prognosticate is formal and clinical (medical prognosis) and secular, while prophesying is sacred or mock-sacred.
  • Presaging: Similar because both mean "indicating something to come"; differs because presage is used of omens and events that foreshadow (the storm presaged disaster), not of a person speaking.
  • Portending: Similar because both relate to future events; differs because portend describes what a sign means (clouds portend rain), while prophesying is an act of speech by a person.
  • Soothsaying: Similar because both mean "foretelling the future"; differs because soothsaying is an archaic, slightly superstitious word for professional fortune-telling, while prophesying carries religious weight.
  • Vaticinating: Similar because both mean "prophesying"; differs because vaticinate is a rare, learned Latinate synonym with no religious warmth — purely secular and bookish.
  • Preaching: Similar because both can mean "speaking inspired religious truth"; differs because preaching is teaching and exhortation to a congregation, while prophesying (biblically) is Spirit-inspired utterance that may include prediction.
  • Forewarning: Similar because both involve announcing what will come; differs because forewarning specifically announces danger to enable avoidance, while prophesying may announce anything.
  • Heralding: Similar because both mean "announcing something coming"; differs because heralding is used of events or signs announcing (heralding a new era), not of inspired speech.
