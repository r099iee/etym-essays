Note: The personal name Gumpel arose in southern German dialects through nasal assimilation of Gunt- to Gump- before the labial stop of -pold / -bald, followed by hypocoristic suffixation with -el. Combined with the Slavic patronymic suffix -owicz in Poland, it yielded the hereditary family name of sociologist Ludwig Gumplowicz.
