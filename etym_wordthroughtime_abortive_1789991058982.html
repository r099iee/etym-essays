- Latin abortivus: pertaining to miscarriage - clinical, sorrowful, a birth before its time
- 16th-century English: premature birth, untimely offspring - obstetric and mournful, used of children and plants
- 17th–18th century: transferred to plans and enterprises - an abortive plot, design or rebellion; disappointment enters politics
- 19th-century aesthetic and scientific use (Pater, Darwin, medicine) - abortive organs, abortive renaissances; neutral-technical alongside literary-tragic
- Modern use: failed-to-complete or disease-arresting - formal for fruitless attempts; technical for abortive therapy that stops an attack
