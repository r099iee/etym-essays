- Medieval implement - From the 14th century wheelbarrow (wheel + barrow) names a one-wheeled handcart with two rear handles, a design that has remained essentially unchanged for six hundred years.
- Stable compound spelling - The closed compound wheelbarrow standardises over time from the older wheel-barrow, transparently built from two native Germanic elements.
- Modern everyday and comic use - Today the wheelbarrow is a garden staple, and in children's games the word names the comic 'wheelbarrow race', keeping its plain, practical, homely register.
