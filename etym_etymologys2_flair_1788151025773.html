Note on nuance: the "discernment" sense dates from the 19th century, the "elegance" sense from the early 20th. The dominant scholarly account runs through Latin frāgrāre via a Vulgar Latin *flāgrāre blended with flagrare "to blaze" (the two verbs were easily confused); English flare (to blaze) is a separate word from Old Norse, and the two are only homophones, not relatives.
