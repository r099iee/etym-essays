Note: the second element mouth is secure (Old English mutha from Proto-Germanic munthaz, mouth, opening). The first element Yare is a pre-English river name adopted by Saxons; its meaning is debated (babbling, yew-river, and eddy proposals all lack decisive attestation), so no PIE root is asserted for it. Great was added in late medieval use to distinguish Norfolk Yarmouth from Wight Yarmouth.
