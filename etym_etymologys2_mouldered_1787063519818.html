Note (debate/nuance): The OED derives the verb from mould "loose earth" plus the frequentative suffix -er- (the same formative as in wander from wenden). An older minority view connected it with mould in the sense of "mildew, fungus"; the two derivations may have reinforced each other. American English spells the word moldered.
