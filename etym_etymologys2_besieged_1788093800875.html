Note: The Vulgar Latin *absediāre is a reconstruction justified by the Romance continuum (Old French asségier, Italian assediare, Spanish asediar); the ab-/ob- prefix fluctuation is debated. The English verb is a home-made formation — the Germanic prefix be- on the French-derived verb segen — running parallel to Old French assiéger.
