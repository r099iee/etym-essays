• Content: Similar because it is the same root and means satisfied; differs because content is used mainly after a verb ("she is content"), while contented works naturally before nouns ("a contented smile") and is the fuller, more adjectival form.
  • Satisfied: Similar because both mean one's wants have been met; differs because satisfied implies an expectation or standard has been fulfilled (more active and evaluative), whereas contented describes a calm, settled inner state without complaint.
  • Pleased: Similar because both signal a positive state; differs because pleased usually needs an external cause ("pleased with the gift") and is more momentary, while contented is durable and self-sufficient.
  • Happy: Similar because both express well-being; differs because happy ranges from joy to mere luck ("a happy accident") and is stronger and more demonstrative, whereas contented is quieter, steadier, and focused on the absence of want.
  • Gratified: Similar because both mean satisfaction; differs because gratified suggests delight at having a specific wish granted and has a more formal, self-conscious tone.
  • Fulfilled: Similar because both describe a person whose needs are met; differs because fulfilled implies deep purpose and realised potential (a higher bar), while contented is modest daily satisfaction.
  • Serene: Similar because both convey peace; differs because serene describes an unruffled temperament regardless of circumstances, while contented specifically involves having one's desires met.
  • Comfortable: Similar because both can mean at ease; differs because comfortable is chiefly physical and material ("a comfortable chair, a comfortable income"), whereas contented is mental and emotional.
  • Untroubled / Unworried: Similar because both denote freedom from disturbance; differs because they are negative descriptions (absence of worry), while contented is a positive state of satisfaction.
  • Resigned: Similar because both can involve accepting one's lot; differs because resigned carries a tinge of reluctant surrender, while contented is willing and pleasant.
