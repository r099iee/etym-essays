Note on the debate: The origin of frill is unknown. The OED marks its etymology obscure: the word surfaces in the 1590s with no recorded ancestor, and suggestions of a connection with dialectal forms, onomatopoeia, or a Dutch/Flemish borrowing remain unproven. No earlier stage and no PIE reconstruction can be asserted; this chain therefore begins at the earliest attested form rather than inventing a root.
