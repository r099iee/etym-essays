- Late 16th to Early 17th Century - Borrowed via French feston from Italian festone, initially designating carved architectural relief garlands celebrating Roman feasts.
- 17th to 18th Century - Broadened from classical architectural stonework to actual physical party decorations, floral chains, and theatrical swags.
- 18th Century (Verb Formation) - The verb to festoon emerged, meaning to decorate or drape profusely with garlands.
- 19th Century - Victorian literature popularized figurative uses (festooned with awards, ribbons, or cobwebs).
- 20th to 21st Century - Continues in architectural history, wedding and event design, outdoor electrical lighting (festoon lights), and expressive metaphorical prose.
