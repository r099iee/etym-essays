Note on historical derivation: Sanskrit Tamasā is formed directly from the nominal stem tamas ("darkness, obscurity", Proto-Indo-European *temH-) with the feminine suffix -ā, commonly employed in Old Indo-Aryan hydronyms to denote rivers possessing dark, deep, or forest-shaded waters. It is cognate with Latin tenebrae ("darkness", via *temesrai) and temere ("blindly"), Old Church Slavonic tĭma ("darkness"), and Lithuanian tamsa. While the lexical root denotes darkness, epic commentary notes the poetic contrast where its waters are lauded as akardama ("clear, mud-free").
