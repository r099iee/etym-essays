snare

↓

ensnared

Note: the prefix en- continues Old French en- from Latin in- meaning ‘in, into’; the link between Germanic *snarhō and PIE *(s)neh- (to twist cord) is the standard handbook derivation though the s- is mobile and the connection carries normal reconstruction uncertainty.
