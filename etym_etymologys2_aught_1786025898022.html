Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*h₂oyu- + *wekti-
"vital force, eternity, ever" + "thing, being, creature"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*aiwiwihti
"ever-creature, any thing at all, aught"
(*aiwaz "ever" + *wihtiz "thing, living being")
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
āwiht / ōwiht
"aught, anything at all, something"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200)
aught / ought
"anything whatever, in any degree"
↓
Modern English (c. 1600)
aught
"anything at all; whatever (pronoun/adverb)"
