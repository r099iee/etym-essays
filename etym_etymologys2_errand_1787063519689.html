Note on debate: The PIE ancestor of Germanic *airundija- is debated. Many scholars connect it to *h₁er- “to go” via the idea “one who goes (on a mission)”, supported by Gothic airus “messenger”. An older theory derived it from a compound meaning “errand of the host/army”. The “goer on a mission” reading is the most widely accepted, but some handbooks label the root “origin uncertain”.
