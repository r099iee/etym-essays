Note on Hybrid Etymology & Suffixation: Chamberlain is a celebrated example of a Germano-Romance hybrid. While the lexical base chamber derives from Greek kamára -> Latin camera, the noun suffix -lain represents the Old French adaptation of the Germanic agent suffix *-ling (denoting origin, affiliation, or function). The Germanic court title was re-borrowed into Gallo-Romance with palatalization of initial /k/ to /tʃ/ (spelled ch-).
