- 16th century - a- + breast; first a nautical and military term — ships or soldiers drawn up side by side with their fronts level in the same line.
- 18th-19th centuries - the phrase 'keep abreast of the times' extends the word from formation to knowledge: staying level with news and progress.
- Contemporary - the literal sense survives for walkers and formations; the figurative sense dominates in journalism, meaning equal to the latest developments.
