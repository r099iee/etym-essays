• spoiled: Similar because both mean overindulged to the point of bad character; differs because spoiled stresses the resulting moral damage or ruin ("a spoiled child"), while pampered focuses on the luxurious treatment itself.
  • indulged: Similar because both mean gratified beyond need; differs because indulged emphasizes yielding to whims and desires, while pampered emphasizes comfort, luxury, and care.
  • coddled: Similar because both mean treated with excessive tenderness; differs because coddled stresses overprotection from harm or difficulty (treating like an invalid), while pampered stresses physical comfort and luxury.
  • mollycoddled: Similar because both mean excessively and fussily cared for; differs because mollycoddled is more explicitly about pampering and coddling together, often implying overprotection or softness, and is more colloquial and pejorative.
  • cosseted: Similar because both mean treated with indulgent tenderness; differs because cosseted evokes petting and cuddling (like a lamb), warm affection rather than material luxury.
  • petted: Similar because both mean treated with affectionate favour; differs because petted emphasizes favoritism and physical affection, while pampered emphasizes indulgence and comfort.
  • overindulged: Similar because both mean gratified beyond moderation; differs because overindulged is more clinical and neutral, applying to appetites (food, drink), while pampered is about overall luxurious treatment.
  • waited on hand and foot: Similar because both describe being served without effort; differs because this phrase is idiomatic and stresses the servitude of others, while pampered emphasizes the comfort received.
  • spoon-fed: Similar because both imply being cared for to excess; differs because spoon-fed stresses passive dependence (ideas supplied, not earned), while pampered stresses comfort and indulgence.
  • babied: Similar because both mean treated as an infant; differs because babied emphasizes dependence and helplessness, while pampered emphasizes comfort and luxury.
