Debate: the root is ultimately imitative/onomatopoeic; etymonline derives it from a Scandinavian source such as Old Norse hvæsa "to hiss", cognate with Old English hwosan "to cough". A link to PIE *kʷes- "to pant, breathe" (cf. Sanskrit śvās- "breath") has been proposed but is debated, since the Germanic forms may be independent sound-imitation.
