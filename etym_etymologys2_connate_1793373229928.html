Note on Etymology: Formed by the regular Latin phonological assimilation of the prefix com- to con- before the initial nasal consonant of nāscī. The base verb nāscī descends from Old Latin gnāscī (from PIE *ǵenh₁-). Notice the close etymological kinship with cognate (where the archaic g was retained before the nasal) and innate (formed with the prefix in-).
