Note: the derivation of altare is debated. The majority view connects it with altus "high", but a rival etymology derives it from adolere "to burn up, consume by fire" (the fire on the altar), which would make the word refer to burnt offering rather than height. Latin also had ara for the ordinary altar, and the plural altaria was often treated as a feminine singular in Late Latin, one route to the Romance forms. Middle English also used auter from Old French autel; the Latinising spelling altar was restored in the sixteenth century.
