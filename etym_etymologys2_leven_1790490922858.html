Note: the elm-tree derivation (Gaelic leamhan) is the usual explanation, supported by many British Leven/Leam names in elm country, but it is debated. An alternative treats Leven as a pre-Celtic hydronym from *leb- to flow (smooth water), with Gaelic elm-meaning attached later by folk-etymology. Both agree the name is Celtic or pre-Celtic, not Germanic or Norse, and distinct from archaic leven lightning, which comes from PIE *leuk- light via Germanic.
