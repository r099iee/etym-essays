• Veddas: Similar because it is the modern spelling of the same ethnonym; differs because it lacks the archaic -h- and is preferred today.
  • Wanniyala-Aetto: Similar because it denotes the same community in self-designation; differs because it means forest-dwellers and carries Indigenous rights connotations.
  • Adivasi: Similar because it denotes Indigenous peoples of South Asia; differs because it is a pan-Indian political term, not specific to Sri Lanka.
  • Aborigines: Similar because it denotes first peoples generally; differs because in English it most often refers to Australian Aboriginal peoples, not Sri Lankans.
  • Bushmen: Similar because it denotes Southern African hunter-gatherers used as a parallel primitive type; differs because it refers to San peoples of the Kalahari, with different language and history.
  • Pygmies: Similar because Victorian writers grouped them with Veddas as small-stature forest peoples; differs because it refers to Central African rainforest groups and is now often considered pejorative.
  • Hunter-gatherers: Similar because it describes the Vedda subsistence mode; differs because it is a descriptive economic category, not an ethnic name.
  • Yakkhas: Similar because Pali chronicles link them to the island's pre-Sinhalese inhabitants; differs because it is a mythologised spirit-people label, not the modern community name.
  • Sinhalese: Similar because they are the neighbouring majority population of Sri Lanka; differs because they are settled agriculturalists with a distinct language and Buddhist civilisation.
  • Tamils: Similar because they are another long-established Sri Lankan community; differs because they are Dravidian-speaking agricultural and coastal peoples, not the forest Veddas.
  • Tasmanians: Similar because old histories pair them as shelterless primitives; differs because they are the Indigenous peoples of Tasmania, now extinct as a full-blood community, with no cultural connection.
  • Negritos: Similar because colonial anthropology classed Veddas with small dark-skinned Asian forest peoples; differs because it is an outdated racial grouping covering Philippine Aeta and others.
