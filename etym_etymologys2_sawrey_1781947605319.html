Debated: The exact form of the surname SAWREY is not securely attested. It may be a spelling variant of the occupational surname Sawyer, or it may derive from a place name. The Cumbrian village of Near/Far Sawrey (where Beatrix Potter lived) is commonly explained as Old Norse saurr "mud, mire" + ey "island, water-meadow", which would make the place name unrelated to sawyer. The sawyer chain itself (PIE *sek- → PGmc *sagō → OE sagu) is secure; only the link to the name is uncertain.
