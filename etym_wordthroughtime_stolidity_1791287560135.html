- Dull brutish stupidity - purely negative, Roman contempt for insensibility in Plautus and Seneca
- Learned dullness in Tudor prose - schoolmaster disapproval, unteachable heaviness
- Victorian peasant and soldierly endurance - ambivalent admiration, noble patience mixed with condescension
- Comic but dignified mask in discomfort - affectionate irony in Wodehouse, keeping face as good manners
- Modern resilience vs. flat affect - split between praised calm under pressure and criticised emotional thickness
