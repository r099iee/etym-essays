Note: jewel is thought to derive from Latin iocus via the "plaything" sense, but the link is debated and the PIE *yek- base is uncertain. Encrust entered English via French (Old French encroster, from incrustare, in- "in" + crusta); the link to PIE *krus- "to freeze" is also uncertain. The compound joins both roots.
