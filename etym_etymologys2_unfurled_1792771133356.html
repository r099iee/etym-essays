Arabic (c. 8th–10th c.)
fardah (فَرْدَة)
"bundle, pack of merchandise"
↓
Old Provençal / Catalan (c. 12th c.)
farda
"bundle, pack, burden"
↓
Old French (13th c.)
farde
"bundle, package, bale"
↓
Old French (late 13th c.)
fardeler
"to tie into bundles, pack up"
↓
Middle French (14th–15th c.)
ferler / ferlier
"to roll and tie sails to yards"
(conflated with fermer "to bind")
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1590)
furl
"to roll up and secure a sail"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1605)
unfurl
"to unroll and spread a sail"
(prefix un- "reversal" + furl)
↓
Modern English (17th c.–present)
unfurled
"spread out from a rolled state"
