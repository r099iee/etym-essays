[Compound Formation: weather + top]
Modern English (Literary Toponym) (1954)
Weathertop
"prominent windy hill crowned by the watchtower of Amon Sûl"

Note on translation methodology: J.R.R. Tolkien constructed Weathertop as the common Westron rendering of the Elvish (Sindarin) toponym Amon Sûl (*amon*, "hill" + *sûl*, "wind"). The compound neatly parallels typical English landscape toponyms (such as *Windy Brow* or *Cloud Fell*) while providing an exact semantic translation of the native Sindarin name.
