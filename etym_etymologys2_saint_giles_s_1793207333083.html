Gille

Giles

Note: Latin Aegidius is traditionally derived from Greek aigidion, young goat, from aix, goat — after the hind of the legend — but the name may equally be of non-Greek origin, so the goat connection is traditional rather than certain; the possessive ’s is the ordinary English parish ellipsis, as in St Paul’s.
