• Betook oneself: Similar because both mean going to a place with deliberate purpose; differs because betook oneself is reflexive, bookish, and personal, while repaired is impersonal and administrative, closer to official prose.
  • Resorted to: Similar because both mean going to a place; differs because resort implies frequent, habitual going (or recourse to a means), while repaired describes a single ceremonial movement to a destination.
  • Proceeded: Similar because both describe moving along a course; differs because proceeded is procedural and stresses continuing, while repaired stresses arriving at and betaking oneself to a specific station.
  • Hied: Similar because both are archaic verbs of going; differs because hied conveys old-fashioned haste and urgency, the opposite of repaired's unhurried dignity.
  • Went: Similar because both express the act of moving to a place; differs because went is the neutral, colourless default of everyday speech, while repaired is formal and freighted with purpose and protocol.
  • Retired (to): Similar because both mean withdrawing to a place, often private; differs because retired suggests leaving a scene for seclusion or rest, while repaired suggests proceeding to a post or place of business.
  • Removed (to): Similar because both are formal verbs of changing one's place; differs because removed is chiefly used of moving residence or headquarters, while repaired is used of a single dignified journey to a destination.
  • Repaired (mended): Similar because both are the same spelling and same verb in form; differs because repaired ("went") comes from Latin repatriare and is archaic and intransitive, while repaired ("mended") comes from Latin reparare and is the ordinary transitive verb of fixing.
