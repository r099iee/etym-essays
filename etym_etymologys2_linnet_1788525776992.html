Note: The word is a direct morphological diminutive built on the noun for flax (lin), reflecting the bird's ancient dietary association with flax and hemp crops. Cognate with French linotte (as in the French idiom tête de linotte, 'scatterbrained') and Spanish pardillo / lúgano.
