- [13th-14th Century] - Concrete military terminology; strictly denoted the iron-shod sliding vertical lattice dropped within castle gatehouses during sieges, carrying visceral connotations of martial security and entrapment.
- [15th-16th Century] - Political and heraldic elevation; adopted as the personal badge of the Tudor dynasty (via the Beaufort lineage), shifting connotation to sovereign legitimacy, majesty, and royal sanctuary.
- [17th-19th Century] - Institutional codification; embedded as the primary seal of the British Parliament and government departments, denoting parliamentary sovereignty, bureaucratic enclosure, and constitutional authority.
- [20th-21st Century] - Metaphorical and systemic abstraction; used in political commentary, economics, and cybersecurity to designate rapid, non-negotiable perimeter shutdowns, trade barriers, or irrevocable defensive lockdowns.
