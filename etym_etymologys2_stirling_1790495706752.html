Note: the ultimate Celtic origin is debated and unresolved. Brittonic place-of-strife and Gaelic stream-pool have both been proposed, but neither is supported by a secure early compound or parallel, and standard authorities mark the name as of uncertain origin. It is unrelated to sterling (coinage, from Old English steorra star). No PIE root can be responsibly assigned.
