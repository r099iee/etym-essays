Note on mythic and linguistic development: In Vedic Sanskrit, vāmana appears in the Taittirīya Saṁhitā and Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa as an adjective meaning "dwarfish, stunted." The myth of Vāmana and Trivikrama represents one of the most remarkable narrative expansions in religious history: the single Rigvedic motif of the solar deity Viṣṇu measuring the cosmos in three strides (trīṇi padā vi cakrame) was synthesized with the Brāhmaṇa tale of the sacrifice assuming dwarf form, culminating in the monumental narrative of King Bali, the three paces, and the cosmic stride. In the Rāmāyaṇa, the name is also assigned to the southern Diggaja, illustrating the sacred practice of conferring avataric names upon cosmic world-guardians.
