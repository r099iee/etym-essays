Debate: English subdue sits between two Latin verbs. Old French soduire itself reflects Latin subducere "to draw away, withdraw" (sub- + ducere "to lead", PIE *dewk-), but the sense "to conquer, subject" was pulled toward Latin subdere "to put under." Both subdere and subducere underlie the modern word.
