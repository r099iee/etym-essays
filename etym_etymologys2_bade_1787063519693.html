Note on conflation: Modern English bid conflates two Old English verbs: biddan “to ask, pray, command” (whose past is bæd → bade) and bēodan “to offer, proclaim, announce” (whose past is bēad). The past tense bade continues biddan's past bæd; the modern spelling with final -e reflects Middle English long-ā bāde. The two verbs merged in form during Middle English, producing the single irregular paradigm bid / bade / bidden.
