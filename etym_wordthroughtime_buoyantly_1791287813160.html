- Buoy as floating mark - anchored float guiding ships; practical nautical object, neutral technical sense
- Buoyant as apt to float - 16th-c. physics and seamanship: that which will not sink; admired reliability
- Buoyant as cheerfully resilient in spirits - 18th-19th c. metaphorical transfer: markets, hopes and people described as buoyant; positive, admiring
- Buoyantly as manner adverb - cheerfully unsinkable way of speaking and acting; breezy Edwardian optimism, Bertie's default mode
- Modern use - both market jargon (buoyant market) and personal manner; still optimistic, now sometimes seen as naive if facts are grave
