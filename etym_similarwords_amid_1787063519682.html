• amidst: Similar because it is the exact synonym, same preposition from the same origin; differs only in the excrescent -st, which gives it a more literary and archaic ring.
  • among: Similar because both mean “in the midst of”; differs because among is used with plural, countable members of a group, while amid takes masses and surroundings.
  • amongst: Similar because it is the -st variant of among, with the same group-membership meaning; differs in register, sounding older and more formal.
  • between: Similar because both locate something by its surroundings; differs because between marks a position separating two (or paired) things, not enclosure within a mass.
  • in the middle of: Similar because it is the literal paraphrase of amid; differs because it is a phrase, clunkier and fully spatial, while amid extends easily to time and atmosphere.
  • during: Similar because both can mean “in the course of (an event)”; differs because during is purely temporal, while amid retains spatial and surrounding senses.
  • within: Similar because both place something inside a sphere; differs because within implies an inside boundary, whereas amid implies being surrounded by an undifferentiated mass.
  • in: Similar because both are simple locating prepositions; differs because in is neutral containment, while amid stresses the middle and the surrounding crowd or atmosphere.
  • mid-: Similar because both draw on the same root and the sense of the middle; differs because mid- is a prefix of compounds (“midnight”, “mid-career”), not a preposition.
  • surrounded by: Similar because both convey enclosure on all sides; differs because the phrase is more explicit about encircling, while amid also covers belonging to a group and being inside a period.
