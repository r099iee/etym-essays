• Confounded: Similar because both serve as mild, old-fashioned minced oaths expressing exasperation at an annoying nuisance; differs because confounded carries a slightly more polite, Victorian, or bewildered gentlemanly register, whereas dratted has a rustic, folksy, domestic flavor.
  • Blasted: Similar because both are euphemistic substitutes for profanity to vent irritation at stubborn objects or people; differs because blasted carries slightly stronger explosive or military vigor (originally implying struck by lightning or ruinous blight), whereas dratted is gentle and domestic.
  • Accursed: Similar because both attribute a curse to the subject; differs because accursed is an elevated, archaic, biblical, or epic term denoting genuinely being under a severe divine curse or doom, whereas dratted is a petty, everyday emotional venting.
  • Cursed: Similar because both originate from wishes for divine punishment; differs because cursed possesses genuine theological or dramatic weight, whereas dratted is a thoroughly sanitized, playful, or harmless minced oath.
  • Pesky: Similar because both describe annoying, troublesome, or evasive nuisances; differs because pesky is purely descriptive of the irritating behavior of pests or children without the historical lineage of being a minced curse.
  • Infernal: Similar because both express strong exasperation by referencing spiritual realms; differs because infernal literally references the fires of hell and conveys higher rhetorical intensity or demonic outrage, whereas dratted is entirely domestic.
  • Darned / Damned: Similar because both express frustration; differs because damned is overt theological profanity, while darned is a minced form of damn, whereas dratted specifically descends from the phrase "God rot".
  • Vexing: Similar because both describe situations causing annoyance; differs because vexing is a formal, objective analytical adjective describing something that causes mental distress or intellectual difficulty, lacking the oath-like character of dratted.
