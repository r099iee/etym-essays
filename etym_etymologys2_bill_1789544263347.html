Note on Convergence: The anatomical noun (avian beak) derives directly from the Germanic root *bilją (cleaving instrument). The distinct document/invoice sense converged phonetically in Middle English from Anglo-French bille (sealed document, petition), itself from Medieval Latin bulla (seal, amulet).
