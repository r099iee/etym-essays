- To flower, to bloom, to blossom - natural vegetative thriving, neutral-descriptive
- To thrive and prosper by God's grace - moralized providential success, positive-pious in Middle English sermons and chronicles
- To embellish handwriting and speech with flower-like strokes - craft virtuosity, admiring of scribal and rhetorical skill
- To brandish, wave showily and sound a fanfare - performative public display, celebratory but edging toward ostentation
- Mere flowerishes / flourishes as empty bombast - pejorative critical turn, suspicious of ornament as deception
- Burkean-modernist flowerishes as meaningful visual rhetoric - rehabilitated analytic-positive, ornament as argument in typography and media theory
