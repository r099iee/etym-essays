(c. 3500–2500 BCE)

Proto-Indo-Iranian
(c. 2000–1500 BCE)

(c. 1200–800 BCE)
upa- + ud- + han- / ghāta
"striking upward, setting into motion"
↓

(c. 5th–4th c. BCE)

"exordium, preface, prologue,
initiating discourse that elucidates
