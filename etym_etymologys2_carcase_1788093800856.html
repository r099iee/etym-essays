Note on the earliest stage: no PIE root can be securely reconstructed for this word — the OED classes the Old French etymon as of unknown or obscure origin, and no reliable pre-Romance ancestor is attested. The chain above therefore begins at the earliest verifiable point, Old French charcois. Three scholarly hypotheses about its deeper source are: (1) derivation from or influence of Latin carō, carnis "flesh" (a Romance formation of the type *caronia, cf. carrion); (2) connection with Italian carcassa, the word for the ribbed hulk of a ship, itself of unclear origin; and (3) an expressive or reduplicated base of the type kark- for hard, rigid, creaking things. None has won universal assent, and the origin must be regarded as genuinely unknown — stated here rather than guessed.
