• Knocked down: Similar because both denote physical impact that forces a standing person to the floor; differs because knocked down is a broad, neutral descriptor that often implies a strike or blow, whereas bowled over conveys continuous rolling momentum sweeping someone off their feet like skittles.
  • Toppled: Similar because both involve a loss of vertical balance resulting in falling; differs because toppled emphasizes top-heavy instability and precarious falling from height, whereas bowled emphasizes forceful dynamic propulsion and sweeping collision.
  • Stunned: Similar because both capture extreme psychological amazement or disorientation; differs because stunned implies a numbing blow or cognitive paralysis, while bowled over suggests an enthusiastic, overwhelming, or sweeping wave of astonishment.
  • Rolled: Similar because both involve rotational motion across a surface; differs because rolled is purely physical and descriptive, while bowled carries specialized athletic connotations (cricket/skittles) and dramatic kinetic impact.
  • Upended: Similar because both indicate turning something upside down or off its feet; differs because upended focuses on the resulting inverted vertical state, whereas bowled focuses on the forward driving force that caused the overturn.
  • Flabbergasted: Similar because both describe total mental astonishment; differs because flabbergasted is an informal, expressive adjective denoting speechlessness and confusion, whereas bowled over carries a vivid metaphorical image of being physically toppled by wonder or gratitude.
