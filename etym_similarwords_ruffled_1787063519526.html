• Rustled: Similar because both describe creating a soft, crackling sound and slight disturbance among dry materials like leaves or fabric; differs because 'rustled' focuses primarily on the auditory friction sound, while 'ruffled' emphasizes physical disarrangement and disturbance of surface smoothness.
  • Disheveled: Similar because both refer to disordering hair, clothing, or plumage; differs because 'disheveled' denotes an untidy, messy end-state (often of personal appearance), whereas 'ruffled' can be a transient motion or light disturbance.
  • Agitated: Similar because both imply emotional or physical disturbance; differs because 'agitated' conveys intense, violent churning or deep psychological anxiety, whereas 'ruffled' suggests milder irritation or disruption of composure.
  • Flustered: Similar because both describe losing calm composure under stress; differs because 'flustered' emphasizes nervous confusion and clumsy haste, whereas 'ruffled' indicates pride or serenity being slightly bruised or unsettled.
  • Disturbed: Similar because both involve upsetting an established equilibrium or smooth state; differs because 'disturbed' is broader, more neutral, and can imply profound damage or disruption, while 'ruffled' is gentle, superficial, or tactile.
  • Rippled: Similar because both describe gentle undulating disruptions across a surface (especially water); differs because 'rippled' focuses on regular wave patterns, whereas 'ruffled' implies irregular frilling, fluffing, or messy scuffing.
  • Tousled: Similar because both denote playfully or casually making hair or fibers untidy; differs because 'tousled' is used almost exclusively for hair and carries warm, affectionate connotations (e.g., tousled curls).
  • Perturbed: Similar because both denote psychological unsettling; differs because 'perturbed' belongs to a formal, cerebral register describing deep mental disquiet, while 'ruffled' retains its tactile metaphor of ruffled feathers.
