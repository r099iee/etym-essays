INSOLENCE
"rude, contemptuous, defiant behaviour or speech
toward authority; arrogant audacity"

Note on nuance: the semantic shift from "unusual" to "arrogant" reflects the Roman cult of custom (mos maiorum): to be "unaccustomed" was to be immoderate and presumptuous. The derivation of solēre from the reflexive root is standard but its exact morphology is debated; no secure alternative exists. The popular mnemonic that reads insolence as "in-solo" (acting as if alone) is a folk etymology—the in- is merely negation, and there is no connection with Latin sōlus "alone".
