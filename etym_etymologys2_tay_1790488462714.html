Note: deeper origin debated. Older handbooks (Holder, Watson, Jackson) derived British *tava from *taus- "silent, quiet" (Welsh taw "silent", goddess the Silent One). Pokorny and Nicolaisen rejected silent as semantically implausible for a spate river and proposed IE *ta- "to flow" giving Celtic *taua "flowing one". Isaac (2005) calls *ta- flow a ghost for *teh2- "thaw, melt" and concludes affiliation and Celticity of Taoua are doubtful, possibly pre-Celtic substrate. Chain above 98 CE is secure; PIE stage is provisional.
