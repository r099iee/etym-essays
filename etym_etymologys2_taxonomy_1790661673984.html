Note: taxonomie was coined by Swiss botanist Augustin Pyramus de Candolle in 1813 in Théorie élémentaire de la botanique, fusing Greek taxis + nomos on the model of astronomie and économie; English borrowed it directly. The two Greek elements descend from distinct PIE roots (*tag- and *nem-), converging only in the modern compound.
