- 1500s-1600s (physical sense) - Meaning "raised on stilts" or built on stilt-like supports; chiefly literal, describing structures and persons elevated on poles.
- 1600s-1800s (figurative transfer) - Shifts from "raised" to "artificially raised": language, style, or behavior that is stiffly formal, pompous, and lacking natural flow; already mildly pejorative.
- 1900s-present (modern sense) - The figurative sense dominates: awkwardly formal, stiff, or unnatural speech and writing; it can also describe jerky, ungainly bodily motion (as of a long-legged bird laboring into flight). The register is critical and negative.
