Debate: θύμος "thyme" must be kept apart
from the homophonous θυμός "spirit, courage".
The herb was named for its fragrant burning
smoke (from θύειν), while θυμός "spirit"
descends from a different PIE root *dʰews-
"to blow, breathe".
