• Annoying: Similar because both cause displeasure; differs because annoying is the mild, general word for minor irritation, while vexing implies a deeper, rattling disturbance of the mind.
  • Irritating: Similar because both cause friction and displeasure; differs because irritating is minor and repetitive friction, while vexing is sharper — it shakes or perturbs rather than merely grates.
  • Exasperating: Similar because both push patience to its limit; differs because exasperating means infuriating to the point of losing patience, while vexing is agitation without complete loss of temper.
  • Perplexing: Similar because both trouble the mind; differs because perplexing centres on confusion and difficulty in understanding, while vexing centres on annoyance and agitation.
  • Bothersome: Similar because both describe something that disturbs; differs because bothersome is a mild, everyday nuisance, while vexing is more pointed and perturbing.
  • Troublesome: Similar because both describe something causing difficulty; differs because troublesome implies causing trouble or problems of any kind, while vexing specifically agitates and irritates.
  • Aggravating: Similar because both describe making a situation worse; differs because aggravating emphasizes intensifying frustration (often informal), while vexing emphasizes the act of troubling or rattling.
  • Provoking: Similar because both can mean deliberately inciting a reaction; differs because provoking means arousing a response (often anger or thought), while vexing is the resulting agitation.
  • Irksome: Similar because both describe something tiresome and annoying; differs because irksome emphasizes tedium and weariness, while vexing emphasizes agitation and disturbance.
  • Galling: Similar because both describe irritation; differs because galling is resentment at humiliation or injustice — something that "galls" like chafed skin — while vexing is general perturbation.
  • Nettlesome: Similar because both sting and irritate like a nettle; differs because nettlesome is a milder, prickly annoyance, while vexing is more active disturbance.
  • Harassing: Similar because both describe persistent troubling; differs because harassing implies sustained, deliberate oppression (its legal sibling "vexatious" is the technical term for such suits), while vexing is a single or repeated irritation.
  • Worrying: Similar because both can cause anxiety; differs because worrying emphasizes concern and fear about a future outcome, while vexing emphasizes present irritation and agitation.
  • Tormenting: Similar because both cause severe disturbance; differs because tormenting is intense, prolonged suffering, while vexing is lighter, sharp agitation.
