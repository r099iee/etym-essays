- [Old French cai/quai] - [A riverbank, sandbank, or embankment; later a solid landing platform for ships]
- [Middle English key/kaye] - [A mooring place; the word was spelled to match its pronunciation]
- [Late 17th century] - [The French spelling 'quay' was adopted while the English pronunciation 'key' was stubbornly retained]
- [Contemporary sense] - [A solid stone or concrete platform running parallel to the water's edge for loading and unloading; formal, nautical register]
