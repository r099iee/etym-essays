Note on nuance: The Proto-Germanic *haftaz is the nominalized past participle of the verbal root *hab-/haf- "to have, hold, lift" (seen in German haben "to have", heben "to lift", and English have, heave), which continues the PIE root *kap- "to grasp". The same PIE root independently produced Latin capere "to take, seize" (source of capture, capable), so haft and capture are distant cousins — a genuine cognate relationship, not a borrowing.
