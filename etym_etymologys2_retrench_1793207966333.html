trenchier

retrenchier

retrenchen

retrench

Note: the deeper origin of Latin truncus is disputed: de Vaan lists it as of unknown etymology, possibly a Mediterranean substrate word, and older links to verbs of rubbing or boring are now discounted, so the chain honestly begins at the Latin noun.
