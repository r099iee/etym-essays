• diligently: Similar in steady, careful application to a task; differs because it emphasizes loving, value-laden attentiveness (from Latin diligere, "to love"), without assiduously's image of seated, permanent attendance.
  • sedulously: Similar in persistent, devoted effort; differs because it stresses sincere, guileless devotion, while assiduously keeps the physical sense of constant attendance — of sitting by a thing until the work is done.
  • industriously: Similar in sustained effort; differs because it stresses energetic, productive busyness, while assiduously conveys the constant, watchful loyalty of a keeper who never leaves his post.
  • painstakingly: Similar in careful, thorough work that accepts trouble; differs because it emphasizes slowness and the willingness to take pains, while assiduously emphasizes unbroken constancy of attendance.
  • meticulously: Similar in exact, thorough care; differs because its root is fear (Latin metus) — fussy, anxious exactness — whereas assiduously implies faithful sitting-by, not anxiety.
  • scrupulously: Similar in painstaking exactness; differs because it is conscience-driven (from scrupulus, "a small sharp stone"), where assiduously is constancy-driven, a matter of posture rather than of scruple.
  • unremittingly: Similar in never relaxing effort; differs because it is entirely about the absence of remission, with no seated image and no hint of devotion.
  • tirelessly: Similar in working without flagging; differs because it stresses energy and endurance, while assiduously stresses attention and loyalty.
  • constantly: Similar in happening all the time; differs because it is the neutral adverb of frequency, without the moral or emotional charge of devoted attendance.
  • faithfully: Similar in devoted, loyal service to a task or person; differs because it is primarily a word of loyalty and duty, while assiduously adds the image of unbroken application.
  • devotedly: Similar in giving oneself wholly to a task; differs because it emphasizes love and dedication, where assiduously emphasizes constancy of effort.
  • earnestly: Similar in serious, sincere effort; differs because it stresses gravity and sincerity of intention, not the permanence of attendance.
  • persistently: Similar in continuing despite difficulty; differs because it emphasizes not giving up, while assiduously emphasizes not ceasing even without difficulty.
  • zealously: Similar in energetic fervour for a cause; differs because it carries religious or crusading heat, while assiduously is cooler, steadier, more patient.
