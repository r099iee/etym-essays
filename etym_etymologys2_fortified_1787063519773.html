Chain for the root FORTIS:
PIE
(prehistoric)
*bhergh-
"high, elevated"
↓
Proto-Italic
(c. 1000 BCE)
*fortis
"strong, elevated, mighty"
↓
Latin
(c. 3rd–1st c. BCE)
fortis
"strong, robust, brave"
↓
Late Latin
(c. 4th c.)
fortificāre (fortis + -ficāre)
"to make strong"

↓

Note: the bridge from PIE *bhergh- "high" to Latin fortis "strong" reflects the ancient association of height with power and solidity (cf. Sanskrit bṛhánt- "high, great"); the exact pre-form is debated. -ficāre descends separately from PIE *dʰeh₁- "to put, make."
