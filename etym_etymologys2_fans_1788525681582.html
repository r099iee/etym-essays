Note on Homographic Streams: Sense 1 (the physical radiating implement, coral frond, and architectural vault) descends strictly from Latin *vannus* as outlined above. Sense 2 (the sports enthusiast or music follower) arose independently in late 19th-century American English as a colloquial clipping of *fanatic* (< Latin *fanaticus*, "inspired by divinity, mad, belonging to a temple", from *fanum*, "sanctuary/temple", from PIE **dʰh₁s-no-*, "sacred"). Both lexical streams have merged phonologically into the modern plural *fans*.
