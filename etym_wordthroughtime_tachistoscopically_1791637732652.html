- Late 19th Century (1880s to 1890s): Birth in Wundtian experimental psychology - Used to describe stimuli presented via falling gravity-slits or mechanical shutters to measure reaction time and reading span.
- Early 20th Century (1910s to 1930s): Gestalt psychology and reading pedagogy - Employed in landmark perception experiments (Wertheimer, Koffka, Arnheim) and reading training programs to test visual synthesis.
- Mid-20th Century (1940s to 1960s): Military recognition training and subliminal advertising panic - Associated with aircraft silhouette spotting for WWII pilots and controversial commercial subliminal messaging studies.
- Late 20th to 21st Century: Modern cognitive neuroscience and psycholinguistics - Specialized methodological adverb in computerized visual masking, masked priming, and fMRI pre-attentive paradigms.
