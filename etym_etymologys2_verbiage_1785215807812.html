Note — Debate: The PIE root of Latin verbum is generally held to be *werdʰ- "word" (cf. English word, German Wort); the older spelling *werǵʰ- is not supported. The formation of French verbiage from the verb verbier plus the suffix -age is debated in detail, but the core chain is secure.
