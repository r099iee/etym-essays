- Late Latin exemplaris - that may be copied, transcript; scribal and legal; neutral and technical
- 1400s Middle English exemplary - manuscript copies and moral patterns; monastic and clerkly
- 1500s humanist moral model - Erasmus, Elyot, Ascham hold up exemplary lives; didactic and princely
- 1600s punitive turn - exemplary justice, exemplary executions; judges exhibit offenders to terrify; stern and public
- 1700s schoolroom pattern girls - exemplary pupils, exemplary needlework; institutional, feminine, pride-tinged
- Victorian Ruskinian exemplary - the conscientious career laughed at yet beloved; earnest, evangelical, familial
- Modern praise-band and law - exemplary service records versus exemplary damages; bureaucratic yet still moral
