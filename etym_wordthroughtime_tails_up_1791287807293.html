- Kennel observation - literal note that hounds run with tails erect when keen and well; neutral technical reading
- Sporting metaphor - transferred to horses, teams and schoolboys: playing with tails up means confident, attacking; admiring and hearty connotation
- Imperative slang - Tails up! as jolly order to cheer up in school, army and club; breezy, upper-class, stiff-upper-lip perception
- Wodehousean catchphrase - Bertie's buoyant but fatuous optimism, instantly undercut by gloomier reality; affectionately comic and slightly inadequate
- Modern survival - quaint British period encouragement, still understood; nostalgic and equestrian rather than everyday
