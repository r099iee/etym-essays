Note: the Celtic high-word is secure (Welsh uchel, Cornish ughel, Breton uhel all mean high), but the direct link to Ochil is debated. An alternative Gaelic derivation from uachail high or ochaill projection has been proposed, and the exact Pictish form is unattested, surviving only in medieval spellings Ochel and Ochil. Best statement: probably Brittonic/Pictish high-place, later Gaelicised and Scotticised; ultimate PIE beyond Celtic remains provisional.
