Pre-Roman Iberian / Celtic substrate (c. 500 BCE)
*kapp- / *kappo-
"hood, head-covering, shell, cape"
↓
Old Spanish / Portuguese (c. 15th c.)
carapacho / carapaça
"shell of a tortoise, crust, covering"
↓
French (c. 1760s)
carapace
"dorsal shell of turtles and crustaceans"
↓
Modern English (c. 1830s–present)
carapace

Etymological Note: Iberian *carapacho* represents a Romance blend influenced by *cara* ("face, head") and substrate *capa* ("cape, mantle"), describing the hooded, enveloping dorsal shell of reptiles and crustaceans.
