Debate: the noun slope is a back-formation from the verb slope (15th c.), an aphetic form of aslope "slanting" from Old English āslopen, past participle of āslūpan "to slip away". The ultimate PIE root *sleub- "to slip, slide" is generally accepted, but the intermediate Proto-Germanic form is reconstructed.
