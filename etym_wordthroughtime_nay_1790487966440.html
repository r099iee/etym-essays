- 13th-14th c. nay versus no rule - nay answers positive questions, no answers negative ones; grammatical and polite
- 15th-16th c. general no and rhetorical nay - distinction collapses; nay becomes emphatic, poetic, northern
- 1590-1611 Shakespeare and King James Bible - nay, hear me; nay, we will not; theatrical and scriptural dignity
- 1670-1800 parliamentary nays - ayes and nays frozen in division procedure; institutional and counted
- 1830s-1880s Ruskinian nay - not only... nay, even...; periodic climax; moral and oratorical
- Modern dialect and fossil - Yorkshire nay, American nays, naysayers; regional, procedural, compound
