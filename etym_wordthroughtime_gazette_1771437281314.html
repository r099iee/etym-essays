- [17th-century news-sheet] - Borrowed from the Venetian gazetta into English as the name of a small periodic news-sheet - the early ancestor of the newspaper.
- [17th-18th century newspaper boom] - Becomes a stock title for newspapers and literary journals across Europe (The Gazette, La Gazette), a general word for news.
- [18th-19th century official sense] - Narrows in England to the official government publication - The London Gazette - the authoritative record of appointments, promotions, proclamations and legal notices; 'to gazette' means to publish officially.
- [Contemporary register] - Chiefly the official state record (formal, bureaucratic, authoritative), with a residual literary sense of 'newspaper or journal'.
