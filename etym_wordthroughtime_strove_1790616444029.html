- Frankish quarrel sense - to brawl, wrangle, contend physically, aggressive interpersonal connotation
- Middle English contention sense - to fight, dispute, vie in battle or law, chivalric and legalistic tone
- Early Modern effort sense - to labour earnestly, to struggle toward perfection, moralized Protestant work-ethic connotation of worthy exertion
- 19th-c. aspirational literary sense - to yearn and strain for ideals like truth, beauty, voice, reverent and romantic connotation
- Contemporary general-literary sense - still elevated past tense strove for dignified past effort, partly yielding to regular strived in casual prose, connotation of serious narrative weight versus plain tried
