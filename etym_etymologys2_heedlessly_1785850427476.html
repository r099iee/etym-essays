Debate: the PIE origin of Proto-Germanic *hōdijaną is uncertain; a root *kā-/*kō- "to pay attention, take care" is often proposed but not securely established (OED: of doubtful etymology). heedless and heedlessly are English formations: -less from PGmc *-lausaz (OE -lēas) "without", and -ly from PGmc *-līka- (OE -līce).
