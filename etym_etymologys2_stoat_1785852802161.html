Debate note: the OED and modern dictionaries mark the etymology as of uncertain origin. The Old Norse connection (stuttr) was favored by older authorities; the 'bold' connection to stout is sometimes repeated but unsupported by clear attestation. No relation to Latin mustela (the zoological genus word for 'weasel') is implied: Mustela erminea is a learned scientific binomial, not part of the English word's own history.
