Note: Classical Latin *lineamentum* was formed from the adjective/noun *linea* ("linen thread, drawn line") combined with the resultative noun suffix *-mentum*. In Classical Latin, Cicero and Vitruvius used the plural *lineamenta* to refer to architectural floorplans and preliminary sketches, from which the anatomical and facial meaning naturally developed.
