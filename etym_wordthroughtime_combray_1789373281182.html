- Celtic & Gallo-Roman Era (c. 1st c. BCE–5th c. CE) - Existed as *Comboriacum*, an obscure rural toponym meaning settlement at a river confluence or valley basin.
- 19th Century - Remained an unassuming agrarian place name in Normandy (Calvados) and other French regions, unnoticed by the wider public.
- 1913 - Explodes into world literature with the publication of *Du côté de chez Swann*; perceived as the quintessential literary landscape of sensory childhood and bourgeois provincial life.
- Mid-20th Century - Cemented in literary philosophy and narratology as the prime philosophical emblem of involuntary memory (*mémoire involontaire*).
- 1971–Present - Enters official French geography through the renaming of Illiers to *Illiers-Combray*, permanently merging fictional myth with physical reality.
