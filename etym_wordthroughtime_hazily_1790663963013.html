- c. 1300 haze as thin mist - concrete nautical and rural word for veiling air; neutral meteorological observation
- 1625 hazy as misty and confused - extends from weather to minds; hazy notions join hazy mornings; mildly negative when applied to thought
- 18th–19th c. aestheticized haze - Romantic painting and poetry make hazy distances beautiful and nostalgic; hazily remembered childhood gains tender value
- Early 20th c. psychological adverb - hazily recalled, hazily understood become standard for half-memory; clinical yet forgiving term for normal forgetting
- Present diagnostic use - as in the source sentence, hazily marks what schooling leaves behind: enough to inhibit, too little to guide; rueful, democratic, gently critical tone
