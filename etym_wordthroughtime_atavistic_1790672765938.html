- Reappearance of ancestral traits after absence - technical biological coinage; atavism in 19th-century Darwinian French and English, neutral and scientific
- Criminal or degenerate throwback to savagery - pejorative Lombrosian sense; atavistic criminal as born savage, now discredited and offensive
- Primitive impulse breaking through civilisation - psychological and literary extension; atavistic fears and urges, mildly negative
- Quaintly old-fashioned or out-of-step concern - ironic essayistic use; almost atavistic to care about rhetoric today, self-deprecating and modern
