Note on uncertainty

The derivation of Latin mons from PIE *men- “to project, stand out” is the standard account but not certain: the noun is an isolated formation, and some handbooks leave its pre-Latin origin unresolved. The Latin phrase, French and English stages of the chain, by contrast, are securely documented.
