• glittering: Similar because both describe bright, shiny small eyes; differs because glittering lacks the small-and-round shape component and can be positive.
  • gimlet: Similar because both describe sharp, piercing observation; differs because gimlet-eyed is purely about keenness, beady also about size and shine.
  • shrewd: Similar because both imply watchful intelligence; differs because shrewd describes judgement, not physical appearance.
  • sharp-eyed: Similar because both mean observant and alert; differs because sharp-eyed stresses keen vision, beady the look of the eyes themselves.
  • bright: Similar because both describe shining small eyes; differs because bright is general and positive, beady often adds cunning or greed.
  • glossy: Similar because both describe shiny surfaces; differs because glossy lacks the small-round and watchful overtones of beady.
  • beaded: Similar because both come from bead; differs because beaded is literal (covered in beads or drops), beady is figurative.
