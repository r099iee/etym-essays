- Strictly medical convulsive - 1680s physicians use for asthma, colic and strictures that contract involuntarily, neutral technical diagnosis
- Poetic pathology - late 18th-century sensibility extends to nervous, hysterical and emotional convulsions, tinged with fashionable morbidity
- General metaphor for irregularity - from 1837 onward any fitful effort, reform or warfare is spasmodic, mildly critical of lack of discipline and system
- Modern analytical adjective - neutral descriptor in medicine and economics for burst-pause patterns, retaining memory of muscle but applied to growth, violence and attention
