Note: the immediate origin of Old French brooillier is debated. It is usually derived from a Germanic *brod- “broth” (cf. broth and brew), but some authorities connect it directly with the PIE root for seething. The chain above shows the usual account; the earliest step should be read as probable rather than certain.
