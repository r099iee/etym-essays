- [Vedic & Epic Genesis (c. 1000–500 BCE)] — mentioned in late Vedic literature and crystallized in the *Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa* as *Daṇḍakāraṇya*, the immense, impenetrable primeval forest south of the Vindhyas — [perception: feared as an untamed, perilous wilderness (*aranya*) infested with demons (*rākṣasas*) and wild beasts, yet sanctified by austere ascetics (*ṛṣis*)]
- [Classical Sanskrit & Purāṇic Era (c. 4th c. BCE–8th c. CE)] — codified in Purāṇic mythology through the legend of King Daṇḍa (cursed by sage Śukrācārya to have his fertile kingdom transformed into an ash-strewn jungle) and featured prominently in classical drama (e.g., Bhavabhūti's *Uttararāmacarita* and Kālidāsa's *Raghuvaṃśa*) — [perception: celebrated as the sublime, tragic theater of heroic exile, profound pathos, and spiritual penance]
- [Medieval Sanskrit Metrics & Hymnology (c. 10th–16th c.)] — generalized in *Chandaḥ-śāstra* (Sanskrit prosody) as a prestigious technical class of expansive metrical stotras (*daṇḍaka-kāvya*, such as the *Śyāmalā Daṇḍakam*), characterized by regular, staff-like rhythmic cadence — [perception: admired by scholars as a virtuoso literary meter evocative of relentless spiritual momentum]
- [Colonial Cartography & British Indology (19th c.)] — identified by British surveyors and Orientalist philologists (e.g., Alexander Cunningham, William Jones) with the rugged, heavily forested plateau of Bastar, the Eastern Ghats, and the Godāvarī basin — [perception: viewed as a fascinating geographical boundary marking the historical frontier between Aryanized northern India and the tribal Dravidian south]
- [Modern Post-Independence Development & Ecology (20th–21st c.)] — institutionalized in Indian governmental policy via the "Dandakaranya Project" (1958) to rehabilitate displaced refugees from East Pakistan in modern Chhattisgarh and Odisha, and today celebrated as a vital indigenous ecological corridor — [perception: recognized both as an enduring epic mythological landscape and a critical modern sociopolitical, tribal, and environmental territory]
