- 1815-1822 invention: military-tactical tool for silent night orders - ingenious, patriotic, utilitarian; perceived as Napoleonic cleverness
- 1821-1829 school trial: phonetic 12-dot sheets tested at Paris blind school - philanthropic but clumsy; pupils find cells too big, perception shifts to well-meaning failure
- Post-1829 eclipse: Braille's 6-dot alphabet supersedes it - from contender to footnote; connotation becomes precursor, stepping-stone, noble dead-end
- 20th-21st c. historiography: revived by disability studies and media archaeology as first binary tactile medium - scholarly, celebratory; modern perception is visionary prototype, missing link to haptics
