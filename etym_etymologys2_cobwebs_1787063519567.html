CONSTITUENT 1: COB (Spider)
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gewb- / *kewp-
"round object, head, vessel, bowl"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*kuppaz
"round vessel, head, round creature"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
coppe
"spider (round-bodied creature)"
(shortened from ātorcoppe)
↓
Middle English (c. 1300)
coppe / cobbe
"spider"

CONSTITUENT 2: WEB (Woven Fabric, Net)
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*webʰ-
"to weave, plait, construct with threads"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*wabją
"woven fabric, net, web"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
webb
"woven cloth, textile, spider's snare"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200)
web / webbe
"woven textile, spiderweb"

COMPOUND SYNTHESIS & PLURAL:
Middle English (c. 1320)
coppeweb / cobweb
"spiderweb, web of a spider"
(coppe + web)
↓
Modern English (plural)
cobwebs
"fine silken spider networks, especially old or dusty (plural)"
