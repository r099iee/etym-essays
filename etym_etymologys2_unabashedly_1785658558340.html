UNABASHEDLY

Note: esbahir = es- (from Latin ex-) + bahir,
whose base baer / batare "to gape" is of uncertain,
probably imitative origin. The "shame" sense of
English abash was reinforced by Old French
abaissier "to lower, humble" (from Latin bassus
"low"); the two Old French verbs are often
conflated in Middle English.
