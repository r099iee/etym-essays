Note on the debate: the verb "char" (to burn, c. 1560s) is generally taken as a back-formation from "charcoal", but the OED marks its origin as uncertain; it may have been influenced or reinforced by Old French "charbonner" (to char), from Latin "carbo" (charcoal, ember). The two candidate sources converge on the same word shape, so no single line of descent can be proven.
