- Medieval craft sense: to furnish with bosses (metal studs on shields, shrines) - martial, devotional, prestigious; connotation of strength and splendour
- Tudor expansion: to carve or swell in relief more generally, also of swollen flesh - artisanal yet increasingly literary; neutral-technical with poetic extension
- Print and paper era (18th-19th c.): blind embossing of stationery, book covers, stamps, and large raised letters for blind readers - genteel, commercial, and philanthropic; embossed = refined, official, accessible
- Industrial mass production: embossed packaging, wallpaper, textiles, credit cards - everyday, authenticating, tactile; connotation of quality-texture and security
- Contemporary digital: emboss filter, embossed UI, laser embossing, Braille embossing - skeuomorphic and inclusive; modern perception is both nostalgic-craft and accessibility-technical
