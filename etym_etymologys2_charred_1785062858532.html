Debate: The verb char is a back-formation (aphesis) from charcoal, ME charcole = OE cerr "turn" + cole "coal", i.e. "coal turned by fire". The path "turn" → "turn by fire" → "burn to charcoal" is sound, but the direct verbal use is a later English development.
