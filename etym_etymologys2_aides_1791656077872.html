Note: While modern British English historically used the spelling aid for both abstract assistance and the person assisting, 20th-century American lexicographical convention firmly standardized aide (preserving the French terminal -e from aide-de-camp) exclusively for human assistants and advisors, reserving aid for inanimate assistance, charitable relief, or mechanical devices.
