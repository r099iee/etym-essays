- Bronze Age & Archaic Greece [Autonomous Earth/Sky Protectress] - Worshipped at ancient prehistoric sanctuaries (Argos, Samos, Olympia) as an independent, all-powerful Queen-Goddess long before the full establishment of the patriarchal Olympian order.
- Classical Greece [Queen of Heaven & Defender of Lawful Marriage] - Codified by Homer and Hesiod as the lawful wife of Zeus (*Hera Teleia*); celebrated as the patroness of brides and motherhood, but feared as the relentless, vengeful punisher of infidelity and illegitimate demigods.
- Hellenistic & Roman Era [Civic Syncretism with Juno Regina] - Merged seamlessly with Roman *Juno*, becoming the quintessential symbol of Roman civic stability, maternal dignity (*matrona*), and imperial majesty beside Jupiter Capitolinus.
- Renaissance to Modern Era [Archetype of Sovereign Matriarchal Wrath] - Transformed in Western art, literature, and analytical psychology into the universal archetype of queenly dignity, spousal entitlement, and the devastating fury of a betrayed, inviolable partner.
