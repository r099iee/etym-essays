• Encouraged: Similar because both convey an increase in confidence; differs because encouraged implies gentle, supportive inspiration or moral reassurance, whereas emboldened implies a potent, assertive surge of daring or audacious risk-taking.
  • Heartened: Similar because both involve an elevation of internal spirit; differs because heartened emphasizes cheerfulness, renewed hope, and emotional relief rather than aggressive boldness.
  • Fortified: Similar because both indicate strengthening against doubt or opposition; differs because fortified suggests defensive, structural resilience and endurance against attack rather than proactive initiative.
  • Inspirited: Similar because both denote an infusion of vigorous spirit; differs because inspirited is more archaic and poetic, highlighting animated vitality and zeal rather than confrontational defiance.
  • Impelled: Similar because both drive a subject toward action; differs because impelled focuses on an overwhelming internal or external force driving behavior, without necessarily implying newfound courage.
  • Galvanized: Similar because both describe a sudden shift from inaction to decisive action; differs because galvanized emphasizes an electric, shock-like catalyst that stirs an entire group into urgent motion.
