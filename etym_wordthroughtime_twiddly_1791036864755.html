- 16th-17th c. verb - to twiddle = to trifle, toy idly; moral contempt for wasted time.
- 17th-18th c. fingers - to turn lightly between finger and thumb; tactile precision without purpose.
- 19th c. thumbs - to twiddle thumbs = to be idle; emblem of boredom and waiting.
- Late 19th c. dials - to twiddle knobs = to adjust tentatively; amateur radio and instrument anxiety.
- Early 20th c. adjective - twiddly = full of fussy little twists; craft and music slang, mildly comic dismissal.
- Late 20th c. to present - twiddly bits = ornamental licks, fiddly admin, nervous gestures; affectionate British informal for overdone smallness.
