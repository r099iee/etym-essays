• Remembering: Similar because both involve bringing something to mind; differs because remembering merely recovers a past fact, whereas bethinking implies active consideration and often a new resolve.
  • Recalling: Similar because both describe retrieving something from memory; differs because recalling stresses the retrieval of a specific fact or event, without the deliberative or reflexive self-address of bethinking.
  • Reflecting: Similar because both describe careful thought; differs because reflecting stresses cool, extended meditation, while bethinking is more decisive and turns on a change of plan.
  • Considering: Similar because both involve weighing options; differs because considering is broad and neutral, while bethinking is reflexive and somewhat archaic, with a self-directed urgency.
  • Realizing: Similar because both can mark a moment when something comes to mind; differs because realizing stresses sudden awareness of a truth, while bethinking stresses deliberate, self-directed consideration.
  • Reminding himself: Similar because both describe the subject prompting his own memory; differs because reminding himself is explicitly self-prompting and modern, whereas bethinking presents the thought as arising within internal deliberation.
  • Calling to mind: Similar because both mean bringing something into awareness; differs because calling to mind is a neutral recall idiom, while bethinking adds the reflexive construction and the flavor of taking thought.
  • Taking thought: Similar because both mean engaging in deliberate reflection; differs because taking thought is a plain idiom of consideration, whereas bethinking is a single lexicalized verb with an archaic register.
  • Musing: Similar because both describe internal thought; differs because musing is dreamy and unhurried, while bethinking is purposeful and often issue-resolving.
  • Pondering: Similar because both involve sustained attention to a question; differs because pondering is weighty but not reflexive, lacking the "think oneself into a course" structure of bethinking.
  • Deliberating: Similar because both describe careful weighing before action; differs because deliberating is formal and can be collective, while bethinking is necessarily reflexive and singular.
  • Cognizing: Similar because both belong to the semantic field of thinking; differs because cognizing is technical psychological jargon, while bethinking is literary and archaic.
