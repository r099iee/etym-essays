Debate: the origin of Latin planta "sole of the foot" is debated — from PIE *pleth₂- "flat, broad" (cf. planus "flat", Greek platýs "broad") or a substrate borrowing. The homonymous planta "young shoot" (source of English plant) may be a distinct word.
