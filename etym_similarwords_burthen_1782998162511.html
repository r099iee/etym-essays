• Burden: Similar because it is the same word and means exactly the same load, obligation, or weight; differs because burden is the plain modern standard spelling, while burthen is the deliberate archaic and solemn register-choice.
  • Load: Similar because it is something carried; differs because it is a neutral physical quantity transported by any means, with no necessary sense of oppression or moral weight.
  • Weight: Similar because it means heaviness or pressure; differs because it is the measurable force of gravity (or any impersonal deadening force), without the bearer's felt struggle that burthen implies.
  • Onus: Similar because it names a responsibility laid upon someone; differs because it is formal and legalistic, while burthen is archaic, poetic, and spiritual.
  • Encumbrance: Similar because it is a hindering load; differs because it is technical and detached (financial or legal encumbrances), lacking the physical and emotional imagery of burthen.
  • Cargo: Similar because it is goods that are carried; differs because it is purely commercial transport freight, with no bearer who feels the weight as a personal trial.
  • Freight: Similar because it names transported goods; differs because it emphasizes the commercial charge for carrying, not the experience of carrying.
  • Charge: Similar because it can mean a responsibility or task committed to someone; differs because it is broader and colder, covering commands, prices, and electrical energy, with none of burthen's weight-bearing imagery.
