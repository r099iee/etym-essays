Note: the PIE stage *mazdo-s is traditional (Pokorny) but rests mainly on the Germanic forms; the link to Latin mālus “mast” is disputed because the sound correspondence is irregular, and the Latin word is sometimes treated as unrelated. A separate homonym, Old English mæst “fallen acorns, pig fodder”, has an entirely different origin and does not belong to this chain.
