• Buzzing: Similar because both denote producing a continuous vibratory sound; differs because 'buzzing' implies lower-pitched, insectoid, or electrical vibration without the necessary implication of high-speed ballistic trajectory.
  • Hissing: Similar because both involve sibilant frictional noise; differs because 'hissing' is produced by escaping steam, serpents, or sharp exhalations, lacking the connotation of rapid kinetic flight through space.
  • Zooming: Similar because both describe rapid movement through space; differs because 'zooming' emphasizes visual magnification or low-pitched engine roar rather than high-frequency aerodynamic sibilance.
  • Streaking: Similar because both denote extremely fast movement; differs because 'streaking' focuses primarily on visual blur, leaving a visible line or flash of light across the visual field.
  • Zipping: Similar because both describe rapid, energetic motion; differs because 'zipping' emphasizes crisp, short, mechanical, or punctual movement.
  • Whirring: Similar because both denote continuous sound during motion; differs because 'whirring' specifically describes the rhythmic, cyclical hum of rotating blades, gears, or wings.
  • Flying: Similar because both denote aerial movement; differs because 'flying' is the broad, neutral term for airborne travel, lacking the sensory depiction of high-velocity auditory friction.
