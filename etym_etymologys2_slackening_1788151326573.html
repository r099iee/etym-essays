Debate note: The PIE stage *sleg- is a hypothetical reconstruction, not a securely attested root; its connection with Latin laxus “loose” (from PIE *slh₁g-) and Greek λήγω (“to leave off, cease”) is plausible and commonly asserted but debated in detail. The chain from Proto-Germanic *slakaz onward is secure and documented.
