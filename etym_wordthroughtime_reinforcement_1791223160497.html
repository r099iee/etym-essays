- Engineering extension (1700s–1800s) - Added material that strengthens structures: reinforced concrete, reinforced seams; practical, industrial, reliability-focused perception
- Moral and social extension (1800s–early 1900s) - Reinforcement of discipline, faith, or property norms through repetition and teaching; didactic, institutional tone as in the source sentence
- Behaviorist technicalization (1910s–1960s) - Skinner and Pavlov formalize positive and negative reinforcement; clinical, scientific, laboratory connotation enters everyday parenting and education
- Contemporary feedback sense - Any loop that entrenches a pattern (media echo chambers, algorithmic reinforcement); neutral-to-critical perception of self-perpetuating systems
