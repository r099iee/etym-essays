Note: the origin of the name is genuinely unknown. Ancient writers offered a folk etymology from King Tiberinus of Alba Longa, who was said to have drowned in the river; modern scholars have proposed an Etruscan or other pre-Latin substrate source, and a link with Celtic *dubros "water" has been suggested but is phonologically weak. The earliest attested form is Latin Tiberis.
