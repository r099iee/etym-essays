• Stubbornly: Similar because both mean refusing to change one's mind or course; differs because stubbornly is the plain everyday native word, while obstinately is more formal, Latinate, and carries a slightly more pejorative edge.
  • Resolutely: Similar because both mean holding firmly to a purpose; differs because resolutely is positive and goal-oriented (firm resolve), while obstinately hints at a fault of refusing persuasion.
  • Determinedly: Similar because both mean proceeding with fixed intention; differs because determinedly is admiring and energetic, while obstinately suggests inflexibility that may be excessive.
  • Persistently: Similar because both mean continuing despite difficulty; differs because persistently stresses repetition over time (acting again and again), while obstinately stresses fixed refusal to yield.
  • Doggedly: Similar because both mean sticking to a task or opinion with tenacity; differs because doggedly suggests patient, gritty pursuit of a goal, while obstinately suggests resistance to persuasion or authority.
  • Tenaciously: Similar because both mean holding on firmly; differs because tenaciously emphasizes gripping or clinging to a position, while obstinately emphasizes the refusal to change it under pressure.
  • Adamantly: Similar because both mean unshakeably fixed in a position; differs because adamantly stresses immovability and finality (like adamant, the unbreakable stone), while obstinately stresses the continuing act of standing against persuasion.
  • Obdurately: Similar because both name hardened, unyielding stubbornness; differs because obdurately is more formal and suggests moral hardness or callousness, while obstinately keeps a blunter everyday stubbornness.
  • Pigheadedly: Similar because both describe mulish refusal to yield; differs because pigheadedly is cruder, informal, and fully pejorative, while obstinately is formal and can be sympathetic in context.
  • Firmly: Similar because both mean unyieldingly; differs because firmly can be calm, admirable, and neutral (a firm answer), while obstinately carries friction and a hint of excessive resistance.
  • Unyieldingly: Similar because both mean refusing to give way; differs because unyieldingly is a transparent neutral paraphrase of the same idea, while obstinately keeps the Latinate moral colouring.
  • Pertinaciously: Similar because both mean clinging to a position with stubborn persistence; differs because pertinaciously is rarer, bookish, and stresses clinging or holding, while obstinately is the ordinary formal word for mulish fixedness.
