[Root 1: HONEY (Old English hunig / PIE *kngk- / *kneko- "golden, yellow")]
PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*kngk- / *kneko-
"golden, yellow, amber-colored"
Note: Replaced the older PIE root *mélit- ('honey', source of Latin mel / Greek méli) in the Germanic branch through taboo-avoidance or color description.
↓

*hunagą
"honey, golden sweet substance"
↓
Old English (c. 5th–11th c.)
hunig
"honey, sweet nectar"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–15th c.)
hony / honi
"honey, sweetness"
