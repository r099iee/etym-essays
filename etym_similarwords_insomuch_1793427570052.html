• Inasmuch: Similar because both are compound conjunctions expressing degree, justification, or scope; differs because inasmuch (invariably followed by as) predominantly introduces a causal premise or rationale (‘since’ or ‘considering that’), whereas insomuch primarily measures magnitude culminating in a concrete consequence (‘to such an extent that’).
  • Insofar: Similar because both delimit the degree or extent to which a proposition holds true; differs because insofar sets conceptual or epistemological boundaries (‘only to the extent that’), whereas insomuch emphasizes an accumulated degree producing an empirical result.
  • So that: Similar because both introduce a clause expressing a resultant condition or consequence; differs because so that is a versatile, neutral modern conjunction that can denote either result or purpose, whereas insomuch is elevated, archaic, and strictly consecutive.
  • To such an extent: Similar because both explicitly quantify the magnitude or intensity of an action before introducing its outcome; differs because to such an extent is a multi-word analytic prepositional phrase in contemporary standard prose, while insomuch is a single fused, classical synthetic connector.
  • Consequently: Similar because both connect an antecedent cause to a consequent effect; differs because consequently is a transitional adverb indicating logical sequence without quantifying the degree of the cause, whereas insomuch embeds the notion of extreme intensity directly into the connection.
  • Hence: Similar because both indicate that one reality flows out of another; differs because hence is a terse, inferential marker signifying ‘from this source’ or ‘for this reason’, lacking the descriptive quantitative scale inherent in insomuch.
  • Whereby: Similar because both serve as formal connective adverbs within intricate sentence structures; differs because whereby denotes the instrument, method, or mechanism through which something occurs (‘by which means’), while insomuch denotes degree and resultant consequence.
  • Wherefore: Similar because both belong to the elevated register of archaic, classical transitions; differs because wherefore signifies ‘for which reason’ or ‘why’, focusing on motivation and deduction rather than degree-driven outcomes.
  • Accordingly: Similar because both denote an action or result corresponding to what preceded it; differs because accordingly emphasizes harmony, obedience, or fitting consequence, whereas insomuch stresses the sheer magnitude that compelled the outcome.
