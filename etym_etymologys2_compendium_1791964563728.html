compendium

Notes: the root is usually cited as *(s)pen- “to draw, stretch, spin” (whence also pendēre “to hang” and English spider). The bookish sense “abridgment” is post-classical; classical Latin uses the noun chiefly for a saving, a profit and a shortcut.
