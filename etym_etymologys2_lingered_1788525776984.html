Note on Phonological Raising: The vowel shift from Old/Middle English e (lengeren) to modern i (lingeren / linger) illustrates the regular phonetic raising of vowels before nasal-velar consonant clusters (/ŋɡ/), a sound change also observed in words like string (compare German Strang / Old English streng).
