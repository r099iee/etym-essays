- Little sister pet-name - affectionate nursery diminutive for a sister or little girl, warm and tender
- Transferred tease for boys - applied to a timid or girlish boy as mockery, affection curdling into gender policing
- General coward insult - broadened to any whiner, quitter, or weak thing, schoolyard and barracks staple, strongly pejorative
- Homophobic slur overlay - used against effeminate gay men, now recognised as hateful and offensive
- Ironic reclamation - poets, queer writers, and feminists quote it back defiantly to expose macho anti-intellectualism
