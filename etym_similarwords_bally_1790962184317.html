  • cursed: Similar because both frame the subject as a source of misery; differs because cursed implies solemn condemnation, misfortune, or supernatural blight rather than lighthearted annoyance.
  • ruddy: Similar because both act as mild euphemistic substitutes for bloody; differs because ruddy plays directly on the reddish, blood-like color and remained common among rural and working-class speakers.
