• sweetbreads: Similar because it is the English name for the same thymus/pancreas glands; differs because it is generic and covers lamb, calf and pork without specifying the financière garnish.
  • blanquette de veau: Similar because it is another classic French veal dish in white sauce; differs because it uses stewed shoulder meat in velouté, not offal in brown Madeira sauce.
  • tête de veau: Similar because it is another prestigious calf dish requiring skilled preparation; differs because it is boiled calf's head with vinaigrette, not sweetbreads.
  • rognons de veau: Similar because it is calf offal in mustard or Madeira sauce; differs because kidneys have strong urinous flavour and firmer texture versus creamy sweetbreads.
  • cervelles de veau: Similar because it is delicate calf offal, often fried; differs because brains are softer, more fragile and usually served with black butter, not financière garnish.
  • foie de veau: Similar because it is prized calf offal in French cookery; differs because liver is mineral, bloody and sliced, while sweetbreads are creamy and mild.
  • vol-au-vent financière: Similar because it uses the identical financière garnish and Madeira sauce; differs because the base is puff pastry filled with chicken and mushrooms rather than sweetbreads.
  • bouchée à la reine: Similar because it is a small pastry with rich white garnish of sweetbreads and mushrooms; differs because its sauce is white suprême/velouté, not brown financière.
  • suprême de volaille à la financière: Similar because it pairs white meat with the same truffles-quenelles-olives garnish; differs because chicken breast replaces veal sweetbreads.
  • quenelles: Similar because they are a component of the financière garnish; differs because they are poached forcemeat dumplings alone, not the whole dish.
  • ris d'agneau: Similar because it is the same gland from lamb; differs because lamb sweetbreads are smaller, stronger and cheaper than calf.
  • veau Marengo: Similar because it is a historic French veal dish in rich brown sauce with mushrooms and olives; differs because it uses braised veal chunks with tomatoes, not sweetbreads with truffles.
