Note: Moria is a coined proper name, not a natural-language word, so no PIE derivation exists — the chain above is the word's real (invented-language) derivation as glossed by Tolkien himself. The resemblance to Hebrew מוריה (Moriah) is a fortuitous homograph which Tolkien, asked in a 1967 letter (no. 297), called unintended while conceding possible subconscious influence. Greek μωρία “folly” (Erasmus's Moriae Encomium) is an unrelated homograph.
