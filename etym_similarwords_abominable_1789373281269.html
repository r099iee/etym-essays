• Detestable: Similar because both describe that which arouses intense hatred, disgust, and moral condemnation; differs because detestable emphasizes personal, emotional, or ethical contempt and hatred (deserving to be detested), whereas abominable conveys a deeper sense of monstrous pollution, religious horror, or atrocious physical and editorial wretchedness.
  • Loathsome: Similar because both characterize that which excites extreme repulsion; differs because loathsome highlights physical, visceral nausea and sickening revulsion (such as a loathsome disease, rotting stench, or slimy reptile), whereas abominable encompasses moral horror, spiritual sacrilege, and severe textual corruption.
  • Atrocious: Similar because both denote exceptionally bad quality, dreadful taste, or horrific cruelty; differs because atrocious emphasizes monstrous, savage violence or glaring, shocking violations of standards, whereas abominable emphasizes the feeling of ill-omened repulsiveness and deep offense to sensibility.
  • Abhorrent: Similar because both describe things that repel the soul and violently clash with conscience; differs because abhorrent emphasizes the reflexive emotional shudder and active shrinking-back of the perceiver (drawing back in horror), whereas abominable characterizes the objective, inherent foulness of the offending entity itself.
  • Heinous: Similar because both describe monstrously evil acts; differs because heinous is reserved strictly for grave, unforgivable crimes against humanity or law that carry enormous legal culpability, lacking the colloquial versatility of abominable (one does not describe bad weather or poor typography as heinous).
  • Odious: Similar because both denote things that arouse strong dislike and aversion; differs because odious emphasizes offensiveness that makes someone or something thoroughly hated, disagreeable, or obnoxious in social conduct, without necessarily reaching the catastrophic or monstrous proportions of abominable.
  • Wretched: Similar because both can describe miserable quality or pitiable conditions; differs because wretched emphasizes pathetic misfortune, squalor, or heartbreaking debasement, whereas abominable implies an offensive, insulting, or outrageous level of badness.
