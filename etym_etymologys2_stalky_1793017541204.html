Proto-Germanic (reconstructed, c. 500 BCE)
*stal-
‘stem, support, something stiff’
↓
Old English (9th c.)
stalu, stela
‘stem, stalk, support’
↓
Middle English (c. 1400)
stalke
‘stem of a plant’
↓
Early Modern English (16th c.)
stalk + -y
‘stem’ + ‘full of’
↓
Modern English (current)
stalky
‘full of stalks’

Note: the path before Middle English stalke is obscure and debated; the link to Old English stalu and stela is the leading handbook hypothesis rather than a fully certain chain, and some scholars leave the Middle English form without a deeper etymology.
