• Emerged: Similar because both mean coming out from within something. Differs because emerged is the modern neutral word for appearing; issued is formal-literary and implies an exit from a specific enclosure (a gate, a door, a source).
  • Came out: Similar because both mean moving out of a place. Differs because came out is plain everyday English; issued carries ceremony and formality.
  • Exited: Similar because both mean leaving. Differs because exited is neutral-technical (exit signs, data structures); issued suggests emergence with presence, not mere departure.
  • Emanated: Similar because both mean flowing out from a source. Differs because emanated is abstract and immaterial (light, odours, influence); issued can be concrete (troops, water, documents).
  • Flowed (out): Similar because both mean moving out of a source. Differs because flowed implies liquid or continuous motion; issued covers any kind of coming forth, including people and commands.
  • Proceeded: Similar because both mean moving onward. Differs because proceeded implies continuing a course, not emerging from a place; issued is about the exit itself.
  • Resulted / Arose / Stemmed: Similar because all mean coming about from a cause. Differs because these are the abstract "outcome" synonyms; issued in this sense is rarer and more formal.
  • Published: Similar because both mean making a text public. Differs because published is specific to books and articles; issued covers orders, warnings, stamps, and supplies too.
  • Released: Similar because both mean letting something go out. Differs because released implies prior restraint (a prisoner, an album); issued implies formal entitlement or authority.
  • Distributed: Similar because both mean giving out to many. Differs because distributed stresses the spreading among recipients; issued stresses the authority that sends forth.
  • Supplied / Provided: Similar because both mean furnishing what is needed. Differs because these are neutral and commercial; issued is institutional and official (military rations, passports).
  • Delivered: Similar because both mean bringing something to its recipient. Differs because delivered is physical transport; issued is authorisation to give out.
  • Emitted / Discharged: Similar because both mean sending something out (gas, light, fluid). Differs because these are technical, of engines and bodies; issued is broader and formal.
  • Promulgated / Proclaimed: Similar because both mean announcing officially. Differs because these are restricted to laws and decrees; issued covers every kind of official sending-forth.
  • Launched: Similar because both mean setting something going. Differs because launched implies a dramatic beginning (a ship, a campaign); issued implies routine authorised distribution.
