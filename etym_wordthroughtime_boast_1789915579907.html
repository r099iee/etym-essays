- c.1300 noise and pride - bost means loud vaunting, glory and arrogance; bosten means to brag
- 16th-17th c. threat and glory - to boast also means to menace loudly and to vaunt lineage and deeds
- 18th-19th c. social provocation - to boast of riches to kin baits envy, as the brother fears daring display
- Victorian moral censure - boasting marks vulgarity against modesty; children's books warn against boasters
- Modern possession and broadcast - hotels and phones boast features neutrally, while social media revives bragging as updating
