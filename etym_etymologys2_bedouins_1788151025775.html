Bedouins

Note on nuance: English shows spelling variants (Bedouin, Beduin, Bedoween) mirroring French bédouin and dialectal Arabic pronunciations; the derivation from Arabic badawī is undisputed. The Arabic form is a nisba (relationship adjective) built on badw "desert," so the word’s literal core is "one belonging to the desert."
