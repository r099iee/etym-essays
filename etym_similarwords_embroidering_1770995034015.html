• embellishing: Similar because both mean adding beauty or decorative detail; differs because embellishing applies to any ornamentation (rooms, writing, music) and especially to inflating a story, while embroidering specifically implies ornamental needlework or the figure of delicate, stitch-like addition to a tale.
  • ornamenting: Similar because both mean adding decorative elements; differs because ornamenting is general and can apply to objects, buildings, or words, while embroidering is rooted in thread-on-fabric craftsmanship.
  • sewing: Similar because both involve needle and thread; differs because sewing is broadly structural and utilitarian — joining, mending, making garments — while embroidering is additive and decorative, adorning an existing surface.
  • stitching: Similar because both describe needlework; differs because stitching is the neutral act of making stitches for construction or repair, while embroidering is the ornamental patterning of those stitches.
  • weaving: Similar because both are textile crafts; differs because weaving creates the fabric itself from warp and weft, whereas embroidering decorates fabric that already exists.
  • decorating: Similar because both mean making something more attractive; differs because decorating is generic (rooms, cakes, walls), while embroidering is tied specifically to needlework or narrative flourish.
  • adorning: Similar because both mean adding beauty; differs because adorning is a general, often verbal or physical ornament, while embroidering carries the specific needle-and-thread image.
  • exaggerating: Similar because both involve adding untrue detail to a story; differs because exaggerating inflates the scale of what is true, while embroidering adds decorative invention that may be false but is ornamented rather than merely scaled up.
  • fabricating: Similar because both can involve adding invented detail; differs because fabricating implies wholesale invention or forgery, while embroidering implies decorating the truth with extra detail rather than replacing it.
  • beading: Similar because both are ornamental needle crafts; differs because beading attaches beads to a surface, while embroidering works thread into patterns.
  • quilting: Similar because both are decorative needle crafts; differs because quilting layers fabric with stitching for warmth and texture, while embroidering decorates a single surface.
