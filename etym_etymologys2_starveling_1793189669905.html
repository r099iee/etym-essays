Note: the chain above Proto-Germanic is disputed: Germanic *sterbaną has no certain cognates outside Germanic, and the link to PIE *(s)ter- (‘stiff’, as in a corpse growing rigid) is a suggestion, not a proof. The honest record begins with Proto-Germanic *sterbaną ‘to die’. Note also the northern semantic narrowing: Scots and northern English used starve for dying of cold (‘starved with cold’), while southern use settled on hunger.
