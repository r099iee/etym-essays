- Old English - woven fillet - narrow textile strip for binding clothes, neutral craft term
- 16th-19th c. - ribbon and measure - red tape binds deeds, tapeline measures cloth, bureaucratic overtone grows
- 1920s-30s - sticky strip - Scotch and surgical tape make to tape mean to seal or mend quickly
- 1950s-80s - recorded medium - to tape a show means to capture sound and video on magnetic ribbon
- Modern - fixed and finished - everything taped out means plans sealed and secure, plus taped evidence keeps recording prestige
