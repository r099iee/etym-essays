Note: the exact PIE reconstruction (with or without s-mobile, and with or without laryngeal detail) is a matter of scholarly convention, but the Germanic meso-stage *staupaz and the Old English adjective stēap are securely attested by the full family of old Germanic forms (Old Norse steypr, Old High German stouf).
