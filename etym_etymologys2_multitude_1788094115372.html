Note on the debate: The origin of Latin multus is disputed. The traditional view links it to PIE *mel- 'strong, great' (cf. Greek mala 'very'); an alternative derives it from *ml̥-to- 'crushed, ground' (a participle of *melh₁- 'to grind'), making multus literally 'ground into many pieces' — attractive but unproven. Etymonline and de Vaan treat the etymology as uncertain; *mel- is the standard textbook reconstruction and is flagged here as debated.
