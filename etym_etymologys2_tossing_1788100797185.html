Note: the origin of toss is unknown — no PIE chain exists. Standard authorities call it obscure; the leading hypothesis is a Scandinavian borrowing (Norwegian dialect tossa “to strew, toss”); an older suggested link to Latin toxāre “to poison” is rejected. The chain therefore begins at the first attestation, per the zero-guesswork rule.
