Note: the origin is disputed. The chain above follows the standard account, which builds the Savoyard word on Latin ad + vallis ("toward the valley"). A rival theory connects the Savoyard form to a Germanic base meaning "to roll" (Alpine German influence); another links it to Latin labina "landslide", from labi "to slide, slip". No single origin is universally accepted.
