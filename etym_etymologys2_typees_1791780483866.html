Typees

Note: In Nuku Hiva, the name Taipi literally designated the people of the Taipivai valley ('water of the full tide'). Neighboring tribes like the Tei'i used 'Taipi' with the pejorative connotation of 'enemy' or 'cannibal', which Melville adopted and anglicized as 'Typee'.
