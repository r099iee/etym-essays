Note: The monetary application arose directly from the hexameter rhyming legend stamped on the reverse of the original Venetian gold coin minted under Doge Giovanni Dandolo in 1284: Sit tibi, Christe, datus, quem tu regis, iste ducatus (“To thee, O Christ, be dedicated this duchy which thou rulest”). The final word of the inscription, ducatus (“duchy”), gave the coin its permanent popular name across Europe.
