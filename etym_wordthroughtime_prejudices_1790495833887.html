- Roman praeiudicium - prior legal judgment, precedent; procedural, neutral
- Medieval praejudicium - Church prejudgment, harm to rights; legal, formulaic
- 14th-16th c. English prejudice - prior opinion, injury; borrowed, double (mind and law)
- Enlightenment prejudice attacked - Bacon’s idols, Locke, Voltaire condemn prejudices; rationalist, hostile
- Burke-Ruskin prejudice defended - prejudices as affectionate wisdom, Tory habit; conservative, tender
- Victorian-Liberal prejudice condemned - racial, class, sexual prejudices exposed; reforming, moral
- Modern implicit prejudice studied - unconscious bias measured and trained; psychological, programmatic
