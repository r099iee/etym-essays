Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500–2500 BCE)
*pelh₁- / *pleh₁-
"to fill; full, multitude, crowd"
Note: Linguistic consensus derives Pleiás from *pleíōn ('more, many') referring to the dense cluster. Ancient folk etymology instead connected it to Greek plein ('to sail') because its rising marked the navigation season.
