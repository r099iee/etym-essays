Note: the PIE noun was heteroclitic, alternating *wódr̥ with the oblique stem *wedn-; Germanic generalised the r-stem. The High German double consonant zz/ss reflects the second consonant shift of West Germanic *t. English water, Greek hýdōr, Hittite wātar and Russian vodá are cognates, not sources.
