- Medieval heart-joining - accorden means to bring hearts together, reconcile enemies and lovers; perception chivalric-Christian, concord as peace of hearts.
- Late medieval granting - to accord favours, thanks, and honours; connotation of lordly largesse, what a joined heart freely gives.
- Early modern voluntary phrase - of one’s own accord fixes as without compulsion; image of acting from one’s own heart, admired as free will.
- 18th–19th-century diplomatic noun - accords as treaties between nations; perception solemn-international, signatures that join national hearts.
- Contemporary corroboration verb - evidence accords with testimony; formal-scholarly for things fitting without contradiction, as when absence of memory accords with late love.
