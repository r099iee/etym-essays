• struck: Similar because both mean delivering a blow; differs because struck is the modern, neutral default past of strike, with none of smote's archaic, biblical, fateful colouring.
  • hit: Similar because both mean dealing a blow; differs because hit is colloquial and usually deliberate, whereas smote is elevated and often names a blow dealt to one by fate or emotion.
  • afflicted: Similar because both mean overcome by a painful force; differs because afflicted names prolonged physical or mental suffering rather than a single sudden blow.
  • seized: Similar because both mean an impersonal force taking hold of a person; differs because seized implies a gripping, holding grasp rather than the one decisive stroke of smite.
  • overcame: Similar because both mean being defeated or dominated; differs because overcame suggests struggle and eventual defeat, while smote is a single instantaneous blow.
