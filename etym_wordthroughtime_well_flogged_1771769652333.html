- [18th century corporal punishment] - ['Flogged' is the plain school-and-navy word for beaten with a whip or birch; 'well-flogged' adds thoroughness]
- [18th-19th century school discipline metaphor] - [Becomes a grim comic boast of classical education: a 'well-flogged critic' is one whose precision was literally whipped into him]
- [19th-20th century rigorous training] - [The literal force fades; 'well-flogged' is used of any thoroughly drilled or disciplined performer]
- [20th century to present ironical echo] - [Survives in memoir and historical writing as a self-deprecating, period-flavoured phrase for the brutal thoroughness of training]
