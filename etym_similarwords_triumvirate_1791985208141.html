• troika: Similar because it is the standard modern term for a ruling or negotiating group of three; differs because it comes from Russian and carries a bureaucratic, often Soviet or diplomatic flavour, whereas triumvirate is classical and literary.
  • trio: Similar because it denotes three people together; differs because a trio is neutral and can be a musical group or a set of friends, with no implication of shared political power.
  • triad: Similar because it means a set of three; differs because it is abstract and often structural (a triad of principles, a criminal triad), not a governing body.
  • trinity: Similar because it denotes three-in-one; differs because it is theological and indivisible, whereas a triumvirate is three separate people sharing power and can break apart.
  • triumvir: Similar because it names a member of such a group; differs because it is the individual, not the body, and in Latin denotes a holder of a specific commission.
  • triarchy: Similar because it means rule by three; differs because it is a rare analytical term for a constitutional form, not the name of a historical alliance or a management team.
  • duumvirate: Similar because it is the analogous term for rule by two; differs because it is much rarer and denotes a formal board of two rather than three.
  • quadrumvirate: Similar because it is the analogous term for rule by four; differs because it is a deliberate coinage and almost always used with humorous or pedantic effect.
  • junta: Similar because it denotes a small group ruling by concerted power; differs because a junta is military and usually seized power by force, whereas a triumvirate can be legal or informal.
  • oligarchy: Similar because it means rule by a small group; differs because it names a class of regime, not a specific number, and carries a stronger condemnatory charge.
  • directorate: Similar because it is a body that directs an institution; differs because it is a formal administrative structure, often larger than three and without the flavour of personal alliance.
