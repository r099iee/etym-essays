Debate: The name is traditionally derived from Hebrew shālaḥ "to send" — the "sent" or "sending forth" waters of the conduit — but this is a Biblical folk-etymology whose exact formation is not fully secure. "Siloa" is the English form used by Milton; the KJV uses "Siloam".
