=== COMPONENT 1: GOD ===
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gʰuto-
"invoked one; that which is libated"
↓
Old English (c. 750 CE)
God
"supreme deity"

=== COMPONENT 2: ROT ===
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*h₁rewd- / *rewd-
"to rot, putrefy, decay"
↓
Old English (c. 850 CE)
rotian
"to decay, decompose, putrefy"

=== MINCED OATH CONTRACTION ===
Early Modern English Imprecation (c. 1650 CE)
God rot (thee / it)!
"may God cause you/it to putrefy"
↓
18th c. Minced Euphemism (c. 1750 CE)
'od rot / od-drot
"sanitized minced curse"
↓
Early 19th c. Contraction (c. 1815 CE)
drat
"curse it!; exclamation of annoyance"
↓
Victorian Adjectival Formation (c. 1840 CE)
dratted
"confounded, damned, annoying"
