• arrest: Similar because it means to seize by legal authority; differs because arrest is purely legal-procedural and implies charges, while apprehend can also mean understand or dread.
  • seize: Similar because it means to take hold suddenly; differs because seize is forceful and general (seize power, seize a bag), while apprehend is formal and person-focused.
  • capture: Similar because it means to take prisoner or take hold; differs because capture suggests warfare, hunting, or winning a prize, while apprehend suggests law enforcement.
  • detain: Similar because it means to hold in custody; differs because detain stresses holding back temporarily, while apprehend stresses the initial laying-hold.
  • grasp: Similar because in the mental sense it means to understand; differs because grasp is everyday and physical as well, while apprehend is formal and philosophical.
  • comprehend: Similar because it means to understand fully; differs because comprehend implies complete encompassing, while apprehend implies immediate direct seeing.
  • understand: Similar because it means to get the meaning; differs because understand is neutral and universal, while apprehend is literary and implies quickness of mind.
  • perceive: Similar because it means to become aware of through senses or mind; differs because perceive stresses sensory intake, while apprehend stresses intellectual grip.
  • dread: Similar because in the fear sense it means to anticipate evil; differs because dread is stronger and more emotional, while apprehend is cooler and more anticipatory.
  • fear: Similar because it means to feel anxiety about the future; differs because fear is general and visceral, while apprehend is formal and implies mental foresight.
  • nab: Similar because it means to catch a wrongdoer; differs because nab is slangy and casual, while apprehend is official and dignified.
