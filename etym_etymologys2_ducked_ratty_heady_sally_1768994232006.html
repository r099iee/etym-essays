Debate: Proto-Germanic *dūkaną has no certain cognates outside Germanic. For rat, the PIE link *rēd- "to gnaw" (cf. Latin rodere) is a common but uncertain suggestion. For head, PIE *káput is widely accepted (cf. Latin caput, Sanskrit kapālam); the shift to *haubudą is regular Grimm's Law.
