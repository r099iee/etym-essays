• Agile: Similar because both describe quickness and ease of movement; differs because agile stresses whole-body athletic coordination, while nimble emphasizes lightness and delicate precision, often of the hands, fingers, or feet.
  • Spry: Similar because both describe lively, energetic movement; differs because spry is used almost exclusively of active elderly people, while nimble applies to all ages and to mental as well as physical quickness.
  • Deft: Similar because both describe skillful, practiced handwork; differs because deft foregrounds competence and precision without necessarily implying speed, whereas nimble joins speed with light, flexible grace.
  • Fleet: Similar because both describe fast movement; differs because fleet refers strictly to swift running or footedness, while nimble also covers fingers, hands, and the mind.
  • Quick: Similar because both mean fast; differs because quick is the general word for speed, while nimble adds lightness, ease, and readiness — quickness without heaviness.
  • Supple: Similar because both describe ease of bodily movement; differs because supple stresses flexibility and bendability, while nimble stresses speed and readiness of action.
  • Lively: Similar because both suggest energetic, vigorous movement; differs because lively is broad (also of mood and color), while nimble specifically denotes quick, light movement.
  • Brisk: Similar because both suggest energetic activity; differs because brisk describes pace and efficiency of effort, while nimble describes the quality of the movement itself.
  • Alert: Similar because both involve readiness and quick response; differs because alert emphasizes watchfulness and attention, while nimble emphasizes the speed and lightness of the physical or mental response.
  • Sprightly: Similar because both suggest light, lively movement; differs because sprightly carries a cheerful, vivacious tone, while nimble is more neutral about mood.
  • Adroit: Similar because both describe skill and quickness in action; differs because adroit emphasizes cleverness and resourcefulness in handling situations, while nimble emphasizes lightness and speed.
  • Lithe: Similar because both describe easy, graceful bodily movement; differs because lithe stresses slenderness and suppleness of build, while nimble stresses quickness of response.
  • Dexterous: Similar because both describe skilled use of the hands; differs because dexterous emphasizes practiced skill and coordination, while nimble emphasizes the quick, light ease of the movement.
  • Acrobatic: Similar because both describe agile bodily control; differs because acrobatic implies trained, spectacular feats of agility, while nimble is the ordinary, everyday quickness anyone might have.
