• Face: Similar in denoting the front of the head; differs because face is the neutral, everyday default term carrying none of the elevation or monumentality, while a visage is looked at as a composed whole.
  • Countenance: Similar in being an elevated word for the face; differs because countenance points to the expression and state of mind the face betrays, whereas a visage is seen from outside, as a form and a record.
  • Physiognomy: Similar in concerning the face's structure; differs because physiognomy is the technical, pseudo-scientific word of the character-reader who analyzes features, while visage contemplates the whole without claiming to read it.
  • Features: Similar in being part of the face's appearance; differs because features names the parts — eyes, nose, mouth — while visage is the entire composed form those parts make up together.
  • Looks: Similar in denoting a person's outward appearance; differs because looks is plural and informal, concerned with beauty or plainness, while visage is singular, formal, and concerned with the face as a visible record.
  • Appearance: Similar in denoting the way a person is seen; differs because appearance is a general word covering dress, posture, and effect, with no anatomical commitment, while visage is specifically the face itself.
  • Aspect: Similar in denoting the way something looks or presents itself; differs because aspect is impersonal and can apply to a direction of view, a facet, or a phase, whereas visage is a face, human or personified, seen whole.
  • Mug: Similar in denoting the face; differs because mug is coarse slang, emphatically ordinary and often comic, the very opposite of visage's elevation.
  • Phiz: Similar in denoting the face; differs because phiz (from physiognomy) is jocular, Victorian, and colloquial, while visage is serious and poetic.
  • Dial: Similar in denoting the face, as a clock-face or watch-face; differs because dial is a colloquial, humorous term treating the face as an instrument to be read, whereas visage treats it as a monument or document.
