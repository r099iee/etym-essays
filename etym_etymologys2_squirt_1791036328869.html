English (modern)
squirt
"thin jet; little"

Note: beyond Middle English the origin is debated and generally taken as imitative (echoic) of the sound of ejection, with no secure PIE root or continental cognate. Comparisons to Low German or Scandinavian forms are suggestive but not proven; the OED treats it as of obscure, probably onomatopoeic origin.
