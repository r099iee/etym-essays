Note: While the base word anarchy had been borrowed into English by the 1530s from French and Medieval Latin as a pejorative term for lawless disorder, the philosophical term anarchism specifically reflects Pierre-Joseph Proudhon's 1840 French coinage anarchisme, which inverted the negative label into an affirmative theory of non-hierarchical social order.
