*bhel-
"to swell, blow up,"
"inflate, blow"

"breathe, inflate"

"sound a horn"
↓

blowen

↓
English
(modern standard)
blow / blown

(past blew, pp blown)
↓
English (dialectal / fixed phrase)
(18th–19th c.)
blowed
"damned, astonished"

Note: PIE *bhel- to swell is the standard source for blow-air via Germanic *blæ- (OED, Etymonline); the noun blow meaning hit is of separate obscure origin and not part of this chain. Blowed is a retained weak -ed participle frozen in oaths while standard usage levelled to blown.
