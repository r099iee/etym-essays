ROOT: jeer (English, first recorded c. 1550) "to deride, mock" — Origin obscure. No PIE ancestry is verifiable; the word cannot be assigned to any known language family, and every proposed connection below is a hypothesis rather than an established fact. The OED's editors treat the origin as unknown.

CHAIN (from first attestation — the one certain record):
1. early Modern English jeer "to deride, mock, make sport of" (verb, first recorded c. 1550) — origin unknown, see hypotheses below.
2. English jeer (noun) "a taunt, a mocking shout" (c. 1570s) — "jeers and catcalls," "jeers of derision."
3. Derived forms in English: jeering (adjective and noun) "mocking, derisive" (16th century); jeeringly (adverb) "in a jeering manner" (16th–17th centuries); jeerer (noun) "one who jeers; a scoffer, a derider" (16th century). ◄ OUR WORD.

HYPOTHESIS 1 — imitative / onomatopoeic origin (unproven; the explanation preferred by the OED's cautious editors): an echoic formation mimicking a harsh, loud cry of derision; cf. obsolete English ger / geer "a cry, a shout" and dialectal gare "to cry out, to gape" (obsolete).

HYPOTHESIS 2 — Dutch loan (unproven): Dutch gieren "to roar, howl, shriek" (of wind, of voices), modern Dutch gieren "to scream with laughter; to roar." Semantic fit: mockery conceived as a "roaring" at someone; if borrowed, the word could have entered early Modern English as jeer.

HYPOTHESIS 3 — German loan (unproven): German scheren "to vex, plague, trouble, harass," root sense "to cut, shear" (cognate with English shear). Semantic fit: mockery as a "shearing" that cuts at a victim's dignity or repute.

FALSE FRIEND (never merge this branch): English cheer "a shout of approval" is NOT related to jeer. Cheer < Old French chiere "face, countenance" < Late Latin cara "face" < Greek karā "head" — a chance resemblance of sound only; the two words have no common root.

NOTE (debated / unknown): because the origin is obscure, no chain can be extended beyond the first attestation; stating the unknown explicitly is the honest record.
