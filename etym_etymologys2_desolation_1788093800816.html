PIE (c. 4500–2500 BCE)
*se-
"self, apart, on one's own"

↓

Latin (3rd–2nd c. BCE)
sōlus
"alone, only, solitary"

↓

Latin (1st c. BCE)
dēsōlāre
"to leave alone, abandon, lay waste"

↓

Late Latin (4th c. CE, Vulgate)
dēsōlātiō
"desolation, abandonment, ruin"

↓

Old French (12th–13th c.)
desolacion
"desolation, ruin, grief"

↓

Middle English (c. 1300)
desolacioun
"devastation, barrenness, ruin"

↓

Modern English (16th c. – present)
DESOLATION
"ruined emptiness; utter loneliness or grief"

Note: The derivation of Latin sōlus beyond Latin is debated; it may continue PIE *se- "self, apart" (also in Latin sē "oneself"), but the formation is not certain.
