Debate: the origin of Latin fatuus is unknown/uncertain. A common guess links it to fārī "to speak" (one who talks nonsense), but there is no consensus; de Vaan leaves it unresolved. English later narrowed the sense from "folly" to "unreasoning romantic passion".
