• Ambiguities: Similar because both denote textual or verbal statements that lack a single, obvious interpretation; differs because ambiguities specifically involve multiple, competing, and often plausible meanings that create interpretive doubt between two or more pathways, whereas obscurities emphasize impenetrable darkness, vagueness, or a complete barrier to comprehension.
  • Enigmas: Similar because both describe puzzling, baffling phenomena that defy effortless understanding; differs because enigmas imply a deliberate, riddle-like puzzle intentionally crafted or possessing an elusive secret key waiting to be cracked, whereas obscurities often arise accidentally from poor drafting, physical decay, illegibility, or unfinished thought.
  • Vaguenesses: Similar because both characterize language or concepts that lack sharp definition; differs because vaguenesses stem from imprecise borders, blurry boundaries, or a lack of concrete specifics (being too general), whereas obscurities can be densely specific yet intellectually dark, cryptic, or impenetrable.
  • Arcana: Similar because both refer to matters hidden from general comprehension; differs because arcana refers exclusively to esoteric, mystical, or secret insider knowledge intentionally guarded from the uninitiated by an elite priesthood or guild, whereas obscurities are dark passages or facts open to all but shrouded in gloom.
  • Intricacies: Similar because both cause difficulty in mental navigation and analysis; differs because intricacies refer to complex, highly elaborate, and interwoven systems whose individual pieces are distinct and clear but whose collective interlocking web is difficult to untangle, whereas obscurities suffer from a lack of clarity in the fundamental pieces themselves.
  • Inconsistencies: Similar because both represent editorial defects found within unrevised manuscripts; differs because inconsistencies denote direct, logical, or chronological clashes between two distinct statements (e.g., a character dying on Tuesday and speaking on Thursday), whereas obscurities denote passages whose intrinsic meaning cannot be deciphered at all.
