Debate: the first element of escafaut is uncertain; the assumed Vulgar Latin *cavafal(l)um is a reconstruction from cavus "hollow" + fala and is not attested, and fala itself is of uncertain (possibly Etruscan) origin. An alternative source is Italian catafalco, whence English catafalque.
