Note: the ultimate origin of Vulgar Latin *barra is unknown; the common suggestion of a Gaulish (Celtic) substrate word meaning "point, tip" (cf. Welsh bar, "top, branch") is plausible but unproven, and no stage before *barra can be asserted. The pressure unit is not descended from the rod-word at all: it was deliberately coined from Greek báros "weight", itself from the secure PIE root *gʷer- "heavy" (cf. Sanskrit gurú-, Latin gravis). Two bars, two genealogies: the unknown Gaulish rod and the Greek weight.
