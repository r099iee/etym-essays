• Radiantly: Similar because both mean shining brightly, with light streaming out; differs because "radiantly" is the ordinary elevated adverb of brightness, while "effulgently" is rarer, more literary, and stresses light that bursts forth from within rather than mere outward glow.
  • Brilliantly: Similar because both describe intense brightness; differs because "brilliantly" stresses sharp, intense, often dazzling light (and, figuratively, intelligence), while "effulgently" stresses abundant, streaming, glowing radiance from an inner source and a much higher literary register.
  • Luminously: Similar because both mean giving off light; differs because "luminously" suggests a serene, self-contained, often diffused glow, while "effulgently" suggests an active, abundant outpouring of brilliance from within.
  • Resplendently: Similar because both describe magnificent, shining splendour; differs because "resplendently" emphasizes rich, gorgeous, often decorated splendour (dazzling finery), while "effulgently" emphasizes pure radiance of light, especially inner light, rather than ornament.
  • Dazzlingly: Similar because both describe light of great intensity; differs because "dazzlingly" means blindingly bright, light that overwhelms and impedes sight, while "effulgently" describes brilliance that delights and discloses rather than blinds, with a solemn, liturgical tone.
  • Brightly: Similar because both mean with light or radiance; differs because "brightly" is the plain, everyday adverb of brightness, with none of the Latinate grandeur, inner-source imagery, or poetic elevation of "effulgently."
  • Beamingly: Similar because both describe light or joy radiating outward; differs because "beamingly" suggests a steady, warm, directed beam (a beaming smile, beaming light), while "effulgently" suggests an abundant, streaming blaze from within and a higher register.
  • Glowingly: Similar because both describe warm, soft light or warmth of expression; differs because "glowingly" suggests a gentle, warm, enveloping glow, while "effulgently" suggests a brilliant, flashing, abundant radiance with ceremonial grandeur.
  • Incandescently: Similar because both describe intensely radiant light; differs because "incandescently" is the technical word for light emitted by a body at high temperature (glowing white heat), while "effulgently" is the poetic word for inner brilliance streaming forth, with no heat or technology implied.
  • Shiningly: Similar because both mean in a shining manner; differs because "shiningly" is a rare, simple adverbial formation with a homely tone, while "effulgently" is the learned, Latinate, sonorous adverb of the same idea.
  • Splendidly: Similar because both describe impressive brightness or magnificence; differs because "splendidly" describes general magnificence, showiness, or excellence in any domain, while "effulgently" is confined to radiance of light and its figurative equivalents.
