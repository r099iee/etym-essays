Note: The formation investītūra arose in Medieval Latin legal terminology during the 11th century to denote the symbolic 'clothing' of a vassal with land or a cleric with ecclesiastical office, deriving directly from investīre ('to clothe'), literally wrapping the recipient in the legal mantle and physical regalia of authority.
