CONSTITUENT 1: ATTER (Poison, Venom)
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*h₁edʰ- / *ait-
"swelling, burning, bitter poison, gall"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*aitrą
"poison, venom, pus, corruption"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
ātor / ǣttor
"poison, venom, gall, toxic matter"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200)
atter / attre
"venom, poison, bitter malice"

CONSTITUENT 2: COP (Head, Top, Spider)
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gewb- / *kewp-
"round object, head, vessel, bowl"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*kuppaz
"round container, crown of head, top, round creature"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c.)
copp / coppe
"summit, head; spider (round-headed creature)"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200)
cop / coppe
"head, spider"

COMPOUND SYNTHESIS:
Old English (c. 9th c.)
ātorcoppe
"poison-head, venomous spider"
(ātor "poison" + coppe "spider/head")
↓
Middle English (c. 1350)
attercop / attercoppe
"spider; spiteful, venomous person"
↓
Modern English / Dialect (archaic)
attercop
"spider; venomous, ill-natured person"
