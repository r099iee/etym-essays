Note: the "belt" origin of Baltic is usually explained either by Latin balteus "belt" or by Germanic *baltaz "belt", as in Danish Bælt, the straits; the two explanations converge on the same image, and the priority between them is disputed. The ancestry of balteus itself may be Etruscan. The Slavic chain's semantic link between "fame" and "word" is standard but debated.
