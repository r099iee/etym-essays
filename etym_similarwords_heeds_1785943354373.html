• Obeys: Similar because both involve following what is said; differs because obeys implies submitting to authority and carrying out orders, while heeds emphasizes careful attention to advice or warnings that may or may not be commands.
  • Listens: Similar because both involve taking in what is said; differs because listens is general auditory perception, while heeds requires taking the content seriously and acting on it.
  • Attends to: Similar because both mean directing the mind to something; differs because attends to can apply to tasks and objects (attend to the fire), while heeds is specifically about advice, warnings, and words.
  • Observes: Similar because both involve careful noticing; differs because observes is primarily visual or neutral watching (observe the rules, observe the stars), while heeds carries a responsive, cautious weight.
  • Minds: Similar because both mean attending to something; differs because minds is colloquial or implies being bothered (do you mind?), while heeds is formal and literary advice-following.
  • Notes: Similar because both mean taking notice; differs because notes can mean simply recording or observing, without the behavioural change that heeding implies.
  • Marks: Similar because both mean registering attention; differs because marks is archaic or poetic for noticing, while heeds implies acting on what is noticed.
  • Regards: Similar because both involve considering something; differs because regards means looking at, considering, or esteeming, while heeds means letting what is heard alter one's conduct.
  • Respects: Similar because both involve taking a rule seriously; differs because respects emphasizes esteem and honouring boundaries, while heeds emphasizes attending to and acting on warnings.
  • Follows: Similar because both mean doing as advised; differs because follows is broader (follow advice, follow orders, follow a trend) and lacks the focused, attentive sense of heed.
  • Takes to heart: Similar because both mean accepting advice seriously; differs because takes to heart adds emotional sincerity, while heeds is more about watchful, responsible attention.
  • Weighs: Similar because both involve considering a warning; differs because weighs implies evaluating or deliberating before deciding, while heeds presupposes taking it seriously and acting accordingly.
