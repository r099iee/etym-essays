• Gatherings: Similar because people come together; differs because gathering is informal and general, while assemblies suggests formality or institution.
  • Meetings: Similar because groups convene for purpose; differs because meetings stress business, while assemblies can be festive balls.
  • Balls: Similar in the Austen dance sense; differs because balls are specifically dances, while assemblies covers many gathering types.
  • Dances: Similar because assemblies were dances; differs because dance names movement, while assembly names the company and event.
  • Congregations: Similar because worshippers gather; differs because congregation is religious, while assemblies is neutral.
  • Conventions: Similar because delegates gather; differs because conventions are large organised conferences, while assemblies can be small and local.
  • Conferences: Similar because people meet to discuss; differs because conferences stress talk, while assemblies stress presence.
  • Legislatures: Similar in the political sense; differs because legislature makes laws, while assembly may only meet socially.
  • Rallies: Similar because crowds gather; differs because rallies are enthusiastic and often political, while assemblies are orderly.
  • Collections: Similar for fitted parts; differs because collections are merely grouped, while assemblies are fitted to work together.
