• Horatian: Similar because both represent foundational classical modes of satire; differs because Horatian satire is gentle, urbane, polished, civilized, and amused by human foibles (using smiling irony to reform social manners), whereas Menippean satire is wildly fragmented, carnivalesque, fantastic, and aimed aggressively at shattering intellectual pretension and philosophical systems.
  • Juvenalian: Similar because both can attack corruption and foolishness with biting sharpness; differs because Juvenalian satire is bitter, moralistic, indignant, and tragic (denouncing vice with fiery solemnity), whereas Menippean satire is surreal, comedic, multi-voiced, and philosophical rather than purely righteous.
  • Varronian: Similar because it is the exact historical Roman term for the same tradition (Marcus Terentius Varro composed 150 books of Saturae Menippeae); differs because Varronian refers specifically to Roman antiquarian fragments, whereas Menippean is the universal critical term in modern world literature.
  • carnivalesque: Similar because Mikhail Bakhtin explicitly identified Menippean satire as the primary literary embodiment of the carnivalesque (subverting hierarchy through parody and bodily laughter); differs because carnivalesque is a sweeping cultural-anthropological phenomenon, whereas Menippean is a specific literary genre and formal rhetorical tradition.
  • burlesque: Similar because both parody elevated, serious subjects through ridiculous imitation; differs because burlesque typically achieves its comic effect through gross disproportion of style and subject (low subject in high style or high subject in low style), whereas Menippean satire engages with serious philosophical ideas through multi-genre medley (prosimetrum).
  • picaresque: Similar because both involve episodic, wandering narratives encountering diverse social strata; differs because a picaresque novel follows the realistic adventures of a roguish lower-class hero surviving in a corrupt society, whereas Menippean satire features intellectual debates, underworld journeys, and fantastic philosophical dialogues.
