PIE (proposed; debated)
*dʰeu-
"to rise in a cloud of dust,
smoke, or vapour"
cf. Sanskrit dhūli "dust",
dhūma "smoke"; relation debated
↓
West Germanic (reconstructed)
*dustą
"dust, fine powder"
↓
Old English (8th–10th c.)
dūst
"dust, powder; earth, ground;
the mortal body"
↓
Middle English (13th c.)
dust
"fine dry particles; ashes"
↓
Modern English (16th c. onward)
dust
"fine dry particles;
powdered matter"
Note: a parallel West Germanic form
*dunstą "vapour, haze" gave Old English
dūnst, Old High German tunst,
German Dunst.
