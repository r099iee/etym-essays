• alighted: Similar because both represent the past tense of landing, descending, and settling down from flight, a horse, or a vehicle; differs because alighted is a longer, more formal, and slightly archaic compound verb, whereas lit is crisp, monosyllabic, and seamlessly bridges avian perching with illumination.
  • perched: Similar because both describe a bird landing and resting securely on a branch, twig, or elevated spot; differs because perched focuses on the sustained balancing posture and grip of the claws upon the perch, whereas lit emphasizes the momentary, delicate act of touching down.
  • settled: Similar because both convey coming to rest on a surface after movement or flight; differs because settled implies making oneself comfortable, ceasing all agitation, or establishing a long-term position, lacking the swift, featherweight descent of lit.
  • roosted: Similar because both describe birds resting upon branches; differs because roosted specifically signifies settling down to sleep for the night on a communal perch or nesting site.
  • touched down: Similar because both denote the exact physical moment of completing a descent to contact the ground or surface; differs because touched down is a modern aeronautical and technical compound, lacking the organic lyrical delicacy of lit.
  • illuminated: Similar because both describe shedding bright light upon an object or room; differs because illuminated is a formal, multi-syllabic Latinate verb emphasizing intellectual clarity, artistic illustration, or radiant brightness, whereas lit is a direct, Germanic physical verb.
  • ignited: Similar because both denote setting a combustible substance on fire; differs because ignited is a formal chemical and technical term focusing on the initiation of combustion, lacking the motion, avian, and figurative senses of lit.
  • kindled: Similar because both describe starting a small fire or arousing a warm emotion; differs because kindled emphasizes the gradual nurturing of flame from sparks and tinder, or the stirring of passion in the heart.
