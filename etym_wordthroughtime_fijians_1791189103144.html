- Pre-contact self-name: Viti (land/people) - local, vanua-bound; no pan-Fiji label, identity is chiefdom/island
- Tongan-European exonym (1770s-1850s): Feejee / Fiji, Fijians in missionary journals - outsider, exotic; connotation is warrior-cannibal, tattooed other (as in your passage)
- Colonial legal category (1874-1970): Fijians = natives under protection vs Indians - paternalist-segregated; perception is noble savage needing trusteeship
- Independence ethnic bloc (1970-2006): Fijian = Indigenous voting bloc vs Indo-Fijian; coups in its name - politicised, majoritarian; connotation is paramountcy and grievance
- Contemporary civic redefinition (2013-present): all citizens are Fijians, iTaukei for Indigenous - inclusive-brand yet contested; modern perception is rugby-famous, tourist-welcoming, climate-vulnerable democracy
