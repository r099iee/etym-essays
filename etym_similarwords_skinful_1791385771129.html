• Bellyful: Similar because both refer colloquially to consuming as much food, drink, or unpleasant experience as one can physically hold or endure; differs because bellyful focuses on digestive capacity or satiety and is equally applied to excessive food or emotional frustration ("I have had a bellyful of your excuses"), whereas skinful is predominantly reserved for alcoholic intoxication.
  • Earful: Similar because both utilize body-part metaphors ending in -ful to denote receiving an excessive, overwhelming quantity of something; differs because an earful refers strictly to verbal scolding, gossip, or an aggressive verbal reprimand, whereas skinful describes drinking or (rarely) physical abuse.
  • Snootful: Similar because both are informal idioms describing heavy intoxication; differs because snootful (from snoot/snout, nose) is an American slang coinage emphasizing drink or narcotics taken up the nose or intoxicating the head, whereas skinful is primarily British/Commonwealth and emphasizes total bodily capacity.
  • Load: Similar because both denote an excessive, intoxicating amount of alcohol carried by an individual; differs because load functions as a broad, generic weight metaphor ("he has a load on"), whereas skinful evokes the vivid, specific anatomical image of an animal wineskin stretched to bursting.
  • Bender: Similar because both pertain to excessive drinking sessions; differs because a bender denotes an extended spree or multi-day drinking binge, whereas a skinful denotes the volumetric intake and physical state of intoxication achieved during a single drinking occasion.
  • Intoxication: Similar because both describe the physiological state resulting from heavy alcohol consumption; differs because intoxication is a clinical, formal, and legal term describing biochemical impairment, whereas skinful is an earthbound, informal, and humorous colloquialism.
  • Tankardful: Similar because both refer to drinking vessels filled to capacity; differs because a tankardful is a literal container measure of beer in a lidded pewter or wooden drinking mug, whereas a skinful transfers the container metaphor directly to the human drinker's own body.
