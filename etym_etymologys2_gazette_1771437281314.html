↓
Venetian (16th c.)
gazeta
"a small coin; the price of a news-sheet"
↓
Italian (mid-16th c.)
gazzetta
"a gazette, small newspaper"
↓
English (17th c.)
gazette
"a journal or newspaper"
Note: The origin of the Venetian coin gazeta is uncertain — possibly from gazza "magpie", the chattering bird, or another source. The news-sheet was named for its price of one gazeta; English gazette arrived via French gazette.
