• Strict: Similar because both mean demanding exact obedience to rules; differs because strict is neutral and rule-focused, while stern adds grim, forbidding severity of manner.
  • Severe: Similar because both mean harsh in degree; differs because severe applies to conditions, pain, or punishment, while stern usually describes personal demeanour or moral strictness.
  • Harsh: Similar because both mean unpleasant and unkind; differs because harsh emphasizes cruelty or grating quality, while stern implies principled firmness.
  • Grim: Similar because both mean unyielding seriousness; differs because grim carries a dark, hopeless, almost menacing tone, whereas stern implies resolute firmness.
  • Austere: Similar because both mean strict severity; differs because austere emphasizes harsh simplicity and lack of luxury or adornment, while stern emphasizes a hard, forbidding expression or discipline.
  • Firm: Similar because both mean unyielding; differs because firm is positive (steadfast, dependable), while stern is cold and forbidding.
  • Rigid: Similar because both mean unbending; differs because rigid can mean physically stiff or inflexible in rules, while stern is specifically about severity of manner.
  • Inflexible: Similar because both mean not yielding; differs because inflexible is neutral on emotion, while stern implies a forbidding emotional posture.
  • Unyielding: Similar because both mean refusing to give way; differs because unyielding can be admirable resolve, while stern has a cold, disciplinary temperature.
  • Uncompromising: Similar because both mean refusing to modify standards; differs because uncompromising is about principle, while stern is about manner and discipline.
  • Forbidding: Similar because both mean discouraging approach; differs because forbidding describes an appearance that repels, while stern is a judgement of character or rule.
  • Dour: Similar because both mean grim and unsmiling; differs because dour is sullen, gloomy, and stubbornly morose, while stern is severe and firm.
  • Hard: Similar because both mean lacking softness; differs because hard is very general (also "difficult," "solid"), while stern is specifically severe authority.
  • Exacting: Similar because both mean demanding high standards; differs because exacting focuses on precise demands for performance, while stern focuses on severity of discipline.
  • Rigorous: Similar because both mean strict and demanding; differs because rigorous is about thorough standards (especially academic or scientific), while stern is about harsh manner or law.
  • Steely: Similar because both mean coldly hard; differs because steely suggests controlled, metallic hardness of nerve or resolve, while stern suggests moral severity.
  • Grave: Similar because both mean serious; differs because grave implies solemnity and weight (of danger or importance), while stern implies severity and reproof.
  • Solemn: Similar because both mean serious; differs because solemn is dignified and ceremonial, while stern is harsh and forbidding.
  • Sombre: Similar because both mean serious and gloomy; differs because sombre is about sadness and darkness of tone, while stern is about strictness and severity.
