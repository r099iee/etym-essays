• Rings: Similar because both are closed circular bands; differs because a ring is any circular band including small jewellery, while a hoop implies a larger, stiffer, functionally binding band.
  • Bands: Similar because both encircle and bind; differs because a band need not be circular and may be flat, flexible, or decorative — a hoop is rigid and closed.
  • Circles: Similar because both name round shapes; differs because a circle is the abstract geometric figure, while a hoop is a physical object.
  • Rims: Similar because both concern a round edge; differs because a rim is the lip or outer edge of a single object, whereas a hoop is an independent band wrapped around it or standing as a separate ring.
  • Collars: Similar because both are encircling fittings; differs because a collar fits snugly around a neck or pipe and often supports or seals, while a hoop binds with tension and is usually larger and stiffer.
  • Ferrules: Similar because both are metal rings or caps on a rod or tool; differs because a ferrule is a short end-cap protecting a single point, not a full encircling band.
  • Grommets / eyelets: Similar because both are small circular fittings; differs because grommets and eyelets reinforce holes and are tiny, whereas a hoop is large, free-standing, and reinforces from the outside.
  • Torus: Similar because both name ring-shaped objects; differs because torus is a mathematical/technical term for the doughnut shape, while hoop is the everyday word.
