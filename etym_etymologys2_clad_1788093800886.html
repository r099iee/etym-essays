Note: the ultimate origin of Proto-Germanic *klaiþą is unknown; no secure PIE root has been established for the base behind "cloth", so the chain begins at Proto-Germanic. "Clad" arises as a contracted participle within English and has co-existed with "clothed" since the 14th century.
