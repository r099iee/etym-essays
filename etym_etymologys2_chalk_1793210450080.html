Ancient Greek (5th c. BCE)
χάλιξ (khalix)
'small stone, pebble'
↓
Latin (borrowed) (1st c. BCE)
calx
'lime, limestone'
↓
Old English (9th c.)
cealc
'chalk; lime, mortar'
↓
Middle English (13th c.)
chalk
'white limestone'
↓
Modern English (current)
chalk
'white limestone; marking'

Note: the standard account derives Latin calx from Greek khalix, but the ultimate origin of the Greek word itself is unknown — it has no agreed Indo-European root — so the chain honestly begins at the attested Greek stage.
