• tetrapods: Similar because both mean four-limbed vertebrates; differs because tetrapod is broader evolutionary term including bipeds, birds, and humans.
  • mammals: Similar because most quadrupeds are mammals; differs because mammals includes bipeds (humans) and swimmers (whales), not defined by leg count.
  • beasts: Similar because both denote four-legged animals; differs because beast is literary and often implies wildness or brutishness.
  • animals: Similar because quadrupeds are animals; differs because animal is the hypernym covering all, from sponges to humans.
  • creatures: Similar because both refer to living beings; differs because creature is vague and emotive, quadruped is precise about feet.
  • critters: Similar because both mean small beasts; differs because critter is informal/American and affectionate, quadruped is technical.
  • four-footed animals: Similar because exact paraphrase; differs because phrase is plain English, quadrupeds is learned Latinate.
  • quadruped robots: Similar because same four-legged body plan; differs because robots are machines, not living quadrupeds.
  • bipeds: Similar because both classify by foot number; differs because bipeds walk on two feet (humans, birds), the opposite group.
  • centipedes: Similar because both are -pede leg-count words; differs because centipedes have ~100 legs and are arthropods, not vertebrates.
  • livestock: Similar because cattle/sheep are quadrupeds; differs because livestock stresses farm ownership and use, not anatomy.
  • fauna: Similar because both refer to animal life; differs because fauna means all animals of a region/era, not just four-legged ones.
