PIE (c. 4500–3500 BCE)
*babl-
"to stammer, babble"
(echoic, reduplicated)
↓
Latin (2nd c. BCE)
balbus
"stammering, stuttering"
↓
Spanish (12th–13th c.)
bobo
"fool, simpleton"
(from balbus; "stammerer" → "fool")
↓
English (c. 1600)
booby
"foolish, stupid, clumsy person"
(borrowed from Spanish bobo)

Note: the booby bird (17th c.) is named for being easily caught; PIE *babl- is echoic.
