• friendliness: Similar because both mean a warm, pleasant disposition toward others; differs because friendliness is general and informal, while affability specifically means easy approachability and ease of conversation, often in public or hierarchical settings.
  • amiability: Similar because both mean a pleasant, agreeable good nature; differs because amiability emphasizes a sweet, agreeable, conflict-avoiding temperament, while affability emphasizes being easy to talk to and approach.
  • geniality: Similar because both mean warm, cheerful friendliness; differs because geniality emphasizes festive, sunny cheer and good humour, while affability emphasizes courteous approachability rather than high spirits.
  • cordiality: Similar because both mean warm sincerity of manner; differs because cordiality is warmth shown in greeting and welcome, while affability is the ongoing ease of being spoken to.
  • approachability: Similar because both mean being easy to reach or talk to; differs because approachability is purely about access (one may be approachable yet terse), while affability adds courteous warmth and readiness to converse.
  • sociability: Similar because both relate to liking and managing company; differs because sociability means the love of and skill in being with people generally, while affability means the quality of being easy to approach and address.
  • warmth: Similar because both mean a kind, friendly quality of manner; differs because warmth is emotional and felt, while affability is behavioural and performative — a cultivated graciousness.
  • graciousness: Similar because both mean kind, courteous manner; differs because graciousness stresses elegant kindness, often from a superior (grace under obligation), while affability stresses the ease of conversation itself.
  • pleasantness: Similar because both mean an agreeable manner; differs because pleasantness is vague and general, while affability is specific to ease of being spoken to.
  • urbanity: Similar because both mean polished, civil social manner; differs because urbanity emphasizes refined, witty, cosmopolitan polish, while affability emphasizes warm approachability without wit or sophistication.
