Note: formed as be- "about" (OE be-/bi- from PGmc *bi from PIE *h1epi "on, upon") + Germanic *cwiss "saying" (kin to quoth), with unetymological -t as in behest. Root *gwet- is debated per Boutkan: proposed cognates in Sanskrit, Welsh, Latin face formal objections, so of no certain IE etymology beyond Germanic.
