Note: *Schaefer-Simmern* is a compound toponymic-occupational surname formed by combining *Schäfer* (occupational: shepherd) with the town name *Simmern* (Hunsrück), adopted by Henry Schaefer-Simmern to acknowledge his geographical roots and distinguish his name in artistic and academic circles.
