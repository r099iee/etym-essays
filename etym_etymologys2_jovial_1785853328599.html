↓ English (c. 1600)
JOVIAL
"cheerful, friendly, full of good humor"
Debate: the shift from "of Jupiter" to "cheerful" runs through
medieval astrology: those born under the planet Jupiter were held
to be genial and good-humored. The astrological sense is attested
in English from the 1580s; the "merry" sense follows by c. 1600.
