- 14th c. - Formed in Middle English (*netten*) from Old English *nett*, meaning to trap birds, fish, or wild game in a mesh.
- 16th c. - Merged with the commercial sense of French *net* ('clean/pure'), giving rise to accounting usage (*to net a clear profit*).
- 18th–19th c. - Expanded to agricultural protection (covering fruit bushes with nets) and botanical enclosures.
- Late 19th c. - Adopted into organized sports journalism (soccer, hockey) for scoring a goal into the net.
- 20th–21st c. - Expanded into digital networks, military data grids, and modern cyber telecommunications.
