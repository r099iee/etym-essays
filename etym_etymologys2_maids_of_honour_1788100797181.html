honour: Latin honōs has no certain PIE etymology (de Vaan: “no convincing etymology”); the -our spelling entered via Old French and was fixed in Britain, while American English writes honor (Webster, 1828). The compound maids-of-honour (maid + of + honour) is attested in English court usage from the 16th century.
