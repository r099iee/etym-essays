Note: The semantic evolution of 'jest' is one of the most celebrated cases of pejoration and specialization in English lexicography. It began as Latin 'gesta' (great, heroic martial deeds performed in battle, as in the 'chansons de geste'). As itinerant storytellers shifted from reciting grave chivalric epics to providing light, comic dinner amusement, the meaning degraded from 'a noble martial exploit' to 'a funny story', then to 'a practical joke or quip', and finally into the modern verbal action of joking or mocking flippantly.
