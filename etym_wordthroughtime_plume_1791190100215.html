- Ornamental feather (14th-15th c.) - knightly plume and heraldic feather; prestige, rank, martial display
- Quill-pen metonymy (15th-17th c.) - plume as pen and authorship; professional pride of men of letters
- Avian verb (16th c.) - birds plume or preen feathers; naturalist and falconry observation, neutral
- Reflexive vanity (17th-18th c.) - to plume oneself on wit, birth, reason; satirical Enlightenment view of human puffery
- Modern ironic idiom - we plume ourselves on tools, language, progress; affectionate-mocking deflation, plus smoke and mantle plume extensions
