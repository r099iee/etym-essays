• Whim: Similar because both name a sudden, unaccountable inclination; differs because a whim is everyday and mild (a passing whim), while caprice carries dramatic, artistic, or volatile weight and often implies a change of mood rather than a single fancy.
  • Vagary: Similar because both denote unpredictable shifts; differs because vagary emphasizes wandering, erratic changes of fortune or behaviour over time, while caprice stresses the suddenness and motivelessness of the turn itself.
  • Impulse: Similar because both are sudden and unreasoned; differs because an impulse is a single inner push to act, whereas caprice is the unpredictable shift of mind or mood itself.
  • Fancy: Similar because both are light, unreasoned inclinations; differs because fancy highlights imaginative liking or preference, while caprice highlights sudden, unexplained change.
  • Whimsy: Similar because both carry playful unpredictability; differs because whimsy is charmingly eccentric or fanciful humour, while caprice can be dangerous and is often morally or legally serious.
  • Quirk: Similar because both describe odd, unexpected behaviour; differs because a quirk is a permanent peculiar habit or trait, whereas a caprice is a sudden, fleeting turn of mind.
  • Notion: Similar because both are sudden mental impulses; differs because a notion is a passing idea or belief, with no necessary action or mood-change, while a caprice issues in unpredictable conduct.
  • Freak: Similar because both denote an unaccountable departure from the norm; differs because a freak (of nature, of fashion) is an exceptional event or oddity, whereas caprice is a wilful or dispositional unpredictability.
  • Volatility: Similar because both describe rapid, hard-to-predict change; differs because volatility is a statistical or market term for measured fluctuation, while caprice is the personal or artistic quality behind such swings.
  • Inconstancy: Similar because both oppose firmness of purpose; differs because inconstancy is a chronic failure to remain stable, while a caprice is a single sudden turn and may be an isolated event.
