• Executioner: Similar because it refers to a person who carries out a death sentence; differs because "executioner" is a broader term encompassing various methods of execution (beheading, firing squad, lethal injection), whereas "hangman" specifically denotes execution by hanging.
  • Headsman: Similar because it is a specific type of executioner; differs because a headsman carries out execution by beheading, typically with an axe or sword, rather than by hanging.
  • Tormentor: Similar because both roles involve inflicting suffering or death; differs because a tormentor's primary role is to inflict pain, often for interrogation or punishment, while a hangman's specific role is to carry out a death sentence by hanging.
  • Public Executioner: Similar because it emphasizes the public nature of the role, common for hangmen historically; differs because it's a descriptive phrase rather than a single noun, and can apply to executioners using other methods if their work was public.
  • Gallowsman: Similar because it directly refers to the gallows, the structure used for hanging; differs because it is a less common and more archaic synonym for hangman, often used interchangeably.
