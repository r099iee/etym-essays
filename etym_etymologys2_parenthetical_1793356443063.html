Note on morphological compounding: The Greek noun parénthesis represents a triple morphological compound: the prefix para- (παρά, "beside, alongside", from PIE *per-) + the prefix en- (ἐν, "in, into", from PIE *h₁én) + the verbal noun thésis (θέσις, "a placing, setting", from the prolific PIE root *dʰeh₁-, "to put, place"). The English adjective parenthetical adds the double Latinate adjectival suffix -ic-al (Latin -icus + -alis).
