Note — a separate noun root (often confused):
Middle English (13th c.)
thrum
"loose ends of warp threads left on the loom; tufts of waste thread"
origin (uncertain; probably Germanic or Scandinavian)
↓
English (16th c.)
to thrum
"to cover or trim with thrums; to make a fringe of loose threads"

Debate: the strumming verb is most likely imitative; the thread noun is a separate, older Germanic word. Their identical form causes centuries of confusion.
