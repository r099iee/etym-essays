Note: Latin verbum derives from PIE *werh₁- ('to speak'), cognate with Proto-Germanic *wurdą (whence Old English word and Modern English word), Ancient Greek ῥῆμα (rhêma, 'word, verb') and ῥήτωρ (rhḗtōr, 'public speaker, rhetor'), and Lithuanian vardas ('name').
