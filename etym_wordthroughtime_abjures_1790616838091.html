- Roman legal sense - abjurare to deny on oath in court, to disavow debt or charge, procedural neutral
- Medieval religious-legal sense - to abjure heresy or the realm under oath, solemn public ritual with exile or penance, fearful connotation
- Early Modern political sense - to abjure allegiance, succession, or popery in loyalty oaths, partisan and coercive connotation
- 19th-c. literary-ethical sense - to abjure pleasure, ambition, or creed as personal vow, romantic-individual connotation of principled self-denial
- Contemporary general-formal sense - to solemnly reject ideology or habit, also personified systems abjuring capacities by nature, ironic-theological connotation as here
