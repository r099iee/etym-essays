Note on the root: PIE *sleydʰ- is the source of a Germanic family — Old English slīdan 'slide,' slīma 'slime,' Middle High German slīten, modern German schlittern 'to slide' — and the derivation is regular. The frequentative step (sliden to slideren) is the same suffix that produced 'flutter' from 'float' and is well documented in Middle English.
