• Eulogy: Similar because both are commemorative tributes honoring a deceased person's life; differs because a eulogy is a spoken speech or oration delivered aloud at a funeral or memorial service, whereas an obituary is a published written article.
  • Elegy: Similar because both honor someone who has died; differs because an elegy is a mournful, reflective poem or musical composition lamenting a death rather than a biographical news profile.
  • Necrology: Similar because both are lists or records of deceased persons; differs because a necrology is typically a formal ecclesiastical, monastic, or institutional list of death dates without the narrative biographical detail of a modern journalistic obituary.
  • Death notice: Similar because both inform the public of a recent death; differs because a death notice is a short, paid classified advertisement placed by the family with funeral logistics, whereas an obituary is an editorial biographical article written by journalists.
  • Memorial / Tribute: Similar because both honor the memory of a departed figure; differs because memorials can take the form of physical monuments, funds, or anniversary celebrations, whereas an obituary is specifically a textual biographical account published upon death.
