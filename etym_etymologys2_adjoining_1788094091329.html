NOTE: The spelling changed from Middle English ajoinen to modern adjoin in the 16th century, re-Latinised to display the prefix ad- of Latin adiungere; the -d was not pronounced before this re-spelling. PIE *yewg- is one of the best-attested roots of Indo-European (Sanskrit yuj- "to yoke", Greek zeugnýnai, Latin iugum "yoke", English yoke itself). The semantic sequence runs from the yoking of oxen, through Latin "to attach", to the 16th-century English state-sense "to lie next to".
