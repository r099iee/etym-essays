Ancient Greek (c. 8th century BCE)
αἰγίς (aigís)
"goatskin," "shield of Zeus/Athena"
[originally a goatskin garment, later a mythical shield]
↓
Latin (c. 1st century BCE - 1st century CE)
aegis
"aegis (of Zeus/Minerva)," "shield"
[direct borrowing from Greek, retaining mythological sense]
↓
English (c. 17th century CE)
aegis
"mythical shield," "protection, sponsorship"
[re-borrowed from Latin, later generalized to abstract protection]
