- Old English breost chest, bosom - front of torso, seat of heart and breath, milk and courage - intimate-vital, poetic and physical
- 14th-16th c. to breast to face with chest - to meet enemy, wave, storm breast-to-breast - martial-heroic, chivalric
- 17th-18th c. to breast a hill, to breast the waves - to climb by chesting, to push through water - pedestrian-nautical, effortful
- 19th c. to breast the tape, to breast the line - athletics and racing victory by torso - sporting-technical, photo-finish
- 19th-20th c. single-breasted, double-breasted, deep-breasted - tailoring and ornithology adjectives of front - sartorial-naturalist, everyday
- Modern breasted ship into water - sci-fi launching shove, chest-shoulder heave - muscular-epic, body as machine
