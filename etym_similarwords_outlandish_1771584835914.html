• Bizarre: Similar because both mean strikingly strange; differs because bizarre emphasises wild incongruity and the grotesque, while outlandish retains the sense of foreignness and alien origin.
  • Strange: Similar because both denote unfamiliarity; differs because strange is the neutral, broad word for any unfamiliar thing, while outlandish is stronger, pejorative, and implies "like something from another land or world".
  • Eccentric: Similar because both describe odd behaviour or persons; differs because eccentric suggests harmless, distinctive quirkiness ("off-centre"), while outlandish is more shocking, alien, and judgemental.
  • Weird: Similar because both connote strangeness; differs because weird suggests the uncanny, the supernatural, or the fated, while outlandish is more about foreign alienness and absurdity.
  • Queer: Similar because both mean odd or strange; differs because queer is a dated general word for odd and now carries strong sexual-orientation senses, while outlandish straightforwardly means "foreign-bizarre".
  • Foreign: Similar because both imply another country; differs because foreign is neutral geography (any other nation), while outlandish is pejorative and means so foreign as to seem uncouth.
  • Exotic: Similar because both mean foreign; differs because exotic carries glamour and allure — foreign but attractive — while outlandish is mocking and grotesque.
  • Alien: Similar because both mean other and unplaceable; differs because alien suggests a different species or world (inhuman), while outlandish describes a human who comes from elsewhere.
  • Odd: Similar because both mean unusual; differs because odd is mild and statistical (deviation from the norm), while outlandish is extreme and pejorative.
  • Grotesque: Similar because both connote distorted strangeness; differs because grotesque emphasises distortion and deformity (comic-horrific), while outlandish emphasises foreignness and unconventionality.
  • Unconventional: Similar because both mean outside the norm; differs because unconventional is neutral or even admiring (creative nonconformity), while outlandish is judgemental and mocking.
