Modern English
PYGMIES

Note: The Greek πυγμή is clearly the immediate base of the ethnic name — the pygmy is “one pygmē (fist) tall”. Its deeper ancestry is debated: scholars compare Latin pugnus “fist” and pugil “boxer” and reconstruct *peuǵ-, but Beekes marks πυγμή’s pre-history as uncertain and possibly Pre-Greek; the Indo-European link is plausible, not proven.
