Note: apo- from PIE *apo- off, away (cognate Latin ab, Sanskrit apa, English off) is secure in Pokorny. Theos is debated: Etymonline gives PIE *dhes- forming religious words, but Beekes treats theos as of unknown origin, possibly Pre-Greek substrate, explicitly not related to Latin deus from *dyew- shine. The derivation apotheoun from apo- plus theos plus factitive -oun is undisputed; only the deeper root of theos is uncertain.
