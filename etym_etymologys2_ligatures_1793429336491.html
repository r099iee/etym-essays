Note: The PIE root *leyǵ- is the ancestor of an immense family of Latin-derived words governing social and legal bonds, including religion (that which binds back), obligation (binding to a duty), league (alliance bound by treaty), alloy (metals bound together), and colleague (one bound with another in office).
