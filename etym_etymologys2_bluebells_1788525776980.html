Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE)
*bʰel- / *bʰlēw-
"to shine, flash, burn; pale, light-colored"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*blēwaz
"blue, livid, dark-colored"
↓
Old French (c. 10th c., from Frankish *blāo)
bleu / bloi
"blue, sky-colored"
↓
Middle English (c. 13th c.)
blew / bleu / bla
"blue color"

↓
