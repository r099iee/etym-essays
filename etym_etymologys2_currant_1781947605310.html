Debated: This is an eponym: currant arose from the phrase "raisins of Corinth", the dried grapes shipped from Corinth (Greek Κόρινθος). The city name is of pre-Greek substrate origin, and its deeper etymology is unknown. The small Ribes berry took the same name by analogy (16th–17th c.).
