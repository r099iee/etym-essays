• willed: Similar because it means left by will; differs because willed is plain and covers all property, while bequeathed is formal and traditionally personal property.
  • left (to): Similar because he left debts to his son; differs because left is everyday and ambiguous, while bequeathed is explicitly testamentary.
  • devised: Similar because it means left by will; differs because devise in strict law is land and houses, while bequeath is money and goods.
  • handed down: Similar because traditions are handed down like legacies; differs because handed down is informal and often lifetime, while bequeathed is post-mortem and documentary.
  • passed on: Similar because property passes on death; differs because passed on is euphemistic and broad, while bequeathed is legal and precise.
  • bestowed: Similar because both give solemnly; differs because bestow is lifetime honour, while bequeath acts only after death.
  • granted: Similar because wills grant sums; differs because grant is general permission or gift, while bequeath is will-specific.
  • settled upon: Similar because settlements convey property to heirs; differs because settle implies trust arrangement, while bequeath implies direct will gift.
  • transmitted: Similar because burdens and genes transmit; differs because transmit is technical or biological, while bequeath is personal and volitional.
  • inherited (from heir's view): Similar because it names the same transfer; differs because inherit is what the heir does, while bequeathed is what the testator did.
