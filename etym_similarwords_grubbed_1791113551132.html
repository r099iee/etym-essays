• Dug: Similar because it means turned earth to extract something; differs because dug is neutral and general, while grubbed implies shallow, laborious, grubby foraging or clearing.
  • Unearthed: Similar because it means dug up and exposed; differs because unearthed is more formal and often figurative for discovery, while grubbed stresses dirty manual effort.
  • Uprooted: Similar because it means tore out roots; differs because uprooted focuses on removal of the plant, while grubbed focuses on the digging process.
  • Rummaged: Similar because it means searched by turning things over; differs because rummaged is lighter and domestic (drawers, bags), while grubbed is earthier and need-driven.
  • Foraged: Similar because it means searched for food in the wild; differs because foraged can sound skilful and naturalist, while grubbed sounds crude and desperate.
  • Scavenged: Similar because it means collected discarded edibles; differs because scavenged implies taking leftovers or carrion, while grubbed implies digging/rummaging effort itself.
  • Scrabbled: Similar because it means scratched and clawed for footing or things; differs because scrabbled stresses frantic clawing motion, while grubbed stresses sustained digging.
  • Rooted (rooted about): Similar because pigs root/grub in soil with snouts; differs because rooted is specifically animal-like nosing, while grubbed is broader human digging.
  • Delved: Similar because it means dug deeply, researched; differs because delved is literary and often intellectual, while grubbed is humble and physical.
  • Excavated: Similar because it means dug out systematically; differs because excavated implies archaeology or construction with method, while grubbed implies informal grubby effort.
  • Scraped: Similar because it means gathered laboriously (scraped a living); differs because scraped stresses thin, barely-sufficient gathering, while grubbed stresses digging motion.
  • Drudged: Similar because money-grubbing overlaps with drudgery; differs because drudged means toiled dully without necessarily digging, while grubbed retains the soil image.
