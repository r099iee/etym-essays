- Vulgar Latin tripalium - Torture device of three stakes, brutal penal connotation, literal instrument of torment.
- Old French travail - Work, toil, suffering, also childbirth pangs, sympathetic perception of labour as affliction, central medieval economic term.
- Middle English travail / travel - Both toil and journey, pilgrim and peasant word, dignified by Chaucer and Bible translation.
- Early Modern split travel vs travail - Travel keeps movement, travail keeps pain, latter becomes elevated and biblical, former everyday.
- Today - Formal literary word for noble hard work and suffering, often used ironically to dignify small troubles, as in Wodehouse's hour of travail.
