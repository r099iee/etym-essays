Note on deeper origin: the pre-Latin ancestry of vēlum is uncertain. De Vaan and other Latin etymologists offer no fully secure PIE etymon; a connection with a weaving root has been suggested but is not firmly established, so no PIE stage is asserted here rather than inventing one.
