• Abundantly: Similar because both mean in great quantity and plenty; differs because 'abundantly' is the direct, general adverb for plenty of anything, while 'copiously' stresses the overflowing, generous volume of an output or flow and is slightly more formal.
  • Profusely: Similar because both mean in great, even excessive quantity; differs because 'profusely' emphasizes lavish, pouring, sometimes wasteful abundance (sweating profusely, apologizing profusely), while 'copiously' emphasizes plentiful supply without the excess overtone.
  • Plentifully: Similar because both mean in large supply; differs because 'plentifully' is the plain word for there being plenty of something, while 'copiously' describes the action of producing or supplying it in volume.
  • Generously: Similar because both can describe giving or providing in large amounts; differs because 'generously' implies a giver's liberality and good will, while 'copiously' is neutral about motive and concerned only with volume.
  • Lavishly: Similar because both mean in large, rich quantities; differs because 'lavishly' implies luxurious, even extravagant abundance (lavishly decorated), while 'copiously' is about plain volume and profusion.
  • Richly: Similar because both can describe abundance and fullness; differs because 'richly' implies wealth, depth, and colour (richly illustrated), while 'copiously' emphasizes sheer amount.
  • Amply: Similar because both mean sufficiently and more than enough; differs because 'amply' stresses that there is enough and to spare (amply supplied), while 'copiously' stresses the great volume itself.
  • Prodigiously: Similar because both mean in remarkably great amount; differs because 'prodigiously' implies something impressively or astonishingly large, while 'copiously' is a calm word for plentiful volume.
  • Liberally: Similar because both can mean in generous quantity; differs because 'liberally' implies open-handed generosity and free giving, while 'copiously' is descriptive of amount, not of liberality.
  • In abundance: Similar because both mean in plentiful supply; differs because 'in abundance' is a fixed phrase, more static and descriptive of a state, while 'copiously' is an adverb of the action that produces or delivers the plenty.
