Note: legio is literally “a choosing out,” hence a levy of troops. The derivation from legere is agreed, but the ultimate PIE root is debated: *leǵ- “to gather” is usually preferred, while some scholars connect the same root with *leǵ- “to speak” (whence lex, logos), since “to gather words” can mean “to read.” The two reconstructions are homophonous and may be historically distinct.
