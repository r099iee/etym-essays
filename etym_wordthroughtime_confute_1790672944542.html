- To check, restrain, repress, bring to naught - concrete Latin sense; confutare pride or counsel, neutral and forceful
- To disprove formally, to refute by argument - transferred to disputation; scholastic and Reformation polemic, prestigious
- To silence or overcome an opponent in debate - personal object; he was confuted, 17th–18th centuries, formal
- Difficult or impossible to confute in the abstract - critical test of meaning; vagueness evades refutation, modern academic use
- Rare learned synonym of refute - contemporary status; confute survives where refute would do, marking classical finality
