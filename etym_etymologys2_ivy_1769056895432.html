Note: Proto-Germanic *ibah- is of uncertain origin. It has been speculatively linked to PIE *h₁ey- "reddish" (perhaps for the plant's berries), but the connection is debated, and a non-Indo-European substrate origin is also possible. No secure earlier stage can be reconstructed.
