Note: The origin of cut is unknown. It appears suddenly in Middle English c. 1250 with no clear Old English ancestor, possibly from a Scandinavian or continental Germanic dialect form; the etymology is genuinely uncertain. The sword-stroke sense in Tolkien's passage is the one inherited from the physical "cut from beneath."
