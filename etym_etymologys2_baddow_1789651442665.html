Note: etymology is explicitly difficult and disputed. Ekwall (English River-Names) calls derivation from OE beadu "battle" formally possible but without parallel and hesitates to accept it; the English Place-Name Society favours a Celtic predecessor Beadwan of uncertain meaning, possibly "birch stream" or the goddess name OIr Bodb / MIr Badb "war-goddess" reshaped under OE beadu influence. Final -an/-en is part of the name, not an inflection. No PIE root is asserted.
