Note: north descends from a Germanic root whose underlying sense is “left” or “below” — the direction on one’s left when facing the rising sun. The -d- of Normandie comes from the oblique stem Normand- of Normant (plural Normanz), not from the Norse form, and the ending -ie is the Latin-French territorial suffix seen in Picardie and Bourgogne. The personal names of Rollo’s band are not recorded; the ethnic term is the earliest secure stage.
