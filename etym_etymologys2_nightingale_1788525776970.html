Note: A native Germanic compound of *niht ("night") and *-gale ("singer", from the strong verb *galan "to sing, incant, chant"). The Middle English insertion of intrusive medial -n- before the velar stop -g- is a regular phonological process of excrescence (nasalization), parallel to *passenger* (from *passager*) and *messenger* (from *messager*).
