throb

↓
English (noun, from the verb)
THROB
"strong, regular pulsing or beating"
Debate: no secure derivation is known. The word is
probably echoic/imitative; a possible but uncertain
link to throe (Old English þrāwung "throes, pang")
has been suggested.
