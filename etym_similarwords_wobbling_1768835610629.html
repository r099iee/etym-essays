• swaying: Similar in moving back and forth; differs because swaying is graceful, rhythmic, and wind-driven, while a wobble is the slack, purposeless rocking of a part loose within its socket.
  • tottering: Similar in unstable movement; differs because tottering describes a standing thing on the point of falling, while wobbling is a side-to-side oscillation that need not threaten collapse.
  • teetering: Similar in precarious unsteadiness; differs because teetering is a balancing on a brink, a tilt about to tip, while wobbling is a loose shaking within a fit - jelly on a plate, a head on a neck.
  • oscillating: Similar in moving to and fro; differs because oscillating is regular, technical, and pendular, while a wobble is irregular, loose, and faintly comic - a rhythm that has gone slack.
  • wavering: Similar in uncertain, unsteady movement; differs because wavering is faint and hesitating - a flame, a hand, a resolve - while wobbling is broader and looser, a rocking from side to side.
  • rocking: Similar in a to-and-fro motion; differs because rocking is a regular, often deliberate and soothing motion, while wobbling is irregular and slack - the loose fit of an imperfectly mounted part.
  • lurching: Similar in unstable movement; differs because lurching is a sudden, violent deviation from a course, while wobbling is a repeated, small, purposeless oscillation.
  • rolling: Similar in unsteady motion; differs because rolling implies rotation about an axis or a ship's heavy wallowing, while wobbling is a small, slack, side-to-side shaking within a fixed base.
  • vibrating: Similar in rapid to-and-fro motion; differs because vibrating is fast, small, and regular - a frequency - while wobbling is slow, loose, and irregular, the rocking of something not truly mounted.
  • vacillating: Similar in going to and fro; differs because vacillating is the mental or verbal wavering between choices, while wobbling is the physical rocking of a loosely fitted thing - though "wobbling opinions" borrows the same physics.
