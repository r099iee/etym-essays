Note: Latin aequus is of disputed deeper origin (no consensus PIE root; de Vaan marks it as uncertain, possibly from h2eyk- or an Italic formation), so the equal-stream is stated as Latin-secure with PIE provisional. The voice-stream wokw-s to vox to vocare is secure (Sanskrit vac-, Greek epos). Their Late Latin union in aequivocare and aequivocatio is fully attested in grammarians and Boethius.
