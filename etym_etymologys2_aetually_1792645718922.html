Note on typographical variation and historical semantics: The surface string aetually is a documented optical character recognition (OCR) error for actually, generated by the graphic confusion between lower-case 'c' and 'e' in broken letterpress typography. Semantically, actually underwent a profound shift: in Late Latin and Scholastic philosophy, actuālis was coined specifically to translate Aristotle’s Greek technical term energeia ("actuality, activity") in contrast to dynamis ("potentiality"). Thus, something existing actually was that which had been brought into physical operation. Over centuries of English vernacular use, this philosophical contrast between "action" and "potential" broadened into the modern contrast between "objective fact" and "mistaken appearance or expectation".
