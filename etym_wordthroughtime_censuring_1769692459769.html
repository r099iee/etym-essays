- Judgment / opinion (late Middle English) - a neutral act of assessing, weighing or giving an estimate of merit.
- Formal condemnation (17th–19th centuries) - hardens into severe, official disapproval; to censure is to sit in judgment over another's character like a censor.
- Official rebuke & self-restraint (Modern English) - lives on as a formal vote of censure against public officials, and as self-censure meaning self-blame or restraint; the moral, judicial weight remains.
