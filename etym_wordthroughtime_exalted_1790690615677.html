- Devotional lifting (Late Antiquity–Middle Ages) - exaltare means to raise thrones and praise God; holy height, reverent and cosmic.
- Feudal promotion (1300s–1500s) - exalted rank and throne; chivalric-honorific, to be exalted is to be knighted or crowned.
- Moral and poetic sublimity (1600s–1800s) - exalted mind, imagination, language; Romantic-transported, lofty can be sublime or bombastic.
- Psychological mood (late 1800s–1900s) - exalted mood or affect; elated to manic, medicalized elevation.
- Modern formal-ironic (present) - exalted office still respectful, exalted opinion of oneself gently mocking; height remains but irony is available.
