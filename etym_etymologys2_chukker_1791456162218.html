Hindi (18th–19th c.)
chakkar

↓
Anglo-Indian English (19th c.)
chukker

↓
chukker
"one period of a polo match"

Note: the PIE form is a reduplicated derivative of the root *kʷel- “to turn”; Sanskrit cakra and English wheel are therefore distant cognates. The Hindi form chakkar shows the regular development of Sanskrit cakra with gemination and a final -ar; English borrowed the Anglo-Indian pronunciation and spelled it with -er or -a.
