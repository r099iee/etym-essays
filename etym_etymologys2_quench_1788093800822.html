↓
Proto-Germanic (reconstructed; origin debated)
*kwenkijaną (causative)
"to cause to go out, extinguish"
↓
Old English (8th–11th c.)
cwencan / ācwencan
"to quench, extinguish, put out"
↓
Middle English (12th–15th c.)
quenchen
"to extinguish; to slake thirst"
↓
Modern English
QUENCH
"to extinguish; to slake; to cool suddenly"

Note: no secure PIE root is established for *kwenk-; the base is confined to Germanic (Old Saxon quencan, Middle Dutch quenchen, Old Frisian kwensa) and is probably expressive. The intransitive partner Old English cwincan "to go out, die" shows the same base.
