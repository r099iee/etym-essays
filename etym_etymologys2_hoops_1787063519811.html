Note on the debated origin: beyond Germanic the root of *hōp is unknown; it has no certain cognates outside Germanic (compare Dutch hoep and Low German hoop; Old Norse hóp "small inlet" is doubtfully connected). Some scholars guess at an Indo-European base meaning "to bend, curve," but the OED marks the ultimate origin as uncertain — the honest answer is that the first hoopmaker's word is lost.
