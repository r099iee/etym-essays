• Broodmares: Similar because both refer to female equines; differs because 'broodmares' specifically denotes adult female horses kept exclusively for reproductive breeding and foal production, whereas 'mares' includes all adult female horses regardless of breeding status.
  • Fillies: Similar because both denote female equines; differs because a 'filly' is specifically a young female horse under the age of four (or five in Thoroughbred racing), whereas 'mares' are fully mature adult females.
  • Dams: Similar because both relate to female horses; differs because 'dam' is a genealogical and pedigree term denoting the female parent of a specific foal, whereas 'mares' is the general zoological and agricultural term.
  • Stallions: Antonym / counterpart; denotes uncastrated adult male horses, contrasting directly with mares in biology, reproductive role, and herd behavior.
  • Geldings: Counterpart; denotes castrated male horses, which are often pastured together with or separated from mares to maintain herd tranquility.
  • Equines: Similar in biological family; differs because 'equines' is a broad taxonomic umbrella encompassing all horses, donkeys, and zebras of either sex.
