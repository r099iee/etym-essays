• Basement: Similar because both are rooms below ground level of a building; differs because a basement is the general understory, often finished as living space, while a cellar is specifically a cool storage vault.
  • Vault: Similar because both are underground rooms for storage; differs because a vault stresses arched structure or high security (bank vault), while a cellar is the homely storage room.
  • Crypt: Similar because both are underground chambers; differs because a crypt is ecclesiastical, under a church, and historically for burial, while a cellar is domestic and for provisions.
  • Undercroft: Similar because both are vaulted underground rooms; differs because an undercroft is the architectural term for a church or castle basement, while a cellar is the everyday domestic word.
  • Pantry: Similar because both are rooms for storing food; differs because a pantry is a smaller food-and-ware room at living level, while a cellar is below ground and cooler.
  • Larder: Similar because both store provisions; differs because a larder is specifically for meat and provisions at living level, while a cellar is the underground bulk-storage vault.
  • Storehouse: Similar because both are places for storing goods; differs because a storehouse may be above ground and commercial, while a cellar is the domestic underground room.
  • Storeroom: Similar because both are rooms for keeping things; differs because a storeroom is general and may be anywhere in a building, without the underground coolness of a cellar.
  • Cave: Similar because both are underground spaces used for storage; differs because a cave is natural, not built, while a cellar is constructed.
  • Dugout: Similar because both are below-ground shelters; differs because a dugout is a rough excavated shelter (or a baseball team bench), not a finished storage room.
  • Sub-basement: Similar because both are the lowest underground spaces; differs because a sub-basement is a further structural level below the basement, while a cellar is defined by storage use.
