Note: the ultimate origin is uncertain. The Old Norse link is probable but rests on a reconstructed form (*dasa, beside attested reflexive dasask "to become weary"); no agreed Indo-European root underlies it, and some scholars treat daze as an isolated Middle English formation without secure Germanic cognates.
