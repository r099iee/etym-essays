Note on the debate: The deeper origin of Latin laqueus is not settled. Earlier handbooks sometimes derived it from a PIE root meaning ‘to bind, snare’, but modern authorities (e.g. de Vaan) find no certain Indo-European reconstruction, so no PIE stage is asserted here; the attested chain begins at Latin.
