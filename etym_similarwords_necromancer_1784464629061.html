• Wizard: Similar because both are magic-users; differs because a wizard is a general spell-caster and sage, while a necromancer specifically works magic of and through the dead.
  • Sorcerer: Similar because both practise magic, often dark; differs because a sorcerer may work any kind of enchantment, while a necromancer's power is tied to death, corpses, and spirits.
  • Diviner: Similar because both claim to see hidden or future knowledge; differs because a diviner foretells through many techniques without necessarily using the dead, while a necromancer conjures spirits for knowledge.
  • Warlock: Similar because both are dark male magic-users; differs because warlock is the popular word for a male witch, without the death-magic specificity of necromancer.
  • Magician: Similar because both work magic; differs because a magician may be a stage performer or a neutral scholar of magic, while a necromancer is bound to the dead.
  • Medium: Similar because both communicate with the spirits of the dead; differs because a medium is a spiritualist intermediary of séances, while a necromancer is a magician who conjures and commands in a darker, older tradition.
  • Shaman: Similar because both act as spirit-intermediaries; differs because a shaman is a tribal healer and guide to the spirit world, while a necromancer is a wizard of the dead in the Western occult tradition.
  • Conjurer: Similar because both summon beings; differs because a conjurer may summon anything and is often an entertainer, while a necromancer specifically summons the dead.
  • Dark wizard: Similar because both are associated with evil and forbidden power; differs because "dark wizard" is a loose modern label, while necromancer is the precise historical word for the practitioner of divination by the dead.
