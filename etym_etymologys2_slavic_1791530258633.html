Note: the derivation of *slovo from PIE *ḱléwos is the standard account, but the semantic step from "fame" to "word" is debated, and some scholars prefer to connect the ethnonym with *slava "glory". The development of Sclavus from "Slav" to "slave" is historically documented; its cause is still discussed.
