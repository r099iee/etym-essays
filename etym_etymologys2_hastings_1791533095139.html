Note: the first element is the Old English personal name Hæsta, which is not independently attested and whose origin is uncertain; it may be a byname from hæst “strife, violence” (Proto-Germanic *haifstiz), but this is a conjecture, not a demonstrated derivation. The suffix -ingas is the Old English group-name suffix (“the people of, the descendants of”), from Proto-Germanic *-ingōz; its deeper Indo-European origin is obscure, so the chain begins at the earliest securely reconstructible stage.
