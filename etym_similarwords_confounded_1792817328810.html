• Bewildered: Similar because both describe a mind lost among alternatives; differs because bewildered is sheer being-lost (the wilderness root), while confounded adds the flavour of things actively mixed up together.
  • Perplexed: Similar because both name puzzled inability to decide; differs because perplexed is knotted (entangled threads of thought), while confounded is blended (liquids poured together) — knots versus mixtures.
  • Baffled: Similar because both admit defeat before a puzzle; differs because baffled stresses the obstacle winning (the safe baffled the thief), while confounded stresses inner mixture and disarray.
  • Flummoxed: Similar because both are comic words for total confusion; differs because flummoxed is breezy Victorian slang with no other senses, while confounded carries its cursing and refuting history alongside.
  • Blasted: Similar because both serve as mild damn-you adjectives (blasted visitors); differs because blasted imagines being struck (by wind, curse, or explosion), while confounded imagines being mixed into ruin.
  • Wretched: Similar because both grumble at nuisances (wretched visitors); differs because wretched pities or despises the thing's misery, while confounded damns its troublesomeness — contempt versus pity.
  • Accursed: Similar because both are softened damnations of nuisances; differs because accursed keeps a whiff of real malediction (under a curse), while confounded has bleached almost entirely into comic irritation.
  • Refuted: Similar because to confound an argument is to refute it; differs because refuted is purely logical disproof, while confounded adds overthrow and shame — the opponent routed, not merely answered.
  • Abashed: Similar because both can leave a person struck silent and red-faced; differs because abashed is shame-facing (eyes down), while confounded is mind-mixed (thoughts tangled) — embarrassment versus bewilderment.
