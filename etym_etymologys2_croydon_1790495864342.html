Note: the saffron-valley derivation (OE croh saffron + denu valley) is the usual account (Ekwall, Mills), supported by early spellings Crogedene and Wandle-valley topography, but it is debated. Alternatives propose OE *crogedene (crooked valley, from croh? crook) or a personal name *Croga + denu. The second element denu valley is secure; the first remains provisional.
