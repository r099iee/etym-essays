• Banter: Similar because both denote lighthearted, playful, and teasing conversation between people; differs because banter is far broader, more colloquial, and common to all social classes, whereas badinage carries an elevated, aristocratic tone suggesting urbane refinement, high wit, and salon elegance.
  • Repartee: Similar because both involve quick, amusing verbal exchanges; differs because repartee specifically stresses the speed, cleverness, and martial quickness of counter-thrusts in retorts, while badinage emphasizes an ongoing spirit of playful silliness and graceful teasing rather than competitive score-settling.
  • Raillery: Similar because both signify gentle, good-humored mockery or teasing; differs because raillery often carries a slightly sharper satirical edge or personal poking, whereas badinage remains persistently buoyant, harmless, and light-footed.
  • Persiflage: Similar because both describe light, frivolous social jesting; differs because persiflage often carries an undertone of cynical flippancy, mocking skepticism, or treating serious topics with deliberate indifference, whereas badinage is warm, affectionate, and socially cooperative.
  • Chaff: Similar because both refer to teasing good-naturedly; differs because chaff (or chaffing) suggests robust, rustic, or schoolboy teasing, often lacking the French salon elegance and verbal polish inherent in badinage.
  • Jesting: Similar because both involve humor and jokes; differs because jesting is a generic, unadorned term that includes clowning, physical jokes, or told anecdotes, whereas badinage is strictly conversational, dialogic, and linguistic.
  • Drollery: Similar because both produce amusement through whimsical humor; differs because drollery centers on odd, eccentric, dry, or quaint comicality, whereas badinage is brisk, interactive back-and-forth social play.
