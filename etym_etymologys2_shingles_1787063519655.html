Note on uncertainty: the pebble sense is of uncertain origin. The Norwegian comparison (singl "gravel, small stones", from singla "to jingle") is the most favoured proposal — pebbles crunch and tinkle — but it is not proven, and dictionaries mark the etymology as obscure. The roof-tile and disease chains above are secure. All three chains end in the identical spelling shingles.
