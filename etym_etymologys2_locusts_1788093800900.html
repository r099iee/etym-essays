Note: Latin locusta has no agreed PIE source — de Vaan (2008) gives “no etymology”; it may be a substrate word or an expressive formation, so the chain honestly begins at Latin. The Romans used one word for both the insect and the crustacean, and the same Latin word also produced English lobster (Old English loppestre), making locust and lobster doublets. The botanical sense (carob) and the American cicada sense are later transfers, not new stages of the chain.
