Debate: low is a Middle English borrowing of Old Norse lágr, connected with English lie via PGmc *lēgaz and PIE *legʰ- "to lie down". The PIE source of land, *lendʰ- "land, open country", is debated: cognates are suggested in Celtic (Old Irish land), but OED treats the ultimate Indo-European etymology as uncertain.
