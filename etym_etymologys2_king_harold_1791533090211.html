Note: king derives from *kuningaz, usually analysed as *kun- “family, kin” plus the patronymic suffix *-ing-, i.e. “member of the noble lineage”; the link to PIE *ǵenh₁- “to beget” is standard but the intermediate morphology is reconstructed. The second element of *Harjawaldaz is *waldaz “ruler, power,” from a root conventionally connected with PIE *wel- “to be strong”; the deeper derivation is debated. English Harold descends from Old English Hereweald but was reinforced in the Danelaw and at court by the Norse cognate Haraldr, which is why the medieval spelling varies between Harald and Harold.
