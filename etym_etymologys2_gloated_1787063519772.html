Note: the origin is debated. Middle English glouten may be reinforced by Middle High German glotzen "to stare, gape"; a deeper link to PIE *ghel- "to shine, gleam" (gleaming teeth in a grin) is plausible but conjectural. The earliest recorded English sense is "to stare," and the smug-satisfaction sense only solidifies in the 18th–19th centuries.
