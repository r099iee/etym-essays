- Old French filz - son in Norman charters; legal, genealogical
- Anglo-Norman Fitz- surnames - Fitzroy, Fitzgerald, FitzJames for sons, often natural sons; heraldic, discreet
- 1670-1734 Berwick FitzJames - James II's bastard becomes Marshal of France; exiled, famous, continental
- 1810 Scott's Fitz-James - Knight of Snowdoun in Lady of the Lake revealed as James V; romantic, chivalric, best-selling
- 1830s Ruskin childhood FitzJames - boy's pattern of kinghood as labour and mercy; idealised, mourned when castles prove empty
- Victorian tourism - Loch Katrine steamers, Ellen's Isle, Stirling guard-room re-enacted; picturesque, commercial
- Modern surname and history - Fitzjames of the Franklin expedition, Spanish Fitz-James Stuarts; genealogical, global
