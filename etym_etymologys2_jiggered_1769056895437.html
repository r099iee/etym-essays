↓ English

↓ English (19th c., British slang)
jigger

↓ English (19th c., British slang)
jiggered
"astonished, confounded;

Debate: jig is of uncertain origin; it may derive from Middle French gigue "a fiddle, jig", but this link is not certain. jiggered, as in the exclamation "I'm jiggered!", is 19th c. British slang for "astonished, confounded", euphemistically "I'll be damned".
