- Old English: fall around and fall to - befeallan is leaves falling around, lot falling to a man; perception is tribal sharing and natural covering.
- Medieval chronicle: come to pass - as it bifel upon a day; perception is annalistic, events recorded because they fell upon witnesses.
- King James era: providential doom - lest evil befall thee; perception is biblical solemnity, chance as God-sent.
- 18th–19th c.: literary archaism - happen to replaces befall in speech; befall retreats to hymns, Gothic, history; perception is elevated and slightly musty.
- Contemporary: fantasy fate - curses befall houses, darkness befalls worlds, If such befall you; perception is epic weight lent to randomness, circumstance dressed as destiny.
