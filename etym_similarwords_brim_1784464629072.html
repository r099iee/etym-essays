• Rim: Similar because it names a circular or linear edge finishing an object; differs because rim is the general word for any circular edge (wheel, glass, lens), while brim often specifically means a hat's projecting edge or a vessel's upper lip.
  • Edge: Similar because it names a boundary or limit; differs because edge is the broadest general term for any boundary, while brim is the specific finishing circumference of a hat or vessel.
  • Margin: Similar because it names an outer boundary; differs because margin is usually the unused space beside text or a margin of safety, not a physical projecting edge.
  • Brink: Similar because it names the edge of something; differs because brink is the edge of something steep or dangerous (a cliff, a crisis), with a sense of imminence, while brim is the neutral finishing edge of a hat or vessel.
  • Lip: Similar because it names the edge of a vessel; differs because lip is a narrower word for the turned-out edge of a container or opening, while brim is the full upper circumference and also a hat part.
  • Border: Similar because it names a boundary; differs because border is a political or decorative boundary line, not a physical projecting edge of an object.
  • Visor: Similar because it is a forward projection of a hat; differs because a visor is only the front peak, while the brim is the full projecting perimeter encircling the hat.
  • Peak: Similar because it is a part of headgear; differs because the peak is a limited forward projection (a cap's peak), while the brim encircles the whole hat.
