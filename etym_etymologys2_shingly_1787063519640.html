Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE)
*sengʷ- / *seng-
"to sing, make a ringing sound"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*singwaną / *singula-
"to sound; rattling coarse gravel"
↓
Old Norse / North Sea Germanic (c. 900 CE)
*singl / singla
"coarse sand, rattling gravel, clatter"
↓
Middle English (c. 1200–1400)
schengil / schyngle
"loose coastal pebbles, gravel"
↓
Early Modern English (late 16th c.)
shingle + -y
"full of pebbles, covered with shingle"
↓
Modern English (Present)
shingly
"abounding in water-worn coastal pebbles"
