Ortega

Etymological Note: The surname Ortega possesses a dual etymological history in Iberian onomastics. The primary philological derivation is habitational, evolving from Latin urtīca ("stinging nettle," related to urere, "to burn") through Vulgar Latin locative formations (*urticēta / *ortēca), designating a place overgrown with nettles (cognate with modern Spanish ortiga). A concurrent secondary influence comes from the Old Spanish common noun ortega, designating the black-bellied sandgrouse (Pterocles orientalis), a game bird whose name derived from Greek órtyx (ὄρτυξ, "quail") through Late Latin ortygometra.
