Note: the origin is genuinely uncertain, and the chain above marks the Romani link as a hypothesis rather than a proven derivation. The Oxford English Dictionary treats the etymology as unknown. The most frequently cited proposal is Angloromani bloke "man", but the evidence is circumstantial. Other suggestions include Dutch blok "block, fool" and English block in the sense "blockhead"; neither has been demonstrated. The word is first recorded in 1851, and its rapid spread in British, Australian and New Zealand English is better documented than its origin.
