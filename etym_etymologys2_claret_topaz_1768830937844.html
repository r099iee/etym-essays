CLARET

↓
PIE (uncertain)
*kelh₁-
"to call, be clear"

↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
clārus
"clear, bright"

↓
Old French (11th c.)
clair
"clear, bright"

↓
Old French (12th c.)
claret
"light red wine"

↓
English (14th c.)
claret
"the light red wine
of Bordeaux"

Debate: the derivation of clārus from PIE *kelh₁- "to call, cry out" (via "resounding" → "clear") is standard but the precise semantic path is debated.

topaz

Debate: the origin is uncertain — topázios may derive from topázein "to seek, divine" (the stone of Topazios island in the Red Sea) or from a Sanskrit or Semitic source.
