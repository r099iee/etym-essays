[Compound Formation: fallow + hide]
Modern English (Philological Literary Coinage) (1954)
Fallohides
"fair-skinned, tall, tree-loving breed of Hobbits"

Note on philological construction: J.R.R. Tolkien deliberately constructed the ethnonym Fallohide as a compound modeled on archaic Old English (*fealu-hȳd*), translating an unrecorded Westron dialectal form. The element fallow preserves the ancient Germanic meaning of "pale, fair, or dusky yellow" (as seen in *fallow deer*), while hide is used in its archaic poetic sense of "skin" or "complexion," literally designating the "fair-complexioned ones."
