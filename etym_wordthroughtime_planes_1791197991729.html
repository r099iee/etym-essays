- Stone rubbing slab (Neolithic) - Practical leveller; neutral grinding stone for shafts and planks, no prestige.
- Roman joiner's plana (c. 100-500 CE) - Professional pride; iron-soled tool of urban furniture makers, sign of Roman comfort.
- Medieval guild tool (500-1500) - Guild-guarded mystery; plane ownership marks master joiner, handled with craft reverence.
- Early modern refinement (1500-1800) - Enlightened precision; great-masted ships and panelled rooms demand true planes, associated with progress.
- Industrial Bailey iron plane (1850-1950) - Democratic and mass-produced; every school workshop has planes, connotation of honest manual training.
- Modern polysemy (1950-present) - Split perception; hand planes nostalgic and artisanal (Sam Maloof glow) while planes as aircraft dominate everyday ear, requiring context to hear the tool.
