- 15th-16th c. Kingdom of Kongo - powerful Christian African kingdom, respected trading partner in Portuguese eyes
- 17th-18th c. vague interior - name fades to coastal fringe as kingdom fragments; blank-map mystery
- 1870s-1890s age of exploration - Upper Congo as adventure and horror; Stanley, missionaries, Heart of Darkness dread
- 1885-1960 colonial Congo - Free State atrocities then Belgian/French administration; pity and protest in world opinion
- 1960-present two Congos - independent DRC and ROC; modern perception of music, crisis, and rainforest ecology
