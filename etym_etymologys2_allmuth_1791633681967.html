Note: Allmuth (more commonly spelled Almuth or Almut) is an ancient Germanic dithematic compound personal name. The first element derives from Old High German adal ("nobility, noble descent"); the second element derives from Old High German muot ("mind, spirit, heart, courage"). In Low German, Frisian, and Westphalian dialects, scribal tradition preserved the unvoiced dental fricative rendered as -th, producing the regional variant Allmuth preserved in the family name of Allmuth Laporte.
