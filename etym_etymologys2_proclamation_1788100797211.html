Note: the noun prōclāmātiō is attested chiefly in later Latin legal and rhetorical prose; the classical word of everyday speech was the verb prōclāmō (used by Cicero in the sense "to cry out loudly"). English royal usage grew out of the medieval practice of "crying" proclamations through the streets.
