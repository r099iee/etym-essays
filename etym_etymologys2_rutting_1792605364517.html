Note on etymological divergence: The biological word rut ("mating season of animals", source of rutting) must not be confused with the common noun rut ("a groove or furrow cut by a wheel; a fixed habit"). The latter emerged in the late 16th century, likely derived from Middle French route ("track, path, road", from Latin rupta [via], "broken road"). The two words are entirely distinct homophones that converged phonetically in Early Modern English.
