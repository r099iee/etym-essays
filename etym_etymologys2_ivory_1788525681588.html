Note: Latin ebur is a Mediterranean/Afroasiatic loan-word from Ancient Egyptian ꜣbw (also preserved in Coptic ebou, Sanskrit ibha- "elephant", and Biblical Hebrew shenhabbīm "teeth of elephants"). The initial vowel shift from e- to i- occurred during the transition from Vulgar Latin into Old French.
