• Prodded: Similar because both involve thrusting a pointed object or finger into something to move or stimulate it; differs because prodded implies a firmer, more deliberate, or continuous push often intended to compel action, whereas poked can be a brief, playful, or casual tap.
  • Jabbed: Similar because both denote a sharp thrust with a point; differs because jabbed conveys much greater suddenness, sharpness, and aggressive force, often bordering on an assault or strike.
  • Nudged: Similar because both involve touching someone gently to draw attention; differs because nudged specifically uses a gentle push with the elbow, shoulder, or snout to coax softly rather than a pointed finger jab.
  • Pried: Similar because both involve inquisitive investigation into obscure or private matters; differs because pried emphasizes persistent, intrusive, and unwelcome curiosity into someone else's secrets, whereas poked suggests casual or exploratory searching.
  • Snooped: Similar because both describe unauthorized searching; differs because snooped implies stealthy, sneaky, and illicit investigation, while poked around can be open and harmlessly curious.
  • Goaded: Similar because both metaphorically stimulate someone into action; differs because goaded involves intense, provocative verbal stinging or persistent pressure to incite a specific reaction.
