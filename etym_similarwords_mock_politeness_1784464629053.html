• Sarcasm: Similar because both express insincerity and a cutting edge; differs because sarcasm is broad hostile irony that need not wear courteous forms, while mock-politeness wears the specific mask of polite manners.
  • Irony: Similar because both say one thing while meaning another; differs because irony may be situational or verbal without performing courtesy, while mock-politeness is specifically performed courteous behaviour with teasing or hostile intent.
  • Condescension: Similar because both involve looking down while seeming gracious; differs because condescension patronizes and may be sincere in its kindness, while mock-politeness performs a courtesy that is really a tease or a mock.
  • Mockery: Similar because both involve derisive imitation; differs because mockery may be open and unmasked ridicule, while mock-politeness keeps the polite surface intact over the derision.
  • Insincerity: Similar because both lack genuine goodwill behind the words; differs because insincerity is a broad quality of any dishonest expression, while mock-politeness names the specific case of insincere courtesy.
  • Affectation: Similar because both involve performing behaviour not genuinely felt; differs because affectation is a mannerism put on to impress, while mock-politeness is politeness put on to deride or test.
  • Pretence: Similar because both involve a false surface; differs because pretence can be neutral or dishonest in any register, while mock-politeness specifies the polite register as the mask.
  • Satire: Similar because both expose through imitation and sharpness; differs because satire is a literary or comic mode with a public target, while mock-politeness is a social manner between individuals.
  • Banter / Raillery: Similar because both involve teasing playfulness; differs because banter is usually friendly and reciprocal, while mock-politeness is edged and can signal distance or contempt.
  • Politeness: Similar because both share the courteous surface; differs because genuine politeness carries goodwill and cooperation, while mock-politeness performs the surface without the goodwill — the crucial distinction of the compound.
  • Civility: Similar because both concern the forms of social courtesy; differs because civility is a general ideal of civil behaviour, while mock-politeness is the performed counterfeit of that ideal.
