Sense 1: Animal Estrus / Mating Cry
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE)
*reuH- / *rū-
"to roar, bellow, grunt, make loud noise"
↓
Proto-Italic (c. 1000 BCE)
*rug-ī-
"to roar, bellow"
↓
Classical Latin (c. 1st c. BCE)
rugīre
"to roar, bellow, bray (of beasts)"
↓
Late Latin (c. 4th c. CE)
rugītus
"a roaring, bellowing; mating roar"
↓
Old French (c. 12th c.)
ruit / rut
"roaring; sexual excitement of deer"
↓
Middle English (c. 1400)
rut / ritte
"annual sexual heat and roaring of deer"
↓
Modern English (present)
rut
"mating season and frenzy of male mammals"
