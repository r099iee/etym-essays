NOTE: The noun is formed on the past-participle stem of dragan — the same -aught pattern seen in taught, caught, sought, brought. Latin trahere "to draw, drag" (tractor, trail) is commonly derived from the same PIE root *dʰregʰ-, though a minority of scholars treat the Germanic and Italic forms as independent; the link is probable but not beyond debate. The US spelling draft is a later orthographic variant of the same word.
