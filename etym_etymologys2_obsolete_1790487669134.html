Note: exact prefix debated. Traditional analysis is ob- "away, against" + soletus / solere "to be wont", i.e. grown out of use; de Vaan notes obsoletus may instead continue ob- + *sole- from opus-related growth "worn by use". Both agree on sense "worn out, disused" by Classical Latin.
