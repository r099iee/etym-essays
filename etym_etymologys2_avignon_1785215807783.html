Debate: the Celtic origin is uncertain — Avennio is first attested in Latin (Pliny the Elder, 1st c. CE). It may derive from a Gaulish river-name built on *abū "river, waters" (a "river town", from its site on the Rhône), or from the personal name Avennius/Avien(n)us. No derivation is securely established.
