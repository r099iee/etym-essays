- Adult male deer/goat/hare - neutral hunting and husbandry term, marking vigour and breeding value
- Frontier buckskin unit of trade - practical economic sense, a skin as currency on the colonial border
- American dollar (a buck, big bucks) - familiar, slightly boastful everyday money slang, animal origin forgotten
- To buck: horse violently arching to throw rider - vivid rodeo image of resistance
- To buck authority / buck the trend - broadened social metaphor for defiance, nonconformity and courage, now dominant in business and politics
