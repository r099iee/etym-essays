Note: Gondolin is an invented word from J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium — the hidden Elven city of Turgon — and NOT a historical loanword or Indo-European cognate. Per Tolkien's linguistic notes (the Etymologies), it is Sindarin gond "stone" + dolen "hidden", i.e. "Hidden Rock"; some versions give an earlier Quenya name Ondolindë "Rock-song" (ondo + lindë).
