No PIE ancestry — a constructed name coined by J. R. R. Tolkien within his invented Elvish languages - Primitive Quendian *gondō 'great stone, rock' (from the Common Eldarin root *GONOD- 'stone') - Quenya ondo 'stone, rock' (the High-elven dialect's regular development of the root) - Quenya Ondolindë 'Rock of the Music of Water' (ondo 'stone' + lindë 'song, music' — the city's original Quenya name, honouring the fountain before Turgon's tower) - Sindarin adaptation Gondolin 'the Hidden Rock' (Tolkien re-analysed the name in the Grey-elven dialect as gond 'stone, rock' + dolen 'hidden', past participle of dol- 'to hide', from the root *DUL- 'to conceal') - English borrowing Gondolin (Tolkien's legendarium; appears in The Hobbit, 1937, and in the posthumous Silmarillion materials)
