- Annual chief magistrate of the Roman Republic - supreme collegial authority, positive republican prestige, symbolized by fasces, curule chair, ivory staff
- Imperial eponymous honor - diminished real power under emperors, retained dignity and dating function, perception shifts to prestigious formality
- Medieval communal magistrate in Italian and Provençal cities - revived republican self-government, mercantile and civic pride
- Early modern merchant consul abroad - practical protector of traders and nationals in foreign ports, commercial rather than military authority
- Napoleonic First Consul - deliberate Roman revival for strong executive republicanism, authoritarian yet legitimizing
- Modern career / honorary consul - bureaucratic helper, visa and citizen-services officer under international law, neutral service connotation
