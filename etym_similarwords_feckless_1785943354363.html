• Irresponsible: Similar because both describe a failure to take on duties and consequences; differs because "irresponsible" is a neutral moral and legal judgment of accountability, while "feckless" adds a tone of pathetic, weak-willed incompetence and lack of force.
  • Shiftless: Similar because both describe a person lacking ambition and resourcefulness; differs because "shiftless" highlights laziness and the inability to "shift" for oneself or find work, while "feckless" highlights the total lack of inner force or effect.
  • Ineffectual: Similar because both mean producing no effect; differs because "ineffectual" is a formal descriptor of outcomes (an ineffectual law, an ineffectual remedy), while "feckless" describes the person's weak character itself.
  • Incompetent: Similar because both describe a failure to get things done; differs because "incompetent" focuses on lacking skill for a specific task or job, while "feckless" describes a general character flaw of spinelessness and aimlessness.
  • Helpless: Similar because both describe an inability to act effectively; differs because "helpless" is neutral and even sympathetic (a helpless baby, a helpless victim), while "feckless" carries contempt for the person's own failure of will.
  • Spineless: Similar because both describe a lack of strength of character; differs because "spineless" highlights cowardice and lack of backbone under pressure, while "feckless" highlights ineffectiveness and lack of purpose rather than fear.
  • Weak: Similar because both describe a lack of force or determination; differs because "weak" is the broad, colourless word (weak tea, weak argument, weak will), while "feckless" is specific to character and carries an old literary flavour.
  • Careless: Similar because both describe neglect of responsibility; differs because "careless" suggests lack of attention or thought in specific actions, while "feckless" suggests an ingrained incapacity for effective action.
  • Idle: Similar because both can describe a person who does not do useful work; differs because "idle" names laziness or inactivity and is neutral-to-slight, while "feckless" names incompetence and uselessness of effort.
  • Ne'er-do-well: Similar because both describe a worthless, unpromising person; differs because "ne'er-do-well" is a noun for a habitual good-for-nothing drifter, while "feckless" is an adjective for weakness of character.
  • Good-for-nothing: Similar because both describe a person of no use; differs because "good-for-nothing" is colloquial and total in its condemnation, while "feckless" is milder, more literary, and more about lack of force than lack of worth.
  • Useless: Similar because both describe a person who is of no practical use; differs because "useless" is blunt, informal, and applies to anything (a useless tool, a useless player), while "feckless" is character-specific and literary.
  • Worthless: Similar because both can describe a person of no value or effect; differs because "worthless" is a stronger, harsher moral judgment of a person's value, while "feckless" stops at a lack of effectiveness.
