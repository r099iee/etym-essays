- Tribal autonym Umoⁿhoⁿ - "upstream people going against the current" - proud migration identity distinguishing upstream movers
- French colonial exonym Maha / Omaha - outsider label for "a Missouri River nation" - neutral-to-exotic ethnographic curiosity
- American treaty tribe 1804-1870s - "signatory nation ceding land" - perception shifts to legal ward / obstacle to settlement, then confined reservation people
- City namesake from 1854 - "booming railroad / stockyards town" - perception becomes Midwestern industriousness, gateway to the West
- 20th-century civic + military icon - Omaha as Berkshire Hathaway hometown and Omaha Beach D-Day symbol - perception of heartland capitalism and heroic sacrifice, largely detached from tribal origin
