- Mythological Female Warrior - A legendary, often feared and admired, race of independent, martial women in ancient Greek narratives.
- Tall, Strong Woman - A metaphorical extension, emerging from the Renaissance onwards, describing women of imposing physical stature and strength, often with a sense of awe or respect.
- Female Athlete/Pioneer - In the 19th and 20th centuries, increasingly used to describe women excelling in physically demanding sports or breaking barriers in male-dominated fields, emphasizing capability and defiance.
- Emancipated/Powerful Woman - Contemporary usage, often referring to women who are independent, assertive, and challenge traditional gender roles, reflecting feminist ideals and societal shifts towards gender equality.
