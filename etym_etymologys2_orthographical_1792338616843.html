Note on dual application: Classical Latin borrowed Greek orthographía with two distinct, parallel technical applications that both persist in modern English: Quintilian and Suetonius used it grammatically for "correct orthography/spelling", while the Roman architect Vitruvius in De Architectura used it geometrically for an upright elevation drawing created by perpendicular projection lines (as opposed to ichnographia, a ground plan, and scaenographia, a perspective view).
