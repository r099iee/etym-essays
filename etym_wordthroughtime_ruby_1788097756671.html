- The red gem (ancient-medieval) - "ruby" (and its ancestor rubinus lapis, "red stone") names the finest red jewel of lapidaries and treasuries; perception: wealth, nobility, talismanic virtue.
- The colour name (16th-18th c.) - ruby moves from stone to deep red itself: ruby wine, ruby lips, ruby glass; perception: warmth, richness, romance.
- Anniversary and jubilee (19th-20th c.) - the ruby wedding (40th) and ruby jubilee make the stone a measure of enduring love and reign; perception: endurance, preciousness, celebration.
- Science and technology (1902-1960) - Verneuil's synthetic rubies, ruby watch jewels, and Maiman's ruby laser; perception: precision, industry, wonder; the talisman becomes a tool.
- Popular culture and software (1939-1995) - Dorothy's ruby slippers, the name Ruby, the Ruby programming language and Japanese rubi glosses; perception: magic, nostalgia, and modern craft wearing a gem's name.
