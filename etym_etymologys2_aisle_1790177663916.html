Etymological Note: The modern orthography aisle is an etymological hybrid resulting from multiple conflations. The true ancestor is Latin ala ("wing"). In Middle English it was spelled ele or ile. In the 16th century, scholars mistakenly connected it with Latin insula / English isle ("island"), inserting the silent s. In the 18th century, lexicographers restored the initial a- to mirror French aile, while keeping the unetymological s, giving us the distinctive modern spelling.
