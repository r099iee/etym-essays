Note: Balticum mare is the earliest attested form of the name (Adam of Bremen, c. 1075); its own origin is disputed. One tradition derives it from Latin balteus “belt” (the sea as a belt of water); another from the Baltic words for “white” (Lithuanian baltas, Latvian balts), giving “the white sea”; a third looks to a Germanic or Norse source. No derivation is conclusive, so the chain begins at the first attestation. The linguistic sense is a nineteenth-century scholarly extension, associated with G. H. F. Nesselmann (1845).
