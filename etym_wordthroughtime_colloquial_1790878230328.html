- 1800s - Victorian linguistic demarcation: Codified in dictionaries as a stylistic label (often warning against colloquialisms in polite formal prose).
- 1920s–1950s - Realist & Modernist literary revolution: Celebrated by writers like Hemingway and Faulkner as a source of raw artistic vitality and authentic American voice.
