Note: the immediate route of the noun into English is not settled: it may come directly from Medieval Latin retaliatio or through a French intermediary. The English noun is recorded from the 1580s, some decades before the verb retaliate (1610s), so the usual “verb + -ion” account cannot be the whole story.
