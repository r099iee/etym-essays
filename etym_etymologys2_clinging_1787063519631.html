Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gley- / *gley-n-
"to stick, glue, cleave, form into a ball"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*klinganą
"to stick together, shrink, draw tight"
↓
Old English (c. 850 CE)
clingan
"to adhere, shrink, coagulate, hold fast"
↓
Middle English (c. 1300 CE)
clingen (part. clinginde / clinginge)
"to stick firmly, grasp, adhere to"
↓
Modern English (c. 1600 CE)
clinging
"holding fast, adhering, emotionally dependent"
