Note: the development of the long-coat sense occurred within Old French; the literal "neck-guard" sense survives only in the compound's parts. The precise reconstruction of the PIE stage behind *halsaz ("neck") is debated; the connection to *kʷel- "to turn" is the standard handbook account. Both PIE roots are securely attested in cognate families (cf. Sanskrit and Greek forms for *kʷel-; Celtic, Italic, Balto-Slavic and Indo-Iranian reflexes of *bʰergʰ-).
