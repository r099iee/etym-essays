Note: Late Latin subiunctīvus was created as a calque (loan-translation) of Ancient Greek hypotaktikē (ὑποτακτική, from hypotássein 'to subordinate, place under'), reflecting the Hellenistic grammatical observation that this verbal mood predominantly occurs within subordinated or dependent clauses rather than principal assertions.
