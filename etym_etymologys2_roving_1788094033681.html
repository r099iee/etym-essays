NOTE: The OED treats the wandering verb rove as a back-formation from rover "pirate" (one who roams the seas robbing). A competing theory links it to the older English archery verb rove "to shoot at a random mark" (16th c., of uncertain origin), where "shooting at random" shaded into "wandering at random"; the two verbs may also have influenced each other. The PIE root *reup- is the standard source of the rob/robber family (cf. rob, robe, bereave, reave).
