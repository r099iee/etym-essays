NOTE: The link between the PIE root *men- "to project" and the threat-words is that projecting points and overhanging rocks are what threaten one; the same root gave Latin mons "mountain". The intermediate Latin states are well attested; the Vulgar Latin singular *minācia is a standard reconstruction based on the Romance outcomes.
