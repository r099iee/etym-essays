• du Bellay: similar as the full name of the same poet; differs by including the noble surname (du Bellay < an Old French family name of the region of Anjou).
  • Ronsard: similar as co-founder of the Pléiade; differs in temperament — Ronsard the confident court laureate, du Bellay the melancholy exile.
  • Jehoiakim: similar as the full Hebrew original of the same name; differs in register — a biblical king of Judah, not a saint or poet.
  • Petrarch: similar as an earlier poet of love and classical revival who shaped European lyrics; differs in language (Italian), date, and fame.
  • Gioacchino: similar as the Italian form of the name; differs in spelling and pronunciation.
  • Joaquim: similar as the Portuguese form; differs in spelling, stress, and national use.
  • Akim: similar as the shortened Russian form; differs by dropping the sacred compound’s first element.
  • Saint Joachim: similar by name and by medieval sanctity; differs in referent — the father of the Virgin, not the poet.
