Note on the debate: the origin of Proto-West Germanic *wōrig is genuinely uncertain. It may be connected to the root seen in “worry” (Old English wyrgan, Proto-Germanic *wurgijan- “to strangle, to oppress”) — the notion of being worn down or crushed by a burden — but this link is debated and no fully satisfying Indo-European ancestor has been established. The second element -some, from PIE *sem- “one, together”, is secure.
