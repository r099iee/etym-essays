Note: The surname and personal name Lester represents the phonetic spelling of the city of Leicester. In Middle English, unstressed medial syllables in place names ending in -cester (such as Gloucester, Worcester, and Leicester) were systematically elided in speech, contracting three syllables into two. While official administrative cartography preserved the historical spelling Leicester, parish registers and family lineages widely adopted the phonetic spelling Lester.
