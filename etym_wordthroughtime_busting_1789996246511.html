- Burst into tears, song, flame - emotional and sudden perception, inward pressure erupting outward
- Colloquial bust by metathesis (1700s America) - demotic perception, shorter punchier twin for everyday breaking

- Busting into things on own initiative - contemporary managerial complaint, keen but undisciplined energy
