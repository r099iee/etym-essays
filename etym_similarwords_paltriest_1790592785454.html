• smallest: Similar because it marks minimal size or amount in a set; differs because it is neutral and dimensional, while paltriest adds contempt for worthlessness.
  • meagrest: Similar because it denotes miserably inadequate quantity; differs because meagre stresses insufficiency for need, while paltry/paltriest stresses contemptible triviality.
  • pettiest: Similar because it conveys triviality and small-mindedness; differs because petty focuses on narrow spirit or minor grievances, while paltriest focuses on worthless amount.
  • meanest: Similar because in the sense of stingy it denotes miserly smallness; differs because mean also means unkind and average, while paltriest is specifically about trashy insignificance.
  • trifling: Similar because it means of little value or importance; differs because trifling can be light and forgivable, while paltriest is scornful and condemnatory.
  • most worthless: Similar because it marks lowest value; differs because it is analytical and periphrastic, while paltriest is a single literary word with comic-arch flavor.
  • shabbiest: Similar because it preserves the ragged, squalid root sense of paltry; differs because shabby primarily describes appearance, while paltriest more often describes sums and excuses.
