• conscientious: Similar because it too names a person governed by a strong sense of duty and care about doing right; differs because conscientious emphasises inner motive and dutifulness, while scrupulous stresses the anxious, exact attention to small points and the pain of avoiding wrong.
  • meticulous: Similar because both denote extreme care over details; differs because meticulous is purely about procedure and neatness with no moral weight, while scrupulous keeps an ethical dimension — one can be meticulously cruel, but not scrupulously so.
  • punctilious: Similar because both are attentive to small formalities; differs because punctilious concerns etiquette, ceremony, and conventional rules of correctness, while scrupulous concerns conscience and integrity.
  • painstaking: Similar because both describe thorough, careful effort; differs because painstaking describes the labour and sweat invested in a task, while scrupulous describes the internal standard of care — the pebble in the conscience rather than the brow.
  • fastidious: Similar because both are extremely particular; differs because fastidious suggests an aesthetic or squeamish finickiness (a fastidious eater), while scrupulous is moral and exact — bothered by wrong and error, not by dirt or disorder alone.
  • exacting: Similar because both demand high standards; differs because exacting is usually the quality of the standard imposed on others or demanded from outside, while scrupulous is the personal, conscientious standard one applies to oneself.
  • rigorous: Similar because both are strict and precise; differs because rigorous stresses discipline, method, and testing, while scrupulous stresses moral and detailed care — one is a system, the other a temperament.
  • careful: Similar because both involve caution; differs because careful is the broad, unmarked word for caution in any task, while scrupulous is heightened, moral, and detail-obsessed — a specialized intensification of the same idea.
  • honorable / principled: Similar because both adhere to right conduct; differs because honourable and principled describe standing by grand moral codes, while scrupulous fixes on the small, anxious, point-by-point avoidance of wrongdoing.
  • ethical: Similar because both are governed by right and wrong; differs because ethical is systematic and philosophical (ethics as a discipline), while scrupulous is the personal, nearly instinctive twinge of conscience.
