Note on the debate: the OED declares Late Latin gunna of unknown origin. The Byzantine Greek γούνα is the usual proposed bridge (and is itself of uncertain, possibly non-Indo-European origin), but a Celtic source has also been suggested; there is no established Indo-European root, and the chain above must not be pressed beyond the attested stages. What is secure is the documented route gunna → Old French gonne/goune → Middle English goune.
