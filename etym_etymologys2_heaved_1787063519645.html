NOTE — DEBATED / NUANCED STAGE: the strong past hove (OE hōf, plural hōfon, participle hafen) is the older attested form and survives in nautical phrases (hove to, hove in sight). The weak form heaved is a 15th–16th century regularisation that won out in ordinary speech; the two forms coexisted for centuries before the strong paradigm was abandoned outside seafaring idiom. Both are real attested forms; no stages are guessed.
