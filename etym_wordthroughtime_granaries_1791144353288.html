- Early agricultural store-pits and raised huts - neutral practical necessity, survival and seasonal prudence
- Roman and Biblical granaria / Joseph's granaries - positive civic virtue, imperial power, divine foresight and moral lesson about providence
- Medieval tithe barns and manorial garners - feudal, seigneurial control, rent in kind, connotation of lordly abundance and peasant obligation
- Early modern improvement and colonial granaries - progressive, rational farming, mercantile wealth, pride in surplus and empire
- Modern strategic and figurative granaries - technical food-security term plus metaphor for breadbasket regions and reserves of knowledge, largely positive with anxieties about famine, waste and sustainability
