• Changes: Similar because both mean to make different in some way; differs because change is the broad, neutral, everyday native verb, while alter is more formal, Latinate, and suggests a deliberate modification that leaves the thing recognizably itself.
  • Modifies: Similar because both mean to adjust or make different, often in part; differs because modify is strongly technical and usually partial or incremental (modify the settings), while alter can be more consequential and need not be small.
  • Transforms: Similar because both denote making something different; differs because transform implies a dramatic, often complete change of form or nature, while alter typically keeps the identity of the thing intact.
  • Converts: Similar because both mean to change something from one state to another; differs because convert stresses a change of category, use, or belief, while alter simply makes different without asserting a new category.
  • Amends: Similar because both can involve improving something; differs because amend implies corrective improvement of a text, law, or fault, while alter is morally neutral about whether the change is for better or worse.
  • Varies: Similar because both involve difference; differs because vary stresses difference between instances over time (things vary), while alter stresses the act of actively making a thing different.
  • Adjusts: Similar because both denote making small, practical changes; differs because adjust emphasizes fine-tuning to fit (adjust the volume), while alter can be minor or major and does not require a standard to be met.
  • Revises: Similar because both mean to change a piece of work or an opinion; differs because revise implies examining and reworking (especially text), while alter is the general act of change in any domain.
  • Reshapes: Similar because both mean to change the form or direction of something; differs because reshape keeps the forming/outline image and implies restructuring, while alter is the neutral general term.
  • Edits: Similar because both involve changing content; differs because edit is restricted to text, media, or data, while alter covers physical, verbal, and abstract change alike.
