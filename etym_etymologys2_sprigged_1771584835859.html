Middle English (c. 14th c.)
sprigge
"a twig, sprig, small shoot"
↓
English (c. 14th c.)
sprig
"a small shoot or twig"
↓
English (17th c.)
sprigged
"ornamented with sprigs,
twigs, or sprig patterns of flowers"
Note: the origin of ME sprigge is uncertain; a connection to an unattested Old English or Scandinavian word for "shoot" has been suggested, but no secure ancestor is documented. The proposed link to Old English sprec (in the sense "shoot") is not securely attested.
