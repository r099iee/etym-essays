• Peevishly: Similar because both describe irritable, complaining behaviour; differs because peevishly emphasises childish, stubborn bad temper and querulous fault-finding, while fretfully centres on restless anxiety and being eaten away by unease — peevishness is cross, fretfulness is worried and chafing.
  • Querulously: Similar because both describe complaining speech; differs because querulously (from Latin queror, "to complain") focuses narrowly on a whining, plaintive, repetitive tone of voice, while fretfully covers broader anxious, restless behaviour beyond just the complaining tone.
  • Anxiously: Similar because both describe nervous concern; differs because anxiously centres on fear and apprehension about the future, often with an eager, hopeful undertone, while fretfully adds irritation, peevishness, and the chafing, gnawed quality — a vexed anxiety rather than a clean fear.
  • Irritably: Similar because both describe annoyance; differs because irritably is a quick, testy reaction to provocation (annoyed at something specific), while fretfully implies a chronic, restless state of worry and vexation that may have no single target.
  • Nervously: Similar because both describe agitated unease; differs because nervously emphasises jittery fear and lack of confidence, while fretfully adds an impatient, complaining, chafing quality — the person is not just nervous but vexed and being worn down.
  • Restlessly: Similar because both describe uneasy, unsettled motion; differs because restlessly is neutral about emotion (physical fidgeting without specifying feeling), while fretfully names the specific emotion — anxious, peevish irritation — and implies the internal gnawing.
  • Petulantly: Similar because both describe childish, sulky irritation; differs because petulantly (from Latin petulans, "forward, insolent") suggests sulky, pouting bad temper and even insolence, while fretfully suggests anxious, chafing worry rather than pouting defiance.
  • Morosely: Similar because both describe a disagreeable mood; differs because morosely is gloomy, sullen, withdrawn brooding, while fretfully is active, restless, complaining agitation — gloom vs. chafe.
