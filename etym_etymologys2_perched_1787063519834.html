Note: The derivation runs through Latin pertica (a measuring rod and long pole) and is fully Romance; no PIE root is commonly cited for pertica, whose origin is uncertain. The fish called "perch" is a different word from Latin perca (Greek perkē) and is unrelated despite the identical spelling.
