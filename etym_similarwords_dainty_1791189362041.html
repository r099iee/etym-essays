• delicate: Similar because both mean finely fragile and requiring care; differs because delicate stresses fragility and ease of breaking, dainty stresses small prettiness.
  • exquisite: Similar because both mean choice and finely beautiful; differs because exquisite stresses supreme quality and intensity, dainty stresses small-scale neatness.
  • elegant: Similar because both mean refined and tasteful; differs because elegant stresses graceful sophistication, dainty stresses petite prettiness.
  • graceful: Similar because both suggest light refined movement; differs because graceful stresses smooth motion, dainty stresses smallness and neatness.
  • petite: Similar because both imply small neatness; differs because petite is neutral about size, dainty adds prettiness and fussiness.
  • neat: Similar because both imply trim precision; differs because neat is plainer and more functional, dainty is prettier and more fragile.
  • charming: Similar because both please by refinement; differs because charming is broader and warmer, dainty is specifically small and delicate.
  • fastidious: Similar because both imply hard-to-please refinement; differs because fastidious stresses critical pickiness, dainty softens it with prettiness.
  • finicky: Similar because both mean fussy about details; differs because finicky is pejorative fussiness, dainty can be affectionate.
  • squeamish: Similar because both shrink from coarseness; differs because squeamish stresses nausea or moral recoil, dainty stresses affected refinement.
  • refined: Similar because both imply cultivated taste; differs because refined stresses polish and culture, dainty stresses small-scale delicacy.
  • exclusive / choice: Similar because both mean select and excellent (old deynte sense); differs because choice stresses superior quality, dainty stresses small delicacy.
