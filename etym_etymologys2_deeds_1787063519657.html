Note: the same PIE root *dʰeh₁- "to put, place, do" yields, by different paths, Latin facere "to do, make", Greek τίθημι (tithēmi) "to place", Sanskrit dadhāti "to put", and English "do". The Germanic noun *dēdiz shows the expected lengthened-grade vowel from *dʰeh₁- plus the suffix *-tis. The legal sense "written instrument" arose in English during the 14th–15th centuries from the general sense "an act" via the phrase "act and deed", the document being the parties' act made permanent.
