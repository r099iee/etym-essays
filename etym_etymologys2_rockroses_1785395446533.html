Debate: both elements are of uncertain
origin: Medieval Latin rocca may come from a
pre-Latin/Celtic word rather than from
Latin, and Latin rosa is likely a loan from
Greek ῥόδον (itself possibly from a
pre-Greek source), not a native Latin word
descended from PIE *wrd-. The plant is named
for its rose-like flowers on rocky ground.
