- The man and celebrity - In his own lifetime (1788-1824) Byron was a scandalous aristocrat-poet, famous across Europe for Childe Harold and Don Juan and for his 'mad, bad, and dangerous to know' notoriety.
- The Byronic archetype - After his death at Missolonghi the name hardens into a cultural stereotype of the brooding, defiant, sexually magnetic hero, distilled from the man into the Byronic hero of 19th-century fiction and painting.
- Modern literary figure - Today Byron is studied as a major Romantic poet, his fame now shading between scholarly appreciation and the lingering glamour of the 'Byron legend'.
