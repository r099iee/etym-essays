Note (debate): The link between Latin fūr "thief" (and Greek phṓr "thief") and PIE *bʰer- "to carry" — the thief as "one who carries off" — is standard but debated; some scholars reconstruct a separate root *bʰor- "to carry away, steal". The attested Latin → French → English portion of the chain is uncontroversial.
