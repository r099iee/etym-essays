Note (formation): The noun blast is the deverbal partner of blow: both descend from PIE *bʰleh₁- "to blow", blast via the Germanic abstract suffix -st-. The explosive senses follow the invention of gunpowder and its use in mining and war, while the blight sense is older, applied to crops "blasted" by a scorching wind.
