• Capers: Similar because both denote playful, energetic, or mischievous activities; differs because capers emphasizes leaping, dancing, or high-spirited physical escapades, often with an adventurous or illicit flavor.
  • Escapades: Similar because both involve unconventional or reckless actions; differs because an escapade is a distinct, often adventurous journey or undertaking that breaks free from restraint.
  • Pranks: Similar because both involve mischievous deeds; differs because a prank is a deliberate practical joke aimed at a specific victim, whereas antics are general displays of foolish or eccentric behavior.
  • Shenanigans: Similar because both refer to mischievous or deceitful carryings-on; differs because shenanigans is an informal colloquialism that often hints at secret scheming, underhanded maneuvering, or fraud.
  • Buffoonery: Similar because both denote ridiculous, clownish behavior; differs because buffoonery carries a stronger stigma of crude, undignified, or coarse clowning designed purely for cheap laughs.
  • Tomfoolery: Similar because both describe silly behavior; differs because tomfoolery is lighthearted, harmless, foolish nonsense without the grotesque or flamboyant theatricality often present in antics.
