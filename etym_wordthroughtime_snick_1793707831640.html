- 17th Century (Auditory) - A sharp, brief sound, typically from a mechanical device (e.g., a lock or latch). Connotation: Precision, security, completion of a small, functional action.
- 17th-18th Century (Physical) - A slight, precise cut or notch. Connotation: Deliberate, small-scale marking or incision, often for practical purposes.
- 19th Century onwards (Cricket) - A glancing blow off the edge of a cricket bat. Connotation: A subtle, often critical, and potentially game-changing interaction in sport, carrying high tension for players and spectators.
- Modern Usage - Retains all previous senses, used for precise mechanical sounds, small cuts, and specifically in cricket. Connotation: Specificity, precision, and brevity in describing subtle events.
