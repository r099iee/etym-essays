Proto-Algonquian Exonym (c. 1000 BCE)
*a·seni- / *a·senikāwa
"stone, rock / people of the stone"
↓
Mahican / Mohegan (Algonquian) (c. 16th c.)
Osininka / A·senika
"people of the standing rock"
[Exonym applied to their Iroquoian neighbors]
↓
Dutch Colonial Transcription (c. 1614)
Sinnekens / Sennecaas
"Dutch spelling of the Mahican exonym"
↓
English Colonial Adaptation (c. 1650s)
Seneca
"anglicized ethnonym for the Onöndowáʼga꞉"
[Phonetically reinforced by Latin Seneca, 'elder']
↓
Modern English (present)
Seneca
"the Seneca nation; Keepers of the Western Door"
