Note: Latin pinna is treated by de Vaan and Watkins as a variant of penna “feather” (from *pet-), though older scholarship derived it separately from a root meaning “sharp point”; the cushion derivation from coxa is the majority view, but a rival etymology from Latin culcita “mattress” is defended by some scholars.
