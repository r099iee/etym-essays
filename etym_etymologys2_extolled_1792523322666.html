Proto-Indo-European (reconstructed)
*telh₂-
"to bear, carry, lift, weigh"
(ancestor of Greek talanton "balance, talent", Latin tolerare "to endure", and Atlas)
↓
Proto-Italic (reconstructed)
*tol-n-ō
"to lift up, support, raise"
↓
Latin (3rd–2nd c. BCE)
tollere
"to lift, take up, raise, elevate"
↓
Latin (2nd–1st c. BCE)
extollere
"to lift up, raise high; praise, exalt, laud"
(compound of ex- "out, upward" + tollere "to lift")
↓
Middle English (late 15th c.)
extollen
"to lift up; exalt, praise highly"
↓
Modern English (16th c.–present, past participle)
extolled
"praised enthusiastically, exalted with high acclaim"
