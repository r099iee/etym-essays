• Attentive: Similar because it shares the core sense of heedful, observant focus; differs because it is the full modern neutral form, warmer and politer, lacking the clipped archaic hardness and predatory charge of ATTENT.
  • Alert: Similar because it means briskly awake and ready to react; differs because it stresses lively readiness to move rather than sustained stretched listening.
  • Watchful: Similar because it means keeping close guard over something; differs because it implies protective vigilance over time, while ATTENT is a momentary electric lock-on.
  • Vigilant: Similar because it means keenly wakeful against danger; differs because it is more formal and guardianship-oriented, less about hunger and aim.
  • Observant: Similar because it means quick to notice detail; differs because it is cooler and more intellectual, without the coiled bodily tension of ATTENT.
  • Heedful: Similar because it means paying careful heed; differs because it is archaic-moral, stressing obedience and caution rather than predatory poise.
  • Intent: Similar because it means fixed and purposeful, "intent upon" prey; differs because it stresses inward resolve more than outward sensory stretch.
  • Focused: Similar because it means concentrated on one point; differs because it is modern, neutral and optical, without the historical or fierce overtone.
  • Wary: Similar because it means alert to threat; differs because it implies fearful self-protection, while the thrush's ATTENT is confident aggression.
  • Expectant: Similar because it captures braced waiting for what comes; differs because it is softer and hopeful, missing the deadly eye.
  • Keen: Similar because it means sharp-edged perception and eagerness; differs because it is broader (keen eyes, keen hunter, keen interest) and less specifically about stretched stillness.
  • Rapt: Similar because it means wholly absorbed attention; differs because it suggests entranced wonder rather than triggered readiness to kill.
