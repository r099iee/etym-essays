• Anatolia: Similar because it denotes the same peninsula and is now the standard name for it; differs because it is a Greek-derived physical term (anatolē, “sunrise, east”) and in antiquity referred only to the eastern parts, whereas Asia Minor is the Latin historical label for the whole.
  • Asia: Similar because Asia Minor is the western peninsula of Asia and takes its first element from the continent's name; differs because Asia is the whole landmass, and in the Roman period the same word usually meant the province, not the continent.
  • Asia Major: Similar because it is the exact structural counterpart, “Greater Asia”; differs because it is an obsolete bookish label for the rest of the continent and never named a real administrative or cultural unit.
  • the province of Asia: Similar because it supplied the name and was the Roman administrative frame through which the region was governed; differs because it covered only the western third of the peninsula and was a legal district, not a geographical expression.
  • the Anatolian peninsula: Similar because it is a precise descriptive synonym; differs because it is a modern geographical description with no classical or biblical resonance.
  • Turkey: Similar because the Asian part of Turkey occupies the same land; differs because Turkey is a modern nation-state including Thrace in Europe, with political and cultural meanings that “Asia Minor” deliberately avoids.
  • the Near East: Similar because it is a conventional regional label covering the eastern Mediterranean; differs because it is far broader, extending to the Levant, Mesopotamia and Arabia.
  • Ionia: Similar because it names the Greek coastal strip that gave the peninsula much of its cultural prestige; differs because it is a small ethnic and geographical district, not the whole peninsula.
  • the Levant: Similar because it belongs to the same Mediterranean world and appears in the same historical narratives; differs because it denotes the eastern shore of the Mediterranean south of Cilicia, and excludes Anatolia entirely.
