• Optimistic: Similar because both characterize a positive expectation and hopeful outlook regarding future outcomes; differs because optimistic is a broad, everyday cognitive attitude or rational calculation that good will triumph, whereas sanguine conveys an ingrained, spirited, and often passionate warmth of temperament that remains buoyant even when outward evidence is discouraging.
  • Buoyant: Similar because both denote an uplifting, resilient emotional energy that refuses to be dragged down by despair; differs because buoyant emphasizes lightness, elasticity, and the capacity to float back up immediately after psychological distress, whereas sanguine highlights an underlying physiological warmth, hearty confidence, and courageous good humor.
  • Confident: Similar because both express certainty and freedom from debilitating doubt or fear; differs because confident centers primarily on firm reliance in one's own competence, another's reliability, or the certainty of a factual outcome, whereas sanguine emphasizes a radiant, sunny emotional state and hopeful disposition.
  • Hopeful: Similar because both look toward the future with positive anticipation; differs because hopeful can be hesitant, vulnerable, or passive (longing for a favorable outcome without certainty), whereas sanguine denotes a robust, active, and robustly assertive cheerfulness.
  • Roseate: Similar because both describe an outlook or physical complexion tinged with warm, rosy color; differs because roseate (or rosy) often implies a naive, unrealistically romanticized, or sentimentalized distortion of reality (as in viewing the world through "roseate glasses"), whereas sanguine represents constitutional vitality and emotional fortitude.
  • Phlegmatic: Similar historically because both represent fundamental humoral temperaments originating in ancient Galenic medicine; differs diametrically because phlegmatic denotes a cool, sluggish, impassive, and emotionally detached demeanor dominated by watery phlegm, whereas sanguine represents the opposite extreme of warm, lively, passionate, and outgoing blood-driven vitality.
