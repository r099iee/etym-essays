- c.1300 borow - rabbit-hole in warren accounts, utilitarian hunting word
- Late 1300s verb borowen - to dig such a hole, action and dwelling reinforce each other
- 1500s burrow spelling - extends to fox, badger, mole and human dugouts
- 1700s-1800s naturalists - precise warren vs sett vs earth distinctions, plus cosy literary homes
- Modern metaphor - to burrow into blankets, data and scandals, plus ecological engineer prestige
