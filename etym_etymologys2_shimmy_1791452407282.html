Note on the debate: the origin of Late Latin camisia is disputed. It may be a Germanic loan, as the reconstructed *hamithjo suggests, but it may equally be a Gaulish word adopted by Roman soldiers, and some handbooks leave the question open. The descent from camisia through Old French to English chemise, and thence to dialectal shimmy, is not in doubt.
