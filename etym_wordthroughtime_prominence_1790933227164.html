- Late 16th to early 17th Century: Emerged in English as a technical, physical, and topographical term denoting a physical projection, jutting cliff, or anatomical bulge standing outward from a surface.
- Mid-17th to 18th Century: Broadened through spatial metaphor into rhetoric, astronomy, and optics, describing features that stand out sharply against a visual background (such as solar prominences observed during eclipses).
- 19th Century: Solidified into social, professional, and cultural registers to denote high social standing, public renown, fame, and political influence.
- 20th to 21st Century: Expanded into media studies, cognitive psychology, typography, and search engine optimization (SEO), signifying information salience, visual hierarchy, top ranking, and headline positioning in digital interfaces.
