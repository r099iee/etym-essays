PIE (Proto-Indo-European) Substrate / Reduplicated Root (c. 3rd millennium BCE)
*pap- / *pa-pa-
"to swell, puff up; pap, gruel, swollen plant bulb"
Note: Regarded by historical linguists as an ancient Mediterranean substrate or onomatopoeic reduplication mimicking swelling seed-pods, or connected to infantile pap/swelling.
