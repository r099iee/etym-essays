Etymological Note: The transition from Latin restāre ("to remain standing, hold back") to Late Latin *arrestāre involved the prefixation of the directional particle ad- ("to, toward"). This shifted the intransitive sense of "staying in place" into a causative transitive sense: "causing someone or something else to stay in place / bringing another to a dead stop."
