- Fallible as liable to err - medieval theology: all humans can be deceived; humble and doctrinal sense
- Infallible as incapable of error - 14th-16th c. church claims for Pope and councils in faith and morals; lofty, contested, reverent or polemical
- Infallible as unfailing in secular use - 17th-18th c. extended to cures, instincts, judgements and machines; admiring but increasingly hyperbolic
- Infallibly as manner adverb - regularly, unfailingly, with comic certainty; from pulpit to drawing room
- Modern ironic use - will infallibly do equals is sure to go wrong unless money arrives; wry, worldly, acknowledging that human affairs fail even when doctrine does not
