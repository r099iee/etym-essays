PIE *nem- "to take, allot, deal out" ↓ Proto-Germanic *nemaną "to take" ↓ Old English niman "to take, seize, grasp" ↓ Old English nēmel "quick to seize, quick to grasp" ↓ Middle English nimele "quick to seize, nimble" ↓ English (16th c.) nimble "quick and light in movement or action; agile"
Note: Old English nēmel meant literally 'quick to seize' (from niman 'to take'); the modern 'quick, agile' sense generalizes that grasping, seizing sense.
