Note: The ultimate PIE root of Latin penus / penetrare is debated; the traditional “food / provisions” root *pen- is widely repeated in dictionaries, but some scholars regard the source as uncertain or as a separate homonymous root. The internal Latin derivation (penus → penitus → penetrare → penetrabilis → impenetrabilis) is secure.
