Expressive / Onomatopoeic Substrate (Northwestern European) (medieval period)
*trug- / *trugier / *strug-
"sound of heavy, dragging footsteps in mud; to tramp, wander, beg"
Note: Etymologists acknowledge uncertain origin. Likely an expressive phonetic creation mimicking heavy footfalls in mud (compare dialectal Scots trug, English tread/tramp), with possible influence from Old French trucher ('to beg, wander as a vagrant', of Celtic origin, from Gaulish *trug- 'wretched, pitiful').
