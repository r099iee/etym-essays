Proto-Chukotko-Kamchatkan (c. 1000 BCE)
*alyat- / *aləw-
"island, land in the sea"
[Also influenced by Chukchi/Koryak alí-ut "islanders"]
↓
Siberian Koryak / Aliutor (c. 17th c.)
alí-ut / aliat
"islanders, inhabitants of offshore islands"
[Alternative hypothesis: from Unangam Tunuu allíthuk "community, host"]
↓
Russian (c. 1750s)
алеут (aleut)
"native islander of the Aleutian chain"
[First recorded in Siberian promyshlenniki logs]
↓
Russian (plural) (c. 1760s)
алеуты (aleuty)
"Aleut people, islanders"
↓
French (c. 1778)
Aléoutes
"the Aleut people"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1784)
Aleutians / Aleuts
"inhabitants of the Aleutian archipelago"
↓
Modern English (present)
Aleuts
"Indigenous maritime people of the Aleutians"
