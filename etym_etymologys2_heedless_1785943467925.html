Note: the PIE ancestry of the root behind Germanic *hōdijaną is debated — the link to *kā- / *keu- "to perceive, heed" is speculative and uncertain. The suffix -less goes back to PGmc *-lausaz "loose, lacking" (ultimately PIE *lew- "to loosen, separate").
