- Straightforward speech vs verse - Classical Latin prosa oratio, neutral-technical distinction, prose as unbound direct discourse
- Monastic learned writing - Medieval Latin and Old High German prosa, chronicles and sermons, sober-utilitarian prestige
- Skill and courtly mastery - Medieval German kunst as knowledge and craft, admired accomplishment without fine-art autonomy
- Fine art with autonomy - 1790s Kant to Schiller, Kunst becomes purposeless beauty, enabling idea that prose itself could be art
- Philological prestige term - 1898 Norden Antike Kunstprosa onwards, ancient pedigree and taxonomy of periods and clausulae, specialist-reverent
- Living modernist practice - 1920s–1950s Thomas Mann and Hofmannsthal as masters, celebrated high style, then suspected mandarin ornament
- Borrowed rallying cry for information age - Late 20th-c. English stylistics imports Kunstprosa to defend style as infrastructure of attention, polemical-rehabilitative
