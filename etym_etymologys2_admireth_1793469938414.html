Note: The base verb descends from PIE *(s)mey- (the source of English smile and Latin miraculum), while the verbal inflection represents the ancient Proto-Indo-European primary active suffix *-eti, preserved as Old English -eþ and Early Modern English -eth before being supplanted by northern -s.
