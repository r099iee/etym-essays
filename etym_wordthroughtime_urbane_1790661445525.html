- Of the city - Latin urbanus as belonging to Rome
- City wit as norm - urbanitas meaning refined speech against rusticity
- Courtly politeness - French urbain and English urbane for polished persons
- Polite-letters ideal - Addisonian ease makes urbanity a prose virtue
- Extended to style - urbane essays, reviews, and conversation praised
- Media persona - urbane presenter as calm civilised mediator
- Ironic urbane - smoothness suspected as evasive or privileged detachment
