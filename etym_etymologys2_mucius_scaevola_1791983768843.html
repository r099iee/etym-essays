Note: the origin of Mucius is uncertain. It is generally thought to be Etruscan, like many Roman gentilicia of the early Republic, but no Etruscan antecedent is attested, and no Latin root yields a convincing sense; the resemblance to mūcus “mucus” or mucēre “to be mouldy” is accidental. The PIE stage for Scaevola is reconstructed from the comparison of Latin scaevus with Greek σκαιός; the exact preform is debated.
