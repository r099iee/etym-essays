Note: PIE *reup- to snatch or break is Pokorny's proposal, cf. Lithuanian raupti and Old Church Slavonic rupiti; the laryngeal shape *Hreup- is debated. The Germanic *raub- family is secure in OED and Etymonline: Gothic biraubon, Old High German biroubon, Old Saxon birobhon. The -t participle in bereft parallels lost, cleft, and reft.
