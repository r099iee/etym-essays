DESPATCH
"Brisk promptness and decisive speed in
getting business done; rapid, efficient
settlement or sending off of matters."
Debate: the route to English is uncertain — dispatch may come from Italian dispacciare, Spanish despachar, or French dépêcher, and the ultimate origin is itself debated (Latin dis- + pactum "agreement", or dis- + pedica "fetter" via Old French despeechier). despatch is a 17th-century British variant spelling.
