PRISMATIC
Greek (5th c. BCE)
πρίζειν prizein
"to saw"

↓
Greek (3rd c. BCE)
πρίσμα prisma
"something sawed,
a prism"

↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
prisma
"a prism"

↓
English (16th c.)
prism
"a solid with parallel,
similar ends"

↓
English (18th c.)
prismatic
"of or like a prism;
rainbow-coloured"

↓
English (present)
PRISMATIC
"having many colours
like a rainbow"

Note: A PIE *per- "to strike, cut" has been proposed behind Greek prizein "to saw", but this connection is uncertain; the name likely refers to something sawn or cut into facets.
