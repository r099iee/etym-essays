Note: In English, the adjective diffuse (/dɪˈfjuːs/) entered first in the late 14th century directly reflecting Latin diffūsus via Old French, while the verb diffuse (/dɪˈfjuːz/) was re-borrowed or formed directly from the Latin participial stem in the 1520s, leading to the distinct phonological voicing contrast between adjective and verb.
