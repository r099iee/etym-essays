commence

Note: Classical Latin lacked a verb *cominitiare; it used incipere or coepisse. The compound *cominitiare formed in Vulgar Latin by prefixing intensive/collective com- to initiare (to initiate, enter upon mysteries, from in-ire "to go into"). This replaced incipere across western Romance (Old French comencier, Italian cominciare, Spanish comenzar).
