- 19th-century coinage: to put into a capsule - literal pharmaceutical and botanical use, neutral technical register
- Late 19th–early 20th century: to wall off or sheathe - extended to pathology and engineering; protective, isolating connotation
- Mid-20th century: to summarize compactly - figurative leap into journalism and criticism; admiring connotation of pithiness
- Late 20th-century computing: data encapsulation - specialized OOP sense; positive connotation of clean modular design and information hiding
- Contemporary general use: to capture the essence - ubiquitous vogue word in media and academia; sometimes criticized as a cliché for any summary
