Note on the debate: Latin lūdus "game, play" has no securely established Indo-European etymology; a proposed PIE root *leyd- "to play" is hypothetical and not universally accepted. The chain therefore begins at attested Latin lūdus rather than at a reconstructed PIE root, respecting zero-guesswork. The prefix ad- "toward" combines with lūdere to give allūdere, with assimilation of d to l (the doubled l preserved in English spelling).
