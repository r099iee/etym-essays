Note: The prefix com- (from Latin cum, 'together, thoroughly') functions here as an intensive prefix, intensifying the base verb damnare from merely imposing a penalty to rendering an irrevocable, absolute judicial verdict. In Anglo-French and Middle English orthography, an intrusive unetymological -p- appeared between m and n (condempnacion) due to transitional bilabial plosion, which was discarded in Renaissance English spelling.
