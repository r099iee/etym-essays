• Aaron: Similar because brother, spokesman, and priest alongside Moses; differs because Aaron is priestly mediator, Moses is prophetic lawgiver.
  • Abraham: Similar because both are patriarchal founders of covenant; differs because Abraham founds by promise and migration, Moses by exodus and law.
  • David: Similar because both lead Israel and mediate divine will; differs because David is warrior-king, Moses is prophet unallowed to enter land.
  • Elijah: Similar because both are wonder-working prophets confronting kings; differs because Elijah works in Israel's kingdom, Moses in Egypt and wilderness.
  • Joshua: Similar because successor who completes entry; differs because Joshua conquers, Moses legislates and intercedes.
  • Muhammad: Similar because both are law-bearing liberators honoured in Islam; differs because Muhammad is final seal in Islamic theology, Moses is precursor.
  • Lawgiver: Similar because Moses archetypically gives law; differs because lawgiver is generic title also for Hammurabi or Lycurgus.
  • Liberator: Similar because Moses frees from bondage; differs because liberator is secular-political, Moses is theological.
  • Prophet: Similar because Moses is called prophet par excellence; differs because prophet is office, Moses is unique name and narrative.
  • Patriarch: Similar because both command ancestral reverence; differs because patriarchs are Genesis fathers, Moses is Exodus mediator.
  • Musa: Similar because Musa is Arabic form of same figure; differs because Musa signals Qur'anic register, Moses biblical English.
  • Moshe: Similar because Moshe is Hebrew original; differs because Moshe signals Jewish insider use, Moses Anglicised.
