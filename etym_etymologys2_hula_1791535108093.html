Proto-Polynesian (c. 1000 BCE)
*sula
"to perform a ritual dance or motion"
↓
Proto-Central Eastern Polynesian (c. 900–1200 CE)
*hula
"ritual dance accompanied by chant"
↓
Hawaiian (18th c.)
hula
"sacred dance, chant, and storytelling; to dance"
↓
English (early 19th c.)
hula
"Hawaiian traditional dance"

Note: Non-Indo-European; member of the Austronesian language family (Malayo-Polynesian branch). Entered English through accounts of Pacific voyagers and missionaries in the Hawaiian Islands.
