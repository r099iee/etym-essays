Note: Brun- as personal name Bruno is the standard view (Förstemann, Niemeyer); a minority well/spring (Brunn-) proposal is phonologically possible but lacks early spellings and is generally rejected. PIE *bher- to PGmc *brunaz is secure for the colour adjective; the further use as a name is onomastic, not purely phonological.
