=== ROOT 1: COB (SPIDER / HEAD) ===
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gēbh- / *gēb-
"head, gable, rounded top"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*kuppa- / *kobbō
"round vessel, bowl, head, lump"
↓
Old English (c. 850 CE)
copp / ātorcoppe
"top, head; venomous head (spider)"
↓
Middle English (c. 1300 CE)
coppe / cob
"spider; round creature"

=== ROOT 2: WEB (WOVEN FABRIC / NET) ===
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*webh-
"to weave, plait, braid"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*wabją
"web, woven fabric, net"
↓
Old English (c. 750 CE)
webb
"woven textile, tapestry, snare"
↓
Middle English (c. 1250 CE)
webbe / web
"woven cloth, spider's snare"

=== COMPOUND CONVERGENCE ===
Middle English Compound (c. 1325 CE)
coppeweb / cobwebbe
"spider-web, woven trap of a spider"
↓
Modern English (Attested Plural)
cobwebs
"spider webs, especially old, dusty networks"
