Etymological Note: The sound shift from Old English Granta- to Norman Cante- / Cam- is a classic instance of Anglo-Norman phonetic accommodation, as French scribes could not easily pronounce the initial consonant sequence /ɡr/ combined with the nasal /nt/. The river name Cam is a historical back-formation created centuries later from the city's altered name.
