Note on semantic preservation: The morphological architecture of Latin convocātiō has remained remarkably stable for over two millennia. The root *wekʷ- is one of the most prolific in Indo-European, generating Sanskrit vāc ("voice, speech"), Ancient Greek épos ("word, song, epic"), and Latin vōx ("voice"). While its sister derivative provocation shifted toward emotional incitement and evocation toward imaginative recall, convocation retained its original institutional sense: the literal, collective calling together of an authoritative body under a single roof.
