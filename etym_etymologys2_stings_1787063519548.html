Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*steh₁g- / *steg-
"to prick, pierce, sharp, pointed"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*stinganą
"to prick, stab, pierce with a sharp point"
↓
Old English (c. 8th c. CE)
stingan / stincg
"to prick, stab, pierce; sharp pointed organ, venomous bite"
↓
Middle English (c. 12th–15th c.)
stingen / stynge
"to pierce with a weapon or venomous organ; a puncture"
↓
Modern English (c. 16th c.–present)
stings
"venomous piercing organs; acute burning wounds; sharp weapons"
