- Latin ante / abante: before, in front - neutral preposition of space and time
- Old French avant: before, forward - everyday word plus military use for the forward division
- Middle English vantgard / vanguard: the army’s front - assimilated, warlike, concrete
- 19th-century French avant-garde reborrowed: artists as advance troops - polemical, utopian, self-mythologizing connotation under Saint-Simon and Marx
- 20th century: the avant-garde as institution - admiring then ambivalent; from insult to honour to cliché, with museums of the once-shocking
- Present clipped avant: shorthand for experimental chic - knowing, journalistic, sometimes ironic flavour
