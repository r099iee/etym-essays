• CUMBERSOME: Similar because both describe something heavy and awkward to deal with; differs because cumbersome stresses the burden or encumbrance of carrying, while unwieldy stresses size and awkward shape defeating the hands.
  • BULKY: Similar because both describe large physical size; differs because bulky merely means large, and a bulky object may still be perfectly manageable, whereas an unwieldy one resists handling.
  • CLUMSY: Similar because both mean awkward; differs because clumsy implies poor design or ungracefulness and applies to people as much as to things, while unwieldy is about sheer size and weight defeating control.
  • PONDEROUS: Similar because both mean heavy and slow; differs because ponderous leans toward slow, dull, laborious style and movement, while unwieldy stresses the specific awkwardness of handling.
  • UNMANAGEABLE: Similar because both mean difficult to control; differs because unmanageable covers anything that resists control (a crowd, an argument), while unwieldy specifically names size and weight defeating the hands.
  • UNGAINLY: Similar because both describe awkwardness of shape or movement; differs because ungainly stresses lack of grace and elegance, while unwieldy stresses difficulty of handling due to mass.
  • AWKWARD: Similar because both mean hard to deal with; differs because awkward is a broad word for clumsiness of many kinds, while unwieldy is specific to objects and systems too large or heavy to manage.
  • HEAVY: Similar because both describe great weight; differs because heavy is a neutral measure of weight, while unwieldy adds the difficulty of handling and control.
  • HULKING: Similar because both describe massive, awkward objects; differs because hulking stresses sheer bulk and clumsiness of presence, while unwieldy stresses the practical failure of manageability.
  • BALKY: Similar because both describe things that resist being directed; differs because balky describes a thing that refuses to work or move (a balky engine), while unwieldy describes a thing that cannot be easily handled at all.
