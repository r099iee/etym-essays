Note on the debate: The etymology of rouse is genuinely disputed. The Latin recusare → Old French reuser chain is the traditional account (and the source of English ruse), but some philologists doubt it because of the semantic jump from "refuse / drive back" to "shake feathers"; others propose an imitative origin or a Scandinavian source. The earliest secure English evidence is the falconry verb rousen "to shake the feathers", and any ancestor beyond it is reconstructed or contested.
