Note: The PIE root *peh₂w- ("few, little, small") was exceptionally generative across Indo-European languages. In Latin, it yielded paucus ("few"), paulus ("little", origin of the name Paul), and the compound pauper ("poor", literally "producing little"). In Greek, it produced pauros ("small, few") and pais ("child"). In Germanic, via Grimm's Law (*p -> *f), it yielded Gothic fawai, Old English fēaw, and Modern English few. Thus, Latinate paucity and native Germanic few are ultimate etymological cognates.
