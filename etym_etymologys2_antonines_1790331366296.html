Etymological Note: The root gentilicium Antonius is of pre-Roman Etruscan origin; a traditional Renaissance folk etymology once attempted to link it to Greek anthos ("flower") or to a mythical figure *Anto* (son of Hercules), but modern comparative linguistics confirms its Etruscan onomastic provenance.
