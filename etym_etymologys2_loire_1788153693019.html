*Ligēr / *Ligēra
"river name; perhaps ‘the one that flows’,
or ‘the muddy one’ (meaning debated)"
↓

Liger
"the river Loire"
↓
Old French(10th–12th c.)
Loire / Loyre
"the river Loire"
↓
Modern French / English(16th c. – )
Loire
"longest river of France; by extension,

Note on the debate (zero guesswork): The Gallic name behind Latin Liger is not fully recoverable. Some scholars derive it from a Celtic root meaning ‘the flowing one’, others compare it with words for ‘mud’ or set it beside the Welsh lli, ‘current, flood’; the pre-Roman form *Ligēr is a reconstruction, not an attested word.
