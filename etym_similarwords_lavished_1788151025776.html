• Showered: Similar because to shower praise on someone is to lavish it; differs in being a metaphor strictly of quantity falling at once, with no nuance of waste or of personal generosity.
  • Heaped: Similar as a metaphor of piling abundance (heaped with honours); differs in being physical and static, whereas lavished implies an active pouring out.
  • Poured: Similar as the closest kin image — water poured out without measure; differs in being broader (poured can be neutral: poured the tea), while lavished carries excess and generosity.
  • Bestowed: Similar in the sense of formally conferring (bestowed a knighthood); differs because bestow is ceremonious and controlled, without the flood-like excess of lavish.
  • Squandered: Similar as spending wastefully without stint; differs in moral tone — squandering is always condemnable waste, while lavishing can be admired (lavished love).
  • Wasted: Similar in the misallocation sense (intelligence wasted); differs by being purely negative, whereas lavished retains the double register of generous or wasteful.
  • Indulged: Similar in yielding abundantly to wants; differs in being about satisfying desires (indulged in luxuries), not about outpouring toward others.
  • Splurged: Similar as casual extravagant spending; differs in register — splurge is slangy and consumerist; lavished is dignified and can apply to immaterial gifts.
  • Spent: Similar in the basic act of expending; differs in being wholly neutral — all lavishing is spending, but most spending is not lavishing (amount, excess and generosity are the extra meanings).
