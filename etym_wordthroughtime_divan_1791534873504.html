- Late 16th c. – Middle Eastern administrative council - Evoked exotic oriental statecraft, diplomatic formality, and imperial bureaucracy of the Ottoman Empire and Persian court.
- Early 17th c. – Council chamber & architectural hall - Connoted grand government rooms surrounded by raised perimeter platforms and cushioned seating.
- Late 17th to 18th c. – Anthology of Middle Eastern verse - Associated with high literary culture, poetic registers, and Islamic lyrical traditions (e.g., the *Divan* of Hafez).
- Early 19th c. – Low, luxurious backless sofa - Shifted into Western domestic vocabulary, evoking romantic orientalism, bohemian indolence, and exotic aesthetic lounging.
- Mid-19th c. – Cigar salon or coffeehouse - Connoted masculine bohemian socializing, intellectual debates, and tobacco connoisseurship in Victorian London and Paris.
- 20th c. to Present – Upholstered bed base / functional furniture - Domesticated and standardized into commercial furniture terminology (divan bed), losing exotic associations in favor of utilitarian bedroom storage and comfort.
