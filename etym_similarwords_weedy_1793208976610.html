• Overgrown: Similar because both describe vegetation run riot beyond tending; differs because overgrown stresses size and neglect in general, while weedy stresses the low unwanted plants themselves — a garden can be overgrown with roses but never weedy with them.
  • Unkempt: Similar because both picture neglected, straggling growth; differs because unkempt applies mainly to hair, hedges, and appearances in need of combing or clipping, while weedy names the actual invasion of weeds.
  • Rank: Similar because rank growth is coarse, lush, over-luxuriant vegetation; differs because rank stresses excessive vigour and often bad smell, while weedy stresses the worthlessness of the plants, however thin they are.
  • Straggly: Similar because both evoke thin, irregular, untidy growth; differs because straggly describes the shape — scattered and uneven — while weedy describes the composition, ground full of weeds.
  • Scrawny: Similar because a scrawny person is thin and puny just like a weedy one; differs because scrawny stresses bony thinness of flesh, while weedy adds feebleness and lack of vigour, picturing someone who would snap in a wind.
  • Skinny: Similar because both mark thinness; differs because skinny is neutral description of low body fat, while weedy is a verdict — thin plus weak, and faintly contemptuous.
  • Puny: Similar because both mean small, weak, and unimpressive; differs because puny stresses smallness of size and strength in anything, while weedy pictures specifically the etiolated plant or youth, tall yet frail.
  • Spindly: Similar because spindly legs and stems are long, thin, and weak like a weed's; differs because spindly is about disproportion — long and thin like spider legs — while weedy is about sickliness and lack of substance.
  • Gangly: Similar because the gangly teenager is the weedy teenager's twin; differs because gangly is affectionate about awkward tall youth, while weedy doubts whether he will ever fill out or toughen up.
  • Frail: Similar because both suggest breakable weakness; differs because frail is delicate and often sympathetic — frail health, frail elderly — while weedy is dismissive, weakness you could have trained away.
