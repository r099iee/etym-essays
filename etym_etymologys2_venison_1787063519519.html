PIE (Proto-Indo-European) (c. 3500 BCE)
*wenh₁-
"to desire, to strive for, to wish, to win"
↓
Proto-Italic (c. 1000 BCE)
*wenā-je-
"to chase, to pursue, to hunt (literally 'to strive after')"
↓
Latin (c. 200 BCE)
venari
"to hunt, to pursue game"
↓
Latin (c. 50 BCE)
venatio
"hunting, the chase, game taken in hunting"
↓
Old French (c. 1100 CE)
veneison / venaison
"meat of game animals, the quarry of the hunt"
↓
Middle English (c. 1300 CE)
venesoun / venison
"flesh of beasts of the chase, especially deer"
↓
Modern English (Present)
venison
"the culinary meat of a deer"
