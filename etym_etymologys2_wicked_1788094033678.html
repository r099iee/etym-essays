Note on debate: the Origin of the adjective is uncertain. The connection to Old English wicca 'sorcerer' (hence 'one who deals with evil spirits') is the traditional account, but some scholars derive the Middle English wikke from Old Norse víkja 'to turn aside' (i.e. one who turns from the right path), or regard the Old English ancestry as unproven; the Oxford English Dictionary marks the origin as uncertain.
