- [16th century to write in] - [From the image of writing a name to a work; 'ascribe' first means to attribute a work, authorship, or date]
- [17th-18th century credit or impute] - [Extends to qualities, merits, and motives: to assign responsibility or credit to a cause or person]
- [19th century careful attribution] - [Refines into a formal, judicial word for reasoned attribution, especially in criticism and historiography]
- [20th century to present formal use] - [Thrives in academic and legal prose; less common in everyday speech than 'attribute' or 'credit', keeping its bookish, deliberate tone]
