Debate: Latin largus has no secure
PIE etymology — its origin is unknown.
The PIE *ambhi for by is a reconstruction
(reduced form *bhi). In the nautical
phrase, "by" = close to the wind and
"large" = off the wind, covering all
points of sailing; hence "on the whole".
