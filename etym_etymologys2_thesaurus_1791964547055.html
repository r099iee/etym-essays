Note on uncertainty

The origin of Greek thēsauros is disputed. The traditional derivation connects it with tithēmi, “to place, put” (PIE *dʰeh₁-), giving the sense “something laid down, a deposit”; this is semantically attractive but the second element, -sauros, has never been explained, and Beekes classifies the word as probably Pre-Greek. It is therefore presented above as a substrate word of unknown source rather than as a secure Indo-European derivative.
