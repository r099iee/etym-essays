• Cosmology: Similar because both concern the universe and its structure; differs because cosmology is the theoretical, mathematical study of the origin, evolution, and large-scale structure of the universe, while cosmography is the descriptive, cartographic account of its features.
  • Geography: Similar because both describe the surface of the world; differs because geography maps the earth alone, while cosmography embraces the heavens and the seas as well in one unified picture.
  • Astronomy: Similar because both treat the celestial bodies; differs because astronomy is the specialised quantitative science of the heavens, while cosmography is the broader, older descriptive whole that includes earth and sky together.
  • Cartography: Similar because both produce maps and charts; differs because cartography is the technical art of mapmaking, while cosmography is the comprehensive written-and-mapped description of the universe of which maps are one product.
  • Topography: Similar because both describe physical features of a place; differs because topography maps a local area's relief and detail, while cosmography takes the whole cosmos as its subject.
  • Geodesy: Similar because both concern the shape and measurement of the earth; differs because geodesy is a precise mathematical science of the earth's figure, while cosmography is descriptive and universal in scope.
  • Worldview / Weltanschauung: Similar because both can name a comprehensive picture of reality; differs because worldview is philosophical and subjective, while cosmography originally meant the physical, descriptive science of the world and heavens.
  • Mappa mundi / World map: Similar because both are products of cosmic description; differs because the mappa mundi is a single medieval artefact, while cosmography is the encompassing genre and discipline that produces such maps.
  • Encyclopaedia: Similar because both aim at comprehensive description; differs because the encyclopaedia covers all human knowledge, while cosmography covers the physical world and heavens specifically.
  • Ptolemaic system / System of the world: Similar because both describe cosmic order; differs because these name a particular astronomical model, while cosmography is the descriptive art that set that model in words and maps.
  • Astrography / Uranography: Similar because both are descriptive sciences of the heavens; differs because these technical modern coinages map the stars specifically, while cosmography unites heaven and earth in one older discipline.
