Note: OED states bag is of multiple origins, partly early Scandinavian (Old Icelandic baggi, Norwegian bagge bundle, plump person) and partly French bague (itself Germanic). OED rejects derivation from belly/bellows words as without evidence and rejects a Celtic origin as untenable; Etymonline agrees (probably Old Norse baggi). Further Scandinavian etymology is uncertain with no secure PIE root, so zero guesswork stops at baggi/bagge. The -y adjectival suffix is regular English.
