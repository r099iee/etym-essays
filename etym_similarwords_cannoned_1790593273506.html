• crashed: Similar because it denotes violent impact; differs because it implies damage and noise rather than elastic rebound.
  • collided: Similar because it means struck together while moving; differs because it is neutral and technical, lacking the cannonball imagery and comic force.
  • slammed: Similar because it conveys forceful hitting into something; differs because it stresses impact and sound, not rebound or hurtling motion.
  • ricocheted: Similar because it means bounced off after striking; differs because it emphasizes glancing deflection of a projectile, not a heavy body blundering in.
  • caromed: Similar because it is the billiards term for successive striking and rebound; differs because it is American and technical, while cannoned is British and more general.
  • barrelled: Similar because it describes rushing headlong into something; differs because it stresses uncontrolled forward motion rather than rebound after impact.
  • ploughed: Similar because it suggests driving into an obstacle with momentum; differs because it implies pushing through rather than bouncing off.
  • bumped: Similar because it means knocked against; differs because it is much weaker, small-scale and often harmless.
