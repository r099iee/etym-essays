SINISTER
↓
Latin (uncertain PIE origin)
sinister
"left, on the left; unlucky"
↓
Old French (12th c.)
senestre
"left, on the left side"
↓
English (15th c.)
sinister
"left; then evil, ominous"
Note: PIE origin unknown; the left

so 'left' came to mean 'unlucky'.
