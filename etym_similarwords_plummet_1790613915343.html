• plunge: Similar because it means to fall or dive steeply and suddenly; differs because plunge can be deliberate and into a medium (plunge into water/work), while plummet stresses helpless vertical free-fall.
  • nosedive: Similar because it denotes a steep headfirst drop; differs because nosedive is aviation slang implying head-down attitude, while plummet is general and weight-like.
  • crash: Similar because it means to fall violently with impact; differs because crash foregrounds destructive collision at the end, while plummet foregrounds the falling trajectory itself.
  • drop: Similar because it means to fall to a lower level; differs because drop is neutral and can be slight or controlled, while plummet is always steep, swift, and alarming.
  • tumble: Similar because it means to fall end over end; differs because tumble implies rotation and disorder, while plummet implies straight-line verticality.
  • sink: Similar because it means to descend to a lower level; differs because sink is gradual and often into liquid or gloom, while plummet is rapid through air or numbers.
  • collapse: Similar because it denotes sudden failure and fall; differs because collapse stresses structural giving-way, while plummet stresses trajectory from high to low.
  • slump: Similar because it means to fall in price, performance, or posture; differs because slump suggests sagging into a low heap, while plummet suggests arrow-like descent.
