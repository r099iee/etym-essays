Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*pleu-
"to flow, float, fly, swim"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*flutrōną / *flotōną
"to float repeatedly, drift hither and thither, toss on waves"
↓
Old English (c. 950)
floterian / flotorian
"to float about, flutter, be tossed by waves"
↓
Middle English (c. 1350)
floteren / fluteren
"to flutter, flap the wings, toss about, fluctuate"
↓
Modern English (present)
flutter
"to flap wings rapidly and lightly; a state of nervous agitation"
