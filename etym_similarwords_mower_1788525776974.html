• Reaper: Similar because both cut down agricultural vegetation during harvest; differs because a reaper specifically cuts mature grain or cereal crops (wheat, barley, rye), whereas a mower historically cuts grass, hay, and meadows, and in modern usage primarily refers to lawn-trimming machines.
  • Harvester: Similar because both gather mature crops from fields; differs because a harvester is a broader, comprehensive term denoting an individual or large multi-functional machine (like a combine) that cuts, threshes, and winnows entire crops, whereas a mower specifically cuts stems at the base.
  • Sickleman: Similar because both are manual agricultural laborers cutting vegetation with a bladed hand-tool; differs because a sickleman wields a short, hooked sickle with one hand while holding the stalks with the other, whereas a traditional mower wields a long two-handed scythe in wide standing sweeps.
  • Trimmer / Brushcutter: Similar because both cut down unwanted grass, weeds, and overgrowth; differs because a trimmer or brushcutter is a specialized handheld tool (often with a spinning nylon cord or metal blade) designed for edging, tight corners, and rugged brush, rather than broad, uniform surface cutting like a mower.
  • Scytheman: Similar because both refer to a person wielding a scythe; differs because 'scytheman' specifically highlights the weapon-like implement being carried, whereas 'mower' designates the functional role or action of grass-cutting and extends to mechanical machinery.
  • Combine (Combine Harvester): Similar because both are agricultural machines operating on standing crops; differs because a combine is a massive motorized industrial vehicle that combines cutting, threshing, and cleaning of grain in a single pass, far beyond the scope of a standard grass mower.
  • Lawn Mower: Similar because it is the primary modern mechanical realization of the word; differs because 'lawn mower' is a specific compound restricted to residential and municipal turf maintenance, whereas 'mower' retains both the historical human agent noun and heavy agricultural mowing implements.
