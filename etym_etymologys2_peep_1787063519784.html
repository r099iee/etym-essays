Debate / nuance: The look-word's ultimate origin is uncertain. It is a variant of the family behind peek and is probably an expressive or imitative formation linked to Dutch kēken 'to look'; it is not a regularly inherited Germanic word, so no earlier Indo-European stage can be confidently reconstructed.
