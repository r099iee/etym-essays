- [Rolling down] - "Devolve" (from the Latin for rolling down) begins literally meaning to roll or fall downward.
- [Transfer of power] - It shifts to legal and political use: to pass power, duties, or responsibility down to another body or level, as a sovereign or senate devolving authority upon subordinates.
- [Modern devolution] - Today the word anchors modern "devolution" — the transfer of central government powers to regional or local bodies — retaining the image of authority rolling downward from a higher seat.
