- [late Middle English, from Latin tumultus] - a violent commotion or disturbance, especially of a crowd; public uproar or even insurrection.
- [16th–18th century] - extended from external noise to inner states: mental and emotional agitation, confusion, and upheaval.
- [Contemporary] - still denotes a loud, confused noise made by a large mass of people, and figuratively any condition of churning disorder; intense and turbulent in register.
