Proto-Indo-European (Compound) (c. 3500 BCE)

Prefix: *uper
"over, above, beyond"
↓ Proto-Germanic *uber
↓ Old English ofer "over, above"

Base Verb: *legh-
"to lie down, recline"
↓ Proto-Germanic (causative) *lagjaną
"to cause to lie, place, set down"
↓ Old English leċġan (past leġde / ge-leġd)
"to lay, place, deposit"
↓
Old English compound (c. 9th c. CE)
oferleċġan
"to lay over, cover, place on top of"
↓
Middle English (c. 1380s)
overleyen (past participle overleid)
"to cover with a layer, plate with gold or silver"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 16th c.)
overlay (past participle overlaid)
"to spread over; to laminate or veneer"
↓
Modern English (present)
overlaid
