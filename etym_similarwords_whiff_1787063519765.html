• Puff: Similar because both name a short burst of air, smoke or vapour; differs because puff is bigger and more deliberate (a puff of smoke from a chimney), while whiff stresses faintness and is far more often used of scent.
  • Scent: Similar because both can denote a smell reaching the nose; differs because scent names the odour itself (and is used of animals' scent trails), while whiff names the brief moment of perceiving it.
  • Smell: Similar because both relate to olfaction; differs because smell is the general, neutral word for any odour and is also the verb, while whiff is specific to a faint, fleeting encounter.
  • Gust: Similar because both denote a burst of moving air; differs because gust implies a strong, noticeable blast of wind, while whiff implies a light, barely-there current.
  • Breath: Similar because both can mean a barely perceptible amount of air; differs because breath is tied to lungs and respiration (and metaphorically to life), while whiff is about wind, smoke or scent.
  • Trace: Similar because both denote a very small or faint amount of something; differs because trace is purely quantitative and abstract (a trace of iron in water), while whiff remains tied to air, smell and momentary perception.
  • Hint: Similar because both can mean a subtle suggestion of something; differs because hint is abstract and often intentional (a hint of a smile), while whiff implies something sensory or atmospheric that is faintly perceptible.
  • Waft: Similar because both denote a gentle movement of air carrying a smell; differs because waft is more deliberate and poetic, emphasizing the slow carrying of the scent rather than the brief encounter with it.
  • Sniff: Similar because both involve taking in a small amount of air through the nose; differs because sniff is an action (verb and noun), while whiff is what is encountered, not the act of sampling it.
  • Tinge: Similar because both denote a slight admixture or flavour of something; differs because tinge is usually visual or emotional (a tinge of regret), while whiff is olfactory or atmospheric.
