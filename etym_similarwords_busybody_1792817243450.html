• Meddler: Similar because both interfere in others' affairs; differs because meddler is the plain general term, while busybody adds a comic picture of bustling officiousness.
  • Nosy parker: Similar because both pry into what is not their business; differs because nosy parker stresses curious snooping, while busybody stresses active interference.
  • Interferer: Similar because both get involved uninvited; differs because interferer is rare and neutral, while busybody is the idiomatic, mildly contemptuous everyday word.
  • Gossip: Similar because busybodies typically spread what they learn; differs because gossip names tale-bearing talk itself, while busybody names the interfering person.
  • Snoop: Similar because both pry into private matters; differs because snoop implies secretive spying, while busybody interferes openly and officiously.
  • Telltale: Similar because both report others' doings; differs because telltale is usually a child who informs on peers, while busybody is typically an adult managing neighbours.
  • Officious person: Similar because both offer unwanted help and supervision; differs because officious is formal and stresses asserting authority, while busybody is homely and stresses curiosity.
  • Blabbermouth: Similar because both cannot keep out of others' affairs; differs because blabbermouth cannot keep secrets, while busybody cannot keep out, talk versus interference.
