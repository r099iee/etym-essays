Note: While the primary derivation stems from Latin bracchium ("arm", through diminutive brāchītella / bracellum referencing arms crossed in prayer), a well-known folk etymology proposes Medieval Latin pretiola ("little rewards"), supposedly given by monks to children learning their prayers. Historical linguistic evidence decisively supports the bracchium lineage via Old High German brezitella, with the initial p- reflecting Upper German (Bavarian-Austrian and Palatine) consonantal devoicing.
