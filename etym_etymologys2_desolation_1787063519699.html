Latin (origin uncertain)
sōlus
alone, solitary, abandoned
↓
Latin (1st c. BCE)
dēsōlāre
to make quite alone, to abandon, to lay waste
↓
Late Latin (4th c. CE)
dēsōlātiō
a leaving alone, a forsaking, desolation
↓
Old French (12th c.)
desolacion
desolation, devastation
↓
Middle English (14th c.)
desolacioun
devastation, abandoned condition
↓
English (present)
Desolation
devastated wasteland; profound abandonment

Note: The origin of Latin sōlus
is unknown; some scholars suggest
an Etruscan or Italic source, but
the root cannot be securely traced
beyond Latin.
