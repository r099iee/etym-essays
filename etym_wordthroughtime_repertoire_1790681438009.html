- Latin repertorium - inventory or list of things found, legal and administrative finding aid
- French répertoire - alphabetized index, directory of addresses and plays, 15th-17th c. reference tool
- 19th c. theatrical borrowing - company stock of performable pieces, repertory theatre in rotation
- Early 20th c. musical extension - singer or instrumentalist repertoire as mastered works
- Mid-20th c. social science metaphor - role repertoire, verbal repertoire, behavioral repertoire, dramaturgy of Goffman
- Contemporary general skill sense - repertoire of strategies, antibodies and responses, any ready-to-perform stock
