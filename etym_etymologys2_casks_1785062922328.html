Debate: The Spanish origin is probable but debated: English cask (16th c.) may come from Spanish casco, perhaps via French casque "helmet, skull". The semantic shift "skull / broken fragment" → "barrel" is not fully explained, and the link of casco to Latin quassare (frequentative of quatere) is uncertain.
