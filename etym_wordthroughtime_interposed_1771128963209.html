- Physical spatial sense - first signified placing one object physically between two others, a purely positional act of being 'in the middle'.

- Legal & formal extension - shifts to inserting a document, objection, or plea between procedural steps, gaining a measured, procedural gravity.

- Conversational & social sense - moves from things to persons, meaning to step into a discussion to clarify or correct, shedding any sense of rudeness and taking on the tone of a deliberate mediator.

- Contemporary sense - a cool, authoritative verb for strategically placing one's voice or presence across a gap in understanding, implying guidance and course-correction rather than mere interruption.
