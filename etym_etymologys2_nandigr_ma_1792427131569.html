[Root Word 1: nandi (delight/joy)]
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*ned- / *nend-
"to rejoice, be glad, celebrate"
↓
Proto-Indo-Iranian (c. 2000 BCE)
*nand-
"to rejoice, take delight in"
↓
Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE)
nándati (नन्दति)
"he rejoices, delights, prospers"
↓
Sanskrit (nominal derivative) (c. 1000 BCE)
nandi- (नन्दि-)
"joy, delight, prosperity; rejoicing one"

[Root Word 2: grāma (village/community)]
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*gʷrém-o- / *gʷrem-
"heap, gathering, assembly, troop"
(source of Slavic gromada "crowd, heap")
↓
Proto-Indo-Iranian (c. 2000 BCE)
*gráHmas
"mobile clan, troop of warriors, settlement"
↓
Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE)
grā́ma (ग्राम)
"host of men, pastoral clan; rural settlement"
↓
Classical Sanskrit (compound toponym) (c. 500 BCE – Present)
Nandigrāma
"village of joy; holy retreat of Bharata near Ayodhya"
