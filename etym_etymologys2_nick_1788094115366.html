Note on the debate: The OED classes the origin of nick "notch" as uncertain. The Romance chain above (via niche, ultimately Latin nīdus "nest") is followed by the American Heritage Dictionary, but the phonetic bridge (niche → nick) is disputed. The main rival theory derives nick from Middle Dutch nik / Middle Low German nicke "notch, groove, crack", a word of obscure deeper origin; if that theory is right, no PIE ancestor can be reconstructed. The idiom "in the nick of time" (1580s "in the nick") probably reflects the notches of a tally stick marking time.
