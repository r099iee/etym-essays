Note: The word is historically a plural ("pledges"); modern truce results from re-reading the -s as an ordinary plural ending in the 16th century, exactly as pea came from pease. Cognates include Gothic triggwa "covenant" and German Treue "fidelity"; true and troth share the same Germanic base.
