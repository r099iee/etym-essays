• Nargothrond: Similar because both are the great hidden Elven realms of the First Age, destroyed by dragons (Glaurung) or goblins; differs because Nargothrond was hollowed beneath the hills by Finrod, while Gondolin was a city of white stone built above ground within encircling mountains.
  • Citadel: Similar because both denote a fortified stronghold; differs because citadel is a generic functional term for a defended place, without Gondolin's mythic history and doomed beauty.
  • Stronghold: Similar because both name a defended place; differs because stronghold names mere function and could describe any fortress, while Gondolin names one irreplaceable, legend-freighted city.
  • Valinor: Similar because both are Elven realms of supreme renown; differs because Valinor is the immortal land of the Valar across the sea, undestroyed, whereas Gondolin was a hidden, mortal-built city that fell.
  • Khazad-dûm: Similar because both are great cities of ancient Middle-earth renowned for their craft; differs because Khazad-dûm was an underground Dwarven realm dug for treasure, not a concealed Elven city raised in secret.
