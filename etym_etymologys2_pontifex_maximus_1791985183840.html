Two components, each with its own history. In pontifex the second element -fex is securely from facere “to make”; the first, pons “bridge”, is disputed in origin and the “bridge-maker” sense is a tradition, not a proof.

Debate: Varro's derivation from pons + facere is formally sound but semantically unproved; the bridge over the Tiber and its rites support it, while an Etruscan origin for pons and a lost sense “path” have both been proposed. No secure pre-Latin etymon can be cited.
