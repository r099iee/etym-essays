Note: Proto-Germanic heringaz is widely cited but its deeper Indo-European source is unknown; no secure PIE root is asserted. Folk links to here (army, shoal as army) or hoary (grey) are unproven and treated as conjectural. The Old English, Norse, Dutch haring, and German Hering forms are certain and continuous.
