• Guessed: Similar because both mean formed an opinion without certain evidence; differs because guessed is the loose, ordinary word with no implication of method, whereas surmised suggests a more perceptive, intuitive leap.
  • Conjectured: Similar because both mean formed a hypothesis without proof; differs because conjectured is heavier and more intellectual, implying deliberation over possibilities, whereas surmise moves swiftly and sympathetically.
  • Inferred: Similar because both mean reached a conclusion from indications; differs because inferred claims the conclusion follows from evidence by reasoned argument, while surmise makes no such claim, moving instead by intuition.
  • Deduced: Similar because both mean reached a conclusion from given facts; differs because deduced claims logical necessity, proceeding by strict reasoning from premises, while surmise is a lighter, more intuitive leap on slender indications.
  • Suspected: Similar because both mean believed without certainty; differs because suspected carries distrust and a negative flavour, utterly foreign to the warm, affectionate insight that surmise can convey.
  • Presumed: Similar because both mean taken as true without full proof; differs because presumed is the legalistic relative, a supposition accepted as true until disproved, whereas surmise is a tentative, intuitive guess.
