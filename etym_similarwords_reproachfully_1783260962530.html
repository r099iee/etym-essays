• Accusingly: Similar because both express blame; differs because accusingly implies a specific charge levelled at someone, while reproachfully is vaguer but weightier — a pointing-out rather than a named accusation.
  • Reprovingly: Similar because both denote correction; differs because reprovingly suggests the mild, corrective tone of a teacher or parent correcting an error, far gentler than reproach's sting of blame and disappointment.
  • Censoriously: Similar because both are critical of faults; differs because censoriously stresses harsh judgement of opinion or conduct, whereas reproachfully carries a moral sting of blame and disappointment.
  • Contemptuously: Similar because both express strong disapproval; differs because contemptuously adds disdain and personal superiority without any moral claim, while reproachfully presupposes a violated standard of right.
  • Disapprovingly: Similar because both express disapproval; differs because disapprovingly is the neutral, everyday word, lacking reproachfully's literary force and sense of grievance.
  • Scoldingly: Similar because both express blame; differs because scoldingly implies vocal, lecturing reproof, while reproachfully can be silent — a look, a sigh, a gesture.
  • Upbraidingly: Similar because both mean with reproach; differs because upbraidingly is rarer and more archaic, strictly verbal censure, without reproachfully's expressive breadth.
  • Admonishingly: Similar because both express correction; differs because admonishingly is instructive and mildly warning, closer to advice than to the moral rebuke of reproachfully.
  • Critically: Similar because both evaluate fault; differs because critically is analytic and cool (a critical review), without reproachfully's personal, moral colour.
  • Judgementally: Similar because both express censure; differs because judgementally stresses passing judgement on another and is often negatively coloured, while reproachfully is more dignified and specific.
  • Disparagingly: Similar because both belittle or blame; differs because disparagingly lowers the worth of a person or thing, while reproachfully charges with a fault against a standard.
  • Rebukingly: Similar because both express sharp disapproval; differs because rebukingly is sharper and more public, a direct reprimand, while reproachfully can be a quiet look of disappointment.
  • Chidingly: Similar because both express mild blame; differs because chidingly is gentle, almost affectionate scolding, lighter than reproachfully's moral weight.
