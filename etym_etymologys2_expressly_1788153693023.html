Note on the debate (zero guesswork): The deeper root of Latin premere is disputed — often set to PIE *per- ‘to strike’ with an m-extension, but sometimes to a root *pres-; no attested cognate family settles it. The chain from *per- is therefore a scholarly reconstruction, not a documented word.
