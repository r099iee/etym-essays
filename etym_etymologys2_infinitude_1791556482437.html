Early Modern English (c. 1610s)
infinitude
"state or quality of being infinite"
↓
English (modern)
infinitude
"boundless extent or condition, infinity"

Note: English *infinitude* was coined during the early 17th century by attaching the abstract Latinate nominal suffix *-ude* (from Latin *-udo*, stem *-udin-*, found in *solitude*, *fortitude*, *multitude*) directly to the stem of Latin *infinitus* ("unbounded"), serving as a poetic and philosophical counterpart to *infinity* (borrowed earlier from Old French *infinité*).
