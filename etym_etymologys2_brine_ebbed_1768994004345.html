BRINE
↓
Proto-Germanic (reconstructed; uncertain)
*brīną
"brine, salt water"
↓
Old English (c. 9th c.)
brīne
"brine, salt water"
↓
English (present)
brine
"highly concentrated
salt water"

Debate: the PIE origin is
uncertain. Candidates include
PIE *bʰrey- "to cut, ferment"
or *gʷrī-n- "heavy"; a substrate
origin is also possible.

ebb

uncertain; ebb is cognate with
