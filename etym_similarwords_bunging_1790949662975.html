• Chucking: Similar because both denote throwing or tossing an object casually, informally, or without careful aim; differs because 'chucking' is slightly broader in global English and often implies discarding or casually passing something to someone, whereas 'bunging' (predominantly British and Australian) often conveys a careless, hasty, or forceful stuffing/hurling action.
  • Hurling: Similar because both involve propelling an object through the air; differs because 'hurling' conveys intense physical force, great speed, or dramatic anger, whereas 'bunging' is colloquial, mundane, or moodily casual.
  • Flinging: Similar because both describe throwing with a swift, unrestrained arm movement; differs because 'flinging' emphasizes emotional abandon, sudden impulse, or romantic/dramatic flair, whereas 'bunging' is gritty, informal everyday slang.
  • Plugging: Similar because in its mechanical sense, 'bunging' means stopping up a hole; differs because 'plugging' is the universal technical and standard term for sealing any hole or pipe, whereas 'bunging' specifically relates to casks, barrels, or stopper holes (or informal stuffing).
  • Lobbing: Similar because both describe tossing a projectile; differs because 'lobbing' specifically implies throwing something in a high, arcing trajectory at relatively low velocity, whereas 'bunging' does not specify trajectory.
  • Pitching: Similar because both refer to throwing an object; differs because 'pitching' implies deliberate delivery, controlled aim, or baseball/cricket mechanics, whereas 'bunging' denotes careless or distracted tossing.
  • Slapping: Similar because both can describe hurriedly placing or applying something; differs because 'slapping' implies a flat physical impact against a surface (e.g. slapping paint on a wall), whereas 'bunging' emphasizes throwing, tossing, or stuffing.
  • Bribing: Similar because in political and underworld British slang, 'bunging' means giving illicit money or backhanders; differs because 'bribing' is the formal legal term for corrupt payment, whereas 'bung' is street and tabloid slang.
