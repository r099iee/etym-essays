• Boor: Similar because both imply a lack of refinement; differs because 'boor' emphasizes crude manners and social awkwardness, while 'Philistine' specifically targets cultural and intellectual insensitivity.
  • Vulgarian: Similar because both suggest a lack of taste and refinement; differs because 'vulgarian' often implies an ostentatious display of wealth without genuine taste, whereas 'Philistine' is broader, encompassing indifference to high culture and intellectual pursuits.
  • Ignoramus: Similar because both imply a lack of knowledge; differs because 'ignoramus' simply means an ignorant person, while 'Philistine' implies a *willful* or *active* disdain for intellectual and artistic pursuits, not just a lack of knowledge.
  • Materialist: Similar because both prioritize material possessions; differs because 'materialist' focuses on the pursuit of wealth and goods, while 'Philistine' extends to a broader indifference or hostility to non-material, cultural, and intellectual values.
  • Lowbrow: Similar because both describe a lack of sophisticated taste; differs because 'lowbrow' is more descriptive of preferences (e.g., preferring popular entertainment over high art), while 'Philistine' carries a stronger judgmental connotation of active disdain or smug indifference to high culture.
