Middle English (14th c.) shoggen "to shake, move with jerks" ↓ Middle English (possibly aphetic) joggen "to shake, jog, jolt" ↓ English jog "to move with a jolting, shaking pace" ↓ English (18th c.) jig-jog "a jolting, jogging pace" ↓ English jigjog "a jerky, bouncing motion or pace"
Note: an echoic, reduplicative formation (jig + jog); the origin of jog itself is uncertain — possibly aphetic of Middle English shoggen 'to shake, jog'.
