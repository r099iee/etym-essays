Note: bilge is generally taken as a 16th-century dialectal or nautical variant of bulge (vowel alternation u → i), not a separate borrowing; some older dictionaries suggested direct Old French or Middle Dutch influence, but the bulge-variant account is now standard. The slang “nonsense” sense is a 20th-century shortening of bilge water.
