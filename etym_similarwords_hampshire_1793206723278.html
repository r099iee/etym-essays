• Wessex: Similar because both name the same ancient south-western heartland of Alfred and his heirs; differs because Wessex is the vanished Saxon kingdom — and Hardy's literary region — while Hampshire is the living county inside it.
  • Wiltshire: Similar because it is the chalk-down neighbour sharing Salisbury Plain, Stonehenge country, and sheep-and-corn farming; differs because Wiltshire centres on Salisbury and Swindon, while Hampshire centres on Winchester and the Solent ports.
  • Dorset: Similar because it is the next shire west along the Channel, equally rural and literary; differs because Dorset is Hardy's thatched cottage country round Dorchester, while Hampshire is Austen's and Cobbett's, with the great naval harbours Dorset lacks.
  • Sussex: Similar because it is the next shire east along the coast, downland and Weald to the sea; differs because Sussex looks to Brighton and Chichester, while Hampshire looks to Southampton, Portsmouth, and the New Forest.
  • Berkshire: Similar because it marches with Hampshire on the north and shares the downs and the Thames valley fringe; differs because Berkshire is Windsor-and-Reading royal river country, while Hampshire is the down-and-harbour county of Wessex.
  • Kent: Similar because both are great southern counties of corn, hops, ports, and invasion history; differs because Kent is the garden county facing France across the Strait, while Hampshire faces the Atlantic approaches and guards the western Solent.
  • Surrey: Similar because it neighbours Hampshire on the east with heath, pine, and commuter villages; differs because Surrey is the stockbroker belt pressed against London, while Hampshire keeps far more open downland and forest.
  • Winchester: Similar because the city is the historic soul of the county, its capital and cathedral town; differs because Winchester is one city within the shire, while Hampshire is the whole sweep from Andover to the sea.
  • The New Forest: Similar because it is Hampshire's most famous wild tract, ponies, deer, and ancient oaks; differs because the New Forest is one district of heath and woodland, while Hampshire is the county that holds it with much else besides.
