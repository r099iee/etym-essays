Debate note: The word is first attested in Latin as catacumbas, and is very probably of Greek origin: Greek κατακύμβας (katakýbas), literally "near the hollows", was a place-name applied to the cemetery district below Rome. The element κύμβη (kýmbē) "a hollow, bowl" is generally considered Pre-Greek (Beekes).
