Note: origin uncertain. The older noun lumber "disused household goods, a pawnbroker’s store" may derive from Italian lombardo "a pawnbroker", the Lombards being the great medieval moneylenders and pawnbrokers; this is unproven. The verb lumber "to move heavily" and the timber sense are related extensions.
