- Old English swift / swifan - turning, sweeping quickly; motion with curve; physical
- Middle English swift (adj.) - rapid, prompt, ready; surname by 1150s; everyday speed
- 15th c. Scottish ydy / eddy - whirlpool turning back; fishermen's word; local and watery
- 1660s swift (bird) - sickle-winged screamer named for speed; ill-omened, summer
- 1730–1810 to eddy / eddying - verb and participle spread south; smoke, crowds, and thought eddy; generalising
- Victorian swift-eddying (Ruskin) - garden-door Tay, clear-brown, infinite to child; literary, precise, wondrous
- Modern technical eddy - fluid dynamics, turbulence, and climate models; scientific, while swift-eddying stays poetic
