Branch 1: bleary
Proto-Indo-European (c. 3000 BCE)
*bʰle-
"to blow, swell, weep, bleat"
↓
Proto-Germanic (c. 500 BCE)
*blērjanan
"to weep, shed tears, blur the eyes"
↓
Middle Low German / Middle Dutch (c. 12th c.)
blerren / bleren
"to bleat, cry, shed tears, blur"
↓
Middle English (c. 13th c.)
bleren / blere
"to have watery, dim, rheumy eyes"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 16th c.)
bleary
"dimmed with tears or rheum, watery"
