• Offered: Similar because both mean presenting something for acceptance; differs because offer is the broad, neutral default (a cup of tea or an opinion), while proffered is more formal, physical, and ceremonious — held out in the hand.
  • Extended: Similar because both mean holding something out; differs because extended is general (an arm, a leg, a period), while proffered specifically means presenting an object for acceptance with courtesy.
  • Tendered: Similar because both are formal words for presenting; differs because tendered is chiefly legal and commercial (tender payment, tender a resignation), while proffered is courtly and physical.
  • Presented: Similar because both mean giving or showing something; differs because presented is broader (a lecture, an award, a bill), while proffered implies holding it out in the open hand for the other to take.
  • Submitted: Similar because both offer something for acceptance; differs because submitted implies documents or proposals handed upward for judgement, while proffered is equal and physical.
  • Held out: Similar because both describe extending something in the hand; differs because held out is plain, neutral description, while proffered adds formality and anticipatory courtesy.
  • Volunteered: Similar because both give something unsolicited; differs because volunteered applies to offers of help or information (often verbal), while proffered is a physical object held out.
  • Handed: Similar because both mean physically giving something; differs because handed is casual and neutral, while proffered is ceremonious and anticipatory.
  • Bestowed: Similar because both mean giving something to another; differs because bestowed implies a gift given from above or by one in authority, while proffered is a horizontal, courteous gesture between equals or a servant's dutiful act.
  • Suggested: Similar because both may present something for consideration; differs because suggested is strictly verbal and tentative, while proffered involves the gesture of holding something out.
