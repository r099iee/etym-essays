• bewildered: Similar because both denote mental confusion; differs because "bewildered" stresses losing one's bearings entirely (as in a wild, tangled place), while "baffled" adds a sense of active defeat by an obstacle.
  • perplexed: Similar because both mean puzzled; differs because "perplexed" is slightly formal and stresses the knottiness of the problem, "baffled" its frustrating check.
  • puzzled: Similar because both mean unable to make sense of something; differs because "puzzled" is milder and more neutral, "baffled" implies a harder, more defeating mystery.
  • confounded: Similar because both mean thrown into confusion; differs because "confounded" is emphatic, often humorous or archaic, and once meant "damned" (a confounded nuisance).
  • mystified: Similar because both mean rendered unable to understand; differs because "mystified" implies the presence of a mystery, "baffled" a block to comprehension.
  • confused: Similar because both denote mental muddle; differs because "confused" is general (and can concern identity or direction), while "baffled" is about an obstacle to comprehension.
  • flummoxed: Similar because both mean utterly at a loss; differs because "flummoxed" is informal or jocular, "baffled" more neutral.
  • nonplussed: Similar because both mean rendered unable to respond; differs because "nonplussed" is often misused to mean unimpressed, while "baffled" is unambiguous.
  • stumped: Similar because both mean brought to a stop by a problem; differs because "stumped" is informal and concrete (as of a tree stump blocking a path).
  • at a loss / lost: Similar because both mean unable to proceed or understand; differs because these are phrases describing a state, "baffled" the participle of a defeating act.
  • thwarted: Similar because both mean prevented from achieving a purpose; differs because "thwarted" is purely about obstruction, with no cognitive confusion.
  • frustrated: Similar because both mean prevented from succeeding; differs because "frustrated" adds annoyance and can be emotional, "baffled" about defeat by confusion.
  • foiled: Similar because both mean stopped or turned back; differs because "foiled" describes outward defeat of a plan, "baffled" often an inner mental check.
  • defeated / checked: Similar because both mean overcome or stopped; differs because these describe military or competitive defeat, while "baffled" is usually intellectual.
  • dumbfounded: Similar because both describe astonishment combined with confusion; differs because "dumbfounded" emphasizes surprise (being struck speechless), "baffled" the frustration of an unsolved problem.
  • flabbergasted: Similar because both denote being astounded into confusion; differs because "flabbergasted" is jocular and about sheer surprise, "baffled" about the puzzle itself.
  • bamboozled: Similar because both denote being confused; differs because "bamboozled" implies deception or hoax, "baffled" genuine mystery.
  • boggled: Similar because both mean made unable to take something in; differs because "boggled" suggests the mind's sheer refusal under enormity, "baffled" a clean obstacle.
