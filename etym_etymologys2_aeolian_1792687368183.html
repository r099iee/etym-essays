Note on historical debate: Philologists debate whether the proper name Aiolos (Αἴολος) derives directly from the PIE root of blowing (*h₂wéh₁-) via an archaic zero-grade extension, or whether it was originally an expressive descriptive adjective αἰόλος ("nimble, shifting, glistening", applied to snakes, insects, and changing winds) that was subsequently personified in myth. Leading Hellenic etymologists (such as Pierre Chantraine and Robert Beekes) favor the derivation from descriptive αἰόλος ("rapid, variable"), which itself may be an Indo-European formation reflecting the kinetic fluttering of air currents. In all branches, the semantic connection to the rapid variability of wind remains undisputed.
