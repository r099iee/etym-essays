Debate note: the pre-Scandinavian ancestry is uncertain. The link to the PIE scatter-sprinkle base *(s)p(h)er(e)g- (family of speckle) is plausible but not certain; the OED labels the older origin obscure. The Norse and English stages are solidly attested.
