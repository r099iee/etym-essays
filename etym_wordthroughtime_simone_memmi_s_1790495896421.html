- Hebrew Shimon, he who hears, God has heard; tribal patriarch, earnest and theocratic
- Greek Symeon and Simon, Septuagint and New Testament apostles and zealots; reverent, scriptural
- Latin Simon and Simeon, Vulgate saint's name, popes and martyrs; universal Christian
- Italian Simone, vernacular forename in Dante's age; chivalric and civic, masculine see-MO-neh
- c. 1284 Simone son of Martino born in Siena, apprentice to Duccio; artisanal promise
- 1315-1344 Simone master, Maesta, Assisi, Avignon, Petrarch's Laura; courtly, golden, European fame
- Memmo, pet form of Guglielmo and William, Tuscan workshop nickname; familiar and familial
- Memmi patronymic, sons and descendants of Memmo, Lippo Memmi; guild-solidarity marker
- 1550 Vasari conflates Simone with Memmi, Lives calls him Simone Memmi; authoritative error
- 19th-century English Simone Memmi, Lindsay, Jameson, Ruskin use Vasarian form; romantic, Pre-Raphaelite idol
- Modern correction to Simone Martini, archival research restores Martini; scholarly and precise, Memmi retained for Lippo
- English possessive -es to 's, Old English stanes to King's, Memmi's; from inflection to clitic, now stylistic attribution
