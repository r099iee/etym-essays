Proto-Indo-European (c. 3500 BCE)
*ned- / *ned-o-
"to bind, knot together; reed, rush"
(source of Latin nodus "knot")
↓
Proto-Indo-Iranian (c. 2000 BCE)
*nadás
"reed, water-rush, marsh stalk"
↓
Vedic Sanskrit (c. 1500 BCE)
nadá- / nala- (नद / नल)
"reed, cane, hollow aquatic stalk"
(liquid shift d/r/l characteristic of Indo-Aryan)
↓
Epic Sanskrit (c. 500 BCE)
nala (नल)
"hollow conduit; name of mythic builder & king"
↓
Classical Sanskrit & Modern Usage (c. 2nd c. BCE – Present)
Nala
"epic engineer of Rama's bridge; king of Nishadha"
