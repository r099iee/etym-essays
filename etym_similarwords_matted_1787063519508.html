• Tangled: Similar because both describe strands or elements snarled together in disordered complexity; differs because *tangled* implies knotting and intertwining that may still retain loose air spaces and distinct strands, whereas *matted* denotes a flattened, dense, compressed, felt-like solid mass.
  • Felted: Similar because both refer to fibers compacted tightly together without weaving; differs because *felted* is a technical, deliberate textile manufacturing process using heat, moisture, and agitation on animal wool fibers, whereas *matted* often implies unintentional neglect, decay, or wild natural compaction.
  • Knotted: Similar because both describe fibers or cords bound inextricably together; differs because *knotted* involves distinct localized ties, loops, and interlocking hitches, whereas *matted* is an extensive, broad, uniform sheet or clump of snarled fibers.
  • Snarled: Similar because both describe messy, disorderly entanglements of hair, thread, or vegetation; differs because *snarled* emphasizes localized, aggravating jams and twists (often with aggressive emotional connotations), whereas *matted* emphasizes compressed thickness and impenetrable density.
  • Intertwined: Similar because both involve multiple filaments crossing and looping around one another; differs because *intertwined* carries a graceful, harmonious, or structural connotation (as in braided vines or intertwined destinies), whereas *matted* conveys coarse, chaotic, flattened unruliness.
  • Disheveled: Similar because both describe untidy, neglected hair or plumage; differs because *disheveled* refers to general rumpled disorder and unkempt appearance without requiring the dense, solid physical binding intrinsic to *matted* fibers.
  • Matte: Similar because it is the homophonic adjectival base referring to dull, non-glossy surfaces; differs because *matte* (or *mat*) strictly denotes light reflectance and flat optical finish, whereas *matted* as a participle primarily denotes the physical state of compacted, tangled fibers.
