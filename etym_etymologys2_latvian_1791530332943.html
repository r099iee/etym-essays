Note: the ethnonym is usually derived from the tribal name of the Latgalians (Latgali), whose territory Latgale may contain gals “end, border.” The root Lat- itself is obscure: it has been connected with a river name and with a Baltic root meaning “to flow,” but neither is established. The modern national name Latvija is a nineteenth-century formation; earlier sources use Latin Lettones/Lettigalli and German Lette. English Latvian replaced the older Lettish in the twentieth century.
