• Upheld: Similar because it is the modern equivalent with the same meaning; differs because "upheld" is the regular, neutral participle of today, while "upholden" is archaic and poetic.
  • Supported: Similar because both mean held up; differs because "supported" is general and physical or figurative, without the specific "held aloft/kept from falling" force of "upholden."
  • Sustained: Similar because both mean maintained over time; differs because "sustain" stresses continuous nourishment or endurance, while "upholden" stresses keeping something from falling.
  • Maintained: Similar because both mean kept in good condition; differs because "maintained" is neutral, administrative, and lacks the ceremonial dignity of "upholden."
  • Held up: Similar because this is the literal paraphrase; differs because it is conversational and physical, with none of the archaic elevation of "upholden."
  • Bolstered: Similar because both mean reinforced; differs because "bolstered" is a padding metaphor, informal and modern, used of confidence or arguments.
  • Defended: Similar because both can apply to rights and causes; differs because "defended" implies active resistance against attack, whereas "upholden" implies supporting from below.
  • Upborne: Similar because both are archaic strong participles meaning held up; differs because "upborne" is even rarer and stresses being carried or supported from beneath, while "upholden" stresses active keeping-up.
  • Beholden: Similar because it is a surviving relative of "holden"; differs in meaning entirely — "beholden" means "indebted, obliged," not "upheld."
