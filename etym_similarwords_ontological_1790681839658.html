• metaphysical: Similar because both concern ultimate reality; differs because metaphysical is broader (including cosmology, theology) and often pejoratively abstract, ontological is specifically about being.
  • existential: Similar because both concern being and anxiety; differs because existential stresses lived experience and choice, ontological stresses structure of what is.
  • cosmological: Similar because both concern reality at large; differs because cosmological concerns origin and order of the universe, ontological concerns modes of being as such.
  • essential: Similar because both concern what things are; differs because essential means necessary to identity, ontological means pertaining to being itself.
  • fundamental: Similar because both mark depth; differs because fundamental is generic (basic), ontological is technical (about being).
  • constitutive: Similar because both denote what makes something what it is; differs because constitutive is Kantian-constructive, ontological is realist about being.
  • transcendental: Similar because both are high philosophical adjectives; differs because transcendental concerns conditions of possibility of experience, ontological concerns being independent of experience.
  • phenomenological: Similar because both describe philosophical approaches to being; differs because phenomenological concerns appearing to consciousness, ontological concerns being qua being.
  • epistemological: Similar because both are -logical philosophical adjectives and often paired; differs because epistemological concerns knowledge and justification, ontological concerns existence — the canonical contrast.
  • teleological: Similar because both classify kinds of explanation; differs because teleological concerns purposes or ends, ontological concerns being.
  • cathegorical / categorical: Similar in classifying what there is; differs because categorical concerns logical classes, ontological concerns real kinds.
  • noumenal: Similar because both point beyond appearances; differs because noumenal is specifically Kantian thing-in-itself, ontological is broader.
