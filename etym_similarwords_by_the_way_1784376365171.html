• Incidentally: Similar because both introduce a tangential remark in passing; differs because incidentally is more formal, written, and can mean "by chance," while by the way is warmer, conversational, and purely a discourse marker.
  • By the by: Similar because both are fixed phrases for introducing a side-note, and both began as spatial phrases (by the by = "beside the by, the path"); differs because by the by is rarer, older in register, and distinctly British in flavour.
  • In passing: Similar because both mean "as an aside, not as the main point"; differs because in passing emphasizes brevity and lack of emphasis, while by the way actively flags a topic change or digression.
  • Parenthetically: Similar because both mark words inserted as a side remark; differs because parenthetically is formal and grammatical (a true parenthesis), while by the way is a loose conversational marker.
  • Speaking of which: Similar because both introduce an additional related remark; differs because speaking of which must attach to something just mentioned, while by the way can start a wholly new topic.
  • BTW: Similar because it is the abbreviation of by the way with the same discourse function; differs because BTW is informal, typed, and confined to digital text.
  • Anyway / anyhow: Similar because both manage topic shifts and returns to the main point; differs because anyway signals a return or dismissal of a topic, while by the way introduces a new aside.
  • Along the way: Similar because both derive from spatial "on the path"; differs because along the way keeps its literal spatial sense ("during the journey") and is not used as a discourse marker.
  • As a matter of fact: Similar because both introduce an additional point in speech; differs because as a matter of fact emphasizes factual correction or confirmation, while by the way merely flags a digression.
  • On the side: Similar because both can mean "as an incidental addition"; differs because on the side usually refers to extra activities (a job, a dish), not to conversational digressions.
