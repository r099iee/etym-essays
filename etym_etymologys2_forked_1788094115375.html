Note: Latin furca 'pitchfork, fork' has no secure Indo-European etymology; it is probably a loanword from an Italic or Mediterranean substrate (some compare Etruscan forms), so no PIE chain can be reconstructed — the chain therefore begins at Latin. English took the word directly from Latin into Old English (forca), reinforced by later borrowings via Old French fourche.
