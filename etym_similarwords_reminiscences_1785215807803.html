• Memoirs: Similar because both are written accounts of remembered life; differs because memoirs are a sustained narrative organized around public events and roles, while reminiscences are looser, more anecdotal, and devoted to portraits of other people and places.
  • Recollections: Similar because both describe the summoning of past experience; differs because recollections stress the mental act and may refer to a single episode, while reminiscences imply an accumulated, often published collection.
  • Memory: Similar because both have to do with the past; differs because memory is the neutral cognitive faculty or a single stored item of information, without reminiscence's implication of narrative or published retrospect.
  • Autobiography: Similar because both recount one's own past; differs because an autobiography is a systematic, continuous life-narrative, whereas reminiscences are episodic, selective, and impressionistic.
  • Remembrance: Similar because both denote the keeping of the past in mind; differs because remembrance is more ceremonial and inward (remembrance of the dead), while reminiscences are anecdotal and outward, often published.
  • Anecdotes: Similar because both are short accounts of past incidents; differs because an anecdote is a single pointed story told for entertainment or illustration, while reminiscences are a body of recollections, usually personal to the author.
  • Confessions: Similar because both are first-person retrospective writings; differs because confessions foreground moral or spiritual self-revelation (as in Augustine or Rousseau), while reminiscences are portraits and episodes without that confessional programme.
  • Retrospect: Similar because both look back over the past; differs because retrospect is a mental survey or a critical review of a period, not the accumulated personal memories themselves.
  • Memorabilia: Similar because both preserve the past; differs because memorabilia are objects and mementoes kept as souvenirs, while reminiscences are written or spoken recollections.
