• Passed: Similar because it denotes the elapsing of time; differs because passed is neutral, objective, and general, whereas wore conveys a perceptible sense of slowness, heaviness, fatigue, and gradual friction as time moves forward.
  • Elapsed: Similar because it marks the transit of hours; differs because elapsed is a formal, technical, or legal term indicating the measured expiration of a duration without any evocative atmospheric mood.
  • Dragged: Similar because it describes time moving with painful slowness; differs because dragged emphasizes extreme, tedious, agonizingly sluggish delay, whereas wore describes a steady, relentless, organic progression.
  • Eroded: Similar because it describes material depletion through friction; differs because eroded focuses primarily on geological, chemical, or systemic destruction, whereas wore applies equally to garments, expressions, time, and physical surfaces.
  • Donned: Similar because it relates to clothing; differs because donned is the punctual act of putting clothing on at a specific moment, whereas wore is the continuous state of having it on one's body.
  • Attired: Similar because it pertains to dressing; differs because attired is a formal, elevated participial adjective describing complete ceremonial ensemble rather than the universal, active verb wore.
  • Frittered away: Similar because it relates to time slipping away; differs because frittered implies careless, wasteful squandering of time, whereas wore describes the natural, unavoidable elapsing of hours.
