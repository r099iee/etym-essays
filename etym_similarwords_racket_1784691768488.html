• Noise: Similar because both are audible sound; differs because "noise" is the neutral, all-purpose term for any sound, while "racket" is noise judged objectionable, carrying impatience and distaste.
  • Din: Similar because both are loud, unpleasant sounds; differs because "din" stresses prolonged, deafening, overwhelming volume, while a racket can be a smaller but still irritating clatter.
  • Clamour: Similar because both are loud confused outcries; differs because "clamour" suggests shouting or urgent outcry, while "racket" is the clash of many inanimate sounds.
  • Hubbub: Similar because both are noisy disturbances; differs because "hubbub" is a confused murmur of many voices, while "racket" is boisterous, clattering, and reproachfully loud.
  • Commotion: Similar because both are noisy disturbances; differs because "commotion" emphasises the general agitation and movement, while "racket" emphasises the sound itself.
  • Uproar: Similar because both are violent noisy disturbances; differs because "uproar" is larger in scale and often a crowd's roar, while a racket may be a smaller but still offensive clatter.
  • Clatter: Similar because both are sounds of things striking together; differs because "clatter" is a looser, hard, knocking sound, while "racket" is a judgment on overall objectionable noise.
  • Rumpus: Similar because both are noisy disturbances; differs because "rumpus" is comic and domestic in register, while "racket" is more general and reproachful.
  • Pandemonium: Similar because both are wild disorder and noise; differs because "pandemonium" is total, chaotic uproar, far larger in scale than a mere racket.
