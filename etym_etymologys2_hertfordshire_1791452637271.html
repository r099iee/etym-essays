Note: the name is a compound. Its second element, ford, descends from PIE *per- “to lead, pass over” through Proto-Germanic *furduz and Old English ford. Its third element, scīr “district, administrative division,” descends from PIE *sker- “to cut, divide” through Proto-Germanic *skīrō. Bede (731) records Herutford; the county name is first recorded in the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle in 1011, with some annals preserving earlier forms.
