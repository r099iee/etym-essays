Note: deriving Germanic *feraz from PIE *per- (go through, fare, risk) is the widely given account linking fear, fare, and peril, but the semantic path from travelling to danger passes through debated intermediate senses (journey → ambush on the road → peril), so the deepest link is marked as probable rather than certain. The English stages are fully attested.
