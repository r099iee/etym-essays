• Dash: Similar because it denotes a sudden speedy run; differs because a dash can be heedless, showy, or heavy, while a dart is always light, straight, and precisely aimed.
  • Sprint: Similar because it means running at full speed over a short distance; differs because sprint is athletic and sustained over metres, while dart stresses the single explosive launch and brevity.
  • Scurry: Similar because it captures small creatures moving fast; differs because scurry implies shuffling, undignified haste with many small steps, while dart implies one clean line of flight.
  • Bolt: Similar because it means a sudden escape run, often of horses or frightened people; differs because bolting stresses panic and uncontrollability, while darting keeps aim and control.
  • Flit: Similar because it means light rapid movement, of birds and butterflies; differs because flitting wanders and hovers between points, while darting commits to a single target line.
  • Missile / javelin: Similar because these are the literal ancestors, the thrown weapons; differ because they name only the object, never the motion, while dart uniquely names both weapon and dash.
  • Glance (darting glance): Similar because eyes move in quick aimed bursts; differs because glance names only the look, while dart covers the full body’s flight as well.
