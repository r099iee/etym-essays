Neoclassical Compound / Portmanteau coined in the United States (1951)

Element 1: ethno-
PIE *swedʰ-no- "one's own group, custom, people"
↓ Ancient Greek ἔθνος (éthnos) "nation, people, tribe, caste"
↓ Late Latin / French ethno- "relating to race, people, or culture"

Element 2: -logue
PIE *leǵ- "to gather, collect, speak"
↓ Ancient Greek λόγος (lógos) "word, speech, account, reason"
↓ Ancient Greek κατάλογος (katálogos) "enrollment, register, catalogue"
↓ Old French catalogue "ordered inventory, list"
↓ English suffix / blend -logue "catalogue, discourse, register"
↓
Coined by Richard S. Pittman (SIL) (1951)
Ethnologue
"catalogue of the world's ethnic languages and peoples"
