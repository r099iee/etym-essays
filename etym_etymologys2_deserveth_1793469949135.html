Note: The base verb reflects a profound semantic shift in Late Latin, where intensive physical service (dē-servīre) came to signify the legitimate moral title or entitlement earned through such service, while the verbal ending preserves the ancient Proto-Indo-European third-person primary suffix *-eti.
