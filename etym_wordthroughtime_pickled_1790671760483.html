- Steeped and kept in salt liquor - neutral kitchen and provisioning craft, valued preservation against winter hunger
- Bottled specimen and curiosity - early modern collectors keep oddities pickled in spirits; wonder mixed with morbidity
- Fixed in a predicament - to be in a pickle; being bottled up becomes being stuck, rueful colloquial comedy
- Deadened by simplification - Victorian and modern critics use pickled for embalmed teaching, heritage piety and reassuring formulas; pejorative, reformist sting
- Soaked in drink - 19th–21st-century slang for drunk; jocular, affectionate, self-mocking rather than condemnatory
- Digitally and institutionally pickled to this day - reputations, traditions and specimens fixed in amber, formaldehyde or the internet; contemporary sense of irreversible, sour preservation
