Debate note (zero guesswork): the origin of Old English cȳme itself is disputed. Some scholars compare it with a West Germanic base meaning "delicate, frail" (the source of modern German kaum "scarcely"); others seek an Indo-European base of "vigour, strength"; neither hypothesis is securely proved. The popular idea that comely comes from come ("one who comes well") is a folk etymology rejected by the OED.
