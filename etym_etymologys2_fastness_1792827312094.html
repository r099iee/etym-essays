Proto-Germanic (reconstructed, c. 500 BCE)
*fastaz
‘firm, fixed, steadfast’
↓
Old English (9th c.)
fæst
‘firm, fixed, secure’
↓
Old English (9th c., noun formation)
fæstnes (-nes suffix)
‘firmness; fortified place’
↓
Middle English (13th c.)
fastnesse
‘firmness; stronghold’
↓
Modern English (current)
fastness
‘stronghold; firmness, speed’

Note: deeper than Proto-Germanic the origin is disputed: older handbooks suggest a link with PIE *past- meaning ‘solid, firm’, but this is uncertain, so the chain properly begins at the securely attested Germanic adjective.
