- Old English (geomrian / geomor) - 'to lament, to mourn, to bemoan', adjective 'sorrowful': the high register of elegy, the grief of a lone wanderer or a fallen lord.
- c. 1300 (Middle English yameren) - 'to mourn, complain, whine, chatter': the word begins its slide down the scale of dignity.
- 15th c. - a petty, querulous complaint; grief gives way to fuss.
- 17th-18th c. - the noise of an irritating, persistent fuss; informal and dismissive in register.
- Modern - a continuous, loud, querulous whine or nagging, of people, radios, and dogs alike; faintly comic and pathetic, as in yammering on or yammering away.
