Note: the Latin verb incurrere is in- “into” plus currere “to run,” and its past participle incursus supplies the noun incursio. The PIE root *ḱers- “to run” is supported by Sanskrit carati “he moves” and Avestan caraiti “he moves, grazes”; the Latin family also gives cursus, cursor, excursus, occur, recur and curriculum. The reconstruction is standard, though the exact laryngeal shape is debated.
