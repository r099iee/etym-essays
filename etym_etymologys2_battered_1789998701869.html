Note on debates: the deeper origin of Latin battuere/battuere is disputed and historically nuanced. Etymonline, the OED, and Walde-Hofmann note it as probably of Gaulish (Celtic) origin, cf. *battu- "to strike," while others suggest a Germanic or expressive onomatopoeic formation (cf. Old Norse berja "to strike," Gothic *bautjan). De Vaan treats it as a Late Latin variant of battuere with uncertain prehistory, possibly sound-symbolic. No secure PIE root is posited; the chain above therefore starts at securely attested Latin and marks earlier prehistory as debated rather than inventing a PIE form.
