Debate
wilder may be the comparative of wild ('more wild') or the verb to wilder ('to make wild', a back-formation of bewilder); Tolkien's Wilderland (The Hobbit, 1937) is a modern revival. The PIE root for wild, *wel- 'to tear, hunt?', is uncertain and not securely attested.
