- Prehistoric headland - burial and fort without surviving name; silent
- 14th c. Douglas Tantallon - new curtain wall on old rock; baronial and defiant
- 1491-1528 sieges - king versus Angus, cannon against wall; contested and famous
- 1651 slighting - Cromwell's guns breach and abandon it; ruined and roofless
- 1808 Marmion Tantallon - Scott peoples the empty wall with earls and pursuivants; romantic and crowded
- 1830s Ruskin childhood Tantallon - boy finds stone without men; present, empty, and puzzling
- Modern heritage Tantallon - guarded ruin, seabirds, and guidebook; conserved and visited
