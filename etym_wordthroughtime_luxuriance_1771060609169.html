- Lush, excessive growth - Descended from “luxury,” it first described rank, wildly abundant vegetable growth, a purely botanical abundance.
- Sensual excess and opulence - Broadened metaphorically into rich, extravagant abundance of any kind, gaining connotations of sumptuousness, intemperance, and almost sinful excess.
- Contemporary Sense - Today it means the quality of being lush, thriving, and abundant in growth, and by extension a figurative richness of style or ornament; it retains a heavy, opulent, sensual register.
