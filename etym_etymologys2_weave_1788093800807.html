Note: PIE *webʰ- 'to weave' is securely reconstructed from Greek hyphaínein 'to weave' and Sanskrit ūrṇavābhí 'spider' (lit. 'wool-weaver') among others, so this chain is attested in the usual sense at every stage. The motion sense ('weave through traffic') is probably a separate word of its own origin — connected by the OED with Old Norse veifa 'to wave, swing' — and is not part of this textile-verb chain.
