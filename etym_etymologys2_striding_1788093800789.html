↓
Modern English (from the 16th c.)
stride

Modern English (present)
STRIDING
“walking with long, decisive steps”
“stride + -ing (participle/gerund)”

Note (debated): the PIE reconstruction *streidh- (Watkins, Pokorny) is not universally accepted — the Germanic “strive, contend” branch (German streiten, Old Norse stríða) may belong to a separate root; English shows only the walking sense at every period. The -ing suffix continues the Germanic participle/gerund endings (OE -ende / -unge, levelled to ME -ing).
