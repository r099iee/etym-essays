Debate: the adverb is an English formation in -ly (OE -līce, PGmc *-līka-) from the adjective grievous; Middle English greuously is attested c. 1300, and early spellings included grevously and grieviously before the form grievously stabilized. The link of Latin gravis to PIE *gʷerh₂- is standard (cf. Greek barús, Sanskrit gurú).
