• ventilators: Similar because both refer to mechanical devices or openings designed to move, circulate, or exhaust air; differs because ventilators are utilitarian, stationary architectural or mechanical air-exchange systems, lacking the broad radiating geometry, portable elegance, and enthusiast senses of fans.
  • blowers: Similar because both describe mechanical or manual instruments that propel currents of air; differs because blowers emphasize high-pressure directional air blasts (such as leaf blowers or furnace blowers) rather than radial, folding, or decorative leaf-like structures.
  • flabella: Similar because both designate large, ornate ceremonial handheld fans composed of peacock feathers or parchment used in royal and liturgical processions; differs because flabella is a rare, highly specialized liturgical and antiquarian Latinate plural, whereas fans is universal.
  • gorgonians: Similar because the "fans of coral" in the context are precisely the marine organisms known as gorgonian sea fans; differs because gorgonians is the formal taxonomic classification for horny octocorals, whereas fans describes their visual, radiating morphology.
  • aficionados: Similar because both describe passionate, knowledgeable enthusiasts or devotees of an art, sport, or pursuit; differs because aficionado (borrowed from Spanish) carries an aristocratic connotation of refined, connoisseur-level appreciation, whereas fans can range from casual spectators to wild, frenzied masses.
  • devotees: Similar because both identify ardent followers dedicated to a person, cause, or activity; differs because devotees implies deep, almost religious reverence, solemn commitment, or lifelong loyalty, whereas fans often emphasizes energetic popular entertainment engagement.
  • enthusiasts: Similar because both describe people who display lively interest and excitement for a hobby, subject, or team; differs because enthusiast is a milder, more intellectual or recreational term, lacking the mass-cultural fervor and fandom identity often associated with fans.
  • zealots: Similar because both originate from intense, uncompromising devotion to a belief, figure, or group; differs because zealots carries a heavy, often negative connotation of fanatical, dogmatic extremism, political militancy, or intolerance.
