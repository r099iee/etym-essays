• Indiscriminately: Similar in acting without selection or preference; differs because indiscriminately stresses the mere absence of care or selection, while promiscuously carries the fuller idea of mixing many different kinds together.
  • Randomly: Similar in lacking a governing principle; differs because randomly emphasizes pure chance with no pattern at all, whereas promiscuously implies an open, freely-mixing eclecticism rather than statistical chance.
  • Haphazardly: Similar in disorderly, unsystematic manner; differs because haphazardly implies carelessness and unplanned disorder, while promiscuously can be a deliberate, even scholarly, choice not to discriminate.
  • Arbitrarily: Similar in not following a rule; differs because arbitrarily suggests choice made on whim or authority without reason, whereas promiscuously suggests an openness to everything mixed together.
  • Casually: Similar in doing something without formality; differs because casually is light and informal — without fuss — and carries no sense of mixing or mingling of kinds.
  • Unselectively: Similar in taking everything without picking; differs because unselectively is the neutral, formal term for the absence of selection, while promiscuously adds the image of many kinds mingled together.
  • Liberally: Similar in drawing freely and generously; differs because liberally praises abundance and generosity, while promiscuously stresses the lack of distinction in what is drawn.
  • Freely: Similar in an unconstrained manner; differs because freely suggests openness and ease without any hint of disorder, while promiscuously keeps the 'mixing without distinction' core.
  • Eclectically: Similar in drawing from many varied sources; differs because eclectically implies a tasteful, principled selection from many schools, while promiscuously implies no such principled selection at all.
  • Heterogeneously: Similar in involving a mixture of different kinds; differs because heterogeneously describes the mixed character of the result — a heterogeneous collection — rather than the act of taking without distinction.
  • Without distinction: Similar in making no difference between things; differs because the phrase is a plain description, while promiscuously compresses the same idea into a single word with a slight self-deprecating or playful edge.
  • Liberally (sexual register): In the sexual sense, promiscuously is similar to freely or casually in behaviour; differs because only promiscuously carries the strong moral disapproval of non-exclusive sexual behaviour, the modern dominant reading.
