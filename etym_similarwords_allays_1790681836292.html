• calms: Similar because both reduce agitation; differs because calm is general and colloquial, allay is formal and typically takes fears or doubts as object.
  • soothes: Similar because both ease pain or feeling; differs because soothe suggests gentle comforting touch or voice, allay suggests diminution of intensity.
  • eases: Similar because both lessen burden; differs because ease is broader (ease pain, ease restrictions), allay is restricted to fears, suspicions, suffering.
  • alleviates: Similar because both relieve suffering; differs because alleviate is clinical and measurable (alleviate symptoms, poverty), allay is emotive and epistemic.
  • assuages: Similar because both pacify grief or appetite; differs because assuage is even more literary and often implies satisfying an appetite, allay implies quieting alarm.
  • mitigates: Similar because both make less severe; differs because mitigate is legal-technical and applies to consequences or punishment, allay applies to feelings.
  • relieves: Similar because both lift pressure; differs because relieve implies removal of load, allay implies softening of perception.
  • appeases: Similar because both pacify; differs because appease often implies concession to demands and can connote weakness, allay is neutral soothing.
  • pacifies: Similar because both quiet disturbance; differs because pacify suggests restoring peace after conflict, allay suggests settling nascent worry.
  • mollifies: Similar because both soften anger; differs because mollify focuses on anger and implies coaxing, allay focuses on fear and implies reassurance.
  • quenches: Similar in archaic allay-thirst sense; differs because quench implies total extinction by liquid, allay implies partial soothing.
  • dispels: Similar because both remove doubts or fears; differs because dispel implies scattering by proof or light, allay implies calming without necessarily refuting.
  • reassures: Similar because both settle insecurity; differs because reassure is interpersonal and explicit, allay can be effected by things, theories, or silence.
