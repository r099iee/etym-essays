- Early 16th Century (c. 1520s): Skeptical doubt and moral uncertainty - Applied to actions, motives, or loyalties that were questionable, vacillating, or untrustworthy.
- Late 16th to 17th Century: Rhetorical and linguistic duplicity - Used of oracles, riddles, and political statements capable of double meanings (equivocation).
- 18th to 19th Century: Philosophical and scientific inexactitude - Denoted intellectual propositions lacking precise definitions or empirical boundaries.
- 20th to 21st Century: Gestalt psychology, art theory, and computing - Celebrated as a generative feature of visual art, optical illusions (ambiguous figures), and syntactic parsing challenges in natural language processing.
