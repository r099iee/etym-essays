Note: English also contains two historical homographs: the mechanical clamping tool vice/vise, which derives from Old French vis (‘screw’), from Latin vītis (‘grapevine, spiral tendril’); and the preposition/prefix vice (‘in place of’), from Latin vicis (‘turn, alternation, office’), as in vice versa and vice-president.
