• Imagining: similar as forming something in the mind; differs in being broader and freer — any fantasy counts, whereas conceiving implies forming something definite and coherent enough to be held and developed.
  • Envisaging / Envisioning: similar — picturing something in the mind's eye; differs in modern colouring (20th-century coinages) and in focusing on the imagined result rather than the act of formation.
  • Devising: similar — forming a plan or scheme; differs in implying deliberate construction toward a practical end, while conceiving may remain purely mental with no intention of execution.
  • Formulating: similar — putting an idea into definite shape; differs in emphasising expression in words, whereas conceiving precedes expression.
  • Understanding / Comprehending / Grasping: similar in the sense "take in with the mind"; differs in direction — these mean receiving and mastering what already exists, while conceiving often means producing something new. The overlap is real: "I cannot conceive how..." equals "I cannot understand how...".
  • Visualising: similar in mental imagery; differs in being strictly about images and scenes, not ideas, plans, or relationships.
  • Planning: similar in the devising sense; differs in being procedural, organised, and future-oriented.
  • Dreaming up: similar casual idiom for free invention; differs in register — whimsical and informal, where conceiving is weighty and precise.
