- Trim and seaworthy (of ships) - compact, well-built to weather storms, admired as competent craftsmanship
- Tight-fitting and well-closed - extension to garments, lids, and harbours, practical and neat
- Warm, cozy, and sheltered indoors - domestication into parlours, beds, and cottages, affectionate and comfortable
- Tenderly held close (of people and babies) - sentimentalization toward maternal care and intimacy
- Modern snugly as cozy-tight manner - broadly positive blend of exact fit plus felt warmth and security
