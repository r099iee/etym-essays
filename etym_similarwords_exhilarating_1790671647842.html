• thrilling: Similar because both mean exciting with shivers of delight; differs because thrilling stresses suspense and trembling edge, while exhilarating stresses cheering and enlivening.
  • invigorating: Similar because both mean filling with energy; differs because invigorating stresses restoring vigour and health, while exhilarating stresses gladdening and lifting mood.
  • stimulating: Similar because both mean rousing activity; differs because stimulating is broader and can be intellectual or physiological, while exhilarating is specifically joyful stimulation.
  • uplifting: Similar because both mean raising spirits; differs because uplifting is gentler, moral or emotional elevation, while exhilarating is livelier and more physical.
  • electrifying: Similar because both mean suddenly exciting; differs because electrifying implies shock-like intensity and crowd voltage, while exhilarating implies buoyant cheer.
  • intoxicating: Similar because both mean headily exciting; differs because intoxicating warns of loss of control like drink, while exhilarating keeps clarity and health.
  • bracing: Similar because both mean freshening like cold air; differs because bracing stresses tonic sharpness, while exhilarating stresses joyful lightness.
  • euphoric: Similar because both mean intensely happy; differs because euphoric is stronger, clinical, sometimes artificial high, while exhilarating is natural lively gladness.
  • enlivening: Similar because both mean making lively; differs because enlivening is quieter, making vivid, while exhilarating is stronger, making joyful.
