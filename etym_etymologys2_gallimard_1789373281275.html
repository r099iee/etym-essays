Note: Onomastic consensus (Dauzat, Morlet, Tosti) identifies Gallimard as an occupational nickname for a scribe, copyist, or public scrivener. An alternate Germanic hypothesis derives the name from the Frankish personal name Walmarus / Galimar (*walha- 'foreigner/Celt' + *mērijaz 'famous').
