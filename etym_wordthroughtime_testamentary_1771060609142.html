- Strict legal sense (inheritance & wills) - Since Middle English, used almost exclusively in law and probate to describe anything relating to a last will and testament; formal, juridical register.
- Expanded probate sense - Extended across estate administration to cover testamentary trusts, testamentary capacity, and testamentary intent; stays firmly within legal and administrative language.
- Figurative/literary sense - Now also used loosely and solemnly of anything that bears final witness or lasting proof of something (a testamentary memorial); shifted from dry legal jargon to an elevated, reverent register.
