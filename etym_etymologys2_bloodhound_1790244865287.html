Etymological note: The name bloodhound does not strictly refer to tracking wounded or bleeding prey, as is often popularly assumed. Rather, historical evidence indicates it originally denoted a hound of 'pure blood'—an aristocratic breed maintained through meticulous, unmixed pedigree by the monks of Saint-Hubert and medieval noblemen.
