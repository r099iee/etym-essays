- 14th to 15th Century (Middle English): Architectural window screens and shutters - Denoted openwork wooden or metal grilles placed across windows before glass was affordable, providing ventilation and security.
- 16th to 17th Century: Garden pergolas, arbors, and heraldic frets - Expanded to ornamental gardening screens for roses and vines, and heraldic decorative motifs resembling woven ribbons.
- 18th to 19th Century: Early crystallography and mineralogy - Borrowed by mineralogists (Haüy, Bravais) to denote the regular, repeating internal geometric array of atoms forming mineral crystals (Bravais lattices).
- 20th to 21st Century: Quantum physics, abstract algebra, and cryptography - Transformed into foundational concepts in pure mathematics (partially ordered sets with meet and join) and modern lattice-based cryptography (post-quantum encryption algorithms).
