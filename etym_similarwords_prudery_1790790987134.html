• Priggishness: Similar because both denote an irritating, superior attitude of moral uprightness; differs because priggishness emphasizes smug, pedantic adherence to rules, punctilio, and superior manners across all intellectual and social conduct, whereas prudery focuses specifically on modesty, sexual squeamishness, and anti-sensual taboos.
  • Puritanism: Similar because both enforce strict moral codes and reject sensual indulgences; differs because puritanism is an expansive theological, political, and philosophical system of disciplined community living, while prudery is primarily an emotional posture of squeamish modesty or behavioral shock at sexuality.
  • Modesty: Similar because both involve restraint, decency, and avoidance of vulgarity; differs because modesty is a genuine, healthy, and culturally admired virtue of unpretentious decency, whereas prudery represents an exaggerated, rigid, or hypocritically affected excess of modesty.
  • Primness: Similar because both manifest stiff, formal propriety and severe decorum; differs because primness emphasizes rigid, neat, and excessively formal physical demeanor and manners, without necessarily centering on moral or sexual taboos.
  • Fastidiousness: Similar because both involve squeamishness and high sensitivity to perceived improprieties; differs because fastidiousness relates to exacting taste, cleanliness, and perfectionism in details, rather than moral or erotic squeamishness.
  • Demureness: Similar because both display reserved, modest, or shy behavior; differs because demureness can be playful, gentle, or pleasantly reserved, whereas prudery is censorious, judgmental, and defensive.
  • Sanctimony: Similar because both exhibit outward displays of moral righteousness; differs because sanctimony emphasizes hypocritical, ostentatious religious holiness, whereas prudery centers specifically on shock and distaste toward bodily, erotic, or vulgar topics.
  • Coquetry: Contrasting term denoting playful flirtatiousness and sensual teasing designed to allure without immediate commitment.
