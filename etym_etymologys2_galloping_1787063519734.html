NOTE: The Old Frankish compound *wala hlaupan is a reconstruction; alternative accounts derive Old French galoper from a Vulgar Latin or Gaulish verb. The deeper ancestry of *wala (possibly from PIE *wel- “to will, wish”, whence English well) and *hlaupan (English leap) is itself debated and cannot be established with certainty.
