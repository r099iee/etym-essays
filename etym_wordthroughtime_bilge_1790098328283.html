- 1510s ship’s belly - bilge (variant of bulge): the rounded lowest hull inside; workmanlike, salty, technical perception
- 17th–18th c. naval system - bilge water, pumps, soundings: daily dread of leaks and stench on men-of-war and merchantmen; fearful, disciplined perception
- 19th c. iron and steam - bilge keels, bilge alarms, engine-room bilges of oil and coal dust; industrial, engineering perception
- 1920s slang turn - bilge, bilge water for worthless talk in wardrooms and barracks; mocking, masculine, naval-slang perception
- Modern double life - yachtsmen pump bilges while newspapers pump bilge: one foul sump, two registers, both vivid; practical and insulting perception
