Latin (c. 1st c. BCE)
reddere ("to render") + vos ("you")
"render yourselves, present yourselves"
↓
Middle French (Military Imperative) (c. 16th c.)
rendez vous
"assemble yourselves, report to duty"
↓
Middle French (Noun) (c. 1590s)
rendez-vous
"an appointed military assembly point"
↓
Modern English (17th c.)
RENDEZVOUS
"an agreed meeting place or scheduled gathering"
