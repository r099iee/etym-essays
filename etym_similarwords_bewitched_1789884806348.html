• Enchanted: Similar because both mean put under a spell; differs because enchanted is often delightful and fairy-like, while bewitched stresses witchcraft and malign control.
  • Spellbound: Similar because both mean held as if by a spell; differs because spellbound stresses fixed attention, while bewitched stresses magical causation.
  • Entranced: Similar because both mean rapt and captivated; differs because entranced is dreamy and aesthetic, while bewitched keeps a darker sorcery echo.
  • Captivated: Similar because both mean seized in mind; differs because captivated is neutral and social, while bewitched implies uncanny power.
  • Fascinated: Similar because both mean irresistibly interested; differs because fascinated is intellectual curiosity, while bewitched is will-overriding charm.
  • Charmed: Similar because both imply magical pleasing; differs because charmed is light and benevolent, while bewitched can be fearful or erotic.
  • Hexed: Similar because both mean cursed by magic; differs because hexed is American/Dutch for a malicious jinx, while bewitched is broader tale-English.
  • Cursed: Similar because both suffer evil invocation; differs because cursed stresses doom and blight, while bewitched stresses spell-control and transformation.
  • Mesmerised: Similar because both mean fixed and helpless before power; differs because mesmerised is hypnotic and modern, while bewitched is folkloric.
