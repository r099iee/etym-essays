Note on historical morphology: The transition from Latin īra to īrāscī represents a classic Indo-European inceptive/inchoative derivation using the suffix -sc-, denoting the process of entering into a state (i.e. "to begin raging" or "to flare into anger"). The subsequent attachment of the potential passive suffix -bilis created a term denoting the inherent propensity or liability to be thrown into that state.
