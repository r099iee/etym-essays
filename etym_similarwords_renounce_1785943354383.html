• Relinquish: Similar because both mean giving something up; differs because relinquish implies yielding or surrendering a physical object, position, or control, often under pressure or with reluctance, while renounce is a formal verbal declaration of rejection that need not involve handing anything over.
  • Abjure: Similar because both mean solemnly giving up; differs because abjure specifically requires swearing a formal oath (abjuring a belief, abjuring allegiance), while renounce may be a declaration without a sworn oath.
  • Abdicate: Similar because both mean giving up a position; differs because abdicate applies specifically to a monarch or high ruler surrendering supreme power, whereas renounce extends to any right, claim, belief, or connection.
  • Repudiate: Similar because both mean rejecting a claim or connection; differs because repudiate emphasizes rejecting the validity or truth of something - a debt, a contract, an accusation - while renounce emphasizes surrendering one's own right or belief.
  • Forswear: Similar because both mean swearing to give something up; differs because forswear is native Germanic, carries the strong colour of an oath against a habit or passion (forswearing drink), and is more literary and emphatic than renounce.
  • Disavow: Similar because both mean publicly denying connection with something; differs because disavow denies any responsibility, knowledge, or authorship (disavowing a statement), while renounce is an active declaration of giving up what one formerly held.
  • Disown: Similar because both mean formally rejecting a person or connection; differs because disown applies specifically to refusing to acknowledge a family member or a piece of property, a private and often emotional act, while renounce is a broader, more public declaration.
  • Abandon: Similar because both mean leaving something behind; differs because abandon is the general, neutral verb for deserting or giving up with no required formality, while renounce insists on an explicit, usually public declaration.
