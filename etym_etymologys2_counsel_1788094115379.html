Note on the debated etymology: The origin of Latin consulere is genuinely uncertain. The most common proposal derives it from com- “together” + a root *sel- “to take, gather” (cf. Greek heleîn “to take”); a rival view connects it with sedēre “to sit” (“to sit together”). Neither proposal commands universal assent; the PIE stage is therefore marked as proposed, not established.
