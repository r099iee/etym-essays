- Antiquity (Antiquity–1st c. CE) - Founded as a Phoenician/Carthaginian trading settlement (*Šll*) and later integrated into the Roman Empire as the frontier river port of *Sala Colonia* in Mauretania Tingitana.
- 10th–14th Century - Re-founded as an Islamic trading and spiritual hub under the Zenata, Almoravid, Almohad, and Marinid dynasties; fortified with great sea-facing ramparts and the Bab el-Mrissa sea gate.
- 17th Century (1627–1668) - Golden Age of Corsair Autonomy: Flooded by expelled Andalusian Hornacheros and Moriscos, Salé declared itself an independent maritime republic (the *Republic of Salé* or *Republic of Bou Regreg*), terrorizing European shipping with the famed 'Salé Rovers'.
- 18th–19th Century - Re-absorbed into the unified Alaouite Sultanate of Morocco; bombarded by French and British fleets to suppress privateering.
- 20th–21st Century - Developed into a vibrant residential, cultural, and artisan sister city to Morocco's capital Rabat, renowned for its historic medina and Islamic architectural heritage.
