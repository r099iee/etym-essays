• Red: Similar because both name the color red; differs because red is the pure color term, while ruddy means reddish with a warm, healthy, glowing quality, usually of faces or light.
  • Rosy: Similar because both describe a reddish, healthy complexion; differs because rosy is softer, pinker, and suggests youth and delicate beauty, while ruddy is deeper and heartier.
  • Rubicund: Similar because both mean reddish, especially of the face; differs because rubicund is a formal, learned, often jocular word for a florid complexion, while ruddy is plain and robust.
  • Florid: Similar because both describe a red-flushed face; differs because florid can also mean over-elaborate (of style) and implies heavy, possibly unhealthy redness, while ruddy implies vigorous health.
  • Crimson: Similar because both are red; differs because crimson is a specific deep, vivid red of blood or dye, without the warm brownish glow of ruddy.
  • Rufous: Similar because both mean reddish-brown; differs because rufous is a technical word used in zoology and ornithology for rusty-brown coloration, not for faces or light.
  • Scarlet: Similar because both are red; differs because scarlet is a bright, vivid red that emphasizes intensity of hue, while ruddy is a warm, moderate, healthy reddishness.
  • Copper: Similar because both denote reddish-brown; differs because copper suggests the metallic sheen of the metal, while ruddy describes organic, glowing redness in people and light.
