Note: the origin of Hellēnes is disputed. It is often linked to the Thessalian region of Hellas and to the sanctuary of Dodona, and some scholars connect it with the Selloi; the story that the Greeks descend from a hero named Hellen, son of Deucalion, is a later genealogical myth, not a linguistic derivation. The name may be pre-Greek. "Greek" itself is a different, Latin-derived exonym from Graeci.
