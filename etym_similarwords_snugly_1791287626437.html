• cozily (cosily): Similar because it means warmly and comfortably; differs because cozily stresses soft warmth while snugly stresses tight, gap-free fit.
  • comfortably: Similar because it means with ease and contentment; differs because comfortably is broad and neutral while snugly specifies enclosed tightness.
  • tightly: Similar because it means with close fit and no looseness; differs because tightly can imply restriction or tension while snugly implies pleasant security.
  • securely: Similar because it means safely held without slipping; differs because securely stresses safety while snugly stresses warmth and coziness.
  • warmly: Similar because it evokes heat and affection; differs because warmly can be purely emotional while snugly is physical enclosure.
  • neatly: Similar because it suggests compact exactness; differs because neatly stresses tidiness while snugly stresses enveloping comfort.
  • compactly: Similar because it means fitted into small space; differs because compactly is technical and neutral while snugly is domestic and tender.
  • close: Similar because it means near with little gap; differs because close is generic proximity while snugly adds comfort to closeness.
  • intimately: Similar because it suggests tender closeness; differs because intimately is relational or sexual while snugly is usually innocent and thermal.
  • smugly: Similar in sound and in the sense of self-contentment; differs because smugly is critical of complacency while snugly is approving of comfort.
