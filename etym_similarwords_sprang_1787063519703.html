• leapt: Similar because both are past tenses meaning to jump suddenly; differs because "leapt" (leaped) is purely about jumping through the air, while "sprang" also covers bursting forth, issuing, and originating.
  • jumped: Similar because both name a sudden movement off the ground; differs because "jumped" is neutral, generic, and covers every kind of hop, while "sprang" implies suddenness, vigor, and often the idea of emergence.
  • bounded: Similar because both mean moving forward in energetic leaps; differs because "bounded" emphasizes repeated elastic leaps (like a deer), while "sprang" is a single sudden action.
  • vaulted: Similar because both mean leaping over or onto something; differs because "vaulted" implies clearing an obstacle with a swing or spring, while "sprang" is more general.
  • burst forth: Similar because both mean issuing suddenly and forcefully; differs because "burst forth" is a phrasal verb emphasizing violence and volume, while "sprang" can be gentle or quick.
  • gushed: Similar because both describe water or liquid issuing out; differs because "gushed" emphasizes abundant flow and also means effusive speech, while "sprang" stresses the sudden start of the flow.
  • welled up: Similar because both describe liquid rising or issuing from beneath; differs because "welling up" is gradual and rising, while "sprang" is sudden and outward.
  • arose: Similar because both can mean to come into being or originate; differs because "arose" is formal, abstract, and static, while "sprang" is more physical, dynamic, and sudden.
  • emerged: Similar because both mean coming out into view; differs because "emerged" is gradual and neutral, while "sprang" implies speed and force.
  • originated: Similar because both can mean to have a beginning or source; differs because "originated" is analytical and passive (usually with from/in), while "sprang" is vivid and verbal.
  • erupted: Similar because both mean bursting out suddenly; differs because "erupted" suggests violence, fire, or volcanic force, while "sprang" need not be violent.
