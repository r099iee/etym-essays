• Hurling: Similar because both denote throwing with great physical force; differs because 'hurling' emphasizes sheer muscular strength, heavy mass, and violent trajectory, whereas 'flinging' emphasizes swiftness, suddenness, and wide arm motion.
  • Tossing: Similar because both involve casting something through the air; differs because 'tossing' is gentle, casual, light, or nonchalant, lacking the forceful vigor and emotional momentum of 'flinging'.
  • Casting: Similar because both describe sending an object outward; differs because 'casting' is formal, deliberate, or artistic (such as casting a fishing line, casting spells, or casting lots) with controlled intent.
  • Pitching: Similar because both involve throwing; differs because 'pitching' implies targeted, calibrated delivery toward a specific strike zone or recipient (as in baseball or pitching a tent).
  • Slapping: Similar because both involve swift kinetic limb movement; differs because 'slapping' denotes striking a surface flatly rather than propelling an object through the air.
  • Heaving: Similar because both involve throwing heavy items; differs because 'heaving' emphasizes the immense bodily strain and slow lifting required to move an enormous weight.
  • Slinging: Similar because both describe throwing with an arc; differs because 'slinging' implies the use of a strap, harness, sling, or looping lever to propel the projectile.
