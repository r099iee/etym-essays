• Somatic: Similar because both mean "of the body"; differs because somatic is strictly clinical and physiological (as in somatic symptom disorder), while corporeal carries the philosophical soul-versus-body contrast.
  • Physical: Similar because both mean "material, tangible"; differs because physical is the generic term for all matter and nature, while corporeal is anchored to the idea of a living body versus spirit.
  • Bodily: Similar because both mean "pertaining to the body"; differs because bodily is plain everyday English, while corporeal is formal and philosophically weighted.
  • Carnal: Similar because both relate to the flesh; differs because carnal is loaded with moral and sensual judgment, while corporeal is neutral and descriptive.
  • Material: Similar because both mean "of matter"; differs because material refers to stuff in general, while corporeal implies a formed, organized, living body.
  • Incarnate: Similar because both involve having a body; differs because incarnate is theological and implies a spirit taking flesh, while corporeal simply names the bodily state.
