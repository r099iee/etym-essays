• Happiness: Similar because both denote a positive emotional state of joy, contentment, and well-being; differs because happiness is an everyday, broad term describing any agreeable mood or satisfaction, whereas felicity conveys an elevated, refined, or transcendent sense of intense bliss, blessedness, or elegant appropriateness.
  • Bliss: Similar because both signify supreme, undisturbed delight and ecstasy; differs because bliss emphasizes an overwhelming, almost mystical or heavenly state of pure emotional rapture and complete oblivion to worldly cares, whereas felicity encompasses intellectual aptness, harmonious literary style, and cultivated well-being.
  • Beatitude: Similar because both designate supreme blessedness and joyful perfection; differs because beatitude is steeped in Christian theology, referring specifically to the eternal, divine blessedness enjoyed by the saints in the presence of God or the blessings proclaimed by Christ in the Sermon on the Mount, whereas felicity operates across secular literature, philosophy, and everyday aesthetics.
  • Aptness: Similar because both denote striking suitability, fitness, and precision in expression; differs because aptness focuses functionally on correctness, logical relevance, and exact correspondence to the subject matter, whereas felicity adds an essential dimension of grace, beauty, aesthetic charm, and inspired literary luck.
  • Elegance: Similar because both characterize refined, graceful, and tasteful style in writing or behavior; differs because elegance focuses on restraint, polished simplicity, and polished poise, whereas felicity emphasizes the fortuitous, inspired knack of hitting upon the exact, delightful phrasing.
  • Euphoria: Similar because both describe heightened states of intense joy or elation; differs because euphoria is an acute, often fleeting or chemically/biologically induced surge of intense psychological excitement that may border on irrational mania, whereas felicity is an enduring, tranquil, or harmoniously composed state of flourishing.
