Note: The modern verb to pounce arose via functional shift from the Middle English noun pounce ("a hawk's claw"), which itself derived from Old French poinçon ("a punch"), because raptor claws pierce flesh like punching bodkins. A separate scribal noun pounce (fine powder for parchment) derived from Latin pūmex ("pumice"), merging phonetically with the falconry term.
