sluggard

Note: the base slugge is itself of obscure origin, likely imitative of slow movement or borrowed from Scandinavian, compare Norwegian slugg meaning a heavy slow person; the -ard suffix arrived with Norman words such as coward and drunkard and became a native English scorn-marker.
