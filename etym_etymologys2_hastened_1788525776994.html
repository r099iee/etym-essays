Modern English (present)

Note: The Germanic noun *haifstiz was borrowed into Gallo-Romance during the Merovingian/Carolingian period, becoming Old French haste, which was subsequently re-borrowed into English following the Norman Conquest. In the 16th century, English added the native Germanic formative suffix -en to create the expanded verb hasten.
