• rare: Similar because both denote something uncommon; differs because rare simply marks low frequency without judging quality, whereas recherché implies rarity that has been deliberately sought out and selected, carrying an evaluation of excellence.
  • obscure: Similar because both place the thing out of the common way; differs because obscure stresses difficulty of understanding or lack of fame, whereas recherché stresses deliberate selection and an air of exclusivity.
  • exotic: Similar because both suggest something unusual and striking; differs because exotic points to foreignness and strangeness from afar, whereas a recherché item need not be foreign at all — it may be a rare native form pursued for its refinement.
  • esoteric: Similar because both describe what is known only to a few; differs because esoteric emphasizes knowledge restricted to an inner circle or initiates, whereas recherché emphasizes the quality of having been carefully chosen as choice and rare.
  • arcane: Similar because both denote what is hidden from ordinary understanding; differs because arcane stresses secrecy and the guarded mystery of esoteric lore, while recherché stresses refined selectivity rather than secrecy.
  • recondite: Similar because both describe the abstruse and out-of-the-way; differs because recondite is a learned word for knowledge that is hard to penetrate, whereas recherché is a term of taste describing things chosen for their rare excellence.
  • abstruse: Similar because both can describe difficult, out-of-the-way matter; differs because abstruse is purely intellectual, describing what is hard to comprehend, whereas recherché spans objects, tastes, and manners as well as ideas.
  • affected: Similar because both carry a hint of pretension; differs because affected is the pejorative label for behaviour put on for effect, whereas recherché names the quality itself (choice, out-of-the-way) and only secondarily suggests the showing off.
  • pretentious: Similar because both can imply display for its own sake; differs because pretentious openly accuses someone of claiming more importance than is warranted, while recherché may be genuinely deserved praise that merely risks seeming showy.
  • precious: Similar because both describe over-refined, affected taste; differs because precious is used chiefly of affectedly dainty language or manners, while recherché attaches to the object or subject itself as rare and choice.
  • refined: Similar because both imply polish and cultivation; differs because refined describes any carefully polished style or manners, without the idea of rarity, whereas recherché adds the deliberate seeking-out and the out-of-the-way quality.
  • exquisite: Similar because both are terms of praise for delicate perfection; differs because exquisite stresses intense beauty or precision of workmanship, while recherché stresses rarity and selectivity rather than beauty as such.
  • choice: Similar because both mean "selected with care"; differs because choice is a plainer word applying to any good specimen among many, whereas recherché adds the foreign register and the sense of recondite exclusivity.
  • select: Similar because both imply picking out the best; differs because select often refers to a restricted group or high standard (a select company), while recherché emphasizes the searching that found the rare object.
  • out-of-the-way: Similar because both name what lies off the beaten track; differs because out-of-the-way is colloquial and geographic, describing places or bypaths, whereas recherché is a refined term of taste for choice and rare things.
