• blotches: Similar because they are irregular marks; differs because they soak or pool, not necessarily rubbed.
  • smudges: Similar because they are blurred dirty marks; differs because they are lighter, often from fingers.
  • streaks: Similar because they are elongated marks; differs because they are linear, not necessarily greasy.
  • daubs: Similar because they aredabs of paint/substance; differs because they stress clumsy application.
  • blurs: Similar because they lose sharpness; differs because they are optical, not necessarily dirty.
  • stains: Similar because they soil; differs because they penetrate and persist.
  • spots: Similar because they are small marks; differs because they are round and neat.
  • splotches: Similar because they are irregular patches; differs because they are splash-like, not rubbed.
  • slurs: Similar because in the moral sense they defame; differs because they are verbal, not physical.
  • libels: Similar because they damage reputation; differs because they are legal-written falsehoods.
  • smears (verb: slanders): Similar because same form attacks name; differs because noun stresses the mark itself.
  • films: Similar because thin coatings cover; differs because they are even and often transparent.
  • traces: Similar because faint marks remain; differs because traces need not be dirty.
  • blemishes: Similar because they mar a surface; differs because they are flaws, often permanent.
