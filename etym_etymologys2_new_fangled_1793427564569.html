↓

↓
Modern English (current)
new-fangled
‘newly invented, faddish, suspiciously novel’

Note: The second element -fangled is an archaic grammatical relic containing the Old English verb fōn (‘to catch, seize’) combined with the Germanic dispositional/frequentative suffix -el (the same formative found in brittle, from break). The pseudo-participial suffix -ed was added in the 16th century by analogy with past participles.
