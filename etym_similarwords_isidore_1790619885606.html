• encyclopedist: Similar because Isidore compiled universal knowledge; differs because it is a role, not a person, and usually denotes Diderot's circle.
  • etymologist: Similar because Isidore追 traced word origins; differs because modern etymology is scientific while his was allegorical.
  • Boethius: Similar because he also transmitted antiquity to the Middle Ages; differs because he focused on logic and music in Ostrogothic Italy.
  • Bede: Similar because he was a roughly contemporary encyclopedic monk; differs because he worked in Northumbria on history and computus.
  • Cassiodorus: Similar because he founded Vivarium to copy classics; differs because he was a statesman-monk, not a bishop-scholar.
  • Pliny the Elder: Similar because his Natural History was Isidore's chief model; differs because he was a Roman imperial compiler, not Christian.
  • Varro: Similar because his De lingua Latina pioneered Latin etymology; differs because he was pagan republican, largely lost except via Isidore.
  • Archbishop: Similar because it was Isidore's office; differs because it names rank, not achievement.
  • Doctor of the Church: Similar because it is his title; differs because it is honorific shared with Augustine and others.
  • compiler: Similar because he gathered excerpts; differs because it understates his synthesis and can sound pejorative.
