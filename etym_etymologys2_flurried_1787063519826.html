Note on the debate: the origin of "flurry" (1686) is uncertain. The OED suggests an echoic / onomatopoeic formation in the family of "flutter", "fluster", and "flirt", possibly related to English dialect "flurr" ("to scatter, toss about"). No earlier ancestor is attested, so no pre-1686 stage can be reconstructed without guesswork.
