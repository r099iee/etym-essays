- Visigothic capital - Toledo became the capital of the Visigothic kingdom, and its famous Third Council (589) under King Recared marks the abjuration of Arianism, so the name entered history as the emblem of Germanic kings entering Catholic civilization.
- Medieval Christian Spain - as the medieval capital of Castile, seat of the School of Translators and of the primatial see of the Spanish church, Toledo stood for the meeting of Muslim, Jewish, and Christian learning.
- The name's extension - by the 17th century Toledo had lent its name to a celebrated style of steel and sword, so the word doubled as a byword for quality metalwork.
- Contemporary sense - Toledo remains a living Spanish city and UNESCO site, still naming a style of ornamental steel, while also designating an American city in Ohio.
