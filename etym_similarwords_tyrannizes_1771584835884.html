• Rules: Similar in meaning exercises sovereign authority over; differs because rules is neutral, legitimate governance, with none of the lawlessness, cruelty, and abuse that tyrannizes implies.
  • Reigns: Similar in meaning holds sovereignty over; differs because reigning is legitimate, ceremonial, or merely nominal rule, whereas tyrannizing is rule by caprice and fear.
  • Dominates: Similar in meaning exerts control over; differs because domination is broader — it can be passive (a mountain dominates a view) and need not be cruel or political, while tyrannizes names active, despotic, cruel control.
  • Oppresses: Similar in meaning treats with harsh, burdensome power; differs because oppresses describes the grinding condition of the ruled (injustice weighing them down), whereas tyrannizes focuses on the conduct and caprice of the ruler.
  • Bullies: Similar in meaning uses power to intimidate or coerce; differs because bullying is small-scale, informal, physical, and petty, while tyrannizing inflates the same behaviour to the register of despotism.
  • Subjugates: Similar in meaning brings under control by force; differs because subjugation is the act of conquest, subduing an enemy or people, whereas tyrannizing is the ongoing abuse of power already held.
  • Enslaves: Similar in meaning deprives of freedom; differs because enslavement is outright ownership or total bondage, an extreme beyond the household tyranny Lady Wang describes.
  • Dictates: Similar in meaning gives commands; differs because dictating concerns the issuing of orders and may be perfectly legitimate, while tyrannizing names the cruel, capricious abuse of that authority.
  • Coerces: Similar in meaning forces someone to act; differs because coercion is pressure applied to a specific act, while tyrannizing is a whole pattern of despotic rule over another's life.
  • Terrorizes: Similar in meaning governs by instilling fear; differs because terrorizing stresses the fear produced in the victim, whereas tyrannizing stresses the lawless, self-serving power of the ruler.
  • Persecutes: Similar in meaning treats a person or group cruelly; differs because persecution is systematic harassment of a group for identity or belief, whereas tyrannizing is the capricious domination of a tyrant over those subject to him.
