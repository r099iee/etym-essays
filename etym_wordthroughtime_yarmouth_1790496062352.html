- Brittonic river name, babbling or eddying water; Celtic and opaque
- Saxon Gerne-mutha, harbour at the river mouth; working and tidal
- 1086 Domesday Gernemutha, fishing vill with herring dues; Norman and taxable
- 1208 King John's charter, free borough and fair; civic and ambitious
- Medieval Jernemuth and Yernemouth, great herring mart of the North Sea; Hanseatic and rich
- 16th-17th century Yarmouth Roads, Crusoe's wreck and Dutch wars; stormy and strategic
- 1724 Defoe praises the finest quay after London; admiring and commercial
- Victorian resort plus curing yards, piers, Rows, and bloaters; smoky and holiday
- Ruskin's grandfather's Yarmouth, sailor's embarkation and delightful return; familial and romantic
- Modern Great Yarmouth, offshore wind, heritage Rows, and beach; post-fishing and renewable
