- Biblical Antiquity to 18th c. - Biblical Genealogy: Linked exclusively to the biblical patriarch Shem (Genesis 10) as a genealogical lineage of ancient Near Eastern peoples.
- Late 18th c. (1781) - Rigorous Linguistic Classification: Coined by August Ludwig von Schlözer in Göttingen as a strictly scientific philological descriptor for related Afroasiatic languages like Hebrew, Arabic, and Aramaic.
- Mid-to-Late 19th c. - Pseudo-Scientific Racialization: Misappropriated by racial theorists like Renan and Gobineau to construct a rigid 'Semitic' racial archetype, leading to Wilhelm Marr's 1879 coinage of 'Antisemitismus'.
- Early-to-Mid 20th c. - Polarized Political Ideology: Overshadowed almost entirely by aggressive anti-Semitism in Europe, while occasionally championed in philosemitic and cultural defenses of Jewish civic equality.
- Contemporary Era - Asymmetric Morphological Base: Almost obsolete as an independent conversational term; preserved in historical linguistics and functioning overwhelmingly as the root for 'anti-Semitism'.
