NOTE: The origin of "mound" is genuinely unknown - no PIE or other ancestor can be responsibly reconstructed. The leading theories are (1) derivation from Old English mund "hand, protection, defence" via the idea of a protective earthwork; (2) derivation from post-classical Latin mundus "world, earth" via Anglo-Latin or Middle French; and (3) an independent native or dialectal "heap" word. None is decisive, so the chain above begins at the first attested English form.
