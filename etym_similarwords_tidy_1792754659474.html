• neat: Similar because both describe an environment or person characterized by orderly arrangement, cleanliness, and the absence of mess; differs because neat emphasizes visual simplicity, trimness, and precise compactness, whereas tidy carries stronger connotations of ongoing habitual upkeep, domestic orderliness, and colloquial quantitative abundance.
  • orderly: Similar because both refer to arrangements where everything is in its proper place and governed by systematic structure; differs because orderly operates at a higher, more formal or institutional register (such as an orderly procession or orderly file storage), whereas tidy is intimate, domestic, and colloquial.
  • trim: Similar because both suggest cleanliness, proportion, and well-groomed fitness; differs because trim specifically denotes physical compactness, sleekness, or the clipping away of superfluous margins (a trim beard, a trim yacht), lacking tidy's secondary economic sense of "substantial."
  • spruce: Similar because both denote a fresh, crisp, well-groomed appearance; differs because spruce typically applies to personal dress and grooming rather than spacious domestic environments, and carries a flavor of smart, dapper self-presentation.
  • shipshape: Similar because both describe an environment where all gear, tools, and items are stowed in their designated compartments; differs because shipshape derives from nautical discipline, conveying rigorous functional seaworthiness and readiness for heavy weather, whereas tidy is comfortable household parlance.
  • considerable: Similar because in quantitative contexts, both denote an amount worthy of respect and satisfaction; differs because considerable is formal, analytical, and strictly scalar, whereas tidy (in "a tidy sum") is colloquial, folksy, understated, and emotionally satisfied.
  • substantial: Similar because both qualify a financial amount or tangible asset as solid and significant; differs because substantial focuses on weight, material reality, and legal or fiscal consequence, whereas tidy conveys an aestheticized, pleasant sense of comfortable sufficiency.
  • handsome: Similar because both can describe a generous or pleasingly large quantity (a handsome profit / a tidy profit); differs because handsome emphasizes elegance, dignified generosity, or striking visual proportion, whereas tidy highlights compact, self-contained adequacy without ostentation.
  • uncluttered: Similar because both identify spaces free from chaotic piles or unnecessary litter; differs because uncluttered describes the negative state of absent junk, whereas tidy actively asserts methodical, attentive housekeeping.
  • methodical: Similar because both suggest an underlying psychological adherence to systematic habits; differs because methodical describes deliberate cognitive processes and procedures, whereas tidy describes the visible aesthetic outcome of physical tidiness or financial consolidation.
