• Euskara: Similar because it is the native name of the same language; differs because it names the language specifically and carries the insider, official register rather than the English ethnonym.
  • Euskaldun: Similar because it refers to a Basque person or speaker; differs because it literally means "one who has Basque" and is a native identity term, not an English ethnonym.
  • Vascon: Similar because it names the ancient people from whom the Basques descend; differs because it is a historical and archaeological label for the Roman-era tribe, not for modern people.
  • Gascon: Similar because the name derives from the same Latin Vascones; differs because Gascons are a Romance-speaking people of Gascony, not Basques, and their language is Occitan.
  • Aquitanian: Similar because it is an extinct language of south-western Gaul widely thought to be related or ancestral to Basque; differs because it is attested only fragmentarily and is not the same language.
  • Iberian: Similar as a pre-Roman, non-Indo-European language of the Iberian Peninsula; differs because Iberian is a different, poorly understood language and is not Basque.
  • Celtiberian: Similar as an ancient Iberian language; differs because it is Celtic and Indo-European, the opposite of Basque in linguistic affiliation.
  • Biscayan: Similar as a Basque dialect and regional identity; differs because it names one western variety, not the whole people or language.
  • Pyrenean: Similar as a geographical descriptor of the same mountain region; differs because it is not an ethnic or linguistic term and can apply to French, Spanish and Andorran contexts.
  • bodice: Similar in the garment sense as a fitted upper-body garment; differs because bodice is the generic term, while a basque is a specific style extending to the hips.
  • corset: Similar as a shaping undergarment; differs in construction, historical period and the fact that a corset is typically laced and boned rather than a fitted bodice.
  • beret: Similar as a Basque-associated item of dress; differs because it is a soft round cap, not a bodice, and is worn by many other peoples too.
