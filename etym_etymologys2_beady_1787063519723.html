Debate note: the PIE etymon of the Germanic prayer word is uncertain. The usual guess links it to *bʰedʰ- to bend (prayer posture), but this is not universally accepted, and it must not be confused with the homophonous PIE *bʰedʰ- to dig, pierce (source of Latin fodio). The Germanic stage and the English metonymy are solid.
