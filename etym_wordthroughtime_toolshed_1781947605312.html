- The garden outbuilding - From the 19th century toolshed (variously tool-shed or tool shed) names the small outbuilding where garden implements are stored, a plain compound of tool and shed.
- Compound consolidation - In the 20th century the closed form toolshed becomes standard, replacing the hyphenated spelling of the Edwardian era.
- Modern comic overtone - In later British culture the word gains a mildly comic aura (the shed as a man's private retreat), while still naming the practical storehouse of the gardener's implements.
