• Socialism: Similar because advocates social ownership and critique of capitalism; differs because in Marxist stages socialism is transitional state ownership, communism is final stateless goal, and in Western use socialism often means regulated welfare capitalism.
  • Marxism: Similar because theoretical foundation of modern communism; differs because Marxism is the analytic method and critique, communism is the political goal and movement built on it.
  • Leninism / Marxism-Leninism: Similar because vanguard-party doctrine of actually existing communism; differs because specific to Lenin's theory of imperialism and democratic centralism, not all communists accept it.
  • Collectivism: Similar because prioritizes group over individual ownership; differs because broader, includes non-Marxist communal economies and even corporate collectivism, less specific about class revolution.
  • Communalism: Similar because small-scale living with shared goods; differs because usually apolitical intentional communities or tribal organization, without revolutionary state project.
  • Egalitarianism: Similar because seeks equality; differs because moral principle compatible with liberal democracy, does not require abolition of private property.
  • Capitalism: Similar because antonym in same economic-systems frame, useful contrast; differs because private ownership, market allocation and profit motive are opposite of common ownership.
  • Anarchism / anarcho-communism: Similar because also aims at stateless classless society; differs because rejects vanguard party and transitional state, seeks immediate abolition of hierarchy.
  • Maoism: Similar because variant of communism emphasizing peasantry and continuous revolution; differs because specific Chinese historical strategy, not universal definition.
  • Trotskyism: Similar because revolutionary communist current; differs because advocates permanent world revolution against Stalinist socialism-in-one-country.
  • Distributism / cooperativism: Similar because advocates widespread / cooperative ownership; differs because retains private/cooperative property and markets, rejects nationalization.
  • Kibbutzism / utopian socialism: Similar because voluntary communal living; differs because small voluntary communes within capitalism, not state seizure of power.
