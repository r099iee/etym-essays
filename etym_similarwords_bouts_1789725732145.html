• Attacks: Similar because they denote sudden onsets of illness or violence; differs because attack stresses aggression and severity, bout stresses bounded episode with recovery.
  • Fits: Similar because they mean paroxysms of coughing or temper; differs because fit implies convulsive suddenness, bout implies a round or spell.
  • Spells: Similar because they mean short periods of weather or sickness; differs because spell is vaguer and often milder, bout suggests a contest with the ailment.
  • Episodes: Similar because they are discrete instalments of illness or story; differs because episode is clinical-literary and neutral, bout is pugilistic and colloquial.
  • Paroxysms: Similar because they are sharp recurrences of pain or cough; differs because paroxysm is technical-medical and peak-focused, bout covers the whole round.
  • Matches: Similar because boxing bouts are matches; differs because match is the scheduled contest, bout is each round or the whole encounter in combat sports.
  • Rounds: Similar because bouts consist of rounds and illnesses go rounds; differs because round is one circuit or bell-to-bell unit, bout is the full contest or illness spell.
  • Sessions: Similar because they are bounded periods of work or drinking; differs because session is deliberate and often pleasant, bout can be involuntary suffering.
  • Flare-ups: Similar because arthritic bouts are flare-ups of chronic disease; differs because flare-up stresses re-ignition from baseline, bout stresses the contest itself.
