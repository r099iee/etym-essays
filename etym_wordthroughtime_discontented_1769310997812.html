- [Negative formation sense] - "Discontented" began as the simple negative of "content," meaning not satisfied or at ease with one's present state.
- [Pervasive restlessness sense] - It evolved to describe a deep, chronic inner restlessness — a nagging dissatisfaction that colours one's whole temperament rather than a single specific complaint.
- [Grumbling class sense] - In earlier centuries "the discontented" could denote a restive portion of society grumbling against its lot, lending the word overtones of agitation and complaint.
- [Contemporary Sense] - Today it describes an unsatisfied, uneasy state with one's circumstances, often suggesting self-pity, irritability, and a permanently unfulfilled disposition.
