Debate note: The etymology is genuinely uncertain. The OED marks prod as of obscure origin. The most commonly proposed hypothesis links it to Middle English brode ("goad") from Old Norse broddr ("spike"), with an ultimate PIE root *bʰrews- ("to swell, sprout" — source of bristle and bride in some analyses); however, the vowel shift and the exact pathway are not fully explained, and an independent, onomatopoeic coinage cannot be ruled out.
