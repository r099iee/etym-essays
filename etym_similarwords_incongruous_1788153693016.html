• Incompatible: similar because both name a failure to fit together; differs because incompatible is logical and absolute (things that cannot coexist), while incongruous is perceptual and aesthetic (things that look wrong together, even when coexistence is possible).
  • Inconsistent: similar because both describe self-contradiction; differs because inconsistent operates within one set of claims or acts, whereas incongruous operates between a thing and its setting.
  • Out of place: similar because both mean not belonging to the surroundings; differs in register — the phrase is plain and spoken, the adjective is literary and precise.
  • Inappropriate: similar because both condemn a mismatch; differs because inappropriate judges the act against rules or decency, incongruous judges the fit against perception and proportion.
  • Discordant: similar because both express jarring disunity; differs because discordant belongs to sound, harmony and opinion (a discordant note, voice), incongruous to any visible or thinkable juxtaposition.
  • Ill-assorted / mismatched: similar because both describe elements that do not go together; differs in being more casual and more expressly about pairs (mismatched socks), incongruous about a thing versus its frame.
  • Anomalous: similar because both pick out what deviates from the expected order; differs because anomalous stresses the statistical and scientific outlier, incongruous the aesthetic or social clash.
  • Apt / congruous (antonyms): similar by being the exact opposite member of the same family; differs in polarity: congruous things “run together” fittingly, and naming the antonym sharpens the boundary.
