- "17th c. - laboratory and pharmacy: 'desiccated' salts and powders; a quiet technical verb borrowed from learned Latin"
- "18th-19th c. - explorers and geographers: the 'desiccation' of regions becomes a scientific question — did Central Asia and the Sahara dry up in historic times? Victorian geography writes whole debates in the word"
- "20th c. - ecology and conservation: desiccation names a process of land degradation; wetlands 'desiccate'; droughts write the word into the news"
- "Today - a double life: laboratory precision at one end (desiccators, desiccants), and at the other a strong figurative sting — 'desiccated' minds, styles, and institutions, witty and dry as bone"
