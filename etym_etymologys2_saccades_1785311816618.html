Debate: the origin of Middle French
saquer "to pull, jerk" is uncertain —
possibly from Germanic *sakkōn "to put
in a sack" (via sac "sack") or of
imitative origin. The English eye-movement
term (1859) was borrowed from the French
horsemanship term via the notion of a
quick, jerky pull.
