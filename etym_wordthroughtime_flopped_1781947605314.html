- Echoic origins - Flopped (from the echoic flap/flip/flop family) has long named a body falling or sitting down heavily and limply, the sound and action matching.
- Theater slang offshoot - In the 1890s American theater slang, 'a flop' becomes a play that fails, as if collapsing heavily on stage; the verb and noun diverge into distinct careers.
- Modern informal use - Today flopped is a warmly comic, informal verb for a total, cushioned collapse of exhaustion, and floppy (hanging loosely) even gave the world the floppy disk of 1971.
