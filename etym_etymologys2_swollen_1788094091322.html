NOTE: The participial form is inherited directly: Old English geswollen (with the ge- prefix of past participles, dropped in Middle English). Cognates confirm the Germanic verb: Old Norse svella, Old Frisian swella, Old Saxon swellan, Middle Dutch swellen, Old High German swellan, German schwellen, Swedish svälla. The PIE root *swel- "to swell" (Pokorny) is supported by the early Indo-Iranian cognate Sanskrit śvayati "to swell, grow" (Rigveda). Caution: PIE *swel- "to swell" is a different root from the homophonous *(s)wel- "to burn, shine" (the family of Latin sōl "sun"); the two are routinely confused in popular etymology but are separate roots with separate descendant families.
