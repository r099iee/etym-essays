Note on Orthography & Phonology: The spelling tomb preserves the Latin/French consonant b, which became silent in late Middle English as the final cluster /mb/ simplified to /m/ (compare womb, bomb, comb). The vowel shifted during the Great Vowel Shift from Middle English /uː/ to modern /uː/ with a distinct rounded back quality.
