- Archaic-Doric maritime towns - Lindos, Ialysos and Kamiros known as seafarers and colonists; perception is brave Doric sailors on the edge of the Greek world
- Classical-Hellenistic superpower - after 408 BCE synoecism, Rhodes as rich, independent naval arbiter and art centre crowned by the Colossus; connotation of wealth, liberty and technical mastery
- Roman to Byzantine province - admired for schools and laws but politically eclipsed; perception shifts to venerable cultural relic and pilgrim stop
- Hospitaller frontier fortress 1309–1522 - Christendom's bulwark celebrated for chivalric resistance; emotional register of heroic siege and romance
- Ottoman then Italian then Greek resort - from 1522 Ottoman town to 1912 Italian colony to 1948 Greek region to modern UNESCO mass-tourism icon; now connotes sunshine, heritage layering and holiday Rhodes
