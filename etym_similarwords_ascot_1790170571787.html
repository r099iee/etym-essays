  • Cheltenham: Similar because both are jump-and-flat festival courses; differs because Cheltenham is March National Hunt heartland, while Ascot is flat summer royalty.
  • Aintree: Similar because both are iconic racecourses; differs because Aintree hosts the Grand National steeplechase, while Ascot hosts royal flat pageantry.
  • Derby (the race): Similar because both denote elite flat contests; differs because Derby is a single Epsom classic, while Ascot is a whole five-day meeting.
