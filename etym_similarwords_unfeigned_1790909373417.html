• Sincere: Similar because both denote genuineness and absence of deceit; differs because 'sincere' is a common, general descriptor for honest intent or feeling, whereas 'unfeigned' carries a heightened literary, solemn, or scriptural weight, explicitly emphasizing that an emotion is not simulated or staged.
  • Heartfelt: Similar because both describe deeply felt, authentic emotions; differs because 'heartfelt' emphasizes emotional warmth and deep personal resonance, whereas 'unfeigned' specifically highlights the lack of hypocrisy, playacting, or contrivance.
  • Genuine: Similar because both signify realness rather than imitation; differs because 'genuine' applies equally to material objects (genuine leather, genuine diamonds) as well as emotions, whereas 'unfeigned' is restricted almost exclusively to human emotions, faith, and conduct.
  • Artless: Similar because both denote freedom from deceit and artificiality; differs because 'artless' often carries connotations of natural simplicity, innocence, or lack of sophisticated skill, whereas 'unfeigned' simply asserts truthfulness without implying naivety.
  • Unaffected: Similar because both describe behavior free from pretense; differs because 'unaffected' focuses on natural manners and modesty without pretension, whereas 'unfeigned' emphasizes the absolute truth of deep internal feelings.
  • Hypocritical (Antonym): Directly opposite because 'hypocritical' denotes feigning virtuous character, moral beliefs, or feelings that one does not actually possess.
