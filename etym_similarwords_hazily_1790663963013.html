• vaguely: Similar because it denotes lack of precision; differs because vaguely is broader and can imply evasiveness, while hazily suggests a soft veil over something once clearer.
  • dimly: Similar because it means faintly perceived or remembered; differs because dimly stresses weak light or fading strength, whereas hazily stresses diffused obscurity.
  • faintly: Similar because it means weakly or barely; differs because faintly concerns intensity, while hazily concerns distinctness of outline.
  • mistily: Similar because it evokes seeing through moisture; differs because mistily is often tearful or sentimental, while hazily is neutral and cognitive.
  • foggy / foggily: Similar because both name atmospheric obscurity; differs because fog is thicker and more blocking, while haze is thinner and merely softening.
  • hazy vs. blurry: Similar because both lack sharpness; differs because blurry often comes from motion or focus failure, while hazily implies an intervening veil of mist or time.
  • indistinctly: Similar because it means without clear boundaries; differs because it is formal and technical, whereas hazily carries atmospheric and memorial colour.
  • obscurely: Similar because it denotes lack of clarity; differs because obscurely can imply deliberate concealment or difficulty, while hazily is usually innocent forgetting or weather.
  • dreamily: Similar because it suggests soft unfocused perception; differs because dreamily adds reverie and pleasure, whereas hazily need not be pleasant.
  • nebulously: Similar because it means cloud-like and formless; differs because it is bookish and often criticizes woolly thought, while hazily can be affectionate or forgiving.
