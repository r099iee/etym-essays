Note on uncertainty: The link to *dʰewbʰ- “deep / dive” is the traditional story — the bird named for its sudden plunging dive — but the etymology is not certain; some scholars suspect the Germanic word is merely echoic of the dove’s deep “dū…ū” coo. English is not descended from Latin columba: the Romance descendants of that word (French colombe, pigeon) are entirely separate lines.
