- Shaded, shadowed place - Old French sombre as gloomy spot under trees or clouds; literal and neutral
- Dark in colour - transferred to clothing, skies and rooms that absorb light; tasteful but muted perception
- Gloomy in mood - applied to faces, thoughts and prospects; melancholy and serious connotation
- Gravely serious in manner - Victorian moral sense: sombre response, sombre duty; dignified, adult, unsmiling
- Modern blend - both colour term (sombre suit) and mood term (sombre news); restrained, realistic, slightly literary in British English
