Debated step: the Arabic-Persian side is the most cited reconstruction (Greek sigillátos "sealed, stamped" via Latin sigillum "seal") but several handbooks label Persian saqirlāt "of obscure origin"; the Greek link is plausible inference, not a surviving textile contract, and the direction of borrowing between Arabic and Persian is itself disputed.
