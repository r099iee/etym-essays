- **Ancient Gaulish Settlement** - A sacred site centered around a spring and a local deity, Nemausus, revered by the Volcae Arecomici tribe.
- **Roman Colonia Augusta Nemausus** - A flourishing, strategically important Roman city, a beacon of Roman civilization and engineering in Gaul, perceived as a center of power and culture.
- **Medieval Nismes** - A regional center, still retaining some Roman structures, but its primary identity shifted towards a market town and a hub for textile production, particularly serge. Its Roman grandeur was largely forgotten or overshadowed by local feudal politics.
- **Early Modern Nîmes (16th-18th Century)** - A significant industrial center, especially for textiles, with its 'serge de Nîmes' gaining international renown. The city's name became associated with quality fabric, though the direct link to the fabric 'denim' was still evolving.
- **19th-20th Century Nîmes** - Re-discovery and appreciation of its Roman heritage, leading to its recognition as a major tourist destination. The city's name became globally recognized through the widespread adoption of 'denim,' though many users of the fabric were unaware of its geographical origin.
- **Contemporary Nîmes** - A vibrant modern city that proudly embraces its dual identity as 'the French Rome' and the birthplace of denim. It is perceived as a cultural and historical treasure, a blend of ancient grandeur, Mediterranean charm, and a subtle, yet profound, influence on global fashion.
