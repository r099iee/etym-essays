- General sense (succession & inheritance) - From the 16th century, used broadly of inheritance and the transmission of property, titles, or qualities to successors; neutral, legal-philosophical register.
- Biological sense (19th century) - Adopted by Darwinian and pre-Mendelian biology as the key term for the transmission of physical and mental traits; solemn, scientific register.
- Contemporary sense - Largely overshadowed in technical science by genetics and DNA, but still common in everyday and popular usage for the passing on of characteristics; shifted to a more informal, general register.
