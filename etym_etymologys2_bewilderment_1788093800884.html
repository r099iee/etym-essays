Note: no secure PIE reconstruction exists for the element behind "wild"; a pre-Germanic root *welt- / *welət- has been proposed (Pokorny) but is debated, so the chain begins at Proto-Germanic. The verb wilder and the noun bewilderment are English-internal formations of the 17th–19th centuries, not inherited words.
