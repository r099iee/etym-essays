*k̂as- / *kʷas-
"to shake, break, shatter into pieces"
↓
Latin (c. 100 BCE)
quassāre (frequentative of quatere)
"to shake violently, shatter, break"
↓
Vulgar Latin (c. 500 CE)
*cascāre / *cascum
"a broken piece, potsherd, hard shell, skull"
↓
Old Spanish / Portuguese (c. 1200 CE)
casco
"skull, potsherd, helmet, ship's hull, wooden barrel"
↓
Middle French (c. 1500 CE)
casque
"helmet; hollow casing; wooden barrel"
↓
Early Modern English (c. 1550 CE)
caske / cask
"a staved wooden barrel for holding liquids"
↓
Modern English (Plural c. 1600 CE)
CASKS
"multiple staved wooden barrels for storing or aging liquids"
