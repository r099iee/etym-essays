• Moonlight: Similar because it is the same lunar glow under its plain name; differs because moonlight is neutral and literal (astronomy, romance, navigation), while moonshine leans poetic or sly — light with a wink, or nonsense and liquor beyond it.
  • Nonsense: Similar because both dismiss talk as empty of sense; differs because nonsense is the blunt general verdict, while moonshine paints the emptiness — shining, silvery, and faintly affectionate rather than cross.
  • Rubbish: Similar because both rubbish talk as worthless; differs because rubbish (British) is brusque and contemptuous (that's rubbish), while moonshine laughs where rubbish sneers — glow versus garbage.
  • Balderdash: Similar because both are old jolly words for foolish talk; differs because balderdash is a loud tavern word for windy pretension, while moonshine is cooler and more luminous — froth versus phosphorescence.
  • Twaddle: Similar because both name silly idle chatter; differs because twaddle is petty and twittering (gossip over tea), while moonshine can be grand — tall tales, political promises, lover's vows.
  • Drivel: Similar because both condemn talk as empty flow; differs because drivel drools (slow, stupid, dribbling), while moonshine shines — drivel is contemptible, moonshine merely ungraspable.
  • Bootleg: Similar because both name illicit liquor and its trade; differs because bootleg stresses illegal sale and smuggling method (the bottle down the boot), while moonshine stresses secret night manufacture — the still rather than the deal.
  • Hooch: Similar because both are slang for strong rough liquor; differs because hooch (from Hoochinoo) is general rotgut of any origin, while moonshine is specifically the untaxed night-distilled spirit with its folklore of stills and revenuers.
  • Illusion: Similar because both name brightness that deceives; differs because illusion is the psychologist's and magician's formal term (optics, stagecraft), while moonshine is the poet's — deception you half enjoy being under.
