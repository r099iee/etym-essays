• Carried: Similar because both mean moved from one place to another as a load or passenger; differs because carried is the plain, everyday, unmarked term, while borne is elevated, literary, and implies weight, dignity, or endurance.
  • Transported: Similar because both mean conveyed from place to place; differs because transported suggests deliberate, organized conveyance over a distance, while borne emphasizes passive support or endurance in a single act.
  • Conveyed: Similar because both mean carried from one point to another; differs because conveyed implies careful, intentional transfer of a message, goods, or person, whereas borne is more physical and often more passive.
  • Lifted: Similar because both involve raising something from a lower to a higher place; differs because lifted is a completed upward action, while borne describes the ongoing state of being carried or supported.
  • Supported: Similar because both mean held up from beneath; differs because supported is active and structural (a beam supports a roof), while borne is passive - the weight is borne by something else.
  • Endured: Similar because both can mean suffering or hardship tolerated; differs because endured is the usual active verb for withstanding pain, while borne is its passive participle - pain is endured by a person but is borne as a load.
  • Brought: Similar because both are participles of carrying motion; differs because brought means caused to come to a place by a person's action, while borne emphasizes being carried as weight without the agent's motion being specified.
  • Born: Similar because it is historically the identical participle, and the two words are pronounced alike; differs because born is now restricted to the sense of coming into being (he was born), while borne keeps every other sense - carried, supported, endured, and also 'given birth to' (has borne a child).
  • Shouldered: Similar because both mean taken on a burden; differs because shouldered is active and implies accepting responsibility by choice, while borne is passive and describes the weight already carried.
  • Sustained: Similar because both mean kept up under pressure or held without breaking; differs because sustained emphasizes continued maintenance or support (sustained effort, sustained damage), while borne emphasizes the act of carrying or enduring itself.
