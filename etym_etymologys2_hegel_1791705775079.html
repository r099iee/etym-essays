Modern German Surname (c. 18th c.)
Hegel
"German Idealist philosopher"

Note: Hegel is a South German (Alemannic/Swabian) topographic surname originating as a diminutive of Hag ('hedge, fence, enclosure'). In world philosophy, the unadorned name universally designates Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel (1770–1831).
