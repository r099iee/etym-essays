- 18th–19th c. - Literal military fortification and civil flood defense: stacking bags filled with sand to absorb cannon fire and floods.
- 1880s–1900s - 19th-century underworld crime weapon: a weighted sock of sand used by street footpads to knock victims senseless.
- 1910s–1930s - Literary and colloquial expansion: describing individuals physically concussed or psychologically dazed and stunned by shock.
- 1940s–1970s - Poker, golf, and drag-racing slang: deliberately playing below one's ability to deceive opponents before pouncing.
- Late 20th–21st c. - Corporate, legal, and political jargon: blindsiding an adversary with concealed evidence or sudden hostile takeovers.
