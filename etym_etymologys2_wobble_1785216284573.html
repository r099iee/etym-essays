Debate: origin is uncertain per the OED. Wobble is first recorded c. 1650s (variant of wabble), possibly from Middle Low German wabbeln or a Scandinavian cognate; the -le suffix is frequentative and the base is probably echoic. No secure pre-English ancestor is known.
