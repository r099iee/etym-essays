• Revereth: Similar because both express high spiritual and moral regard for excellence; differs because revereth involves sacred awe, solemn veneration, and humble deference before divine or majestic authority, whereas admireth includes aesthetic appreciation and intellectual wonder.
  • Venerateth: Similar because both denote profound respect for worth and dignity; differs because venerateth emphasizes age, historical sanctity, and institutional holiness (e.g., venerating an ancient sage or shrine), while admireth focuses on the brilliance and beauty of virtues or achievements.
  • Marveleth: Similar because both share the ancient root meaning of being struck with wonder; differs because marveleth denotes passive astonishment, bewilderment, and surprise without necessarily implying moral approval, whereas admireth blends wonder with positive evaluation.
  • Esteemeth: Similar because both involve assessing someone as having high value; differs because esteemeth is analytical, measured, and rational (a deliberate judgment of worth), while admireth carries emotional warmth, awe, and enthusiastic appreciation.
  • Laudeth: Similar because both involve honoring excellence; differs because laudeth is an active, vocal performance of public praise and singing of hymns, whereas admireth is primarily an internal attitude or disposition of contemplation.
  • Extolleth: Similar because both celebrate greatness; differs because extolleth (literally ‘lifting up high’) focuses on verbal amplification and public proclamation, whereas admireth denotes the internal sentiment of reverence and wonder.
  • Wondereth: Similar because both stem from cognitive amazement; differs because wondereth often conveys doubt, questioning, or curiosity (‘he wondered why’), while admireth is settled, captivated, and admiring.
  • Applaudeth: Similar because both express approval of noble action; differs because applaudeth is kinetic, demonstrative, and immediate (like clapping hands), whereas admireth is enduring, inward, and deeply reflective.
