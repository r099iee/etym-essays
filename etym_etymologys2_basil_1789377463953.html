Pre-Greek Substrate / Mycenaean (c. 15th–12th c. BCE)
*gʷa-si-le-u (Linear B: qa-si-re-u)
"local chieftain, nobleman, official"
Note: Mycenaean Greek qa-si-re-u denoted a local chieftain. Etymology likely pre-Greek substrate, later elevated to sovereign king.
