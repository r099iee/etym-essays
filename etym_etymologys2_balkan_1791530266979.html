Note: the pre-Turkish origin is unknown. The usual suggestion is a Thracian or Dacian substrate word for a wooded mountain, but no securely attested form survives, so the first line above is a hypothesis, not a documented stage. The meaning "wooded mountain" within Turkish is itself inferred from the range's appearance rather than from an independent gloss.
