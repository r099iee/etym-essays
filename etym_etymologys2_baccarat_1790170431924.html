Note: Origin unknown (CNRTL, Etymonline, OED). Probably borrowed from Provençal bacarra, game of the Midi. The common Italian baccara zero theory and derivation from the Lorraine town Baccarat (glass) are unproven: French is attested before Italian (Panzini), and the town name is an unrelated homonym. 1935 argot baccara faillite is secondary.
