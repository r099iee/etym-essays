Debate: haw (of hawthorn) and the fruit
haw both trace to OE haga "hedge"; haguþorn
literally means "hedge-thorn". The PIE root
*kagʰ- is a reconstruction (Pokorny), and
the exact Proto-Germanic intermediate form
is debated.
