- 14th-15th c. legal ending - formal termination of claims, actions, hostilities in law and chronicles
- 16th-17th c. religious-philosophical rest - cessation from labour, sin, desire; moral and Sabbath overtones
- 18th-19th c. medical-industrial stop - cessation of breathing, pulse, work, payment; measurable endpoint
- 20th c. behavioural-health term - smoking cessation, cessation programmes; deliberate quitting as treatment
- Modern formal-literary use - without cessation as elegant intensifier for relentless, non-stop action
