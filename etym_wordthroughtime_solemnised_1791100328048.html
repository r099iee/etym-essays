- Roman antiquity - Sollemnis as annual religious festival - calendrical, communal, festive
- Late Latin / Medieval - Solemne as grave religious rite - reverent, awe-filled, ecclesiastical
- 14th-16th century English - Solemn as dignified and serious - respectable, weighty, moral
- 1590s-1700s - To solemnize: to celebrate with rites or make grave - active, ceremonial, Shakespearean
- Victorian era - To solemnise marriage/funeral duly - legalistic, institutional, Dickensian irony
- Present day - Chiefly legal term for performing marriage - technical, formal, rarely used for mood
