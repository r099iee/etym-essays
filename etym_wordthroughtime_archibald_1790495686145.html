- Old High German Erchanbald - precious-bold, bishop and warrior name; German and boastful
- Norman Archambault - Frankish name in French mouths; feudal and carried to England
- Scots Douglas Archibald - the Grim, Bell-the-Cat, and Angus earls; border, powerful, and ballad-sung
- Scott Archibald (1808) - Marmion's grave counsellor-earl; romantic and honourable
- 1830s Ruskin missing Archibald - castle without lord; empty, puzzling, and mourned
- Victorian Archibald everywhere - ministers, bankers, and emigrants; respectable and ubiquitous
- Modern Archie - short, cheeky revival for babies and princes; familiar and light
