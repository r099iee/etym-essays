Note: Vulgar Latin *barra is of uncertain origin. A Gaulish *barros derivation proposed by Gamillscheg is regarded by the OED as discredited because it does not suit the sense. Welsh bar and Irish barra are loans from English, not sources. PIE *dek- (2) follows Watkins via Etymonline; OED notes primary Germanic sense was hairy tail or tuft of hair.
