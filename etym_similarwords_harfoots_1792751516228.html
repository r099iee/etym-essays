• Stoors: Similar because both are the primary historical kindreds or breeds of Hobbits who migrated from the Vales of Anduin; differs because Stoors were broader, heavier, river-dwelling boat-lovers who wore boots and could grow down on their chins, whereas Harfoots were smaller, browner, burrow-digging, strictly beardless, and unshod.
  • Fallohides: Similar because both constitute ancient ancestral branches of the Hobbit race; differs because Fallohides were taller, fairer-skinned, tree-loving, adventurous, and of aristocratic temperament (founding the Took and Brandybuck lines), whereas Harfoots were the common, numerous, earth-dwelling agrarian majority.
  • Hobbits: Similar because Harfoots are themselves Hobbits; differs because Hobbits (Halflings) is the overarching species and cultural ethnonym comprising all three branches, whereas Harfoots designates that specific ancestral strain.
  • Halflings: Similar because both denote the small mortal race created by Tolkien; differs because Halflings is the external exonym used by Men of Gondor and Rohan based on their diminutive height, whereas Harfoots is an internal tribal/kindred name.
  • burrowers: Similar because both describe organisms that dig underground dwellings; differs because burrowers is a broad zoological or descriptive term applying to badgers, moles, or subterranean creatures, whereas Harfoots are sentient humanoid folk who transformed burrowing into cozy domestic architecture (smials).
  • Periannath: Similar because both designate the diminutive folk of Middle-earth; differs because Periannath is the formal Sindarin Elvish collective name for Hobbits, whereas Harfoots is the anglicized translation of the specific rustic Westron breed.
  • troglodytes: Similar because both inhabit subterranean dwellings; differs because troglodytes carries connotations of primitive, savage cave-dwelling creatures, whereas Harfoots constructed warm, luxurious, paneled, and carpeted underground smials with round doors and pantry-lined hallways.
  • kindred: Similar because both denote family, clan, or ancestral tribal groupings; differs because kindred is an abstract sociological term for blood relations, whereas Harfoots is the proper name of this specific historical clan.
  • dwarf-friends: Similar because Harfoots lived long with Dwarves and acquired many craft traditions from them; differs because dwarf-friend is an honorific title given to outsiders (like Gimli's companions), whereas Harfoots is an ethnic breed with an inherent historical affinity for mountain Dwarves.
  • rustic: Similar because Harfoots embody the simple, pastoral, and earthy lifestyle; differs because rustic is an adjective for unrefined countryside folk in general, whereas Harfoots is a proper fictional ethnonym.
