Notes: the cognomen adds the suffix -iānus “belonging to” to the nomen Quīntilius, which is itself formed from the praenomen Quīntus. The spelling Quinctilius also occurs for the clan; the difference is orthographic and gentilicial, not a separate derivation. The English form probably came through French Quintilien or directly from Latin, dropping the final -us. The other two names are separate: Marcus is a praenomen of uncertain origin (anciently linked with Mars), and Fabius is the gentilicium of the Fabii, traditionally connected with faba “bean”.
