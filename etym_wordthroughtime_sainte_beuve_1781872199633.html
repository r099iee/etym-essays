- The critic in his own century - Charles Augustin Sainte-Beuve (1804-1869) was the dominant French literary critic of the mid-19th century, famous for his biographical portraits of writers.
- Authority in Victorian England - In the later 19th century the name becomes a critical warrant in English essays (Pater, Arnold), standing for intimate, impressionistic taste against abstract rule.
- Modern reassessment - In the 20th century Proust's Contre Sainte-Beuve reopens the case, and the name now denotes a historical method — biographical criticism — debated and largely superseded in modern theory.
