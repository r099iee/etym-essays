Debate: Old English beorg "protection" and beorg "hill" are homophones from distinct PIE roots (*bhergh- "protect" vs "high"); herebeorg "army-shelter" uses the protective sense. The modern spelling harbor/harbour results from 16th-c. metathesis of er → ar and vowel reduction.
