• Slid: Similar because both are smooth motion along a surface; differs because sliding is smooth, continuous, and often deliberate or elegant, while slithering is sinuous, twisting, or uncontrolled.
  • Slipped: Similar because both involve loss of grip on a surface; differs because slipping is a sudden, brief loss of footing, while slithering is a longer, repeated, gliding motion.
  • Glided: Similar because both are smooth, quiet motion; differs because gliding is graceful and effortless, often through air or water, while slithering is wormy, snake-like, and along the ground.
  • Skidded: Similar because both are uncontrolled sliding; differs because skidding is a violent, screeching sideways slide (vehicles, tyres), while slithering is slower and sinuous.
  • Crawled: Similar because both are low, ground-level motion; differs because crawling uses limbs or is on hands and knees, while slithering is limbless or whole-body gliding.
  • Crept: Similar because both are slow, quiet motion; differs because creeping emphasises stealth and slowness, while slithering emphasises the slithery, twisting motion itself.
  • Wriggled: Similar because both are twisting motion; differs because wriggling is quick, worm-like squirming in place or over short distances, while slithering is smooth forward gliding.
  • Squirmed: Similar because both are twisting body motion; differs because squirming is restless, uncomfortable writhing, often in place, while slithering is directional locomotion.
  • Snaked: Similar because both describe snake-like motion; differs because “snaked” as a verb emphasises a winding, turning course, while slithering emphasises the gliding, sliding contact with the ground.
  • Sidled: Similar because both are cautious, unobtrusive movement; differs because sidling is moving edge-on to stay unnoticed, while slithering is the physical glide of a body over a surface.
  • Oozed: Similar because both suggest slow, semi-fluid motion; differs because oozing is of thick liquids or crowds — viscous and passive — while slithering is the active, sinuous movement of a body.
