• Thereafter: Similar because both are formal pronominal adverbs indicating subsequent time; differs because thereafter simply means "from that point forward in time" without implying immediate succession, urgent reaction, or direct causal consequence, whereas thereupon denotes promptness or direct consequence.
  • Consequently: Similar because both express that one event follows as a result of another; differs because consequently is purely logical and causal without inherent temporal immediacy, while thereupon often unites instant temporal succession with causal connection.
  • Subsequently: Similar because both denote an event happening after an antecedent event; differs because subsequently is a neutral temporal marker that can describe occurrences separated by days, years, or centuries, whereas thereupon implies an immediate or consecutive follow-up.
  • Forthwith: Similar because both denote prompt, non-delayed action; differs because forthwith is an imperative or procedural command meaning "without delay; right now," whereas thereupon is a descriptive connective linking two narrative actions.
  • Thereby: Similar because both are archaic-formal pronominal compounds; differs because thereby means "by that means, through that agency, or in that manner," emphasizing the instrument or mechanism rather than the temporal sequence or subject matter.
  • Whereupon: Similar because both denote an immediate sequel to an event; differs because whereupon is a relative or connective adverb introducing a subordinate clause ("he bowed, whereupon the crowd cheered"), whereas thereupon is an independent adverb modifying the main predicate.
  • Immediately: Similar because both indicate instantaneous succession; differs because immediately is common, direct, and conversational, lacking the stately register, legal precision, and causal nuance of thereupon.
