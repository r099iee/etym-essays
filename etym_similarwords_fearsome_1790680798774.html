• frightening: Similar because it denotes causing fear; differs because it is the plain general term, while fearsome adds awe and formidable scale.
  • fearful: Similar because it shares the fear root and can mean fear-causing; differs because it more often means afraid, and sounds milder and older.
  • terrifying: Similar because it denotes inspiring terror; differs because it is stronger, naming extreme fright rather than dread-tinged impressiveness.
  • formidable: Similar because it denotes impressively powerful opposition; differs because it stresses difficulty to overcome rather than emotion of fear.
  • daunting: Similar because it denotes disheartening by difficulty; differs because it centres on discouragement, not menace.
  • menacing: Similar because it denotes threatening harm; differs because it stresses the promise of evil intent, while fearsome stresses the overwhelming effect.
  • intimidating: Similar because it denotes making others feel cowed; differs because it focuses on the social act of overawing rather than inherent fierceness.
  • alarming: Similar because it denotes raising fear suddenly; differs because it stresses the startling warning, not sustained dread.
  • awesome: Similar because both use -some and once meant dread-tinged wonder; differs because awesome has brightened toward admiration, fearsome keeps its darkness.
  • dreadful: Similar because it denotes inspiring dread; differs because it now often just means very bad, bleached of literal fear.
  • redoubtable: Similar because it denotes worthy of fear and respect; differs because it is formal and often admiring, while fearsome is visceral.
  • ferocious: Similar because it denotes savage fierceness; differs because it stresses violence of attack, while fearsome stresses the fear it produces.
