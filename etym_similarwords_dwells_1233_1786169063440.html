• Resides: Similar because it denotes living or abiding in a specified place as one's home; differs because resides is formal, official, or administrative (a legal address, a jurisdiction), whereas dwells carries literary, poetic, and spiritual depth.
  • Inhabits: Similar because it means occupying a place; differs because inhabits is typically used of populations or species in a region or ecosystem, whereas dwells centres on personal, intimate, continuous living.
  • Abides: Similar because it means remaining or staying in a place or state; differs because abides stresses patient endurance and standing fast under conditions (with spiritual weight), whereas dwells stresses settled residence and mental lingering.
  • Lives: Similar because it is the plain, everyday word for residing; differs because lives is neutral and all-purpose, whereas dwells is elevated and adds depth of presence.
  • Sojourns: Similar because it means staying in a place; differs because sojourns explicitly implies temporary, transient lodging, whereas dwells implies permanence and rootedness.
  • Lodges: Similar because it means staying in accommodation; differs because lodges suggests rented, temporary rooms or physical embedding (a bullet lodged in a wall), whereas dwells means a settled home.
  • Tarries: Similar because it means remaining or lingering; differs because tarries is a short-term delay, whereas dwells in residence is permanent (though the idiom dwell on preserves the tarrying sense).
  • Lingers: Similar because it means remaining for a time; differs because lingers is short-term and often about reluctant departure, whereas dwells (especially dwells on) implies sustained, repeated return.
  • Stays: Similar because it means remaining in a place; differs because stays is general and short-term, whereas dwells is weighty and long-term.
  • Remains: Similar because it means continuing in a place or state; differs because remains is colourless and passive, whereas dwells adds settled, deliberate presence.
  • Settles: Similar because it means establishing residence; differs because settles emphasizes the act of becoming established, whereas dwells describes the ongoing state of living there.
  • Occupies: Similar because it means inhabiting a space; differs because occupies is neutral about permanence and often legal or military in tone, whereas dwells is warm and permanent.
  • Broods (on): Similar to the mental sense of dwells on (lingering over thoughts); differs because broods implies dark, gloomy, resentful rumination, whereas dwells on is a broader, more neutral mental lingering.
  • Perseverates (on): Similar in the clinical sense of dwelling on a single thought; differs because perseverates is a technical psychiatric term for pathological repetition, whereas dwells on covers ordinary preoccupation.
  • Harps (on): Similar in returning repeatedly to a subject; differs because harps on implies irritating repetition in speech, whereas dwells on may be silent mental occupation.
  • Haunts: Similar because a memory can dwell in the mind as something that haunts it; differs because haunts personifies the memory as actively pursuing the person, whereas dwells is the person's own lingering attention.
