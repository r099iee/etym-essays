NOTE: The PIE root *(s)twer- is a standard reconstruction (Watkins; Pokorny), linking Latin turba “turmoil” and Greek tyrbē “tumult”, but some scholars treat the Germanic stir-family as an independent expressive formation, so the PIE link is probable rather than certain. The prefix a- is the reduced Old English preposition on, the same element as in asleep, afoot, abed.
