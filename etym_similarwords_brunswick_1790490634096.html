• Braunschweig: Similar because it is the identical city in German; differs because Braunschweig is the official German form, while Brunswick is the English exonym used in histories and London naming.
  • Hanover / Hannover: Similar because it is the sister Welf capital and Hanoverian dynastic partner; differs because Hanover became the electorate and kingdom, while Brunswick remained the duchy.
  • Brunswick Square (Bloomsbury): Similar because it carries the same name in London; differs because it is a garden square and address, not a German city or state.
  • New Brunswick (Canada): Similar because it commemorates the same House of Brunswick; differs because it is a Canadian province named for George III, not the German territory itself.
  • Duchy of Brunswick / Brunswick-Lüneburg: Similar because it is the state ruled from the city; differs because it names the principality and army, while Brunswick alone often means the town.
  • House of Brunswick / House of Welf: Similar because it is the dynasty seated at Brunswick; differs because it names the family and kings, while Brunswick names the place.
  • Wolfenbüttel: Similar because it was the later Brunswick dukes' residence town; differs because it is a distinct town and palace, not the capital itself.
  • Bloomsbury: Similar because Brunswick Square lies within it and shares its professional-literary character; differs because Bloomsbury is the whole district, while Brunswick Square is one square within it.
  • Black Brunswickers: Similar because they were Brunswick troops famed in Britain; differs because it names soldiers and their legend, not the city or square.
  • Caroline of Brunswick: Similar because she gave the London square its name; differs because she is a person and queen, while Brunswick is the place and house she married into.
