- Bedding & straw (14th c.) - straw or rushes strewn as bedding for animals; preserves the core idea of things scattered over the ground.
- Portable couch (14th–17th c.) - a covered bed carried on poles; an aristocratic, ceremonial register that later fades from common use.
- Offspring sense (18th c.) - the animals produced at one birth (“a litter of kittens”); domestic and affectionate.
- Scattered refuse (18th–20th c.) - discarded paper, cans, and waste left in open public places; acquires a strongly negative, civic and environmental register tied to anti-litter campaigns.
- Contemporary sense - the dominant meaning is dispersed public waste (trash); the brood sense survives, and the verb “to litter” means to strew carelessly.
